- Reference equality is initialization-source identity, not evidence that a

- Small disturbance-free drift and finite values are not a stability proof,
  disturbance-response result, robustness result, or safety certificate.
- Current, voltage, saturation, slew, action identity under perturbation,
  achieved P/Q, cross-channel response, decoupling, controller value,
  information value, MARL, topology generalization, EMT, HIL, and deployment
  remain untested.
