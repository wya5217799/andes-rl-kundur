## Conclusions

- **Bounded positive finding**: ANDES 2.0.0 can construct four `REGCV1`
  records on the unchanged packaged Kundur network with exact static-generator
  ownership, and its `RenGen` interface can route isolated direct writes and
  restores to each record at the observed zero-valued references.
- **Decisive negative finding**: this registered status-disabled-chain
  formulation does not clear the native TDS initialization gate. A converged
  power flow, correct object inventory, `u=0` readback, and software setpoint
  identity are therefore insufficient evidence of a valid converter-level
  dynamic plant.
- **Route decision**: R384 stops the registered `REGCV1` formulation before a
  signed dynamic `Pref/Qref` authority test. No deterministic P/Q-decoupling,
  residual-headroom, information-value, MARL, robustness, or topology study is
  authorized on this formulation.

## Limits and causal boundary

- The formal JSON localizes the failure to native TDS initialization after
  successful setup, power flow, inventory, and direct setpoint checks. It does
  not archive equation-level residuals and therefore does not prove which
  internal equation or parameter caused the initialization failure.
- The machine-readable `next_gate` auxiliary field is not scientifically
  admissible on the STOP branch. The immutable artifact is preserved rather
  than rewritten; this feed records the conflict and fails the field closed.
- The `dynamic_chain_replacement` check proves only that the 12 source records
  read back `u=0`; it does not prove that every internal equation was removed
  from the initialized DAE. The negative finding is for the complete
  registered construction, not for `REGCV1` in every network or for ANDES as a
  platform.
- The observed zero-valued `Pref/Qref` baselines mean the exact next-float
  write/read/restore result cannot establish nonzero active/reactive power
  authority, sign, gain, settling, cross-response, or achieved P/Q.
- No valid TDS trajectory exists, so stability, transient performance,
  electrical limits, robustness, topology generalization, EMT, HIL, and
  deployment remain untested.
