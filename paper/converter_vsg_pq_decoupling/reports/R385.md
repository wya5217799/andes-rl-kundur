- [CLM-1070] The record reports zero initialization residuals over 112 DAE

  `6.765994431390254e-10` for omega, and `8.335846879425901e-08` for voltage.
  These are preserved observations from an invalid round and are quarantined
  from scientific claims.
- [CLM-1070] The preserved classifier returned
  `STOP-REGCV1-CLEAN-INITIALIZATION`; its only false check was
  `post_init_references`. The recorded static-generator source values were
  `(p,q)=(0,0)` at generator 1 and `(7,0)` at generators 2--4, whereas REGCV1
  references were `(7.268029078708952,1.0946333735232954)`,
  `(7,2.280479537682396)`, `(7,2.3238455255936796)`, and
  `(7,1.060909399595964)`.
- [CLM-1070] The sealed runner takes the static `p/q` read inside
  `post_init_references()` only after `system.TDS.init()`. That ordering
  violates the plan's required post-power-flow, pre-init source snapshot.
- [CLM-1070] Installed ANDES REGCV1 declares the linked StaticGen index with
  `replaces=True`; its `Pref/Qref` services are initialized from linked
  StaticGen `p/q`. During TDS initialization, ANDES's DAE compactor marks each
  replaced static generator and sets its effective status to zero. Therefore
  the post-init static values are not a valid measurement of the registered
  pre-init source. The mismatch cannot support a scientific STOP.

## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment -- ANALYSIS-INVALID**: the formal endpoint was captured
  at the wrong lifecycle time. The immutable STOP output is preserved but is
  excluded from scientific use.
- **Cause boundary**: this is a runner/endpoint-order defect, not evidence that
  REGCV1 failed initialization, passed initialization, has physical authority,
  or is suitable for P/Q decoupling. Completed solver steps do not override the
  prospective endpoint violation.
- **Eligible successor**: R385 is not retried. One separately registered and
  sealed successor may snapshot linked static-generator `p/q` immediately
  after power flow and before `TDS.init()`, retain those immutable values, and
  compare them with post-init REGCV1 references under the same card, topology,
  thresholds, and no-training boundary.
