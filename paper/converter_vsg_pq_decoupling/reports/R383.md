  prospective manuscript line because it changes the device object, actuator
  authority, endpoints, initialization risk, and claim boundary relative to
  the stopped R382 formulation.

  algorithm family.
- **Platform/topology decision**: retain ANDES 2.0.0 and Kundur two-area network
  connectivity for the opening mechanism study; change only the prospectively
  registered converter/VSG device formulation and parameters.
- **Execution decision**: the next evidence round asks only whether the four

  `Pref/Qref` authority. Controller design and training remain unauthorized.
