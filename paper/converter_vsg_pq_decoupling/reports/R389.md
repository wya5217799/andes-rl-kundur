## Conclusions

- **Valid bounded negative**: the exact ANDES 2.0.0, unchanged Kundur,
  four-stock-REGF2, installed-default-card object passes structural and native
  initialization checks but fails the pre-registered no-exogenous-action
  stationarity gate over 0.2 seconds.
- **Failure boundary**: the result does not show malformed construction,
  reference loss, nonzero initialization residuals, clamped limits,
  non-finite values, native nonconvergence, or broad electrical-envelope
  failure. It shows coherent drift larger than the registered clean-object
  threshold on this one deterministic trajectory.
- **Route decision**: Q-0107 closes negative. Stop stock REGF2 before testing
  Paux/Qaux authority, P/Q decoupling, deterministic controllers, headroom,
  information value, or MARL. Do not tune the card, relax the threshold,
  change tolerance/horizon, or retry inside R389.

## Limits

- One phasor-domain simulator, one topology, one operating point, one stock
  parameter card, one numerical tolerance, and one 0.2-second trajectory with
  no exogenous post-init action or disturbance were tested. The result is not
  a general REGF2/VSM rejection.
- No action, disturbance, controller, or learning policy was tested. The feed
  supports no authority, decoupling, coordination, controller-value, learning,
  generalization, robustness, safety, EMT, HIL, real-time, hardware, or
  deployment claim.
- Time-domain growth does not distinguish a physical small-signal mode from a
  numerical/model interaction. No eigenanalysis, tolerance sensitivity, gain
  ablation, causal identification, or region-of-attraction analysis was
  performed.
- Electrical-envelope compliance does not certify hardware safety; the failed
  drift gate is a stationarity criterion, not a protection or grid-code test.
