## Frozen setup

Zero-training boundary extension. The sealed 24-scenario bank, q0 arm, and
frozen centralized TD3 seeds 17/53/89 covered by current correction CLM-0610
were reused without selection or retraining. The R286 declared Line_4/5/6
inter-area corridor proxy was the only plant change: each line's resistance
and reactance were multiplied by
`k in {2.5, 3.0}`. The two co-primary lower-is-better endpoints remained
`normalized_sync_loss_hz2` and first-3-s
`fast_inter_area_iae_hz_s`. The registered estimator was a hierarchical
seed-then-shared-scenario ratio-of-means bootstrap with 10,000 resamples and
seed 2026073001. The decision thresholds and all action, storage, injection,
completion, and failure-retention guards were frozen before traces.

## Conclusions

- **C1 - bounded robustness result**: on the sealed 24-scenario Kundur bank,
  the frozen centralized differential-allocation controller retains material
  improvement on both registered physical endpoints when the declared
  Line_4/5/6 corridor resistance/reactance multiplier is extended through
  k=3.0, without retraining. [CLM-0650]
- **C2 - boundary interpretation**: the scanned range establishes survival,
  not the collapse point. The synchronization-loss advantage declines more
  than the first-3-s inter-area advantage, but both remain interval-supported
  and above the registered 50% retention threshold at k=3.0. [CLM-0650]
- **C3 - evidence tier**: this is sealed-bank time-domain evidence for one
  frozen centralized controller family and one declared impedance-scaling
  proxy. It is not a stability certificate, SCR measurement, topology
  generalization result, decentralized/MARL result, or deployment claim.
  [CLM-0650]

## Limits

- One Kundur topology, one 7<->8 triple-circuit corridor definition, the same
  24 signed-PQ disturbance scenarios, and only k=2.5 and k=3.0 were tested.
- Corridor resistance/reactance scaling is a declared weaker-tie proxy. No
  unit-valid SCR conversion, second corridor, outage, fault, or protection
  model was evaluated.
- The evaluated learned arm is centralized TD3 with frozen seeds. No
  weak-grid retraining, checkpoint selection, shared-policy comparison,
  decentralized execution, or new information pattern was introduced.
- The endpoints quantify relative synchronization loss and the first-3-s
  inter-area transient. They do not establish sustained common-frequency
  restoration.
- Guard passage means no recorded violation under this tested contract; it
  does not establish formal safety or stability.
- The location read has n=6 scenarios per group and is descriptive.
