## Conclusions (bounded)

- C1 [linearized small-signal]: static differential allocation materially
  modulates classical inter-area mode damping in the learned beneficial
  direction, and the mapping is not globally monotone. The manuscript may
  state only this bounded empirical form — any "allocation creates damping"
  or monotone-law phrasing is out of bounds.

  grows as aggregate inertia shrinks — placement matters more when inertia
  is scarce; the motivation anchor for a weak-grid (SCR) study.
