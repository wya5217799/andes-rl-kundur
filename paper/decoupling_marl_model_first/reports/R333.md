## Limits

- Evidence covers one existing active-load device, one signed amplitude and
  rectangular waveform, two operating points, one unchanged topology, and the
  phasor-domain electromechanical simulator only.
- The bank identifies one physical disturbance column, not the arbitrary
  four-node disturbance vector assumed by the frozen R329 package. It neither
  identifies a complete physical disturbance matrix nor validates a successor
  estimator-controller.
- One approximately odd-symmetric signed pair does not establish local or
  global linearity, other load locations, reactive-load disturbances, outages,
  larger amplitudes, other timings, other topologies, or other operating
  conditions.
- The plan described the signed-pair midpoint diagnostic as an energy ratio,
  while the sealed classifier evaluates the normalized L2 amplitude residual
  defined above. Independent recomputation gives literal squared-energy ratios
  of `0.0000063621` and `0.0000049831`; those also pass the unchanged `0.10`
  bound, so the mismatch does not change the formal `QUALIFY` classification.
  It does narrow the interpretation to approximate odd symmetry for the
  registered pair.
- Reduced-versus-physical agreement is an open-loop prediction result. It is
  not controller performance, frequency restoration, closed-loop stability,
  safety, robustness, or causal attribution to retained cross-coupling,
  decoupling, coordination, distributed execution, or learning.
- EVAL is intentionally not run because no learned or distributed policy is
  under examination; its diagnostic score cannot replace the successor
  physical-disturbance and deterministic-control gates.
