## Frozen setup

- Reuses the R359/R360 development/holdout partition byte-for-byte: sixteen
  R352 development pairs (ten R358 accepted physical witnesses as positive
  targets, six inherited relaxed-infeasible as all-zero negative controls),
  sixteen staggered-rise holdout pairs, twenty-five samples, two startup-zero
  steps, and the `0.05` system-pu edge limit with the exact physical
  projection.
- The only change from R360 is the information path: every edge actor still
  receives the exact R359 fifteen-field `LocalEdgeObservation` at the causal
  pre-action instant, and additionally receives the frozen four-field
  neighbour message (frequency deviation, RoCoF, SOC, voltage) of its two
  one-hop neighbour nodes on the frozen communication ring
  `{(0,1),(1,2),(2,3),(0,3)}`, ordered `(source-side neighbour,
  target-side neighbour)`: `(0,1)->(3,2)`, `(1,2)->(0,3)`, `(2,3)->(1,0)`.
  The observation is therefore exactly twenty-three fields; messages carry no
  neighbour action, edge flow, command, scenario identity, disturbance
  identity, future value, realized endpoint, or oracle value. The forbidden
  field list is R360 plus `other_edge_actions`, `neighbour_commands`, and
  `neighbour_edge_flows`.
- The controller family now contains four pre-registered tuning-free
  non-neural maps: the R359 fixed affine plus the R360 RBF kernel ridge
  (frozen median-distance width, `1e-3` regularization), k-NN (`k=5`), and
  quadratic polynomial basis (standardization, no regularization). No
  hyperparameter, kernel, seed, reward, or candidate scan exists; no neural or
  reinforcement-learning algorithm is used.
- The registered endpoint groups (common-coordinate IAE and
  differential-coordinate energy under nominal and mismatch-bounded response
  maps) and all thresholds (2% paired mean, one-sided 95% upper bound,
  subgroup direction, 1.05 worst-case ratio) are unchanged from R359/R360.
- The decision semantics are a pre-registered OR over all four families: any
  passing family yields the positive class; a failure of every family further
  weakens the surveyed information-path hypothesis and stops this route.

## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment - NO-NEIGHBOUR-LEARNABLE-STRUCTURE**: on the exposed
  finite development bank, extending each edge actor's information path with
  the frozen one-hop neighbour messages does not allow any of the four
  pre-registered tuning-free non-neural map families to recover the R358
  physical headroom while meeting both registered endpoint groups. The
  R359/R360 negative results therefore persist under the strongest surveyed
  information-path extension that keeps the map families tuning-free and the
  bank frozen.
- **Question disposition**: Q-0098 closes negative for the registered
  information extension and frozen finite development bank. The surveyed
  information-path hypothesis is further weakened: one-hop neighbour messages
  at this fixed structure do not introduce the surveyed learnable structure.
- **Programme boundary**: the result does not establish that a nonlinear or
  neural residual is impossible in general, that neighbour information is
  globally insufficient, that a common-channel residual authority or
  consensus-estimated global quantity cannot open headroom, or that
  multi-agent learning lacks value; it also does not exhaust every possible
  non-neural function class or every possible message design. Any continuation
  must register a mechanistically different falsifiable question under a new
  round.

## Limits

- The tested objects are four deterministic tuning-free maps per edge on a
  frozen twenty-three-field observation, fitted and judged on an exposed
  finite linear-response development bank. They are not neural networks,
  nonlinear plants, or reinforcement-learning algorithms.
- The information extension is one specific frozen message contract: four
  fields (frequency deviation, RoCoF, SOC, voltage) from the two one-hop
  ring neighbours per action edge. Prediction sharing, DMPC-style trajectory
  exchange, dynamic-average consensus quantities, attention-weighted
  messages, and common-channel residual authority are mechanistically
  different and remain untested.
- The exact information contract and three independent edge actions are
  represented offline; no deployed distributed runtime or nonlinear plant
  trajectory is executed.
- Holdout inventory identity and source closure were checked prospectively,
  but holdout residual labels and counterfactual endpoints remain unexamined
  and no holdout gate is evaluated because the development gate fails.
- The evidence covers one fixed Kundur topology, two exposed operating
  points, selected disturbance channels and signs, and twenty-five response
  samples. Stability, safety, robustness, topology generalization, hardware,
  and deployment remain untested.
- R358 physical witnesses still establish per-case action-space existence in
  ten exposed cases; the R359/R360/R361 negative results do not invalidate
  that earlier physical-feasibility result.
