## Frozen setup

- Reuses the R359/R360/R361 development/holdout partition byte-for-byte:
  sixteen R352 development pairs (ten R358 accepted physical witnesses as
  positive targets, six inherited relaxed-infeasible as all-zero negative
  controls), sixteen staggered-rise holdout pairs, twenty-five samples, two
  startup-zero steps, and the `0.05` system-pu edge limit with the exact
  physical projection.
- The only change from R361 is the message content: every edge actor still
  receives the exact R359 fifteen-field `LocalEdgeObservation` at the causal
  pre-action instant, and additionally receives a four-field prediction
  message from each of its two one-hop neighbour nodes on the frozen
  communication ring, ordered `(source-side neighbour, target-side
  neighbour)` exactly as in R361: `(0,1)->(3,2)`, `(1,2)->(0,3)`,
  `(2,3)->(1,0)`. Each four-field message is the frozen R341 order-12
  separate-input point model's causal open-loop prediction of that neighbour
  node's frequency deviation over the next four `0.2`-second samples,
  generated from the neighbour's causal pre-action sample with the frozen
  disturbance-augmented estimator (R344 frozen output scales, `0.05`
  disturbance scale, `0.01` measurement fraction), zero future residual
  control, and the estimated disturbance held constant. The observation is
  therefore exactly twenty-three fields, the same as R361, with message
  content upgraded from snapshot states to causal model predictions.
- The prediction generator is frozen and tuning-free: no parameter, horizon,
  seed, reward, or candidate scan exists; no neural or reinforcement-learning
  algorithm is used.
- The controller family contains the same four pre-registered tuning-free
  non-neural maps as R361: the R359 fixed affine plus the R360 RBF kernel
  ridge (frozen median-distance width, `1e-3` regularization), k-NN (`k=5`),
  and quadratic polynomial basis (standardization, no regularization).
- The registered endpoint groups (common-coordinate IAE and
  differential-coordinate energy under nominal and mismatch-bounded response
  maps) and all thresholds (2% paired mean, one-sided 95% upper bound,
  subgroup direction, 1.05 worst-case ratio) are unchanged.
- The decision semantics are a pre-registered OR over all four families: any
  passing family yields the positive class; a failure of every family further
  weakens the surveyed information-path hypothesis and stops this route.

## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment - NO-NEIGHBOUR-LEARNABLE-STRUCTURE**: on the exposed
  finite development bank, replacing the one-hop neighbour snapshot messages
  with the frozen R341-model causal prediction trajectories does not allow
  any of the four pre-registered tuning-free non-neural map families to
  recover the R358 physical headroom while meeting both registered endpoint
  groups. The negative results therefore persist across both surveyed
  information extensions: snapshot messages (R361) and DMPC-style shared
  predictions (R362).
- **Question disposition**: Q-0099 closes negative for the registered
  shared-prediction contract and frozen finite development bank. The surveyed
  information-path hypothesis is further weakened across its two strongest
  variants.
- **Programme boundary**: the result does not establish that a nonlinear or
  neural residual is impossible in general, that neighbour information is
  globally insufficient, that a common-channel residual authority or
  consensus-estimated global quantity cannot open headroom, or that
  multi-agent learning lacks value; it also does not exhaust every possible
  non-neural function class, prediction generator, or message design. Any
  continuation must register a mechanistically different falsifiable question
  under a new round.

## Limits

- The tested objects are four deterministic tuning-free maps per edge on a
  frozen twenty-three-field observation, fitted and judged on an exposed
  finite linear-response development bank. They are not neural networks,
  nonlinear plants, or reinforcement-learning algorithms.
- The prediction message is one specific frozen contract: the R341 order-12
  separate-input point model's four-step open-loop frequency-deviation
  prediction with frozen estimator parameters and zero future residual
  control. Other prediction generators, horizons, or model-update schemes are
  mechanistically different and remain untested.
- The exact information contract and three independent edge actions are
  represented offline; no deployed distributed runtime or nonlinear plant
  trajectory is executed.
- Holdout inventory identity and source closure were checked prospectively,
  but holdout residual labels and counterfactual endpoints remain unexamined
  and no holdout gate is evaluated because the development gate fails.
- The evidence covers one fixed Kundur topology, two exposed operating
  points, selected disturbance channels and signs, and twenty-five response
  samples. Stability, safety, robustness, topology generalization, hardware,
  and deployment remain untested.
- R358 physical witnesses still establish per-case action-space existence in
  ten exposed cases; the R359/R360/R361/R362 negative results do not
  invalidate that earlier physical-feasibility result.
