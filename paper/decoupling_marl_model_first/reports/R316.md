## Limits

- One modified Kundur phasor-domain plant, one fixed electrical topology, two local convex holdout points, small active-power inputs, deterministic 5-s responses, and one order-10 ERA family.
- The registered mismatch envelope is empirical on this finite bank: total NRMSE at most 0.15 and peak-normalized vector residual at most 0.20. It is not an uncertainty set with probabilistic coverage or a robust-stability certificate.
- Parent FIR versus ERA identifies reduction fidelity only. Retained-cross versus cross-deleted identifies information value inside this model family only.
- No controller, closed-loop disturbance rejection, communication graph/runtime, distributed agent, reward, learning, topology change, robustness population, EMT/HIL, or deployment was evaluated.
