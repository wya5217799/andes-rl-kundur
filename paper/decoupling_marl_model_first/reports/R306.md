## Frozen setup

R306 adds a separate model-first path without changing legacy V4 or
`AndesBaseEnv`. The prospective plant uses the detected 60-Hz ANDES base,
retains original Kundur G4, disables the case `Line_8` toggler, disables
disturbance and communication failure, initializes with PFlow plus 0.5 s TDS,
and executes exactly five 0.2-s intervals. Every input is zero: no PQ edit, no
GENCLS M/D increment or write, and no ESD1 active-power request. The live
system-base GENCLS target is M/D = 400/200 for each 200-MVA VSG proxy on the
100-MVA system base. Required telemetry covers request, external command,
internal ESD1 power/current/limit/recovery values, achieved power, SOC, actual
M/D arrays, solver state, and the four declared graph/index maps.

## Conclusions

- **Implementation decision**: the new physical-60-Hz, no-M/D-write execution
  seam satisfies the frozen Stage-0 initialization, timing, base conversion,
  actuator-layer telemetry, and graph/index invariants on one nominal canary.
- **Legacy boundary**: the result validates only the additive model-first
  path. It does not reclassify, rescale, or repair historical V4 traces,
  checkpoints, metrics, or claims.
- **Research gate**: `stage1_authorized=false` and
  `training_authorized=false`. A signed active-power Stage-1 experiment, any
  controller comparison, and any learning require a separately reserved and
  sealed round.

## Limits

- This is one deterministic no-disturbance validity canary on one modified
  Kundur phasor-domain plant, not a performance experiment or statistical
  population.
- Zero input verifies readback consistency and absence of hidden drift; it does
  not establish signed actuator authority, local linearity, recovery from a
  pulse, or achieved differential-power neutrality.
- The pure code implements the exact inertia-weighted transform, inverse,
  block reconstruction, source-positive incidence, descriptor Schur identity,
  and finite-difference input-Jacobian seam. R306 does not yet validate a full
  trajectory-linearized predictor or mismatch set.
- No DAPI, DMPC, centralized MPC, neural residual, comparator arm, learning,
  topology variation, uncertainty, robustness, safety certificate, EMT, HIL,
  or deployment result exists in this round.
