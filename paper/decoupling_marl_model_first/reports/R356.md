## Limits

- The result covers one fixed Kundur topology, two exposed operating points,
  four exposed disturbance channels, both signs, twenty-five response steps,
  and the R341 linear response maps. It is a finite development-bank decision,
  not a population estimate.
- Infeasibility is relative to the simultaneous two-percent improvement rule
  and the registered response coordinates. It does not prove that every
  smaller improvement target, every individual scenario, every alternative
  endpoint, or every different deterministic baseline lacks a residual.
- The relaxation removes rather than validates actuator, energy, timing,
  information, estimator, and nonlinear-plant constraints. Its accepted
  infeasibility certificates are one-way rejection evidence; its ten optimal
  solutions are not positive physical-control evidence.
- No neural policy, multi-agent cooperation, training seed, reward,
  architecture, unseen operating point, topology, simulator, delay, dropout,
  fault, outage, stability, safety, robustness, hardware, or deployment claim
  is tested.
- The exploratory one-percent sensitivity is excluded from the formal decision
  and from every claim.
