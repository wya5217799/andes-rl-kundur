## Frozen setup

- R341 applied the frozen R339 separate-input construction method at two
  prospectively registered fresh points, producing point-specific order-12
  candidates. It registered two new waveform families, both signs, and
  channel-specific admissible amplitudes.
- The tested inputs are active-load-consumption disturbances on a 100 MVA
  system base: positive means increased consumption and amplitude is in system
  per-unit. They are physical disturbances, not controller actions. Outputs
  are four inertia-weighted frequency-deviation coordinates formed from
  `(f - 60 Hz) / 60` and sampled every `0.2 s`; both reported errors are
  dimensionless and subtract the point-specific zero reference.
- The sealed bank contained 66 records over 1000 samples: one zero reference
  and 32 disturbed records at each fresh point. The complete inventory,
  thresholds, guards, process ceiling, stage order, and stopping outcomes were
  fixed before physical execution.
- Execution was staged as zero references; Bus 7/8 ramp-hold sentinels at both
  points; Bus 14/15 ramp-hold records at both points; and separated-pulse
  records at all four channels and both points. A complete valid negative
  stage could stop later work; no intermediate metric could alter the frozen
  bank.
- Sixteen single-threaded workers were permitted for eligible records. No
  retry, threshold repair, point replacement, waveform replacement, model
  repair, controller execution, or learning was permitted after opening the
  formal bank.
