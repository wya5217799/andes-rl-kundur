## Conclusions

- **Cause classification - pole only**: R317's infeasible selections were caused before constraint evaluation by the one-sample-delay augmented poles of the DC-inverse static law.
- **Grid decision - do not widen**: the smallest registered scalar already failed, and the radii increased sharply for larger candidates. Extending the outcome-seen scalar grid is not an admissible repair.
- **Single repair specification**: the only eligible next controller form is a full-order observer-based discrete-time quadratic regulator synthesized directly on the one-sample-delay augmented realization. It must retain full common/differential cross blocks, the same R316 model authority, and the unchanged physical node governor; the DC inverse and scalar grid are retired.
- **Comparison and title boundary**: R318 estimates no cross-feedback performance value and supplies no physical, distributed-agent, or learning evidence. The fixed conference title remains unchanged and prospective.

## Limits

- This is a deterministic replay of one rejected controller family on two reduced models. It is a pole-cause diagnosis, not a new controller efficacy study.
- No governed development trace was reached, so no power, ramp, SOC, control-effort, or output-performance difference between arms was observed in R318.
- Observer realizability, state-estimation error, constraints, nonlinear DAE behavior, physical disturbance rejection, current/voltage limits, recursive feasibility, robust stability, communication, distributed execution, agents, MARL, topology generalization, EMT/HIL, and deployment remain untested.
- The pole-only result does not prove that static feedback, cross-feedback, decoupling, or any broad controller class is impossible.
