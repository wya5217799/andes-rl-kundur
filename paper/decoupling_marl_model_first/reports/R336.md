## Conclusions

- **Physical-package judgment - BLOCK**: the four registered load changes are
  physically executed and observable, but the immutable deterministic model
  does not represent the complete package within the frozen waveform envelope.
- **Location boundary**: Bus14/Bus15 records pass while Bus7/Bus8 records fail
  at both points. This is a repeatable association, not an identified causal
  mechanism and not evidence that ANDES or the controller algorithm fails.
- **Rank boundary**: numerical full rank and power conservation of a poor-fit
  least-squares map are diagnostics only. They cannot override failed
  development and untouched-response gates.
- **Programme boundary**: Q-0086 closes negative. No deterministic physical
  bridge or controller is eligible. At most one separately registered
  development-first diagnosis of location-dependent input dynamics may follow.
