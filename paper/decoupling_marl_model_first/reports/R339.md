## Frozen setup

- R339 uses the unchanged installed ANDES 2.0.0 Kundur plant, original G4 and
  Line_8 in service, the R336 four-PQ baselines, zero ESD1 request, and the
  already exposed HS0 and HS1 operating points. It creates no nonlinear
  trajectory.
- Sixteen isolated single-threaded Python processes extract distinct columns:
  two points by independent control/load input families by four channels. A
  same-path capacity canary passed before the final seal. Every same-point job
  must return a bit-identical equilibrium and descriptor snapshot before
  columns are combined.
- Zero-time-constant differential rows are folded into the augmented
  algebraic system. The Schur reduction uses an exact solve, never a
  pseudoinverse, and must match the installed EIG retained-state names and
  matrix.
- Control `Pext0` and physical-load `Ppf` residual derivatives are computed
  independently with central steps `1e-4`, `1e-5`, and `1e-6` system p.u. The
  old static relation between their input matrices is not imposed.
- The sampled realization follows the R336 end-of-held-interval observation:
  `C_post=C A_d` and `D_post=C B_d+D_c` at 0.2 s. The joint ERA candidate is
  frozen at order 12 with 8-by-8 block Hankels, 25 Markov samples, physical
  input-energy normalization, and no pole projection.
- Full model versus exposed nonlinear records must satisfy NRMSE at most 0.15
  and peak-vector residual at most 0.20. Full model versus order 12 must satisfy
  both metrics at most 0.10. The pre-registered stopping outcomes are retained
  without post-result repair.

## Conclusions

- **Input-bridge judgment - ALLOW-CANDIDATE**: the installed full-DAE model with
  separately derived control and physical-load inputs resolves the previously
  registered response mismatch on the exposed bank, and its fixed order-12
  reduction passes the internal approximation gate.
- **Mechanism boundary**: this result shows that the old static projected input
  bridge is inadequate and that the full separate-input formulation is a
  sufficient candidate on the tested records. It does not isolate which single
  change among physical input entrance, retained dynamics, feedthrough, and
  corrected sample timing caused the improvement.
- **Validation boundary**: HS0 and HS1 were both inspected by the advisory
  package and are development-only. Passing them cannot establish unseen-point
  validity; a new prospectively sealed nonlinear bank is mandatory.
- **Programme boundary**: Q-0087 closes partial at the candidate-selection
  level because no single changed component is isolated. The next eligible
  work is only fresh model validation. Physical
  closed-loop control, distributed execution, reward design, agent training,
  and title-result claims remain blocked.

## Limits

- Evidence covers one fixed Kundur topology, four active-load channels, two
  exposed homogeneous operating points, one 0.2-s sampled phasor-domain plant,
  and replay of the existing signed impulse/triangle records.
- The plant uses the repository's PV/GENCLS VSG proxies and separate GFL ESD1
  devices. It is not a unified grid-forming BESS model, EMT model, hardware
  test, or deployment environment.
- No reactive-load change, fault, outage, timing/amplitude family, different
  network, initial-state error, controller action, constraint activity,
  stability margin, safety envelope, or topology-generalization endpoint was
  tested.
- Order 12 is a fixed candidate supported by internal full-order Markov
  approximation. It is not a uniquely minimal physical order or fresh
  nonlinear validation result.
