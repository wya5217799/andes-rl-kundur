## Conclusions

- **Retained-arm cause - isolated**: exact retained state rescues every frozen absolute development gate while the valid delayed-output observer arm fails severely. The estimation layer is therefore the causal blocker for the retained controller under the sealed reduced-model contract.
- **Controller boundary**: the finite-horizon objective, specialized solver, action map, actuator limits, retained dynamics, cases, and execution budget can produce strong development attenuation when supplied the correct retained augmented state. They are not the primary cause of the retained-arm rejection observed in R327.
- **Oracle boundary**: exact state is privileged model-only information. R328 does not provide a deployable observer, prove that one can be constructed from the allowed measurements, or authorize physical/distributed execution.
- **Decoupling boundary**: the cause result applies only to the retained-cross controller. The cross-deleted arm is excluded because its ERA state basis is not shared with the retained executed plant; R328 makes no oracle or causal claim for that arm.
- **Programme boundary**: the next eligible work is one prospective implementable estimator repair using only the declared delayed outputs, control history, and permitted messages, with the controller objective and limits frozen. The holdout, physical simulator, distributed agents, reward, and training remain blocked.

## Limits

- Evidence covers one retained centralized reduced-model realization and deterministic development cases only.
- Exact-state intervention is an oracle diagnostic and changes the information set; its performance is an upper bound, not deployable-controller evidence.
- The result localizes failure to the estimation layer but does not distinguish observer gain, unknown-disturbance treatment, correction timing, numerical conditioning, or estimator architecture.
- Output-energy ratios are squared regulated-output scores, not electrical energy, frequency-restoration evidence, transient-stability evidence, or a safety certificate.
- No cross-deleted state oracle, fresh holdout, nonlinear physical simulator, topology change, communication graph, distributed execution, agent, learning algorithm, EMT, HIL, hardware, or deployment condition was tested.
