## Conclusions

- **Registered result - negative for the exact repair contract**: R326 cannot verify complete numerical equivalence against every registered R325 successful prefix because eight legacy-reference prefixes are not reproduced in the frozen R326 execution environment. The holdout remains protected.
- **Candidate boundary**: the specialized implementation itself completes all 64 development cases, satisfies the registered numerical and explicit-constraint residual checks, and agrees within threshold wherever the complete legacy prefix is recovered. - **Failure boundary**: the evidence localizes the formal failure to legacy-reference recovery rather than candidate solver termination, constraint violation, non-convexity, or nondeterministic replay. The present artifacts do not by themselves identify native-thread count as the causal factor; that remains a separate scratch diagnosis.
- **Comparison boundary**: because the solver-adequacy gate fails, no development performance ratio, cross-block value, decoupling effect, controller superiority, or directional performance estimate is admissible.
- **Programme boundary**: R326 does not authorize holdout interpretation, ANDES execution, a physical closed loop, distributed agents, reward definition, training, or title-result support. Q-0080 remains unresolved pending a separately prospective and minimal reference-recovery test.

## Limits

- Evidence covers one centralized model-only formulation, four frozen reduced-model designs, and the registered development banks.
- Positive definiteness establishes a unique action optimum only for the sealed designs; it is not a general mathematical feasibility result.
- Candidate completion and residual validity are implementation facts, not evidence of physical control quality, stability, safety, or benefit.
- The eight legacy-reference mismatches prevent complete equivalence admission. No post-outcome environment change may repair R326.
- No fresh holdout, nonlinear physical simulator, topology change, communication graph, distributed execution, agent, learning algorithm, EMT, HIL, hardware, or deployment condition was tested.
