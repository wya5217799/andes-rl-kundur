## Frozen setup

- The valid R312 27-trace bank remained the base development source. Only the already observed R313 HP1 point (17 records) was added as declared development data; R313 HP0 and every R314 holdout record were forbidden from fitting.
- HP1 signed responses were amplitude-normalized to 0.05 system p.u., central-differenced, and equally averaged across its 0.025 and 0.065 amplitudes. No quadratic amplitude term was added.
- HQ0 used the fixed `OP0,OP1,HP1` simplex at weights `0.20,0.30,0.50`; HQ1 used `OP0,OP2,HP1` at the same weights. Both were untouched operating-condition holdouts.
- Each point used one zero trace plus paired common and edge-0/1/2 pulses at 0.025 and 0.065 system-p.u. amplitudes, for 34 fresh physical traces. Those amplitudes had appeared at the HP1 development point, so R314 tests new operating conditions, not unseen-amplitude generalization.
- Full and block-diagonal arms shared development data, simplex, weights, amplitude rule, coordinates, horizon, budget, and metrics. The ablation only zeroed common-to-differential and differential-to-common outputs.
- Plant, fixed electrical topology, two-phase solver, 60-Hz frequency base, ESD1 authority path, five active plus twenty recovery samples, and Stage-1 execution guards remained fixed.
- EVAL ran only after all 34 source records verified and audited exactly 24 source-hash-bound edge views; authority remained `EXTERNAL_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED`.

## Conclusions

- **Formal result - local predictor pass**: the frozen local/simplex predictor satisfies every preregistered response and retained-cross gate on the two new operating-condition holdouts.
- **Repair attribution - qualified only**: R314 differs from R313 by both adding HP1 development data and switching to local simplexes. The result validates that combined repair, not the isolated causal value of either change.
- **Matched mechanism result - retained-cross value**: within the R314 local template, retaining measured cross outputs materially improves cross-output prediction over deleting them. This does not establish controller, distributed-agent, or MARL value.
- **Next gate - dynamic mismatch, not control**: passing a finite-horizon pulse-response template does not authorize controller synthesis. A separately sealed low-order dynamic/modal reduction and mismatch-set gate is required first.
- **Title checkpoint - modeling only**: the bounded title direction is `Coupling-Retaining Common–Differential Modeling of Paralleled VSGs: Signed Authority and Local Prediction`. `Coordination`, `Distributed Agents`, and `Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning` remain unsupported in the title.

## Limits

- One modified Kundur phasor-domain plant, one fixed electrical topology, two local convex holdout points, two small pulse amplitudes, deterministic 5-s finite-horizon responses, and one augmented template/simplex implementation.
- The 32 forced trajectories are the unit of the registered worst-case response gate. No stochastic population, uncertainty interval, explicit modal state model, mismatch-set coverage, controller, reward, communication runtime, or learning process was evaluated.
- R314 does not identify whether the added development point or the local rule caused the improvement over R313.
- The retained-cross ablation identifies only information value inside this matched predictor. It does not identify distributed execution, cooperation, control benefit, exact decoupling, topology generalization, stability, or deployment readiness.
