## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment - COMMON-CHANNEL-HEADROOM-EXPANDED**: on the exposed
  finite development bank, extending the zero-common three-edge residual
  basis with the frozen common residual-power channel enlarges the physical
  action-space headroom from `10/16` to `16/16` feasible cases, unlocking
  every previously infeasible scenario with the registered `2%`
  common-coordinate improvement, zero differential degradation, and exact
  physical feasibility.
- **Question disposition**: Q-0100 closes positive for the registered
  mechanism question. The zero-common residual contract itself is a
  structural limiter: the common-coordinate endpoint cannot be improved
  through the zero-sum edge channels alone, while a fleet-equal common
  channel removes it directly. This confirms the deep-research mechanism gap
  (direction 2).
- **Programme boundary**: the result is an information-unconstrained physical
  feasibility statement on the exposed bank; it does not establish that any
  information path or learning method can select the common channel, does not
  change the R359-R362 information-path negatives, and does not authorize
  training, holdout evaluation, simulation, EVAL, stability, safety, or
  topology claims. It authorizes only a separately registered mechanistically
  distinct successor question (e.g., whether a local or consensus-based
  information path can drive the common channel).

## Limits

- The tested object is the frozen four-channel physical action basis on the
  exposed finite linear-response development bank; no deployed runtime,
  nonlinear trajectory, or learning algorithm is involved.
- The common channel changes the residual contract: it breaks the previous
  zero-common fleet-neutrality assumption of the edge residual (the model
  contract previously reserved fleet net power for the deterministic common
  channel). Any successor must re-derive the power/energy contract before
  execution claims are possible.
- Holdout inventory identity and source closure were checked prospectively,
  but holdout cases remain unexamined and no holdout gate is evaluated.
- The evidence covers one fixed Kundur topology, two exposed operating
  points, selected disturbance channels and signs, and twenty-five response
  samples. Stability, safety, robustness, topology generalization, hardware,
  and deployment remain untested.
- R359-R362 information-path negatives remain valid for their registered
  zero-common information contracts; R363 does not overturn them.
