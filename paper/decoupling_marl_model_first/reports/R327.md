Q-0080 may close positive for solver adequacy.
- **Controller result - negative**: after the numerical gate is restored, the unchanged constrained finite-horizon output-feedback controller fails its absolute development admission in both synthesis arms and materially amplifies the registered regulated-output metric relative to zero control.
- **Decoupling boundary**: retaining the measured common/differential cross blocks yields a lower aggregate development mean and worst value than deleting them. This supports only bounded cross-block value inside this failed fixed controller; it is not positive control efficacy, general decoupling value, or title support.
- **Cause boundary**: solver termination, candidate residual failure, explicit actuator-constraint violation, and missing legacy equivalence are no longer the blocking causes. R327 does not distinguish among remaining controller-law, state-estimation, delay, finite-horizon objective, model, or action-timing explanations.
- **Programme boundary**: the next eligible work is one development-only causal diagnosis that separates state-estimation, action timing/sign, and finite-horizon objective effects before any repair. The holdout, ANDES execution, physical closed loop, distributed agents, reward, and training remain blocked.
