## Frozen setup

- The analysis reuses the exposed R344/R341 bank: sixteen paired scenarios,
  two fixed operating points, four active-load locations, both signs, and 25
  samples per scenario. It creates no new physical trajectory.
- The outcome-seeing oracle requests a zero-common three-edge residual through
  the frozen response map. A smooth epigraph represents the common-coordinate
  absolute-value target and an exact convex quadratic represents the
  differential-coordinate target.
- Three starts are fixed before execution: a smooth feasibility start, zero
  edge action, and a freshly recomputed R348 candidate. A start is usable only
  after analytic first-order certification and original-unit power, ramp, and
  state-of-charge checks; the certified start with the smallest edge-action
  norm is selected deterministically.
- The deployable proxy uses only the two endpoints of each edge at the causal
  pre-action time, fits an independent standardized ordinary least-squares map
  per edge with leave-one-scenario-out folds, and projects its requests back to
  physical headroom.
- All sixteen oracle jobs and sixteen local projections run in one reused pool
  of sixteen single-thread workers. No simulator, reward, policy, training,
  distributed runtime, or EVAL entry point is authorized.

## Limits

- The result is create-only analysis of existing traces under one reduced
  response map. The outcome-seeing oracle is an offline upper bound, not a
  deployable controller and not a new nonlinear physical trajectory.
- The bank covers one topology, two locally constructed operating points, four
  load locations, two signs, one amplitude per location, and one short
  waveform. It does not establish unseen-operating-point or topology
  generalization.
- Failure of the registered independent linear edge proxy blocks training
  eligibility; it does not prove that every possible nonlinear estimator or
  richer information pattern is mathematically incapable of predicting a
  residual.
- The mismatch-bounded endpoints are conservative additive-output envelope
  calculations, not physical robustness trajectories or a stability/safety
  certificate.
- No neural policy, reward, training seed, checkpoint, matched learning
  comparison, independent distributed runtime, communication test, or EVAL
  result exists.
