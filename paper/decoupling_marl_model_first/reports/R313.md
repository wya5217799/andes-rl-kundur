## Conclusions

- **Formal result - predictor no-go**: the registered global barycentric response predictor does not satisfy the complete held-out-bank response bound because HP1 edge-0 NRMSE fails prospectively.
- **Failure isolation - operating-point interpolation**: the failure appears at both amplitudes and only at HP1, while HP0 and both amplitude strata otherwise track similarly. Under the frozen decision tree, the only admissible next optimization is one added development operating point plus local/simplex interpolation on a new untouched holdout.
- **Matched mechanism result - qualified retained-cross value**: in this one finite-horizon predictor implementation, retaining measured cross outputs substantially improves cross-output prediction over deleting them. This is not controller, distributed-agent, or MARL evidence.
- **Title checkpoint - model-validation only**: R313 supports `Coupling-Aware` as a bounded modeling choice and documents a predictor limit; it does not support `Coordination` efficacy or `Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning` in a conference title.

## Limits

- One modified Kundur phasor-domain plant, one fixed electrical topology, two convex-hull holdout operating points, two small pulse amplitudes, deterministic 5-s finite-horizon responses, and one template/interpolation implementation.
- The 32 forced trajectories are the unit of the registered worst-case response gate; no stochastic population, uncertainty interval, modal reduction, mismatch-set coverage, controller, reward, or learning process was evaluated.
- The cross ablation identifies only the information value of retaining cross outputs within the matched template. It does not identify common/differential decomposition as a class, distributed execution, communication, agent cooperation, or control benefit.
- The HP1 localization is descriptive under the registered strata, not a claim that one physical parameter alone causes the error.
