## Frozen setup

- The exact two R341 point-specific order-12 predictors retain four separate
  controller inputs and four separate physical-load disturbance inputs. The
  bridge does not replace them with a shared input channel or a fitted
  disturbance-to-control map.
- One centralized full-output constrained receding-horizon controller acts in
  one common and three differential power coordinates. Its estimator,
  horizon, scales, solver, sampling, node power, ramp, energy, state-of-charge,
  internal actuator limits, fallback, and request-command-achieved semantics
  were sealed before every claim-bearing trajectory.
- The physical bank contains sixteen scenarios: two fixed operating points,
  four active-load locations, and both signs. Each scenario has a paired zero-
  control and frozen-controller arm over 25 samples, yielding 32 formal
  trajectories. The paired arms share initialization, disturbance, timing,
  simulator, actuator path, and guards.
- Capacity was selected without reading control endpoints. All 16-, 24-, and
  32-process single-thread rungs were valid; the 32-process rung had the
  highest completed-job throughput and was frozen before rehearsal and seal.
- Two zero-action and sixteen signed-authority canaries had to pass before the
  formal bank. Any integrity, physical, actuator, solver, fallback, timing,
  limiter, energy, state-of-charge, or completion failure stopped the route.

## Limits

- The result covers one simulator, one fixed topology, two locally constructed
  operating points, four active-load locations, two signs, one amplitude per
  location, one 25-sample waveform, and one centralized deterministic
  controller.
- The two coordinate endpoints are finite-bank transient summaries. They do
  not establish global stability, a safety certificate, a region of
  attraction, disturbance robustness outside the registered bank, or
  restoration beyond the sampled horizon.
- The controller uses full output centrally. This result does not establish
  local or neighbour-only information sufficiency, independent distributed
  actions, communication robustness, multi-agent coordination, or topology
  generalization.
- No reward, agent, neural policy, training seed, training budget, learned
  checkpoint, or learning comparison exists in this round. The large
  deterministic improvement may leave little residual value; that question is
  unmeasured and must be prospectively registered before training.
