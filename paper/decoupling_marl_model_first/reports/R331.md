## Conclusions

- **Bridge judgment - BLOCK**: the frozen R329 package cannot authorize a
  physical load-disturbance ANDES closed loop because its disturbance enters
  through the controller-input channel rather than an identified separate
  physical disturbance channel.
- **Root-cause judgment**: the primary failure is a mathematical/input-channel
  mismatch between the reduced test and the declared physical experiment, not
  a general inability of ANDES to represent electromechanical response.
- **Algorithm judgment**: the deterministic estimator-controller line is not
  rejected. R329/R330 remain valid only inside their sealed reduced-model
  contracts; they cannot be transferred unchanged to the declared physical
  disturbance experiment. R329 remains immutable evidence; unless a newly
  identified physical disturbance channel is prospectively proven equivalent
  to its frozen input channel, a separately sealed successor package is
  required.
- **Platform boundary**: the formal bridge judgment is `BLOCK`. The blocker is
  the unidentified disturbance seam, not a general inability of ANDES to
  represent phasor-domain electromechanical response. Any later repaired bridge
  remains limited to that platform scope and cannot evidence omitted fast
  electromagnetic phenomena.
- **Programme judgment**: Q-0084 remains open because the publication gate
  failed. A new correction round must repair the checker and evidence bindings
  before proposing a minimal open-loop disturbance-identification question.
  Deterministic closed loop, distributed-agent implementation, reward design,
  neural training, EVAL, and manuscript result claims remain blocked.

## Limits

- R331 executed no TDS trajectory and estimated no prediction error,
  controller effect, stability margin, safety margin, or computation speed.
- The source-bound classification does not prove that a future physical
  disturbance channel is identifiable, that R329 will survive nonlinear plant
  mismatch, or that the projected-command return path will restore estimator
  consistency.
- The five qualified mappings require later executable falsification. Static
  source consistency cannot replace live M/D readback, induced projection,
  limiter activation, time-grid, SOC-boundary, or deterministic-reset tests.
- The platform ceiling excludes EMT, switching transients, converter inner
  loops, harmonics, negative sequence, hardware, field deployment, and any
  inference beyond the executed ANDES model definitions.
- No comparison arm or performance estimand was executed, so this round cannot
  attribute value to coupling retention, decoupling, coordination,
  distributed execution, or learning.
- EVAL is intentionally not run: there is no learned or distributed policy to
  diagnose, and its diagnostic score cannot repair a failed physical bridge
  contract.
