## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment - NO-NEIGHBOUR-CAUSAL-HEADROOM**: on the exposed finite
  development bank, this one pre-registered fixed affine per-edge controller
  does not recover the R358 physical headroom from the exact prospective-agent
  information path while meeting both registered endpoints.
- **Question disposition**: Q-0096 is eligible to close negative only for this
  fixed standardized affine information-to-action formulation and its frozen
  finite development bank.
- **Programme boundary**: the development stop fires before any holdout
  residual label, counterfactual endpoint, or holdout gate is evaluated, and
  before any physical intervention, simulation, or training. The result
  therefore does not establish that a nonlinear or neural residual is absent,
  that neighbour information is generally insufficient, or that multi-agent
  learning lacks value.

## Limits

- The tested object is one deterministic standardized affine map per edge,
  fitted and judged on an exposed finite linear-response development bank. It
  is not a nonlinear policy, a neural network, or a reinforcement-learning
  algorithm.
- The exact information contract and three independent edge actions are
  represented offline, but no deployed distributed runtime or nonlinear plant
  trajectory is executed.
- Holdout inventory identity and source closure were checked prospectively,
  but holdout residual labels and counterfactual endpoints remain unexamined
  and no holdout gate is evaluated because the development gate fails. This
  round supplies no holdout confirmation, population estimate, training-seed
  uncertainty, or generalization evidence.
- The evidence covers one fixed Kundur topology, two exposed operating points,
  selected disturbance channels and signs, and twenty-five response samples.
  Stability, safety, robustness, topology generalization, hardware, and
  deployment remain untested.
- R358 physical witnesses still establish per-case action-space existence in
  ten exposed cases. R359 tests causal recovery by one affine information map;
  its negative result does not invalidate that earlier physical-feasibility
  result.
