## Official-source scope

- The [official time-domain tutorial](https://docs.andes.app/en/stable/tutorials/04-time-domain.html)
  describes differential-algebraic transient-stability simulation with an
  implicit trapezoidal method and warns that small time constants may require a
  smaller step.
- The [official GENCLS model reference](https://docs.andes.app/en/stable/reference/models/SynGen.html)
  identifies `M` as twice the inertia constant and declares the power-base
  conversion semantics of `M` and `D`.
- The [official ESD1 model reference](https://docs.andes.app/en/stable/reference/models/DG.html)
  and tagged [ESD1 source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CURENT/andes/v2.0.0/andes/models/distributed/esd1.py)
  define signed storage-power capability, active-current dynamics, energy
  capacity, efficiency, and state-of-charge limits.
- The first-party [CURENT software review](https://curent.utk.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Journal_PS_51.pdf)
  separates positive-sequence phasor transient-stability simulation from
  three-phase electromagnetic-transient simulation. This supports a scope
  boundary, not a causal explanation of an offline controller failure.
- The [ANDES framework paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.09455) uses
  vectorization as a numerical implementation property. It does not define
  vectorization as a reduction in the physical model domain.

## Conclusions

- **Direct-cause decision - blocked**: ANDES vectorization or the absence of an
  EMT model cannot be the direct cause of R321/R322 because those rounds did not
  execute ANDES. Their negative controller decisions remain valid on the
  current declared reduced proxy.
- **Semantic-error decision - not identified**: existing official definitions,
  installed-source inspection, measured readbacks, signed-power/SOC checks, and
  focused tests reveal no current unit, base, sign, frequency, or charging-path
  contradiction.
- **Model-fidelity decision - qualified**: parameter realism, proxy identity,
  and fast-dynamics time-step convergence remain unverified. The strongest
  present model claim is limited to one fixed-topology, phasor-domain,
  averaged-model, small-signal object.
- **Programme decision**: place one prospective parameter-provenance and
  time-step-convergence gate before Q-0078. Do not change parameters in place.
  If the gate changes the plant or integration contract, revalidate the
  physical bank and reduced model before any controller synthesis.
- **Platform decision**: do not migrate to EMT merely to explain the existing
  offline failure. Open a separate cross-domain validation question only if a
  future manuscript claim requires switching, unbalance, internal-loop, or
  hardware behavior.
- **Title boundary**: the conference title remains exactly unchanged, but this
  audit supplies no distributed-agent, learning, coordination-efficacy,
  topology-generalization, safety, stability, EMT, HIL, hardware, or
  title-result evidence.

## Limits

- This is a decision audit of existing evidence, not a newly sampled physical
  experiment and not a parameter-identification result.
- Official model documentation establishes variable meaning and solver scope;
  it does not select physically correct values for this project.
- Passing seam tests rules out the checked implementation contradictions only.
  It cannot prove untested source behavior or model adequacy.
- R306/R312 interface checks and R316 finite-bank model fidelity do not test
  time-step convergence, EMT equivalence, converter internal controls,
  unbalanced faults, harmonics, protection, or hardware delay.
- The next gate must freeze its parameter-source rubric, pulse, step sizes,
  endpoints, tolerances, and invalidation rule before seeing new outcomes. It
  must not tune controller weights, horizons, or parameters against R321's
  examination.
