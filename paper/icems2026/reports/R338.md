## Frozen setup

- One modified-Kundur plant, one fixed four-node path, 24 sealed scenarios,
  five predefined training seeds per learned architecture, and 300 formal
  steps per new controller trajectory.
- The distributed actor uses only the two endpoint observations at each edge
  and directly emits the three executed edge actions; its joint critic is
  training-only. The matched single actor sees the joint observation and emits
  the identical three action coordinates.
- Both learned arms use the same selected causal edge prior, projection,
  limits, reward, training budget, bank, seeds, and formal thresholds. The
  selected deterministic edge controller is the strong classical comparator.
- The two co-primary endpoints, hierarchical bootstrap, materiality and
  non-inferiority margins, no-harm guards, and decision tree were sealed before
  formal controller outcomes.

## Conclusions

- C1 [sealed-bank time-domain] The formal classification is
  `NO-NEURAL-INCREMENT`: neither learned residual architecture clears both
  co-primary gates relative to the selected classical edge controller.
- C2 [sealed-bank time-domain] The selected classical edge controller has
  supported incremental value over zero residual on both registered primary
  endpoints for this plant and bank.
- C3 [sealed-bank time-domain] The neighbour-only distributed actor met the
  registered numerical 5% non-inferiority check relative to the matched
  joint-observation actor, but this does not establish a positive distributed
  architecture claim because neither learned arm demonstrated incremental
  value beyond the selected classical controller.
- C4 [comparison-identifiability gate: QUALIFY] The result identifies only the
  executed neighbour-only shared-edge actor, matched joint-observation actor,
  and selected classical controller under their frozen information patterns
  and identical three-edge action formulation; it does not identify MARL or
  learned control as a class.

## Limits

- Covers one modified-Kundur phasor-domain plant, one fixed four-node path,
  one 24-scenario sealed bank, and five predefined training seeds per learned
  architecture.
- The actuation is bounded zero-sum redistribution of VSG-proxy inertia on
  three fixed path edges above a frozen common prior and storage layer; it is
  not four independently actuated physical devices or a unified GFM-BESS.
- The distributed execution has endpoint-only edge observations and no joint
  runtime action server, while the single actor has joint observation; their
  comparison therefore includes an information-pattern difference.
- The registered trace guards establish empirical feasibility only on the
  tested bank. They do not establish a stability or safety certificate.
- Controller-side legacy semantics retain a 50-Hz nominal label, while every
  reported physical endpoint is recomputed on the declared 60-Hz ANDES basis;
  the two bases must not be mixed in manuscript wording.
- Unseen topologies, arbitrary graph sizes, communication failure, motor-heavy
  loads, electromagnetic-transient simulation, hardware-in-the-loop,
  cross-simulator transfer, and deployment remain untested.
- The result does not show that distributed learning, multi-agent methods, or
  learned residual control are generally ineffective.

- **Domain audit**: QUALIFIED — physical 60-Hz endpoints, legacy 50-Hz
  controller semantics, common/inter-area separation, endpoint-only versus
  joint information, identical three-edge action coordinates, storage and
  action feasibility, matched training budgets, and fixed-topology scope were
  checked. The result is empirical and local to the VSG-proxy plus independent
  storage model.
- **External context**: CURRENT — the bounded ICEMS evidence-sufficiency audit
  `docs/research/2026-07-26_icems_evidence_sufficiency_audit.md` covers the
  MARL-versus-classical claim axis through 2026-07-26; R338 introduces no new
  novelty or broad class-level claim.
- **Claim disposition**: QUALIFY — enter as a negative incremental-value gate
  and limitation, not as a positive learned-control or MARL contribution.
- **Allowed claim**: On one modified-Kundur plant and the sealed 24-scenario
  bank, neither the neighbour-only shared-edge actor nor the matched
  joint-observation actor cleared both preregistered primary gates relative to
  the selected causal edge controller; the distributed actor's numerical
  non-inferiority to the joint-observation actor does not establish an
  incremental multi-agent benefit.
- **Stay-out**: MARL, distributed learning, or learned residual control is
  generally ineffective; the distributed architecture is superior or adds
  value; four independent device agents were tested; topology generalization,
  communication robustness, stability, safety, unified GFM-BESS, EMT/HIL,
  cross-simulator, hardware, or deployment claims follow from R338.
