## Frozen setup

- The sustained common-frequency restoration layer and fixed aggregate
  transient-support pulse are frozen before learning.
- Both learned controllers may change only one bounded scalar \(q\), projected
  to the hard-zero-sum differential-inertia vector
  \(q[1,1,-1,-1]\). Storage power is controlled by the frozen slow layer.
- Shared TD3 applies one parameter-shared local actor to four local
  observations and aggregates the four outputs into \(q\). The size-matched
  centralized TD3 actor maps the concatenated observation to the same scalar
  \(q\). Critics, reward, training budget, and evaluation bank are matched.
- Six checkpoints (three predefined training seeds per architecture) were
  frozen before seed 2026072704 generated the independent 24-disturbance bank.
  The controller-blind \(q=0\) screen accepted all cases without redraw or
  outcome-based exclusion.
- The plant is a modified Kundur two-area model using PV+GENCLS VSG proxies
  paired with independent grid-following ESD1 devices. It is not a unified
  GFM-BESS model.

## Observations

- O1 The sealed slow active-power controller established sustained-restoration
  authority: relative to zero support, mean-frequency IAE changed by
  -58.629118% and final-window common error by -77.290429%, with paired 95%
  intervals wholly below zero and all registered guards passing. [CLM-0580]
- O2 The frozen 3-s common-inertia pulse added transient value above the slow
  layer: all four registered fast endpoints improved by 4.29%--28.37%, with
  paired support and all guards passing. [CLM-0585]
- O3 No one of the six endpoints cleared the registered non-additive
  interaction gate in the four-arm fast/slow factorial test; the layers are
  supported as additive, not synergistic. [CLM-0590]
- O4 The outcome-seeing development-bank oracle established an attainable
  differential-allocation margin, improving synchronization loss by 25.64%
  and first-3-s inter-area IAE by 19.66%. This is an upper bound, not causal
  policy performance. [CLM-0595]
- O5 On the independent bank, centralized TD3 versus \(q=0\) improved
  synchronization loss by -24.345456% with 95% interval
  [-31.500633%, -17.481533%] and first-3-s inter-area IAE by -17.040674%
  [-24.595353%, -8.959552%]. Shared TD3 also improved both endpoints:
  -16.793845% [-24.73%, -5.81%] and -9.540768%
  [-17.19%, -0.60%], respectively. [CLM-0610]
- O6 Shared TD3 was worse than size-matched centralized TD3 by 9.981702%
  [1.04%, 26.13%] in synchronization loss and 9.040461%
  [3.09%, 19.54%] in inter-area IAE; all three paired seeds favored
  centralized TD3 on both endpoints. [CLM-0610]
- O7 All 192 formal trajectories completed. Under the prospectively sealed
  one-float32-ULP representation rule, all registered action, storage,
  completion, physical no-harm, and tail checks passed. Exactly five
  `q_slew` audit entries changed false-to-true relative to R279; no trajectory,
  checkpoint, bootstrap sample, effect estimate, or efficacy gate changed.
  [CLM-0610]

## Conclusions (bounded)

- C1 In this formulation, both learned policies exploit a real
  differential-allocation margin above \(q=0\).
- C2 The tested parameter-sharing and aggregation factorization is
  meaningfully effective but inferior to the matched centralized TD3 actor
  when both reduce coordination to the same scalar \(q\).
- C3 The experiment does not identify the incremental value of multi-agent
  architectures as a class. It identifies only the performance of this
  scalar, parameter-shared factorization under the frozen benchmark.
- C4 The fixed causal comparator did not clear both materiality gates. This
  does not establish superiority over classical differential or
  mutual-damping control as a class.

## Limits (scope)

- One modified Kundur topology, one scalar differential control coordinate,
  three predefined training seeds, full state exchange in simulation, and no
  communication-failure study.
- Parameter sharing plus output aggregation is not fully decentralized MARL
  and does not test vector-valued local actions, explicit communication, or
  heterogeneous agent roles.
- A hard-zero-sum input projection does not prove modal or closed-loop dynamic
  decoupling.
- The PV+GENCLS plus independent ESD1 plant does not establish transfer to a
  unified GFM-BESS, motor-dominated microgrid, EMT model, HIL system, or
  deployed controller.
- No topology-generalization, formal stability/safety, or broad
  training-seed-population claim.
- The R277 oracle is not a deployable controller, and R280 is an audit
  correction rather than retraining.
