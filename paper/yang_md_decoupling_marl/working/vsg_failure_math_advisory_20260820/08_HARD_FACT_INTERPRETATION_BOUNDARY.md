# Hard-fact and interpretation boundary

## Hard facts

A statement is treated as a hard fact only when it is a sealed JSON field, a deterministic calculation from named sealed fields, or a directly cited package-source statement. The authoritative index is `evidence/evidence_register.csv`. Derived entries retain the source evidence identifiers or source roots used in the calculation.

## Interpretation

Mechanism labels, causal stories, model reductions, regularity assumptions, and proposed experiments are interpretations or conditional mathematics. They are not promoted to facts by agreement with one table or plot. For M items, the observable matrix specifies a direction that would support or refute the prediction. For P items, the verification plan specifies the missing repo-side calculation.

## HYPOTHETICAL convention

Any quantity absent from the package—finite-difference step size, sample period not sealed in the relevant JSON, materiality threshold, FIR horizon, controller bound, nonlinear remainder radius, or statistical uncertainty—is marked HYPOTHETICAL or left symbolic. The report never back-fills such a value from narrative prose when the brief requires a shipped JSON field.

## Ratio warning

A ratio endpoint can change because its candidate numerator changes, its local-reference denominator changes, or both. Cross-block ratios are not treated as parameter sensitivities when their disturbance/probe identities differ. Likewise, an endpoint ceiling is not a stability boundary unless a verified loop/margin certificate connects them.
