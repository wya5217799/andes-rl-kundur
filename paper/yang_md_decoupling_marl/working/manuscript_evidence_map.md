## Research questions and evidence-bounded answers

| RQ | Question | Evidence-bounded answer |
|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | How should direct-M/D decoupling be evaluated when common support, relative motion, and command stress coexist? | Separate off-diagonal and disturbance-differential endpoint energies, then require physical validity, common-frequency no-harm, peak, RoCoF, action-RMS, action-variation, saturation, and nondegeneracy guards before interpreting endpoint improvement. |
| RQ2 | Does the corrected action object contain a guard-clean deterministic solution on the registered system? | Yes, within the finite bank. The development-selected direct-M/D controller passes 4/4 fresh 30 s profiles, with both endpoint ratios below 0.558 of the zero-action reference on every profile. |
| RQ3 | Does the tested MARL family improve that reference under the complete contract? | Not in the tested canary bank. Although 126/208 frozen policies meet both aggregate 5% endpoint targets, 0/208 passes the complete guard because all 832 policy-profile blocks exceed both action-stress limits; frequency guards add 408 RoCoF and 45 worst-peak failures. |
| RQ4 | Do the registered actor, critic, or interaction contrasts establish an effect above 10%? | No. Neither the 6 s primary analysis nor the separate 30 s sensitivity rejects any member of the Holm-controlled four-contrast family. Positive interaction estimates remain descriptive, not confirmatory. |

## Supersession and stay-out rules

1. R478 corrected the physical object. Pre-R478 directional performance, old repair-ladder plots, and the old positive energy-port narrative cannot enter the abstract, results, discussion, or conclusion.
2. The learned 30 s audit uses four registered canary profiles only. It gives no fresh-bank learned claim.
3. The direct-M/D fresh 4/4 result belongs to the deterministic controller only and is not evidence of MARL success.
4. R483 6 s primary inference and R484 30 s sensitivity are never pooled, averaged, substituted, or called replications.
5. Fresh and canary deterministic banks are never pooled; E7 remains descriptive.
6. `0/208` is a finite tested-family result, not a probability, impossibility theorem, or statement about all MARL.
7. “No material effect established” is not “no effect,” equivalence, or uselessness. The positive interaction estimates are descriptive because Holm correction does not reject.
8. No topology-generalization, cross-H robustness, EMT, HIL, stability-certificate, safety-certificate, deployment, controller-equivalence, or universal-MARL claim enters the paper.
9. The paper states explicitly that the corrected-card system uses one Kundur topology and H0=100 s; the zero-adaptive-action H probe motivates the 30 s tail but cannot support cross-H controller claims.

## Writing gate

The existing R478--R484 evidence is sufficient for the paper's exact, finite-bank,
pre-registered-contract conclusion. No new simulation or training experiment is
required for that claim. Full closure of the second-suite publication-risk audit
would, however, require a new claim-bearing secondary analysis of the existing
trajectories: action-stress margins, neighbouring-threshold and comparator
sensitivity, and inference aligned with an interval for the selected source-effect
estimand. Those analyses were not already sealed and were not run during this
writing revision. A positive MARL, fresh learned-bank, topology, cross-H, or
deployment claim would require a successor experiment and remains outside this
revision.
