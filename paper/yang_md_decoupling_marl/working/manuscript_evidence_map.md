## Research questions and answers

| RQ | Question | Evidence-bounded answer |
|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | How should decoupling-oriented coordination be evaluated when aggregate support and relative motion coexist? | Use separate common/differential endpoints and require common-mode, peak, RoCoF, action-RMS, action-variation, box, slew, and validity guards before interpreting endpoint improvement. |
| RQ2 | Does the tested direct-M/D MARL family improve a strong deterministic comparator while satisfying the physical contract? | No tested learning arm satisfies all guards. Repairs move the residual failure between action stress and frequency restoration rather than closing the contract. This is a bounded family result, not an impossibility claim. |
| RQ3 | Does authentic same-time neighbour information establish a material source effect? | No effect above the pre-registered 10% bar is established for either actor or critic in the clean six-seed row-permuted-placebo factorial. This is not evidence of zero effect. |
| RQ4 | Is the joint physical target itself empty? | No. A distinct structured energy-port controller passes the development and one-use unseen banks and all ten equilibrium-sound frozen topology variants, while its limitations remain visible. |

## Supersession and stay-out rules

1. The clean row-permuted-placebo result E13 is the only headline source-effect conclusion. E12 is protocol motivation, not a competing positive result.
2. Historical three-seed message contrasts do not support a general communication claim and are not carried into the abstract or conclusion.
3. Invalid or scratch runs, including unslewed SAC trajectories and disclosed-development scratch failures, do not provide directional evidence.
4. Direct-M/D and energy-port endpoint ratios are never pooled, ranked, or presented as the same actuator.
5. `B_{u,r}=0` is an equilibrium first-order statement. It does not say M/D has no transient effect and does not identify the sole cause of learning failure.
6. The source factorial estimates a total authentic-source versus row-permuted-source effect, not pure semantic neighbour-information value.
7. Six seeds do not support equivalence or a zero-effect claim. The wording ceiling is “no effect above 10% was established.”
8. No topology-generalization, EMT, HIL, stability-certificate, safety-certificate, deployment, or universal MARL claim enters the paper.
