## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor) requires the study design, handling of data, quantitative or qualitative analysis, reporting choices, and reproducibility provisions to be rigorous enough that the research question can be answered credibly and the work can be independently scrutinized or repeated.

D2 (domain_accuracy) requires domain-specific claims, terminology, prior-work characterizations, and reported results to remain factually consistent with the relevant evidence, although this dimension is not assigned to the methodology seat for scoring.

D3 (argumentative_coherence) requires a traceable and internally consistent path from research question and premises through methods and evidence to the central claims, without unsupported inferential jumps or reasoning defects that undermine the thesis.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance) requires framing, definitions, and implications to be understandable to readers in adjacent fields and requires any interdisciplinary claims to be supported, although this dimension is outside the methodology seat's scoring remit.

D5 (writing_and_structure) requires clear organization and exposition, usable figures and tables, and conformity with the configured venue's presentation conventions, while remaining outside the methodology seat's scoring remit.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution) requires the work's scope, originality, significance, and readership fit to satisfy the configured venue's contribution bar, although the methodology seat does not score this dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Alignment among research questions, study design, data generation and handling, analysis methods and assumptions, statistical reporting, validity safeguards, and enough procedural and artifact detail for independent reproduction.
what_triggers_block: A major but potentially repairable design, data-handling, analysis, reporting, or reproducibility defect prevents the current evidence from supporting a central inference.
what_triggers_warn: A bounded methodological omission or ambiguity reduces confidence or reproducibility but leaves the principal design and inference substantially assessable.
what_triggers_fatal: A foundational design failure, unusable data provenance, or invalid central analysis makes the core result uninterpretable and cannot be repaired without a new study or replacement evidence.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Internal consistency of the thesis and a complete, logically valid chain connecting stated premises, methodological choices, observations or results, uncertainty, and the scope of each conclusion.
what_triggers_block: A major but potentially repairable contradiction or unsupported inferential bridge prevents the current argument from establishing a central claim.
what_triggers_warn: A localized overstatement, missing qualification, or limited reasoning gap weakens part of the argument without defeating its central evidentiary chain.
what_triggers_fatal: The central conclusion is incompatible with the stated premises or evidentiary basis, or the core reasoning is circular or incoherent such that the present study cannot support its thesis.
