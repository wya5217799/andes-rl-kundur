## Contract Paraphrase

D1 requires the eventual study methods and evidence-generation process to withstand adversarial scrutiny: design choices, data treatment, quantitative reporting, and reproducibility support must not leave openings that could explain the result without the claimed mechanism.

D2 requires the argument to rest on technically accurate domain premises and a faithful account of existing knowledge; a factual mistake, distorted precedent, or misuse of specialist language must not become a hidden support for the conclusion.

D3 requires a continuous and internally consistent reasoning chain from premises through evidence to the central conclusion; the adversarial test will look for contradictions, circularity, unsupported leaps, and credible rival explanations that defeat the claimed inference.

D4 requires framing and implications that remain intelligible beyond the immediate specialty, while any bridge to neighboring disciplines must carry real support rather than relying on broad labels or assumed transferability.

D5 requires the paper's organization and presentation to make its reasoning auditable, with clear exposition and usable visual material, while also meeting the formal expectations of the intended publication setting.

D6 requires both a defensible match to the intended readership and a contribution whose originality and importance survive a skeptical challenge rather than depending on assertion, novelty-by-wording, or an inflated statement of impact.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A traceable, contradiction-free chain from stated premises and evidence to the core thesis, including whether credible rival explanations are confronted at the point where they could overturn the inference.
what_triggers_block: A material contradiction, missing inferential link, or unaddressed rival explanation substantially undermines the central argument but could be repaired through major re-analysis or reframing.
what_triggers_warn: The central argument remains viable, yet a localized ambiguity, under-justified inference, or incompletely handled alternative materially reduces confidence and needs targeted revision.
what_triggers_fatal: The core conclusion cannot follow because its decisive premise is contradicted, circular, or wholly unsupported, leaving no defensible central claim without replacing the paper's foundation.
