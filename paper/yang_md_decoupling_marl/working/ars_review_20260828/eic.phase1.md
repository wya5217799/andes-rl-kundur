## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): Editorial acceptance requires the study design, data treatment, statistical reporting, and reproducibility support to meet the field's peer-review standard; the methodology reviewer owns this mandatory assessment.

D2 (domain_accuracy): Editorial acceptance requires the claims, terminology, results, and representation of prior work to be accurate against current domain evidence; the domain reviewer owns this mandatory assessment.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): Editorial acceptance requires a consistent central thesis, valid reasoning, and evidence that actually supports the claims without a central fallacy; the devil's-advocate reviewer owns this mandatory assessment, with methodology also eligible.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): The framing, definitions, implications, and any interdisciplinary claims should be intelligible and substantiated for adjacent-field readers; the perspective reviewer owns this high-priority assessment.

D5 (writing_and_structure): Editorial oversight will assess whether the manuscript is clearly organised and explained, whether figures and tables communicate effectively, and whether presentation follows the configured venue's conventions; this normal-priority assessment belongs to the EIC seat.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): Editorial oversight will assess whether the submission belongs in the configured venue and offers an original, significant contribution of interest to its readership; this mandatory assessment belongs to the EIC seat.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A coherent manuscript-wide organisation, clear exposition, legible and purposeful figures and tables, and compliance with the venue conventions established by the supplied authoritative materials.
what_triggers_block: Pervasive structural, expository, figure or table, or convention failures prevent reliable comprehension or publication readiness and require extensive reconstruction.
what_triggers_warn: The presentation is broadly usable but contains limited organisation, clarity, visual communication, or convention problems that need targeted correction.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Evidence that the topic and article type suit the configured venue and readership, and that the claimed contribution is both original and significant at the level expected by the supplied authoritative materials.
what_triggers_block: Venue fit, originality, or significance is materially unsupported or misframed, but substantial repositioning or stronger substantiation could establish an appropriate contribution.
what_triggers_warn: Fit and contribution are broadly plausible, but their relevance, novelty, significance, or audience value needs limited sharpening.
what_triggers_fatal: The submission is fundamentally outside the configured venue's scope, or its central contribution is demonstrably non-original or too insignificant for that readership such that revision cannot establish fit.
