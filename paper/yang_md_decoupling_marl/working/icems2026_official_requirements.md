# ICEMS 2026 official paper requirements

**Research value: high** -- The current official conference pages, templates, registration page, IEEE conference listing, and IEEE publication-policy manual establish the main formatting and publication conditions; a few submission-system details remain unpublished.

**Source snapshot:** checked 2026-08-16 (UTC+8). This is a venue-requirements memo, not evidence that this manuscript has an accepted digest or is eligible for final-paper submission.

## Confirmed official requirements

### Conference identity and fit

- ICEMS 2026 is the **29th International Conference on Electrical Machines and Systems**, held **in person** at Sands Expo & Convention Centre, Singapore, **24–27 November 2026**. It is listed by IEEE IAS as co-sponsored and in-person. ([conference home](https://www.icems2026.org/), [IEEE IAS schedule](https://ias.ieee.org/conferences/conference-schedule/), [welcome page](https://www.icems2026.org/welcome/))
- The paper fits named topics under **Power Electronics and Motor Drives — Grid-Forming Technologies** and **Energy Systems and AI Convergence — Smart Grids and Microgrids / AI for Electrical Drives and Energy Systems**. Authors must choose a technical track in the submission system. ([topics](https://www.icems2026.org/topics/), [digest submission](https://www.icems2026.org/digest-submission/))

### Length, layout, and front matter

- Final paper: **4–6 pages recommended**. Pages over six cost **S$150 per page**, with an absolute maximum of **8 pages**. Do **not** add page numbers. ([full-paper submission](https://www.icems2026.org/full-paper-submission/), [registration](https://www.icems2026.org/registration/))
- Use the official **A4 IEEE conference template**. The Word file is A4, with a two-column body and Times New Roman styles: title 24 pt, authors 11 pt, affiliations/body/headings 10 pt, abstract/keywords 9 pt, and figure captions/references 8 pt. Its instructions say not to alter prescribed margins, column widths, spacing, or fonts. ([official Word template](https://www.icems2026.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ICEMS_2026_Conference_Template_A4.docx), [official IEEE LaTeX/template page](https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates))
- The full-paper template contains **author names and affiliations, Abstract, and Keywords**. It gives no abstract word limit or keyword-count limit. It warns against subtitles and against symbols, special characters, footnotes, or mathematics in the title or abstract. ([official Word template](https://www.icems2026.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ICEMS_2026_Conference_Template_A4.docx))
- The initial digest was one A4 page including references, PDF only, and was expected to explain the problem, key results, and unique contributions. Its official template contains author names, affiliations, corresponding-author marker, email, and an abstract. ([digest submission](https://www.icems2026.org/digest-submission/), [official digest template](https://www.icems2026.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ICEMS_2026_One_Page_Digest_Template.doc))

### Files, systems, copyright, and originality

- Submission system: **ScholarOne**, [https://mc03.manuscriptcentral.com/tems](https://mc03.manuscriptcentral.com/tems).
- The camera-ready paper must be a **PDF validated by IEEE PDF eXpress**, conference ID **69927X**, then uploaded through the accepted digest's “My Page.” The corresponding author completes IEEE eCopyright through the emailed link. No public maximum PDF file size is stated. ([full-paper submission](https://www.icems2026.org/full-paper-submission/))
- Papers must be original, unpublished, and not simultaneously under review elsewhere. The conference requires substantial difference from prior publications and directs authors to check plagiarism/duplicate publication using IEEE CrossCheck. ([official call for papers](https://www.icems2026.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/CFP-extended2.pdf), [full-paper submission](https://www.icems2026.org/full-paper-submission/))
- The official call states that submissions are peer reviewed for originality, technical/research depth, correctness, relevance, contribution, and readability. ([official call for papers](https://www.icems2026.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/CFP-extended2.pdf))

### Registration, presentation, and publication

- Each accepted paper must be covered by a paid **Regular or Member** author registration by 28 August. Student registration cannot cover or present a paper. One author registration covers one paper; a second costs S$350. Each paper must be presented by an author. ([registration](https://www.icems2026.org/registration/))
- The current registration policy gives the controlling publication caveat: only presented papers will be **submitted to IEEE for possible inclusion** in IEEE Xplore, subject to IEEE scope and quality requirements. The conference also states EI/Engineering Index coverage and says selected papers may be recommended for a separate review for possible publication in *IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications*; neither route is an automatic publication guarantee. ([registration](https://www.icems2026.org/registration/), [digest submission](https://www.icems2026.org/digest-submission/))

### AI policy

No ICEMS-specific AI rule was found on the official public pages. The current **IEEE PSPB Operations Manual 2026**, amended 25 June 2026, requires authors to check AI-produced material for accuracy and plagiarism; acknowledge AI-generated intellectual content, including text, images, graphics, and code; and clearly label AI-generated simulation/emulation data. Pure language improvement of human-written text need not be acknowledged when it adds no intellectual content or change of meaning. ([IEEE PSPB Operations Manual](https://pspb.ieee.org/images/files/PSPB/opsmanual.pdf))

## Unresolved official items

- **Anonymity/review model:** official templates are author-identified, but the conference does not explicitly state “single-blind,” “double-blind,” or “non-anonymous.” Treat the formal anonymity policy as unresolved; do not remove authors unless ScholarOne or the secretariat instructs this.
- The **7 September final-paper cutoff time and timezone** are not stated. Only registration deadlines are explicitly Singapore time.
- The full-paper page says registration/payment by 7 September, while the newer registration page requires author registration by **28 August**. Meet the earlier date unless the secretariat confirms otherwise.
- No public full-paper **PDF size limit**, abstract word limit, keyword count, or general oral/poster duration was found.

## Implications for this manuscript

1. First verify an **accepted digest ID, acceptance email, and ScholarOne access**. Without them, ICEMS 2026 is not presently a confirmed submission route; contact [secretariat@icems2026.org](mailto:secretariat@icems2026.org) rather than assuming the final-paper extension permits a new submission.
2. Target **six pages**, A4/two-column, and use the official template unchanged. Convert `Index Terms` to the template's `Keywords` front matter and supply author/affiliation metadata.
3. Select **Grid-Forming Technologies** as the most direct track; **AI for Electrical Drives and Energy Systems** is the clearest alternative for the MARL emphasis.
4. Plan to complete author registration by **28 August**, then PDF eXpress validation, eCopyright, and final upload before 7 September. Recheck the live pages and ScholarOne immediately before submission.
5. Audit any AI-assisted intellectual content against the June 2026 IEEE policy and add a bounded acknowledgment if required.
