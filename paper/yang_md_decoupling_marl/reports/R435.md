## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V — the R432 hypothesis is REFUTED as a causal claim.**
  With the multiplier held at its starting level for the whole run
  (mechanical check passed), the common cost does not systematically fall
  to the pre-registered bar: only 1/6 paired comparisons reach < 0.8 x
  the R432 control, and within-run improvement holds in 1/6 runs.  The
  claim "the budget mechanism stops pressing early, so nothing drives the
  common-frequency cost down" is not supported: the mechanism was pressing
  and the cost did not obey.  Verdict: `refuted` (external-theory-intake
  format).
- **Finding, trust V — the critic divergence is the surviving lead.** The
  q4/q1 divergence (3.6-17.1) persists even with the multiplier live, so
  the value-estimation instability remains the better-supported mechanism
  lead for the CD family's common-frequency gap (R432 secondary
  hypothesis).
- **Evidence boundary.** Same bundle, seeds, RNG streams as R432; one
  floor value (1.0); reporting-only diagnostics (no classifier run); the
  modest tail improvement (above-budget episodes 1,087 vs 1,584) is
  reported as a secondary observation, not a pass.

## Limits

- One floor value (1.0) tested; intermediate floors (e.g., 0.1) untested —
  but the pre-registered primary criterion was the paired 0.8 bar, and the
  mechanism's pressure was maximal under this factor, so the refutation is
  not an artifact of a weak floor.
- The R432 control itself carries the divergence; the paired comparison
  attributes the delta to the floor only under the same-seed protocol.
- No classifier run (reporting-only, R432-style); no claim about other
  bundles or mechanisms.
