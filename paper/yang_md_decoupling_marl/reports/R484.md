## Frozen setup

- Exactly 208 sealed R483 final policies ran for 150 steps (30 s at 0.2 s)
  on four registered canary profiles with six signed scenarios per profile;
  zero and the frozen R481 direct-M/D law were the comparators.
- Frozen controller actions use the registered 50-Hz control semantics, while
  reported physical frequency endpoints use the Kundur 60-Hz base; M/D
  telemetry and decoding use the corrected device base and one registered
  frequency conversion.
- Zero and direct M/D also ran on four non-pooled R481 fresh profiles. Learned
  policies did not run on the fresh bank.
- The learned complete guard required all four per-profile validity, mapping,
  box, slew, common-frequency, action-stress, saturation, variation, and
  dispersion checks plus at least 5% aggregate improvement in both registered
  decoupling endpoints against direct M/D.
- The four R483 source effects were re-estimated prospectively across the 26
  paired training seeds at 30 s against `log(1.10)`, with the registered
  Wilcoxon/sign-flip switch and Holm family of four. Trajectories are not
  treated as independent inferential units, and the 30-second sensitivity
  remains separate from the R483 six-second primary analysis.

  blocks, and 5,088/5,088 trajectories are valid, with no engineering or
  integrity error (`formal_analysis.json#/execution`,
  `formal_analysis.json#/classification`).
- [CLM-1520] None of the 208 frozen R483 policies passes the complete tested-
  bank guard, and none passes even one of four profile guards. All 832 learned
  policy-profile blocks exceed both registered action-stress limits (action
  RMS and total variation no worse than 110% of direct M/D); 408 blocks also
  fail the RoCoF no-harm check and 45 fail the worst-peak no-harm check

  126/208 policies reduce both equal-weight aggregate off-diagonal response
  energy and disturbance-differential energy by at least 5% relative to
  direct M/D, but zero qualifies because the per-profile physical/action
  guard is mandatory (`formal_analysis.json#/learned_guard/policy_decisions`).
- [CLM-1520] Frozen direct M/D passes the registered 30-second Phase-1A gate on
  4/4 fresh profiles. Its reported off-diagonal ratios to zero range from
  0.3987 to 0.5559 and its disturbance-differential ratios range from 0.3307
  to 0.5580;
  these ratios are report lines rather than gate inputs
  (`formal_analysis.json#/deterministic_fresh_tail`).
- [CLM-1520] Direct M/D also passes 4/4 canary profiles, but this canary result
  is descriptive and is used only to validate the learned-policy reference
  (`formal_analysis.json#/deterministic_canary_tail_descriptive`).
- [CLM-1520] No 30-second source-factor effect establishes improvement above
  the 10% materiality boundary after Holm control. The actor-by-critic estimate

  descriptive; actor main is -4.99%, critic main +9.67%, and critic-by-reward

## Conclusions

- Sealed-bank time-domain evidence supports the bounded classification
  `R483-FROZEN-POLICIES-ALL-FAIL-COMPLETE-GUARD`: none of these 208 frozen
  policies satisfies the registered 30-second complete contract on the four
  canary profiles.
- The endpoint/action split is evidence of a tested trade-off, not evidence
  that the learned policies are unusable in every formulation: many policies
  improve both aggregate decoupling endpoints, while every learned profile
  block exceeds the frozen relative action-stress allowance.
- Frozen direct M/D satisfies the registered 30-second fresh-profile
  feasibility gate. The 30-second source-factor analysis does not establish a
  material main or interaction effect after multiplicity control.

## Limits

- The learned verdict covers exactly the 208 frozen R483 policies, one Kundur
  topology, four registered canary profiles, six scenarios per profile, and
  the frozen 30-second contract. It is not a probability, MARL-family,
  topology-generalisation, safety-certificate, EMT, HIL, deployment, or
  arbitrary-horizon claim.
- Learned policies were not evaluated on the R481 fresh bank; the fresh 4/4
  result belongs only to zero and direct M/D. Canary and fresh banks are not
  pooled.
- The action-stress failure is relative to the frozen direct-M/D comparator
  and the prospectively registered 110% limits. It does not establish an
  absolute actuator-energy or hardware-feasibility boundary beyond the tested
  parameter-modulation model.
- `TAIL-MATERIAL-EFFECT-NOT-ESTABLISHED` is not zero, equivalence, or evidence
  against every source intervention. The 30-second sensitivity neither pools
  with nor replaces the R483 six-second primary analysis.
