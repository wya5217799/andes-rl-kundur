## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** On the frozen geometric amplitude ladder
  (2^-k, k=0..5) over the R410 evaluation profiles and the registered three
  signed pair kinds, the controller-to-controller signed-pair odd response
  of the implemented deterministic law is measured quadratic-leading: log-log
  slopes cluster in [1.68, 1.85] with R2 >= 0.997852 and rise monotonically
  toward 2.0 at the small-amplitude end; all 12 profile-pair blocks classify
  QUADRATIC and none rejects on noise floor, anomalous drop, or mode
  inconsistency.
- **Finding, trust V.** The k=0 bank is bit-identical to the R410
  deterministic evaluation, so the measurement environment is the sealed R410
  environment and the ladder is a pure amplitude scan of it.
- **Theory intake verdict.** C.8's mechanism prediction — "the antisymmetric
  signed-pair difference may also be O(eps^2) rather than cubic" for
  locally Lipschitz same-bias controllers with asymmetric one-sided
  branches — is **supported** for the implemented law on this ladder:
  p_hat in the quadratic band in 12/12 blocks, slope trend toward 2.0, and
  the eps^2-compensated norm is flattest.  The stronger cubic-leading branch
  ("first difference occurs at cubic order") is **refuted** on these
  trajectories within the fitted range.  `undecidable` applies only to the
  plateau criterion, which lacks the frozen 20% stability (O-004); the
  classification does not depend on it.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation, the R410
  profile/controller family, the 2^-k ladder k=0..5, the frozen 30-step
  window, and the fixed L2 normalizer; amplitudes below registered/32 were
  not measured; zero-action is the only second controller; no claim about
  learned policies, other laws, other windows, or asymptotic order beyond
  the measured span.
