## Conclusions

- Sealed-bank time-domain evidence supports only an endpoint/command-activity
  separation for this fixed learner family, budget, topology and benchmark:
  many policies improve both aggregate endpoints, but none satisfies the full
  registered contract.
- The registered actor-source, critic-source and interaction analyses do not
  establish a material source effect. Non-rejection is not equivalence and
  does not show that every source intervention has zero effect.
- The separate fresh-bank direct-M/D result remains the valid deterministic
  feasibility reference; it is not evidence that the learned policies were
  tested on a fresh bank.
- Project-side replay of the external action-guard answer accepts the arithmetic
  but limits its construct: normalized command RMS and total variation measure
  comparator-relative command activity, not actuator energy, wear, fatigue,
  thermal stress, absolute safety or deployment no-harm
  (`paper/yang_md_decoupling_marl/working/gpt_pro_r485_action_guard_solution_20260831/PROJECT_INTAKE_REVIEW.md#disposition`).

## Limits

- Counts cover exactly 208 all-fresh policies, one modified Kundur topology,
  the registered finite banks, named controller family and training budget.
  They are not probabilities or universal MARL, topology-generalisation,
  stability, safety, EMT, HIL, hardware or deployment claims.
- The benchmark profiles are outcome-visible frozen records, not a fresh
  population sample. Learned policies were not evaluated on the separate fresh
  direct-M/D bank.
- Command RMS and total variation are dimensionless normalized summaries over
  the registered horizon and channels. No sealed artifact contains a calibrated
  actuator transfer, damage functional, absolute hardware limit or comparator
  safety certificate; actual hardware harm is undecidable.
- The 16-cell threshold sensitivity is offline reuse of the same traces and
  cannot be treated as new replications or as permission to change the frozen
  primary threshold.
- `MATERIAL-EFFECT-NOT-ESTABLISHED` is not zero, equivalence, source
  irrelevance or proof that communication cannot help.
