## Conclusions

- **Decision, trust S.** R401 freezes and seals the complete Gate A
  three-seed development canary contract and the measured host budget; it is
  the only authority the successor execution round may consume.
- **Evidence boundary.** R401 establishes no training success, no checkpoint,
  no trajectory value, no decoupling, no coordination increment, and no MARL
  value; every number above is a prospective contract or capacity fact.
- **Stop boundary.** A canary `CANARY-FAIL` ends the selected learner without
  algorithm replacement; `CANARY-INVALID` carries no scientific conclusion;
  `CANARY-PASS` authorizes only a separately planned and sealed Gate B
  five-seed held-out comparison, not title evidence.

## Limits

- Covers one ANDES 2.0.0 fixed-connectivity modified-Kundur formulation and
  the fresh eight-profile heterogeneous bank only.
- The capacity projection anchors on the measured 30-step rollout cost and
  explicitly excludes learner-update overhead; the successor round must
  re-check the frozen budget at launch, not silently expand it.
- Three seeds give descriptive seed-level sensitivity; no population
  inference is claimed, and the canary has no 10% floor.
- R399 profiles, traces, values, and checkpoints stay disclosed and are not
  unseen evidence here; historical learner code may be revalidated, never
  its checkpoints.
