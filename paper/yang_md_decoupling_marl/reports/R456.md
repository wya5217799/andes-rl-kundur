## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V — GRADIENT-CONFLICT.** On the registered R425 checkpoint-local
  intervention, the RMS constraint and saved value objective oppose each other with
  material strength in 4/6 checkpoints. This is the only pre-registered deep-cause
  tag that fires.
- **Ceiling disposition.** The multiplier ceiling is not the local cause for the
  saved deterministic policies: their development gaps are negative, so U=10 and
  U=100 are identical and both release the multiplier. The positive R425 training
  tails explain historical pinning at the update-law level, but they describe a
  different behavior/time distribution from the final deterministic state bank.
- **Implementation implication.** R425 updates the dual from executed exploratory
  training actions (`explore_noise=0.1`, no registered annealing), while the actor
  penalty and evaluation concern the deterministic actor. Together with the
  positive-training-tail versus negative-final-policy split, this exposes a
  distribution/phase alignment problem in the constraint loop. Exploration is a
  concrete code-path contributor, but this round does not isolate it from policy
  nonstationarity or the changed state distribution, so it is not named as a unique
  causal mechanism.
- **Physical disposition.** None of the five local interventions fixes frequency
  restoration: every common-frequency and worst-peak block still fails. The tested
  ceiling, step, and per-profile changes are not a feasible-controller witness.

## Limits

- R425 final checkpoints only; R427's normalized-critic checkpoints are not
  intervened on. Six paired checkpoints and one fixed-connectivity development/
  evaluation bank provide descriptive, bounded evidence, not population inference.
- The final deterministic bank and retained training tails differ in policy noise,
  training time and visited states. Their sign reversal establishes distribution/
  phase mismatch, not the unique share attributable to exploration noise.
- Fresh Adam, frozen critic, 16 full-bank updates. No historical optimizer/replay,
  critic retraining, target update, convergence result, KKT certificate, global
  feasibility search or policy-class infeasibility conclusion.
- The RMS conflict rule fires exactly at its minimum support count (4/6); two seed-403
  checkpoints have positive cosine. Treat it as a bounded repeated pattern, not a
  universal gradient theorem.
