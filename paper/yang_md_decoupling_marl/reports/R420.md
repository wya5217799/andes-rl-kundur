## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** The R403-style action-effort term at weight 1.0 in
  the differential channel alone does not close the objective-to-gate gap:
  the canary remains CANARY-FAIL and the no-harm guard profile worsens
  (common-frequency 19 -> 36, worst-peak 28 -> 34), so the R419 residual
  is not a simple missing action penalty.
- **Finding, trust V.** The same term regresses the decoupling endpoints:
  the message arm loses its sub-1.0 advantage (0.6954 -> 2.3641
  off-diagonal ratio) and its message increment over the no-message arm
  flips from +43.45% to -35.25%, the first measured negative message
  increment in the repaired-bundle family.
- **Finding, trust V.** The scalar arm is unchanged (identical medians),
  which pins the effect to the single registered factor and shows the
  differential-channel penalty trades decoupling response for action
  regularization while leaving the common-mode no-harm channel (and its
  guards) untouched.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation, three
  seeds, one frozen weight (1.0), one channel placement (differential
  only); the common channel and scalar reward are not varied.

## Limits

- Negative result scoped to this exact repair: a differential-channel-only
  action-effort penalty at weight 1.0. No weight sweep, no channel-placement
  sweep, and no common-channel no-harm term were tested.
- Guard-passing remains unmeasured: every block still violates guards.
- Three seeds per arm: descriptive medians, no population inference.
- The executed actions always use the conservative projector; the
  differentiable slew map in the learner matches it up to float32
  one-ULP bookkeeping (R402 repair).
