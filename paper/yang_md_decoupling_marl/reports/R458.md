## Frozen setup

- The frozen family contains 350 direct-M/D piecewise schedules. All schedules and
  their static reference are evaluated on `dev_a` and `dev_b`; the selection rule
  reads only these development artifacts.
- Priority 1 selects among schedules that are guard-clean on both development
  profiles; priority 2 permits one development profile; priority 3 is an explicitly
  non-witness fallback minimizing the worst exact guard violation.
- The selected schedule is then evaluated once, unchanged, on four fixed evaluation
  profiles. Each profile aggregates six signed scenarios and applies the exact R452
  endpoint, common-frequency, action-stress, saturation, and validity guards.
- The intervention is bounded M/D parameter modulation, not additive power
  injection. It uses the R439 piecewise execution semantics, fixed Kundur
  connectivity, a 0.2-s sample interval, 30 steps, and no learner; the controller
  observation adapter retains the frozen 50-Hz control semantics while all reported
  frequency endpoints use the 60-Hz physical base.

## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V — GUARD-CLEAN-TRANSFER.** Within the frozen 350-schedule direct-
  M/D family, a schedule selected using only two development profiles is guard-clean
  on both development profiles and on two of four fixed evaluation profiles.
- **Selection disposition.** The result removes the direct selection-on-evaluation
  defect affecting the earlier finite-grid observation. The winner's development
  improvement remains selection-conditional and is not an unbiased effect estimate.
- **Transfer disposition.** The result is a finite-bank existence witness for one
  frozen schedule. The two-of-four count is descriptive because the profiles were
  fixed rather than sampled from a declared population.
- **Mechanism disposition.** The result establishes that the registered direct-M/D
  schedule class contains a partial guard-clean transfer witness; it does not show
  that a learner can discover the schedule or identify why the other profiles fail.
