## Frozen setup

- Arms are `cd_matd3_no_message` and `cd_matd3_message`; seeds are 401--405.
  Each arm/seed owns one prospectively seeded, closed four-profile/six-scenario
  replay with 24 episodes, 720 transitions, ordered minibatch indices and target
  noise. Four cells consume the same replay and start from byte-identical fresh
  weights: `none`, `differential_only`, `common_only`, and `both`.
- Phase 1 freezes actors for 512 critic updates. Phase 2 applies 256 further critic
  updates and 128 delayed actor updates. Selected heads receive output-preserving
  PopArt with beta `1e-3` and sigma floor `1e-4`; unselected heads are unchanged.
- Forty final policies are evaluated on the independent four-profile/six-scenario
  bank: 960 trajectories. A separate two-scenario symmetric calibration evaluates
  two common and six differential initial-action directions at amplitudes
  0.02/0.05/0.10: 3,920 trajectories. Including replay construction, the formal
  run contains 5,120 completed physical trajectories and 153,600 transitions.
- Frozen gates require, separately in each communication arm, at least 4/5 seeds
  with >=20% common held-out TD-RMSE improvement, >=0.25 sign-agreement gain and
  >=20% magnitude-error improvement, >=1% actor mediation, and >=5% common/peak
  physical improvement versus `none` plus >=3% versus `differential_only`.

## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V — COMMON-HEAD-HYPOTHESIS-REFUTED.** On the registered fresh-
  weight fixed-replay slice, correct common-head output-preserving stabilization
  neither reduces common held-out TD error by the required amount nor improves
  common-direction calibration or closed-loop common/peak response.
- **Implementation disposition.** R427 contains a real normalization-semantics
  defect: moving statistics are not accompanied by an output-row remap. R457
  repairs that defect for the tested intervention. Because the repaired cell still
  fails every causal gate, that defect is not a sufficient cause of the controller's
  physical failure.
- **Mechanism disposition.** The actor responds strongly, so the negative result is
  not caused by a frozen primal path. The common critic head does not acquire the
  registered error/calibration advantage, and its induced actor movement is not a
  selective frequency-restoration direction on the frozen banks.
- **Programme disposition.** M2 is terminally closed as a valid refutation. It does
  not authorize PopArt tuning, replay regeneration, online retraining or a new critic
  replacement.

## Limits

- Fresh seeded R425-architecture actors, closed replay and 512+256 critic updates;
  this is not an intervention on historical R425/R427 checkpoints and does not
  reconstruct their online optimizer/replay trajectory.
- Fixed Kundur connectivity, two information arms, five seeds, four development and
  four evaluation profiles, and two calibration scenarios. The result is bounded to
  the registered action directions, banks and update budget.
- First-action local directional calibration does not establish global value-function
  correctness, training convergence, topology robustness, Lyapunov stability,
  deployment safety or critic-class impossibility.
- Refuting this specific common-head stabilization hypothesis does not prove that all
  critic errors are irrelevant; it proves that this head-selective repair lacks the
  required causal chain on the registered slice.
