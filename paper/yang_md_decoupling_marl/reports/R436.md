## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V — NO-LEARNING-INCREMENT.** On the verified
  energy-port object, baseline-anchored residual SAC (zero residual =
  exact bandpass K=3.5) converges to the anchor: both arms pass every
  frozen endpoint and guard on all 10 EIG-sound variants, but never
  improve on the deterministic reference and show no message contrast.
  The learned residual policies are effectively identity (no measurable
  learning value beyond the deterministic controller on this object).
- **Evidence boundary.** Covers the frozen 10-variant bank, 5 seeds,
  one reward family, one learner class, one anchor (K=3.5); the result
  bounds the residual-learning route on this object but does not claim
  universal learning uselessness.
