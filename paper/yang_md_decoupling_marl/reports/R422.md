## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** The common-channel placement of the action-effort
  term does not close the objective-to-gate gap: the canary remains
  CANARY-FAIL, no guard count improves (worst-peak and RoCoF worsen),
  and the term saturates the common-channel Lagrange multiplier at its
  ceiling (median 10.0 in all six runs) instead of gently shaping the
  policy.
- **Finding, trust V.** Combined with the R421 readout (critic
  divergence, vanishing actor gradients, slack budget on the R410
  bundle), the three-base evidence localizes the residual failure to the
  learning dynamics rather than to a missing reward term: the unpenalized
  bundles leave the no-harm budget slack, and the penalized bundle
  saturates it — both leave the guards failing, so the next single
  factor should target value-estimation stability, not further
  reward-shaping terms.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation, three
  seeds, one frozen weight (1.0), channel placement varied across R420
  and R422; no weight sweep and no value-estimation repair tested.
