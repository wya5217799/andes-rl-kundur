  projection to the adapted SAC makes the matched bundle row-valid: all 60
  rows pass the row-validity stage (CANARY-INVALID -> CANARY-FAIL), and the
  message arm becomes the first arm in the matched-bundle family to pass

  tolerance), with valid-bank endpoints below the deterministic reference
  (0.635 / 0.590 times) and a positive message contrast (+25.0 % / +34.1 %
  vs the matched no-message arm).  CANARY-FAIL persists only because both
  SAC arms exceed the action-stress guard tolerance (10 %: action RMS and
  total variation relative to the deterministic reference) and the
  no-message arm additionally fails rocof (16/20) and worst-peak (5/20).
- **Finding, trust V.** The R430 message-arm descriptive advantage survives
  on physically valid trajectories (0.635 / 0.590 times vs R430's
  invalid-row context 0.670 / 0.688 times): the adapted-SAC message arm is
  now a measured valid-bank result, and its message benefit is a real
  measurement, not a slew-artifact.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation, five
  seeds, the matched bundle; the action-stress guard failures compare the
  learned policy's action energy against the local-neighbour deterministic
  reference — a policy-aggressiveness comparison, not a validity failure.
