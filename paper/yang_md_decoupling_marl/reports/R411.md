## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** The frozen negative verdict is amplitude-robust:
  CANARY-FAIL with all 36 guard-failure blocks holds at every amplitude of
  the 0.5-1.5 ladder, and the amplitude-1.0 bank is bit-identical to R410.
- **Finding, trust V.** The quantitative endpoint ratios are not
  amplitude-free: 11/18 cells drift more than 20% across the ladder, and the
  message arm's learning-vs-deterministic ratios grow monotonically with
  probe amplitude. The paper's exact ratio numbers are therefore
  registered-grid values (amplitude 1.0); the qualitative claims (guard
  violations, CANARY-FAIL) do not depend on that choice.
- **Finding, trust V.** The clean negative message increment is
  sign-stable on the differential endpoint at every amplitude and on the
  off-diagonal endpoint at 0.7-1.5 (4/5); it peaks at the registered
  amplitude 1.0 (-78.43% / -26.74%, reproducing R410 exactly). The message
  arm never beats the scalar arm on the differential endpoint anywhere in
  the ladder.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation, the R410
  checkpoint set, the frozen estimator/guard family, and the frozen
  amplitude grid only; oddness of the signed-pair response itself is the
  estimator's assumption and is not separately tested here.

## Limits

- The ladder tests probe-amplitude scaling of the registered probe grid; it
  does not test disturbance-amplitude scaling, localized-probe scaling, or
  the oddness symmetry directly.
- Ratio invariance uses the frozen 20% spread bound and the frozen 5-point
  grid; sensitivity is reported per cell, not as a population claim.
- Three seeds per arm: descriptive medians, no population inference.
- All learning blocks still fail the physical guards at every amplitude, so
  every contrast is measured inside the failing regime.
