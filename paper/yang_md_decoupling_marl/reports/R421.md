## Observations

- **O-001 (CLM-1255) — Anchor R410-BIT-IDENTICAL.** All six runs'
  `final.pt` sha256 equal the R410 same-arm-seed checkpoints; all six
  manifests record 43,200 steps with valid convergence diagnostics
  (see `formal_analysis.json#/r410_anchor` and `/r410_anchor_verdict`).
  The instrumentation is machine-proven non-perturbing.
- **O-002 (CLM-1255) — Optimization-failure signal in 6/6 runs.**
  Critic-loss last-quartile/first-quartile medians rise 24.4x to 126.4x
  (Q1 0.28-1.51 -> Q4 14.2-73.5), and the TD-error standard deviation
  rises 4.9x to 11.3x (see `diagnostic_readout.json#/runs`
  `/failure_flags/ratios`).
- **O-003 (CLM-1255) — Policy-stagnation signal in 5/6 runs.** The actor
  log-gradient-norm Q4/Q1 ratios are negative or sub-0.12
  (-0.40 to +0.12), i.e. actor gradients fall below the log floor over
  training (see `diagnostic_readout.json#/runs` `/trends`
  `/actor_grad_norm_mean`).
- **O-004 (CLM-1255) — Value-estimation signal in 4/6 runs.** Bellman
  residual magnitude Q4/Q1 ratios reach 3.6x-12.6x (one run flips sign,
  one stays at 1.2x) (see `diagnostic_readout.json#/runs`
  `/failure_flags/ratios`).
- **O-005 (CLM-1255) — The common-channel Lagrange budget never binds.**
  The multiplier median stays at 0.0 through training in five runs and
  reaches only 0.073 in one (see `diagnostic_readout.json#/runs`
  `/trends` `/lagrange`), so the common-mode no-harm constraint is
  slack throughout.
- **O-006 (CLM-1255) — No exploration collapse.** Sampled-state variance
  Q4/Q1 stays 0.99-1.15 in all six runs (see
  `diagnostic_readout.json#/runs` `/failure_flags/ratios`).

## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** The diagnostic instrumentation is bit-non-
  perturbing: all six reruns reproduce the R410 checkpoints byte-for-
  byte, so the recorded curves are the exact R410 training dynamics.
- **Finding, trust V (hypothesis-level).** The R410-family CD training
  shows critic divergence (loss and TD-error spread grow 5-126x), actor
  gradient vanishing, and a never-binding common-channel Lagrange budget
  — consistent with a value-estimation divergence driving the actor into
  the guard-failing high-energy policy while the common-mode no-harm
  channel is never actually enforced.  This reframes the R419
  objective-to-gate localization: the gap is not only missing terms but
  a slack no-harm enforcement plus a diverging value estimate.
- **Evidence boundary.** Measured on the R410 bundle (pre-slew repair)
  only; readout classes are pre-registered hypotheses, not attributions;
  the R419-family may differ (R422, in flight, measures the
  common-channel action-effort term on the R419 bundle).
