## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — Q-ENTRY (bandpass_k3p5, also bandpass_k4)**: the
  frozen single-family 0.4 Hz ring-edge bandpass enters Q = {r_d <= 0.95,
  r_cross <= 1.10} on the registered disclosed development bank with every
  guard passing and the strict cross gate (<= 0.95) also passing. P0' is
  answered constructively for this structure class: Q is non-empty, and the
  earlier single-family closures (R406/R407) were boundary artifacts of the
  frozen grids, exactly as the external advisory analysis predicted.
- **P5 disposition**: the product-type tradeoff bound was already falsified
  (R407 K=2 product 0.70125 < 1.045); Q-ENTRY additionally refutes any
  "no finite-order LTI filter structure can enter Q" claim for this
  structure class on this bank. No new tradeoff theorem is proposed.
- **P6 disposition**: the small-gain anomaly is a reference-semantics
  artifact (replacement architecture; K->0 limit = zero-action arm at
  1.2027, smooth monotone curve), with a real but negligible port-map
  zero-sum leak at K^2 order; protocol/artifact causes are excluded (all
  records valid, R407 endpoints reproduce).
- **P8 disposition**: the minimal structural change is none - the frozen
  structure with K extended to 3.5-4.0 suffices; the blend candidates are
  not needed and both fail (bounded). The found arm awaits a separately
  registered held-out gate before any title-supporting use.
- **Authority boundary**: no training, no fresh unseen bank, no held-out
  access, no algorithm replacement; R404 values remain outside title-
  supporting results; the advisory external candidates (ARTIFACTS.json
  v2-external-candidates) are confirmed/falsified here as predictions, not
  adopted as evidence.
