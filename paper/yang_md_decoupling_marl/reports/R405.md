## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — GUARD-FAIL** (sealed disclosed-development bank): the
  candidate-A static M/D homogenization arm fails the frozen no-harm ceiling
  before any endpoint branch of the decision tree is reached. Candidate A
  stops without retry or repair inside this round.
- **Mechanism boundary**: the external solution's first-order moment
  prediction (homogenization zeroes Var(1/M) and Var(D/M^2), hence the
  first-order cross channel) does not transfer to the registered finite-window
  cross-energy metric on the physical plant: the measured cross ratio is
  `2.22x` worse, not improved. The network-asymmetry contribution and the
  reduced effective damping of the homogenized plant dominate the registered
  6-s window response; the time-varying reference law suppresses cross energy
  through its dynamics, which a pure static bias cannot reproduce.
- **Authority boundary**: this round produces no title-positive claim and no
  statement about the A+B combination, MARL, or any trained policy.
