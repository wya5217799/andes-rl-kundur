## Conclusions

- **Sealed-bank time-domain**: at fixed other factors, the authentic same-time
  ring-neighbour source in the critic path lowers held-out primary
  disturbance-differential energy relative to the exogenous random-policy donor
  placebo: geometric improvement 18.8% (paired CI lower bound 13.7% > 10%
  materiality, Holm-controlled). Per the code-verified 2026-08-22 wording
  boundary this is the total source effect — authentic neighbour source versus
  exogenous placebo — never a pure semantic neighbour-information value.
- **Sealed-bank time-domain**: no such effect is measured in the actor factor
  (CI straddles zero).
- The pre-registered classifier `U2-SOURCE-EFFECT-SUPPORTED` holds; no outcome
  authorizes a universal intrinsic communication claim.
- **Theory-intake verdict (retained R472 prediction)**: `supported` — the
  critic-factor `N-P` contrast clears the 10% materiality under Holm control
  with no half/final direction flip and no plateau rows (observables at
  `formal_analysis.json#/primary_holm_tests` and `#/optimization`). The actor
  factor alone would be `undecidable`: CI straddles zero with mixed seed signs,
  so no directional verdict is written for it.

## Limits

- Covers six seeds, the frozen R470 learner/bank/projector, the Kundur two-area
  topology, and the registered scenario bank only; no topology variation,
  other algorithm or bank, probability, deployment, safety, EMT, or HIL claim.
- The `N-P` contrast confounds trajectory same-timing with source authenticity;
  the experiment does not isolate semantic information content.
- Reward-factor cells participate in the factorial means but no reward-level
  inference is drawn; the `P` bank is scenario-matched, not adversarially tuned.
