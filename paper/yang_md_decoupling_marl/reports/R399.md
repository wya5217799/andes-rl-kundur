## Frozen setup

- ANDES 2.0.0 modified Kundur network at 60 Hz; fixed connectivity; four VSG
  proxies; one independent bounded `delta_M_i,delta_D_i` row per VSG; 0.2-s
  updates over a 6-s post-disturbance window.
- Two heterogeneous development profiles and four unseen evaluation profiles;
  each profile contains signed common, differential, and localized disturbance
  pairs. Operating load, VSG M/D mismatch, disturbance location, sign,
  magnitude, and spatial distribution vary prospectively.
- Arms are zero action plus nine matched local-neighbour dynamic M/D laws. One
  global law is selected on development only; a non-deployable oracle may
  select one of the same nine laws per evaluation profile after seeing outcomes.
- PASS requires at least 5% improvement over the selected deterministic law on
  both signed off-diagonal response energy and disturbance differential energy,
  with 3% common/peak/RoCoF no-harm, 10% action-stress no-harm, completion,
  identity, bounds, slew, saturation, and nonconstant independent-action guards.
- Reward and training outputs are absent. Four simulator workers plus one
  launcher use one native numerical thread each.

## Conclusions

- C1 (sealed-bank time-domain): the registered finite-law G1 formulation has no
  demonstrated joint physical headroom beyond its strongest development-selected
  deterministic law on the four evaluation profiles. Both required incremental
  effects are zero, below the prospectively fixed five-percent floor.
- C2 (sealed-bank time-domain): the stop is not caused by convergence, common
  frequency, peak, RoCoF, action stress, saturation, bounds, slew, actuator
  identity, or constant/shared actions. The finite oracle simply cannot improve
  on the same high-gain deterministic law under the registered constraints.
- C3 (registered decision): the line stops before learner implementation or
  training. R399 does not support `Decoupling-Oriented`, `Coordination`, or
  `Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning` as achieved title claims.

## Limits

- The result covers one phasor-domain simulator, one modified Kundur topology,
  six prospectively heterogeneous profiles, three signed disturbance-pair
  families, a 6-s window, and one finite nine-law local-neighbour controller
  family. It is not a theorem that arbitrary direct-M/D actions or reinforcement
  learning have no value.
- The oracle is outcome-seeing but finite-law and profile-conditional; it is not
  a deployable controller and not an unconstrained per-step action optimizer.
- `z_c` and `T_d` are arithmetic common/differential endpoint coordinates, not
  identified eigenmodes. No modal, stability-certificate, topology-general,
  EMT, HIL, hardware, safety, or field evidence is present.
- Evaluation profiles are held out from deterministic-law selection, but the
  four-profile table and leave-one-profile-out range are descriptive finite-bank
  sensitivity only; no population inference is claimed.
