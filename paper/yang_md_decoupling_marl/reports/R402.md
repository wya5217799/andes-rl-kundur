## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** On the sealed fresh four-profile bank, the fixed
  CD-MATD3 learner, its matched no-message ablation, and the fresh
  Yang-compatible scalar TD3 all fail the canary: every learning block
  violates the registered 103% common no-harm and 110% action-stress
  budgets, every arm degrades both registered decoupling endpoints by at
  least 2.9x versus the strong deterministic reference, and the
  message-enabled arm adds no coordination increment (negative versus the
  matched no-message arm).
- **Decision, trust S.** The canary fails; per the R400 amendment the
  selected learner route ends without algorithm replacement, and no Gate B
  comparison is authorized.  R402 supports none of the title terms.
- **Evidence boundary.** This is one fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation
  and one fresh eight-profile bank; it is not a rejection of MARL or
  direct-M/D learning in general, and R399 remains a valid finite non-learning
  stop.
