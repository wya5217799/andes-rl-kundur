## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** The R433-trained SAC arms evaluate row-valid on
  all 10 unseen topologies of the R413 bank with a guard profile
  indistinguishable from the nominal variant: the action-RMS win persists
  10/10 and the frequency-restoration failures persist 10/10, endpoints
  stay 1.19-1.25x the deterministic reference on every variant, and the
  message contrast stays positive (+50-52 / +62-65 %).  Measured topology
  transfer is a stable, invariant profile — neither a variant-specific
  collapse nor a recovery.  This is the first measured topology readout
  for the learning arms (Object A); it supports topology-transfer
  language only in the stability sense, never a pass or generalization
  claim.
- **Evidence boundary.** Ten EIG-sound variants of one frozen bank, one
  fixed-connectivity formulation, two SAC arms, five seeds, the R433
  trained checkpoints only; the two VSG-tie outage variants are excluded
  by the sealed R413 case-level soundness verdict (cited, not re-run).

## Limits

- Covers the 10 EIG-sound R413 variants only; combined (N-2) outages,
  other lines, other impedances, and other topology families are untested.
- The transferred profile is the R433 trade-off state (frequency-
  restoration guards failing, endpoints above the reference); this round
  measures transfer, not repair.
- Two SAC arms, five seeds, descriptive medians; no unseen-bank claim, no
  claim about other trained checkpoints (e.g., R431's), no universal-SAC
  claim.
