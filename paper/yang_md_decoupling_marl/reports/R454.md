## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V — SUPPORTED.** The registered anchor classification is
  `IDENTITY-LOCAL-MAX-SUPPORTED-ON-REGISTERED-SLICE`: zero residual is a local
  maximum of both implemented regularized reward definitions along the four
  tested directions and three conditions.
- **Mechanism disposition.** The near-zero R436 endpoint is consistent with a
  reward landscape whose explicit residual penalty strongly favors the
  deterministic anchor. It is not explained by a frozen optimizer or by the
  feasibility mapper removing residual authority. The saved critic gradients
  are weakly sign-misaligned on the small comparable subset, so critic quality
  remains a contributing implementation concern rather than a unique cause.
- **Algorithm implication.** R436's residual SAC cannot be interpreted as
  having discovered that zero residual is physically optimal. Its stated
  objective itself makes deviations costly, while the available checkpoint
  critic directions only narrowly fail the registered alignment threshold.
