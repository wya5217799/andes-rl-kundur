## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V — GUARD-VIOLATED (action-stress only).** The R439
  time-varying winners (constant (3.0, 3.0) gain schedules) keep the
  decoupling endpoint improvement (r_d 6.0-14.4%, r_cross 7.6-11.9%) and keep
  common-mode no-harm clean, but they do so by raising action effort beyond
  the +10% action-stress budget (action RMS +28-34%, total variation
  +10-17%). The time-varying headroom is therefore an endpoint headroom with
  a measurable action-effort cost, not a fully no-harm decoupling headroom.
- **CLM-1355 disposition.** The literal "cross-response not degraded" clause
  is confirmed and strengthened (r_cross improves on all profiles); the
  correction is that the no-harm half of the decoupling contract (which
  R439's plan pre-registered as "all guards pass" but never checked) is
  violated on the action-stress axis. Common-mode no-harm is intact, so the
  decoupling compatibility is not overturned.
