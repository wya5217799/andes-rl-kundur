## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** The slew-state contract was the dominant endpoint
  blocker: with the previous executed action in the actor state and
  aligned target semantics, the CD arms move from 2.01-5.26x to
  0.70-1.23x of the deterministic reference on the two endpoints, and the
  message increment flips to a clean positive +43.45% / +25.59%; the
  ablation confirms the CD policies actually consume the added feature.
- **Finding, trust V.** The bundle effect does not flip the canary:
  every learning block still violates the action-stress guards and a
  subset of the common no-harm guards, so the residual failure is now
  localized to the objective-to-decision-contract gap (the CD objective
  carries no action-effort or no-harm terms) rather than to state
  representation.
- **Finding, trust V.** The scalar arm neither improves nor uses the
  feature, consistent with its unchanged objective mismatch.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation, three
  seeds, the frozen reward/guard family, the registered single-factor
  repair only.

## Limits

- Guard-passing remains unmeasured: the positive message increment and
  the sub-1.0 endpoint ratios are achieved inside a guard-failing regime.
- Three seeds per arm: descriptive medians, no population inference.
- The differentiable slew map in the learner matches the runtime
  projector up to its float32 one-ULP bookkeeping (R402 repair); the
  executed actions always use the conservative projector.
