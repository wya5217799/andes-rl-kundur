## Conclusions

- Under the corrected-card adaptive eight-arm by 26-seed factorial, none of
  the four registered main or interaction effects establishes improvement

- The actor-by-critic estimate is large enough to report descriptively, but

  interaction claim.
- Reaching the maximum budget without the registered convergence certificate

## Limits

- The result covers the frozen learner, source intervention, reward penalty,
  26 seeds, Kundur two-area system, registered profile bank, and six-second
  evaluation window only. It does not establish topology, algorithm, H0, deployment,
  EMT, HIL, or safety generalisation.
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED` is not equivalence and is not a zero-effect result. The
  four registered interventions also do not isolate pure semantic neighbour
  information from optimization, contemporaneous dependence, source
  authenticity, or distribution shift.
- All 208 cells exhausted the maximum budget, so R483 supplies no convergence
  certificate and no evidence that the adaptive stop was effective.
- The six-second evaluation window is horizon-sensitive in the current
  evidence chain.
  R483 did not test the 30-second tail or the complete corrected-card physical
  guards; therefore it cannot support a paper-level sentence that the frozen
  R483 policies failed the registered complete physical contract on the tested
  bank. A prospective evaluation-only successor is required before even that
  bounded sentence can enter the manuscript; no algorithm-family claim is
  authorized.
