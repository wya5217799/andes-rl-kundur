## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** The implemented constraint terms enter the actor
  loss with a reward sign (verified on the sealed learner by a gradient
  probe; the frozen plan's appended-terms form penalizes them), and the dual
  ascent self-reinforces the weight to the 10.0 ceiling while the residuals
  stay 38.3–90.8× — the actor is actively driven to maximal actuation and
  pins at the bounds, where the slew clip's zero gradient in saturation then
  blocks any corrective signal. The pre-registered criterion (action-stress
  blocks 36 → <36) is not met, and the guard-aligned constraint as designed
  was not actually tested: the negative outcome is the implementation-layer
  manifestation of the R423 constraint-hierarchy diagnosis — a sign defect
  compounded by clip saturation — not evidence about guard-aligned
  constraints as a mechanism class (code-level verification on the sealed
  learner plus a gradient probe; sealed-bank evidence).
- **Finding, trust V.** The resulting bang-bang policy yields the family's
  best common no-harm profile (common-frequency 12, RoCoF 14, worst-peak 24)
  and empties the common budget (λ → 0 in 6/6), at the price of unchanged
  action stress (36/36), the saturation-budget failure (0 → 24), endpoint
  regression against the same-reward R419 base (message arm 0.6954 → 2.3419
  off-diagonal), and a message contrast masked by slew clipping (increment
  0.0000, both arms bit-identical, all 12 eval-file pairs pairwise distinct —
  no verdict on communication value).
- **Finding, trust V.** The critic divergence persists (Q4/Q1 5.98–9.61 ≥ 3),
  so the value-scale layer remains the proximate driver pushing the actor
  into the high-energy region.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation, three
  seeds, one frozen multiplier schedule (step 0.05, ceiling 10.0); the
  sign-defect reading is a code-level verification on the sealed learner
  plus a gradient probe, and the saturation fractions are recomputed on all
  36 blocks — bounded to the tested bundle.
