## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V — REFUTED.** On the sealed R415 records, the frozen
  0.4 Hz bandpass channel is not detuned from the a4_md_relaxed dominant
  differential mode: the peak stays inside the 0.3-0.5 Hz window (0.449 Hz)
  with window energy fraction 0.587, above the passing blocks. The r_d = 0.9712
  failure mechanism is therefore not located by spectral detuning; the
  hypothesis that the relaxed-inertia perturbation moves the dominant mode out
  of the channel is refuted on this bank.
- **Evidence boundary.** Offline diagnosis on 30 sealed records per block;
  covers peak location and window energy only. Gain/phase margin analysis,
  time-domain settling structure, or per-scenario conditioning are untested.
  No claim about the failure's actual cause; no new experiment implied.
