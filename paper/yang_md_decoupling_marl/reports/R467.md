## Conclusions

- **Pole outcome — NO-CROSSING-UP-TO-2S.** On the registered nominal Object B grid, no local sampled pole reaches the unit circle through 2 s of exact command delay. This is a pointwise nominal local result, not a robust delay margin or a continuous-interval theorem.

A crossing inside that interval follows only if performance is continuous between the separately executed endpoints; the experiment itself does not prove that continuity.
- The linear and nonlinear boundaries answer different questions and are not pooled. The absence of a nominal local pole crossing through 2 s coexists with an empirical performance degradation at tens of milliseconds.

## Limits
