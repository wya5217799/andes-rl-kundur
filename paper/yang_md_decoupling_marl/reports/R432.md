## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** The B3 rerun delivers the missing training
  telemetry for the R410 repaired bundle: full per-step critic/actor/
  lagrange/replay traces and per-episode cost/multiplier records, verified
  RNG-transparent by bitcheck.
- **Finding, trust V (mechanism hypotheses, not causes).** The R410-family
  training dynamics show (a) sustained critic divergence (Q4/Q1 6.2-30.5)
  in all six runs, and (b) a lagrange multiplier that decays to 0.0 in
  4/6 runs (0.096-0.118 at the end in 2/6) while the per-episode common
  cost shows no systematic improvement (population 0.008-11.1, 81-480 of
  1,440 episodes per run above the 3.0 budget).  Bounded hypothesis: the
  budget mechanism stops pressing early in training, so nothing drives the
  common-frequency cost down — consistent with the family-wide
  common-frequency no-harm guard failures; the critic divergence persists
  independently.
- **Evidence boundary.** Six runs on the R410 bundle only; the R419+ line
  (R425/R427) already suppressed its critic divergence on a different
  bundle; no guard verdict, no classification, no cross-bundle inference.
