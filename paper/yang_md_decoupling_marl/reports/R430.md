## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** The repository's topology-adapted per-agent SAC with
  the historical normalized reward trains stably on the matched bundle
  (critic loss falls, alpha rests at its lower bound — the opposite of the
  R428 exact-interface collapse) but produces no valid evaluation row: every
  SAC block executes action deltas 5-7x the actuator slew limit because the
  frozen interface has no slew projection, so the bank classifies
  CANARY-INVALID.  This instantiates the pre-registered no-slew-projection
  validity risk as a measured, bounded negative endpoint, not a tuning
  opportunity.
- **Finding, trust V.** Same-harness contrast: the repaired CD family (R425)
  reaches a bank with all 36 rows valid but fails the physical guards
  (CANARY-FAIL: common-frequency 36/36, worst-peak 36/36, RoCoF 14/36, and
  each action-stress guard 12/36 blocks), while the adapted SAC fails at the
  row-validity stage entirely; on this physics, healthy SAC training alone
  cannot satisfy the harness actuator-rate constraint, and the message arm's
  favorable descriptive endpoints cannot be registered as a decoupling or
  coordination result.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation, three
  seeds, the matched 43,200-step budget, the frozen classifier; descriptive
  medians over slew-invalid rows are context, not measurement-of-record.

## Limits

  attempted (pre-registered); a repair would be a new round.
- Three seeds; descriptive medians only; no claim about other SAC variants
