## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** A frozen critic gradient clip (max_norm 1.0) on
  the CD arms damps the measured critic divergence roughly 5-15x
  (Q4/Q1 5.0-8.0 versus 24.4-126.4x unclipped) but does not stop it
  (threshold 3 not met), so the divergence driver is the growing
  target-scale loop rather than unbounded update steps alone — the
  clip-only repair is insufficient by the pre-registered rule.
- **Finding, trust V.** The clip produces the family's first no-harm
  guard gains (RoCoF 26 -> 22 blocks, worst-peak 36 -> 35) and moves
  both CD arms toward the deterministic reference versus R422, while
  action-stress stays 36/36 and the message increment flips negative —
  a bounded critic steers a better-behaved but still guard-failing
  policy, and the invariant action-stress failures are consistent with
  the constraint-hierarchy diagnosis (action statistics absent from the
  training constraint), not with an optimization artifact.
- **Finding, trust V.** The scalar arm is bit-identical to R422 in
  checkpoints and slew diagnostics, pinning every effect to the single
  registered factor.
- **Finding, trust V (CLM-1275).** The modal identities hold exactly on
  the recorded trajectories and the executed actions are
  differential-dominant (fractions 0.36-0.82 across bundles), which
  instantiates the theory-neutral verdict on channel placement: the
  R420/R422 effort interventions are effective-weight and budget
  effects on predominantly differential action energy, not modal
  routing.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation, three
  seeds, one frozen clip value (1.0); the divergence comparison against
  CLM-1255 spans two bundles (R410 vs R422) and is indicative, not
  identically conditioned.
