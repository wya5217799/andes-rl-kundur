## Limits

- The R460 metric bank uses one fixed untrained successor-policy initialization for semantic/metric verification. Its clean completion is not controller-performance evidence.
- The finite-class result does not prove infeasibility of neural policies, continuous schedules, another controller family, or the nonlinear plant. A future neural optimizer returning positive `t` would still require separation of optimizer failure from class infeasibility.
- Four fixed evaluation profiles are a table, not a population sample; no probability, confidence interval, unseen-topology, stability, EMT, HIL, field, safety, or deployment claim follows.
- R431 missing traces cannot be reconstructed from checkpoints. Empty schema placeholders are not evidence that constraints were evaluated during training.
- R456 is post-hoc and frozen-network-local. It cannot be relabelled as the original optimizer trajectory or a KKT/global certificate.
