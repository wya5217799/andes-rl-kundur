  ring-neighbour source versus the pre-registered row-permuted placebo does
  not establish an effect above the 10% materiality bar in either factor

  0.953125; both Holm rows not rejected). Per the pre-registered wording this
  reads as "no effect above 10% was established" — never as "there is no

- **Theory-intake verdict (R476/R475 pre-registered prediction)**: the second
  branch holds — the clean row-permuted placebo establishes neither an actor
  nor a critic effect above 10% under the direct Holm; the effect is not
  established at that bar. No wording beyond that bar is authorized.

## Limits

- Covers six seeds, the frozen R470 learner/bank/projector, the Kundur two-area
  topology, and the registered scenario bank only; no topology variation,
  other algorithm or bank, probability, deployment, safety, EMT, or HIL claim.
- A negative outcome here is resolution-limited: with n=6 the design detects a
  true 20% improvement only about 73.5% of the time; smaller true effects are
  not excluded.
- The `N-P` contrast is a total source effect — authentic neighbour source
  versus the same-time row-permuted placebo; it does not isolate semantic
  information content, optimization margin, or diagonal/self structure.
- The 16 carried-over cells are complete R476 products, never analyzed
  separately; R476 partial output stays excluded as evidence.
