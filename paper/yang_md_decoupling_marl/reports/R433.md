## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** The action-stress penalty (`lambda_p = 10`,
  `-mean(a^2)` on the projected executed action) achieves its direct
  objective — the action-RMS guard passes 0/20 failures (R431: 20/20) —
  but at a systemic cost: the message arm loses its R431
  frequency-restoration passes (common-frequency and worst-peak regress
  0/20 -> 20/20 and 0/20 -> 18/20), endpoints degrade from 0.635 / 0.590
  to 1.229 / 1.221
  times the deterministic reference, the critic loss rises across training
  (q4/q1 2.3-2.9), and alpha leaves the lower bound (0.14-0.19); the
  action-variation guard (per-step change magnitude, untouched by a mean
  squared penalty) still fails 20/20.  The round closes as a measured
  trade-off endpoint: this penalty does not produce a valid bank.
- **Finding, trust V.** The R431 message-arm gains (frequency-restoration
  passes and below-reference endpoints) are not robust to this reward
  shaping: a term that shrinks mean action magnitude shifts the policy
  away from the behavior that restored frequency.  The message contrast
  surviving (+50.5 %/+63.8 %) is on degraded trajectories and does not
  offset the guard regression.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity formulation, five seeds,
  the matched bundle, one pre-selected lambda_p (10.0); no claim about
  other penalty coefficients or shaping forms.
