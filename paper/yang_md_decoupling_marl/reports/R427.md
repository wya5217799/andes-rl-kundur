## Conclusions

- **Finding, trust V.** DR-menu P1 critic target normalization suppresses
  the critic divergence on the R425 bundle: the pre-registered
  original-scale Q4/Q1 < 3 criterion is met (0.32-1.75 vs 4.65-6.29), the
  normalized loss stays O(1), and the untouched scalar arm still diverges
  — the value-estimation layer, the proximate driver since R421, is
  repaired where applied (sealed-bank evidence; frozen classifier).
- **Finding, trust V.** The repair cascades to the actor: log-gradient
  norms stop vanishing, and the action-stress no-harm guards stop failing
  across all 36 CD blocks (R425: 12/36).  The action-stress gap is closed
  empirically even though the guard-aligned dual stays ceiling-capped
  (multiplier 10.0, residuals 2.6-5.4x) — the guard pass is reported as
  an endpoint fact, not as constraint convergence.
- **Finding, trust V.** The residual is now frequency restoration, not
  action stress or value scale: common-frequency and worst-peak no-harm
  guards remain failed in every CD block, and endpoints sit ~3.1-3.4x
  above the unpenalized base with a ≈0 message increment — CANARY-FAIL
  holds with a narrowed residual.
- **Evidence boundary.** One fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation, three
  seeds, one frozen normalization schedule (beta 1e-3, sigma floor 1e-4),
  layered on the R425 bundle; bounded to the tested bundle.

## Limits

- No arm passes all guards (common-frequency + worst-peak remain); no
  guard-passing claim.
- Three seeds per arm (descriptive medians); message increment ≈0 is a
  descriptive mixed-sign median, not population inference.
- One normalization schedule tested; no beta/sigma-floor sweep; the
  Tier-1 early transient (155-326x at 8,640 steps) is reported as a
  screening observation, not a judgement.
- The action-stress guard pass coexists with a ceiling-capped dual
  (residuals 2.6-5.4x) — the causal link between the normalization and
  the guard pass is not isolated (the R425 constraint is also active).
