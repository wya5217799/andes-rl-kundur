## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — STOP-UNSAFE-CONTROL**: R375 repairs the R374 identity
  validity defect and establishes a valid deterministic comparison, but the
  selected distributed controller fails the prospectively registered
  saturation guard on every held-out controller trajectory.
- **Efficacy boundary**: directional decoupling and differential-response
  improvements are visible only as guard-failed diagnostics. They cannot
  support deterministic decoupling efficacy, safe coordination, control gain,
  learning headroom, or title evidence.
- **Formulation decision**: the currently frozen deterministic
  power-reference formulation stops. Its registered rule does not authorize a
  gain change, ramp-limit relaxation, alternative anti-windup design,
  controller sweep, R375 retry, non-learning headroom gate, or MARL training.
- **Programme boundary**: the working title remains fixed, but this result does
  not validate `Decoupling-Oriented`, `Coordination`, or `Multi-Agent
  Reinforcement Learning`. Continuing toward the title requires a separately
  justified and prospectively registered formulation, not reuse of the failed
  controller as a safe residual base.

## Limits

- The result covers one deterministic controller family, four prospectively
  frozen gain pairs, one modified-Kundur topology, and finite development and
  held-out operating-condition banks.
- The saturation is a tested energy-port ramp-projection event, not a formal
  instability certificate, hardware failure, protection action, current or
  voltage violation, or proof that distributed control or MARL is impossible.
- Arithmetic common/differential coordinates are not eigenmodes; the finite
  time-domain response does not establish hard diagonalization, small-signal
  stability, robust safety, topology generalization, or deployment readiness.
- No communication ablation, independent learner, MARL method, neural
  residual, training seed, checkpoint, unseen topology, converter inner loop,
  EMT model, or hardware platform is present.
