## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — STOP-MODEL-FIDELITY**: the registered full-order source
  models are validly constructed, but they do not reproduce the nonlinear
  four-frequency response to any sealed single-control pulse within the frozen
  fidelity limits at either operating point.
- **Model boundary**: source/Jacobian agreement and input-channel rank do not
  transfer to sampled nonlinear trajectory fidelity on this object. The
  registered model-based route stops before controller design.
- **Authority boundary**: this result authorizes neither offline controller
  synthesis, physical closed-loop comparison, training, MARL, nor a retry with
  changed points, pulses, steps, outputs, thresholds, or model order.

## Limits

- Covers one modified-Kundur topology, two fixed operating points, deterministic
  small active-power pulses, four VSG frequency outputs, and one full-order
  local-linear model construction.
- The model has privileged full-state and exact-input access; it is not a
  distributed controller, coordination result, learning result, stability or
  safety certificate, robustness result, topology-generalization result, or
  hardware result.
- Load and joint-input record metrics remain in the formal analysis but cannot
  weaken or reverse the earlier single-control stop.
