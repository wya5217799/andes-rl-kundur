## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — STOP-DEVELOPMENT-NO-CANDIDATE**: the one registered
  second-order neighbour controller is physically valid and reduces the
  development differential-energy endpoint, but it does not improve settling
  by the frozen minimum and violates both probe-cross no-harm checks.
- **Mechanism boundary**: passing the scratch filter-only frequency/step gate
  does not establish closed-loop plant decoupling. The fixed two-stage
  washout mechanism does not qualify as the deterministic baseline on this
  topology, action port, information pattern, bank, and threshold contract.
- **Authority boundary**: this result authorizes no evaluation-bank access,
  non-learning headroom test, retry, gain/order/corner change, training, MARL,
  stability, robustness, topology-generalization, or deployment claim.

## Limits

- Covers one modified-Kundur topology, one fixed two-stage washout candidate,
