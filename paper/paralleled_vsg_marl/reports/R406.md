## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — SWEEP-NO-CANDIDATE**: the frozen first-order
  high-pass damping family has no grid point that passes both registered
  thresholds on the disclosed development bank; the family is closed within
  the frozen grid.
- **Mechanism boundary**: the external solution's linear-interpolation
  prediction (a feasible point near alpha ~ 0.675) is refuted: the
  differential objective becomes feasible only at high alpha, where the
  probe-cross objective is farthest from its ceiling. The two objectives are
  systematically in tension for a first-order frequency-selective channel.
- **Authority boundary**: no held-out bank was touched, no gain/order
  change outside the grid occurred, and no title-positive or impossibility
  claim is made (the closure is bounded to the frozen grid and this family).
