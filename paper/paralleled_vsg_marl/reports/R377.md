## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — STOP-DEVELOPMENT-NO-CANDIDATE (contract-rule floor)**:
  the high-pass channel is physically sound and directionally promising
  (cross-response repaired, differential energy reduced), but the frozen
  one-dt settling improvement rule is unsatisfiable because the local arm
  already sits at the registered settling floor. The stop does not evidence
  absence of differential benefit.
- **Boundary**: this is not an efficacy result. The descriptive development
  ratios cannot support decoupling efficacy, coordination, or learning
  headroom claims.
- **Formulation decision**: the R377 execution records are valid and
  immutable. The next registered action is a separately sealed correction
  round that changes only the settling rule (to "no worse than the local
  arm") and reanalyses the same 60 immutable records before any conditional
  held-out execution. No retry, gain change, or training is authorized.
