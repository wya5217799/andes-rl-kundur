## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — STOP-NO-CONDITIONAL-HEADROOM**: the fixed local-
  neighbour M/D controller is efficacious on the finite development bank, but
  the prospectively registered finite-family conditional oracle does not
  demonstrate enough incremental headroom to justify neural training.
- **Mechanism boundary**: the earlier no-neural-residual risk is not solved by
  reusing direct per-VSG M/D learning.  The observed failure is not action
  flatness: oracle traces are time-varying and select more than one gain pair;
  their attainable aggregate advantage is simply below the frozen threshold.
- **Programme decision**: Q-0103 closes negative and this direct-M/D learning
  formulation stops without an algorithm, reward, network, seed, or training
  sweep.  A new same-title route must materially change the controllable
  coordination mechanism and repeat the object/authority/headroom gates before
  any training.

## Limits

- This is a finite deterministic development bank on one modified-Kundur
  topology.  The 69.92% deterministic reduction and 1.05% oracle increment are
  development-gate values, not held-out estimates or population effects.
- The oracle sees completed outcomes and is non-deployable.  Its failure below
  5% rejects only the registered pretraining screen for this direct-M/D
  formulation.  Best-of-nine family selection is not an upper bound over all
  admissible direct M/D trajectories, so the result neither proves that neural
  headroom is impossible nor shows that MARL, communication, or learned
  coordination has no value as a class.
- The actuator remains GENCLS M/D parameter modulation without converter,
  storage-energy, current, thermal, recovery, hardware, stability-certificate,
  or safety semantics.
- No learned policy, message ablation, training seed, checkpoint, uncertainty
  interval, unseen operating condition, dropout, or topology is evaluated.
  The fixed paper title is therefore still unsupported as a complete claim.
