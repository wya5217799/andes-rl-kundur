## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — ENERGY-PORT-DESIGN-PASS**: the fixed-title line now has
  a source-bound, testable four-actor/four-VSG/four-port implementation
  contract that keeps power commands, torque writes, achieved power, and
  energy accounting distinct.  This pass makes only the next physical object
  test eligible.
- **Object boundary**: the new action enters each governor-free GENCLS VSG's
  mechanical-power balance through its own setpoint seam.  The external energy
  ledger is prospectively bound to that same port's achieved power; it is not
  yet evidence that the composite object behaves as a validated VSG-BESS
  device in time-domain simulation.
- **Reuse decision**: retain the source-traceable energy-projection implementation and the V4
  four-device identity, while excluding the independent ESD1 action object,
  old storage results, direct M/D learning, old checkpoints, and old
  performance claims.
- **Programme consequence**: direct M/D training remains stopped.  The
  energy-port successor may proceed only to a minimal physical gate for
  setpoint resolution, sign, timing, zero-action equivalence, independent
  intervention, achieved-power response, and energy accounting.

## Limits

- This round contains source inspection, pure-contract tests, and a fake
  external-system adapter test only.  It contains no power flow, ANDES TDS,
  disturbance, trajectory, control response, solver result, or uncertainty
  estimate.
- The GENCLS seam is an electromechanical mechanical-torque input.  Converter
  inner loops, DC-link dynamics, switching, reactive-power control, thermal
  dynamics, protection, and hardware realization are not modeled here.
- The external SOC ledger is a prospective feasibility state.  Until the next
  physical gate reconciles commanded torque, `GENCLS.tm`, electrical power,
  losses, hold timing, and reset behavior on the real environment, neither
  physical energy feasibility nor actuator authority is established.
- No deterministic coordinator, differential-mode improvement, non-learning
  headroom, learned policy, communication ablation, stability, safety,
  robustness, held-out condition, topology generalization, or novelty result
  exists for this object.
- The fixed paper title remains a target.  This design pass establishes none
  of the title's decoupling, coordination, or MARL result terms.
