## Limits

- The evidence is a deterministic one-second intervention gate on one modified
  Kundur V4 operating point and topology without a disturbance.  It is not a
  population, uncertainty, robustness, contingency, or held-out evaluation.
- The selected GENCLS seam is an electromechanical mechanical-torque input with
  an external incremental energy ledger.  Converter inner loops, DC-link and
  switching dynamics, reactive-power control, thermal behavior, protection,
  and hardware realization remain unmodeled.
- The gate establishes finite interface behavior, not sustained actuator
  authority, common-frequency restoration, differential-mode suppression,
  controller efficacy, non-learning headroom, stability, or safety.
- No deterministic coordinator, independent learner, message ablation, MARL,
  decoupling improvement, coordination benefit, topology generalization,
  cross-simulator result, HIL/PHIL result, novelty, or publication readiness is
  supported.
