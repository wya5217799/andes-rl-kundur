## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — STOP-DEVELOPMENT-NO-CANDIDATE**: with the corner
  moved below the dominant mode, the low-corner law passes the differential
  energy threshold for the best candidate but fails the probe cross-response
  no-harm ceiling for all four candidates, so no candidate is eligible.
- **Mechanism boundary**: R376 (Laplacian sync amplifies cross-response),
  R377/R378 (alpha 0.60 corner on the 0.4 Hz mode), and R379 (alpha 0.90
  insufficient probe suppression) jointly show that a first-order
  frequency-selective damping channel cannot simultaneously pass the 0.4 Hz
  differential oscillation and reject the sustained action-domain probe
  within the frozen 0.95/1.10 joint thresholds on this plant.
- **Formulation decision**: the first-order damping law family stops. Per
  the Gate B-3 contract, this jointly supports the registered
  no-differential-benefit conclusion for the fixed topology/action domain;
  no filter-order sweep, gain change, retry, random arm, headroom gate, or
  MARL training is authorized.
