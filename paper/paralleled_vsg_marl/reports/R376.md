## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — STOP-DEVELOPMENT-NO-CANDIDATE**: the feasibility-
  native seam is physically sound — identity held on all 60 development
  trajectories with zero projection leakage and full action rank — but the
  four frozen distributed gain pairs add no cross-coordinate decoupling
  over the local controller at the frozen threshold, so no candidate is
  eligible and no held-out bank is executed.
- **Boundary**: the local feasibility-native controller does show physical
  authority over zero feedback on the registered endpoints. This is a
  descriptive development-bank observation only; it is not the registered
  held-out comparison and cannot support decoupling efficacy or learning
  headroom claims.
- **Formulation decision**: per the Gate B contract, the frozen
  deterministic comparison stops. Its registered rule authorizes no gain
  change, threshold adjustment, retry, random arm, or training. The route
  decision authority for any successor formulation remains with
  ROUTE.md#current-gate and the registered feasibility-native contract.
