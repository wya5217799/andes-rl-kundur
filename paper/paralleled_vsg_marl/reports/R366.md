## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — DESIGN-CONTRACT-PASS**: the fixed-title line now has a
  unit-valid, object-matched, permission-matched physical contract for the next
  deterministic efficacy and direct-action-headroom gate.  This is not an
  authorization for the still-blocked full learning comparison.
- **Reuse decision**: reuse the legacy four-VSG plant/decoder, local-agent
  routing, clipping/state/test patterns, and evaluation discipline.  Do not
  reuse old active-power laws, storage results, scalar/edge action objects,
  checkpoints, trajectories, or performance claims.
- **Learning boundary**: the earlier no-neural-residual failure is not assumed
  solved.  It is converted into a prospective kill rule: no neural training
  becomes eligible unless a non-learning oracle demonstrates incremental,
  time-varying direct-M/D targets beyond the selected deterministic method.
- **Question disposition**: Q-0102 may close positive only for the prospective
  comparison design.  Controller efficacy, decoupling improvement,
  coordination value, and MARL value remain untested.

## Limits

- R366 contains static contract verification and unit tests only.  It provides
  no ANDES trajectory, physical controller effect, uncertainty estimate,
  damping improvement, stability result, or learning result.
- The controller operates at the GENCLS parameter seam.  No converter inner
  loop, storage energy, state of charge, current, thermal, recovery, achieved
  hardware command, or deployment constraint is modeled.
- The nine-gain family is prospectively fixed but not yet tuned or selected.
  Calling it strong requires the next development comparison and no-harm gate;
  this round establishes eligibility and fairness, not benchmark quality.
- Learner capacity/parameter sharing, optimizer/hyperparameters, training
  interactions, tuning budget, training seeds/checkpoint selection, and the
  sealed evaluation bank/unit of analysis remain unfrozen.  The full
  direct-MARL comparison therefore remains blocked.
- The 5% and 10% thresholds are prospective decision thresholds, not observed
  improvements.  No superiority, probability, robustness, generalization,
  safety, or publication claim follows from them.
- Delay and dropout are held-out stress dimensions.  The first comparison
  condition remains zero delay and zero dropout on one topology.
