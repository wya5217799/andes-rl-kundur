## Frozen setup

- The object is the existing `AndesMultiVSGEnvV4` electromechanical proxy,
  used without modifying protected environment code.  Four GENCLS VSG units
  are ordered at buses `(12,16,14,15)` and receive four independent normalized
  two-coordinate actions through the public `reset`/`step` interface.
- The canonical LS1 load step is applied with the unrelated built-in line trip
  disabled.  Communication failure and delay are zero; all observation
  augmentations are off.  Every arm runs for 30 decisions at 0.2 seconds on a
  fresh environment with seed 42.
- The sealed eight arms are repeated homogeneous zero action (`zero_a/b`),
  four constant one-actor interventions `(delta-M,delta-D)=(+0.25,-0.10)`,
  one eight-step actor-channel basis fingerprint, and one zero-action
  heterogeneous-damping mismatch `(70,90,130,150)`.
- Repeated zero action defines numerical floors of `1e-7 Hz` and `1e-8 p.u.`.
  Rewards, checkpoints, policies, old result values, and the legacy six-axis
  composite are absent from every gate.
- The simulator's physical frequency base is 60 Hz; the inherited seven-field
  observation normalization retains the legacy 50-Hz constant.  Both are
  recorded explicitly and physical endpoints use 60 Hz.

## Limits

- The evidence covers one modified-Kundur topology, one localized load step,
  one heterogeneous-damping condition, one seed, deterministic repeatability,
  and a six-second horizon.  It is an existence/interface gate, not a
  population or robustness estimate.
- The mismatch arm combines damping heterogeneity and a localized disturbance;
  it does not uniquely identify damping heterogeneity as the cause of the
  differential transient.
- Single-agent arms intervene jointly on M and D.  They prove per-actor vector
  authority; the fingerprint proves per-coordinate readback, but this round
  does not estimate separate dynamic gains for M versus D.
- The VSG is a GENCLS electromechanical proxy with parameter modulation.  No
  converter inner loop, storage energy, state of charge, current, thermal,
  slew-rate, hardware, or achieved deployment constraint is represented.
- The 60-Hz plant versus 50-Hz observation-normalization contract must be
  prospectively reconciled or explicitly matched across every comparator
  before controller efficacy or learning evidence is admissible.
- No control improvement, damping improvement, common-frequency restoration,
  stability, safety, robustness, topology generalization, hardware, novelty,
  or publication-readiness claim follows.
