## Conclusions

- **Primary judgment — sealed-bank time-domain evidence**: the validated four
  VSG-owned active-power-reference ports have bounded, energy-consistent,
  directionally correct authority on the registered common and three
  differential physical modes over this finite three-condition bank.
- **Decoupling consequence — descriptive intervention evidence**: nonzero
  off-diagonal coordinate responses remain measurable, so coordinate
  relabelling alone is not evidence of dynamic decoupling.  The next eligible
  method must explicitly
  reduce cross-mode response and differential motion against a matched strong
  deterministic coordinator while preserving common-frequency and no-harm
  guards.
- **Training consequence**: this result authorizes only prospective
  deterministic decoupler design on the same four ports.  It does not establish
  controller efficacy, decoupling improvement, coordination value, or MARL
  increment, and it does not authorize training.

## Limits

- The evidence covers one modified-Kundur V4 topology, three deterministic
  conditions, one constant signed intervention magnitude, one seed, and an
  eight-second horizon; population uncertainty and held-out evaluation are
  untested.
- The frequency coordinates are arithmetic projections of the four VSG rotor
  speeds, not inertia-weighted COI frequency, bus-frequency estimators, or a
  small-signal modal certificate.
- The port is a governor-free GENCLS `pref/tm` electromechanical seam with an
  external incremental energy ledger; converter inner loops, DC-link,
  switching, reactive power, thermal behavior, protection, and hardware are
  unmodeled.
- The response matrix is a finite signed time-domain intervention result.  It
  does not prove stability, robust decoupling, topology generalization,
  communication value, distributed control efficacy, learning value, or
  deployability.
