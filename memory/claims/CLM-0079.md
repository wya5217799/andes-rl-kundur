statement: |
  R61 Track C — full 5-seed `final.pt` scan refines and partially
  **supersedes CLM-0074**'s optimistic Q-0007 extrapolation.

  ### Method

  Following R60's s50-only cheap probe (CLM-0074: final.pt 0.270 vs
  best.pt 0.109 = 2.5× lift), ran `score_run.py --suffix final` on
  the remaining 4 R57-α LSTM warmup seeds (s49/s51/s52/s53) to
  measure the lift across the full pool.

  ### Result

  | seed | best.pt (6-axis) | final.pt (6-axis) | diff |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | s49 | 0.333 | **0.333** | 0 (within noise) |
  | s50 | 0.109 | **0.270** | **+0.161** ⭐ |
  | s51 | 0.526 | 0.256 | **-0.270** ⚠️ |
  | s52 | 0.415 | 0.334 | -0.081 |
  | s53 | 0.437 | 0.374 | -0.063 |

  **Only s50 has final > best**. Other 4 seeds: training peaks
  mid-run and degrades to final ep.

  ### 5-seed mean under selection rules

  | rule | s49 | s50 | s51 | s52 | s53 | mean |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | all best.pt (current production, R57/CLM-0067) | 0.333 | 0.109 | 0.526 | 0.415 | 0.437 | **0.364** |
  | all final.pt | 0.333 | 0.270 | 0.256 | 0.334 | 0.374 | **0.313** ↓ |
  | **max(best, final) per seed** (Q-0007 oracle) | 0.333 | 0.270 | 0.526 | 0.415 | 0.437 | **0.396** |

  ### Refined Q-0007 payoff vs H1α 0.40

  - **Realistic Q-0007 full-impl payoff** = max-per-seed = 0.396.
    Q-0007's in-training `best_eval.pt` approximates this oracle by
    prospectively capturing each seed's paper-metric peak (instead
    of train-reward proxy).
  - **H1α threshold** = 0.40.
  - **Gap**: 0.004 below threshold — within statistical noise but
    technically NOT a cross.

  CLM-0074's "Q-0007 likely crosses H1α without retraining" is
  **technically supported by 0.004 margin** but with high uncertainty:
  - 5-seed std across seeds is large (s51 0.526, s50 0.109/0.270 range)
  - The s50 final→best swap could be reproducibility-dependent
  - Statistical 95 % CI on 5-seed mean likely spans ~0.05, encompassing
    both 0.36 and 0.41

  ### Mechanism — why s50 is the outlier

  R57 CLM-0065 mechanism: s50's `best.pt` is saved at ep 10 during
  the pre-training random env-warmup. The lstm-lr-warmup-eps 5
  intervention can't fix this — the callback fires before training
  starts. So s50's best.pt is essentially random weights.

  Other 4 seeds (s49/s51/s52/s53): their best.pt is saved during
  actual training (mid-run peak). final.pt is post-peak after
  some degradation — typical late-training instability for LSTM.
  Train reward and paper-metric **correlate during the converging
  phase** of training, so best-by-train-reward incidentally captures
  near-peak paper-metric performance.

  This means **Q-0007's value depends on the training regime**:
  - **High value** when training has large reward-shaping terms that
    create big pre-training spikes (CLM-0073 sanity, CLM-0075 anti-
    smoothness). best.pt locks pre-training → only Q-0007 escapes.
  - **Moderate value** when training has typical reward magnitude
    (R56/R57 default). best.pt is at mid-training peak but final.pt
    is post-peak — Q-0007 captures peak more precisely.
  - **Marginal value** when training converges monotonically and
    holds — best.pt ≈ final.pt ≈ peak.

  ### Implication for next round (R62+)

  To actually cross H1α 0.40 5-seed mean:
  - **Q-0007 alone insufficient** (0.396 max-per-seed).
  - Need to **stack levers**: Q-0007 + (longer training OR larger
    HAWE pool OR new structural element like LSTM+SAC+auto-α).
  - R60 SS-tier list item 2 (LSTM+SAC+auto-α) is the next cheapest
    structural experiment.

  ### CLM-0074 supersession note

  CLM-0074's statement is refined by this claim:
  - 2.5× s50 lift: **stands** (s50-specific observation, real)
  - "5-seed mean → 0.396 with s50 swap": **stands** (now confirmed
    by full scan; max-per-seed is 0.396)
  - "Likely crosses H1α": **downgraded** to "within noise of crossing
    H1α; 0.004 margin against threshold". CLM-0079 supersedes the
    confidence interpretation.
