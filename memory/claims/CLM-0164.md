  **Q-0015 silent-ignore hypothesis REJECTED by micro-probe**, and the
  hypothesis-induced blocker on γ ablation ([[CLM-0163]] R85 PRIORITY 1)
  is released. R81 W9 "4-decimal-identical eval (gamma=0.95 vs gamma=0.99)"
  was misread as evidence of silent-ignore; it is in fact evidence of
  **basin-attractor γ-invariance** within the [0.95, 0.99] range under
  R72_w4 hyper × seed 54 × 75 ep training.

  **Probe** (`probes/q0015_gamma_silent_ignore_probe.py`, zero ANDES,
  pure-pytorch, <5s wall):

  Construct two TD3LSTMAgents with identical RNG seed, identical
  synthetic replay buffer fill (8 episodes × 30 steps × seed=1984), then
  call `agent.update()` once each. Compare stored gamma, critic-loss,
  and weight delta after the update step.

  | Probe metric | gamma=0.95 | gamma=0.99 | |Δ| |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `self.gamma` | 0.95 | 0.99 | differs ✓ |
  | `update_info['critic_loss']` | 2.1815 | 2.1853 | 0.0038 (~0.2%) |
  | `critic_weight_delta L2` | 0.0587748... | 0.0587753... | **5×10⁻⁷ non-zero** |
  | `actor_weight_delta L2` | 0.0 | 0.0 | 0 (policy delay) |

  Gamma is stored AND used in the TD target (`y = r + γ(1-d)·q_tgt`,
  `td3_lstm.py:377`). The critic update differs between gamma values by
  ~0.2% per step. Over 75 episodes × ~100 updates/episode, this should
  accumulate to substantial policy divergence — UNLESS the basin
  attractor is strong enough to draw both gamma settings to the same
  local optimum. R81 W9's identical final eval points to the latter:
  R72_w4 basin is γ-stable in [0.95, 0.99].

  **Implication for [[CLM-0163]] R85 PRIORITY 1 (γ ablation)**:
  proceed, but **widen the γ grid** to include values that should
  perturb the basin meaningfully:
  - γ ∈ {0.5, 0.8, 0.95, 0.99 baseline, 1.0}
  - γ=0.5 collapses to near-myopic (R²=0.93 for instant reward in
    [[CLM-0163]] → policy may be different)
  - γ=1.0 = undiscounted (R²=0.71 for return → policy may converge
    differently)
  - γ ∈ {0.95, 0.99} (basin) is the no-op check from R81 W9

  R93+ γ ablation can use unmodified `--gamma` flag; no infrastructure
  fix needed.

  **No history invalidation**: prior γ-flag-related sweeps (R67–R79
  TD3 / SAC γ sweeps if any) used the same correctly-wired CLI flag.
