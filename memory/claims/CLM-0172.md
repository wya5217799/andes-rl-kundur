statement: |
  R89-W1 audit: ANDES default `kundur_full.xlsx` has only **2 PQ
  loads**, located at Bus 7 (p0=11.59 pu, q0=-0.735 pu) and Bus 8
  (p0=15.75 pu, q0=-0.899 pu), both at 230 kV transmission level.
  Paper Sec.IV-A places 4 ESS at Bus 12/16/14/15 *with co-located
  loads*, and LS1/LS2 disturbances are at Bus 14/15. V4 env
  `_build_system()` (line 290) adds NEW `PQ_Bus14`/`PQ_Bus15` for the
  disturbance ramps, but the original Bus 7+8 transmission loads remain
  active throughout. The actual loaded steady-state system in V4 is:

  | Bus | Load (pu, 100 MVA base) | Source |
  |---|---|---|
  | 7   | +11.59 / -0.735 | ANDES default (transmission) |
  | 8   | +15.75 / -0.899 | ANDES default (transmission) |
  | 14  | LS1 delta only | V4 runtime add |
  | 15  | LS2 delta only | V4 runtime add |

  Two material differences from paper:
  1. **Steady-state load topology**: paper says loads are at the 4 ESS
     distribution buses (12/14/15/16); ANDES default puts them at
     transmission buses (7/8). Disturbance dynamics at the ESS busses
     differ from a system with loads concentrated at transmission level.
  2. **Capacitive q0**: both default PQ loads have NEGATIVE q0
     (-0.735, -0.899 pu, total ~-1.6 pu = 160 MVAr capacitive).
     "Loads" with capacitive q is unusual — likely embeds shunt cap
     compensation in the PQ record. Affects bus voltage profile + tie
     line damping.

  Per `paper_constants.SCENARIOS`, LS1 = -2.48 pu at Bus 14
  (248 MW reduction), LS2 = +1.88 pu at Bus 15 (188 MW increase).
  Relative to total steady-state load of ~27 pu (Bus 7+8 sum), LS1
  is a 9% perturbation. Paper Sec.IV-A doesn't list the steady-state
  load magnitudes, so paper's relative perturbation could differ.
