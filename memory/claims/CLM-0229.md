statement: |
  **R126-W1 (r72_w4 vs r72_w5 obs-responsiveness vs geo, zero ANDES)**.
  R117 (CLM-0217) found r72_w5_lstm_s55 has the highest obs-only ascent
  ceiling (51.9% of saturation) among 9 LSTM ckpts, while R72_w4 SOTA
  is mid-pack (41.4%). R125 figure noted this is the "most obs-responsive
  LSTM in the set" but didn't link it to actual policy quality.

  R126 looks up cached 11-axis geo from `results/research_loop/
  eval_v4_baseline/<ckpt>_summary.json`:

  | Ckpt | obs-only max | h-warm med | 11-axis geo | r72 hyper diff |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | r72_w4_lstm_tau001_warmup5_s54 | 41.4% | ~99.6% | **0.391** (SOTA) | tau=0.001, warmup=5, seed=54 |
  | r72_w5_lstm_tau001_warmup5_s55 | **51.9%** | ~99.7% | **0.317** | tau=0.001, warmup=5, seed=55 |

  Same hyper (tau=0.001, warmup=5, h=64, paper-faithful obs) — only seed
  differs. The **more obs-responsive LSTM has WORSE geo by 0.074 (-19%
  relative)**.

  Reading: in this codebase + paper-faithful obs regime, **LSTM obs-
  responsiveness at h=0 is NEGATIVELY correlated with policy quality**.
  The SOTA learned to defer action to accumulated h (rely on history
  integration); the worse-geo ckpt let obs drive output more directly
  and got a less robust policy.

  This is **consistent with R88 CLM-0161 (on-manifold critic concave
  in steady-state, confused in transient)**: the policy needs **h
  to encode trajectory context**, not just to mediate temporal
  lag. r72_w5 has the right architecture but its training found a less
  history-integrative policy.

  **Implication for R96 (Q-0022) design**: warm-h_0 = MLP(obs_0) should
  output an h_0 that mimics the SOTA's accumulated-h regime (history-
  encoded), NOT just a "saturation-trigger" that bypasses h integration.
  The learnable MLP head should be **regularised** toward
  history-resembling activations, or trained with a curriculum that
  exposes it to h trajectories from successful rollouts.

  Caveat: N=2 (only r72_w4 vs r72_w5 have directly comparable hyper).
  The "obs-responsive = worse" claim is suggestive, not confirmed.
  Would need 3-4 more same-hyper seeds to establish significance.
  N=2 + same-hyper-different-seed is the cleanest available control.

  R96 design implications (revised):
  - Original (R107/R109): MLP h_init random-initialised, learn via
    policy gradient
  - **Revised (post-R126)**: MLP h_init initialised from observed h
    trajectories of R72_w4 SOTA. Concretely:
    (a) During warmup phase, freeze h_init MLP and use the fixed SOTA
        h_average as h_0 — bootstrap before allowing the MLP to adapt
    (b) Add a small loss term ||h_init(obs_0) - h_avg_sota_step_0||²
        for first 25 episodes, then drop it.
    This bootstraps from history-integrative h to avoid r72_w5-style
    suboptimal attractors.
