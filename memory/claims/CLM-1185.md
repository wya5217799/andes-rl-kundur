statement: |
  On the registered 60-record development bank of the paralleled four-VSG energy-port object, the frozen first-order high-pass damping family shows a monotone trade-off across the frozen alpha grid {0.675, 0.625, 0.65, 0.70, 0.725, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85}: the best disturbance differential ratio improves from 0.9498 to 0.9220 (passing the 0.95 threshold at alpha >= 0.75) while the probe-cross ratio moves from 1.3708 to 1.3136, never approaching the 1.10 ceiling. No grid point passes both frozen thresholds, so the sweep classifies SWEEP-NO-CANDIDATE and the first-order family closes within the frozen grid; the external linear-interpolation prediction of a feasible point near alpha 0.675 is refuted.
