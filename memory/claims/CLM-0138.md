  ### Why this matters

  Two readings are consistent with the data:

  1. **v3.1 dampens the perturbation**. CLM-0104's 6-axis -19%
     drift may be a ranker artifact: multiplicative-gating (v3.1)
     pulls outlier axes back, while 6-axis geo-mean does not.
     Under this reading the drift is "real but only visible under
     6-axis", which makes v3.1 the more publication-stable ranker.
  2. **The offending commit is pre-R58**. CLM-0104's reference
     baseline was R57-α (commit 081e754), and the bisection range
     starts at R58 (one commit later). If the drift was introduced
     between R49..R57 (env refactor era) the present bisection
     misses it. R57-α historical ckpts still on disk
     (`results/td3_lstm_h64_warmup5_s51/`) score 0.526 6-axis but
     have not been re-scored under v3.1 in this round.

  Either reading is consistent with the paper's headline numbers
  being stable; we cannot distinguish them from R58..R65 evidence
  alone.

  ### Why R61 was the leading suspect (now de-prioritized)

  CLM-0104 named R61 (`1a3a4ad`, Q-0007 + monitor extension +
  atexit handler) as leading suspect. E3 measures R60→R61 under
  v3.1 at $\Delta v_{3.1} < 10^{-4}$: the atexit handler does NOT
  perturb training-time RNG to a degree that the v3.1 ranker sees.
  This does not exonerate R61 under 6-axis, but removes it as a
  v3.1 culprit.

  ### Implications for paper

  §VII-C "Code-drift caveat" is rewritten to fold in E3: the
  bisection is now complete on R58..R65 under v3.1, the
  "remains open" closer is replaced with the dual-reading above
  (ranker artifact vs pre-R58 commit). Section~\ref{sec:phase3}
  Table~\ref{tab:phase3} gains an E3 row group + an "E3" paragraph.
