  ## Mechanism-explained narrative (paper Section IV-D draft)

  The 91-round plateau is best described by the following set of
  intertwined facts, not a single mechanism:

  1. The R72_w4 LSTM SOTA learns a bang-bang policy (R92) that ramps
     to action saturation by step ~15.
  2. The actor's policy is essentially memoryless w.r.t. action
     prediction from obs (R96 / R132). The LSTM contributes
     representational power mostly to the critic h_critic side
     (CLM-0160) and to step-0 architectural slack (CLM-0188).
  3. The critic on-manifold endorses the actor's choice (advantage +120% \|Q\|).
  4. The 6-axis geo metric specifically rewards the ramp-up trajectory
     shape (dH/dD utilization range), penalising any policy that
     skips the ramp (warm-h_0 saturate-from-start). The paper §IV-C
     cum_rf rewards frequency-integral suppression and is friendlier
     to warm-h_0.
  5. The plateau at geo ≈ 0.39 represents the best ramp-then-saturate
     basin reachable by single-seed 75-ep R72_w4-class hyper. R74/R75
     basins (geo 0.41-0.43) are accessible but not the project's
     declared SOTA by historical convention. r75_baseline (0.430) is
     the empirical 6-axis ceiling at this scale.
  6. cum_rf-only optimisation gives degenerate policies (warm-h_0
     α=1, r67_w2a) that floor on geo. The honest cum_rf Pareto-SOTA
     is α=0.05 warm-h_0 at -0.0501.
  7. Magnitude overfit (R106) shows the policy is distributionally
     trained on random magnitudes but eval-bus-specific overfit at
     scale=1.0.
