statement: |
  **Retracts the headline finding of [[CLM-0163]]**: "obs → return at
  paper γ=0.99 R² = −0.41 (worse than predicting mean)" was an MLP
  regression-noise artifact, not a real value-horizon-mismatch signal.
  Cross-ckpt replication (R96-W1) on the **same** R72_w4 ckpt with the
  same script logic and same regressor architecture but different torch
  RNG init gave R² = **+0.659** (not −0.410) for the γ=0.99 case. Two
  runs, two ANDES TDS rollouts (bit-identical, deterministic env+seed),
  identical (obs, action, reward) trajectory, *very different* fitted
  R² — diagnostic of MLP instability, not value-horizon mismatch.

  **Cross-ckpt R² at γ=0.99 (R96-W1, 4 ckpts × LS1+LS2 × 50 step × 4
  agent, single-seed MLP fit each)**:

  | Ckpt | Algorithm | R² γ=0 | R² γ=0.9 | R² γ=0.99 | R² γ=1.0 | R² obs→a |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | r72_w4_lstm_warmup5_s54  | TD3+LSTM   | +0.887 | +0.418 | **+0.659** | +0.299 | +0.897 |
  | r75_w2_lstm_warmup20_s59 | TD3+LSTM   | +0.904 | +0.340 | +0.625 | +0.539 | +0.952 |
  | r63_w4_td3_combo_s49     | TD3 MLP    | +0.980 | +0.141 | +0.386 | +0.611 | +0.988 |
  | td3_norm_h64_s49         | TD3 MLP    | +0.973 | +0.416 | +0.757 | +0.625 | +0.992 |
  | **median**               |            | +0.939 | +0.378 | **+0.642** | +0.575 | +0.970 |

  At γ=0.99 (paper discount) **all four ckpts give R² ∈ [+0.39, +0.76]**,
  median +0.64 — moderate but POSITIVE. obs IS predictive of paper-γ
  discounted return on the real SOTA trajectory. The "obs cannot predict
  discounted return" framing in [[CLM-0163]] is **wrong**.

  **What CLM-0163 had right** (preserved by this correction):
  - **R2 obs → SOTA action**: cross-ckpt median R² = **+0.970**, range
    [+0.897, +0.992]. The SOTA policy IS essentially memoryless across
    all 4 ckpts. ΔR3-R2 finding (LSTM h_actor +1.8%) was R72_w4-specific
    in R91; not re-tested in R96 but the +0.970 median action-from-obs
    R² independent of recurrence supports the broader "policy is
    near-myopic in obs" finding.
  - **R4 obs → next_obs**: not re-tested in R96, but R91 R²=0.86 is
    independent of the γ-return regression instability.

  **What CLM-0163 had wrong**:
  - The R²=−0.41 at γ=0.99 was a one-bad-MLP-init artifact (R91-W1
    agent3 fit hit −1.713, pulling the cross-agent median negative).
    Different MLP init (R96-W1) gives same-ckpt R²=+0.659. The fit is
    **unstable at N_train=80 with 64-hidden 2-layer MLP** (gross
    overparameterisation, ~10k params for 80 samples).
  - The "plateau is value-horizon mismatch at γ=0.99" mechanism story
    is **withdrawn**. obs has slightly less predictive power at
    γ=0.9-0.99 than at γ=0 or γ=1.0 (median R² 0.38-0.64 vs 0.94),
    but the effect is moderate, not catastrophic.

  **What this leaves of the R57-R82 plateau mechanism question**:
  Still open. Following the [[CLM-0160]] on-manifold-critic-confident
  result + [[CLM-0168]] obs-is-predictive-of-return result, neither
  critic representation nor obs information appears to be the bottleneck.
  Candidates remaining:
  - **Env stochasticity / disturbance variance** (R84-D4, not yet run)
  - **Reward shape** — PHI_ABS=50 dominates per-step; ablate to paper_strict_pure
  - **Policy class limit** — actor MLP capacity / non-convex optimization landscape
  - **Long-horizon credit assignment** under fixed γ=0.99 with 50-step
    episodes (Q-0008, paper 500 ep convergence — partially closed CLM-0146)

  **Methodology lesson for future R{N+1}+ D3-style regressors**:
  - N_train=80 with 64×2 hidden MLP is severely overparameterised; use
    ridge regression or much smaller MLP, OR multi-seed ensemble + report
    R² mean ± std for the agent-level fit, OR collect ≥ 10× more samples
    (multiple IC seeds, longer trajectories).
  - Torch RNG must be seeded (`torch.manual_seed`) before MLP init for
    reproducibility — neither R91 nor R96 seeded torch's RNG.
  - Cross-method validation (linear regression vs MLP) would have caught
    the instability before publication-worthy claims.

  Recommended R97+ priority revision:
  1. **R84-D4 env stochasticity floor** (PROMOTED) — single SOTA × multi
     disturbance / IC seed × deterministic eval, quantify σ_geo / mean_geo.
  2. **Reward shape ablation** — R72_w4 hyper × paper_strict_pure × 75 ep s54.
  3. **γ ablation R93** — STILL motivated by [[CLM-0164]] (γ is correctly
     applied), but no longer load-bearing on a "value-horizon mismatch"
     hypothesis. Lower priority now.
  4. **Multi-seed MLP D3 redo** — to confirm CLM-0168's positive-R² result
     under proper variance reporting before publication.
