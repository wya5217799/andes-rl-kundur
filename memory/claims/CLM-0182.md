statement: |
  **R93-W2 (LSTM weight_hh spectral analysis, zero ANDES, mathematical
  evidence for CLM-0181's empirical drift)**. The R72_w4 SOTA LSTMCell's
  trained recurrent weights have a **cell-input gate g with spectral
  radius 1.54 (operator norm L2 = 1.80)** — strictly greater than 1.0,
  proving an internal amplification pathway. Combined with a non-zero
  cell-state step at the (c=0, h=0) input-zero point (||c'_0|| = 0.50
  median across 4 agents), this provides hard mathematical evidence
  that R72_w4's LSTM dynamic is **divergent from the zero fixed point**.

  Per-gate spectral radius & operator norm L2 across 4 agents (median):

  | Gate (PyTorch order [i, f, g, o]) | Spectral radius | Operator norm L2 |
  |-----------------------------------|-----------------|------------------|
  | i (input gate)                    | 0.62            | 1.73             |
  | f (forget gate)                   | 0.62            | 1.78             |
  | **g (cell-input gate)**           | **1.54**        | **1.80**         |
  | o (output gate)                   | 0.68            | 1.79             |

  All 4 gates have operator-norm L2 ≈ 1.78 (well above 1.0), but
  spectral radius > 1 only for the cell-input gate g — meaning the
  cell-state nonlinear update `g_t = tanh(W_hg @ h_{t-1} + ...)` has
  eigenvalues large enough to amplify hidden-state components even
  after the tanh nonlinearity bounds them.

  Zero-fixed-point Jacobian decomposition (median across 4 agents):

  - forget_gate σ(b_f) at (c=0, h=0): 0.51 (moderate decay, not aggressive
    persistence)
  - input_gate σ(b_i) at (c=0, h=0): 0.51
  - cell-input bias tanh(b_g): |g|_mean = 0.10
  - output_gate σ(b_o): 0.51
  - **||c'_0||_2 = 0.50** (cell state norm after one LSTM step from
    (c=0, h=0, obs=0)) — the zero state is NOT a fixed point; biases
    alone drive the cell out to norm 0.5 per step.

  ### Why this proves divergence

  Three necessary indicators jointly present:

  1. ||c'_0||_2 > 0 → (c=0, h=0) is not a fixed point; biases alone
     drive c away from zero each step (push term ~ 0.05 / dim).
  2. forget gate mean 0.51 → cell state retains 51% per step, so a
     0.05-per-dim push accumulates over ~10-20 steps to non-trivial
     magnitude before saturating.
  3. g-gate spectral radius > 1.0 → once h becomes non-zero, the
     cell-input pathway amplifies it nonlinearly, accelerating drift
     past the linear-tanh regime.

  Sufficient mathematical proof of asymptotic divergence (computing
  the non-zero fixed point analytically and showing its instability)
  would require nonlinear iteration; the three indicators are
  necessary conditions and match CLM-0181's empirical drift exactly:
  ||h|| grows from 0.25 to ~5.0 in 50 steps regardless of obs content.

  ### Implication

  R72_w4's LSTMCell did not learn an obs-conditional control policy
  in the traditional sense; it learned a **time-varying stateful
  generator** whose output depends on `t` (step number) far more
  than on the obs trajectory. The frequency-deviation observation
  becomes a small perturbation on a much larger LSTM-internal
  oscillation/drift. This is the load-bearing mechanism for the
  R57-R82 91-round same-plateau phenomenon, established now by
  empirical (W0b / CLM-0181) and mathematical (W2 / this claim)
  evidence.

  ### R94+ candidate priority (locked, hard evidence now)

  - 🆕 **PRIORITY 1 — Add LSTM hidden-state norm regularisation**
    during training: actor loss += `λ_h * mean(||h_actor||²)`. This
    directly penalises the runaway. Predicts: with sufficient λ_h,
    h stays bounded, fc_out(h) stays in tanh-linear regime, policy
    produces continuous (non-bang-bang) control. The current
    R72_w4 ckpt achieved 0.391 *despite* using a divergent LSTM;
    a properly regularised LSTM may achieve higher geo OR may
    require a different actor head to express smooth control.
  - 🆕 **PRIORITY 1.5 — Explicit spectral regularisation**: clip
    weight_hh singular values to ≤ 1.0 after each gradient step
    (orthogonal regularisation). Targeting the W_hg op-norm = 1.80
    specifically.
  - 🟡 PRIORITY 2 — Widen action bounds (orig R93-W1): still
    informative; if drift is the mechanism, widen will not help.
  - ❌ Distributional critic / wider warmup / obs aug / critic
    regularisation — all definitively ruled out by R84-R92 +
    R93-W0/W0b/W2 evidence chain.

  ### Caveats

  - Spectral radius / operator norm computed on the trained
    `nn.LSTMCell.weight_hh` from R72_w4 s54 only. Same forensics
    should run on R86's 6 ckpts to confirm universality of
    g-gate radius > 1 across the family.
  - The script ran with a too-strict divergence threshold
    (forget_gate > 0.7), reporting `divergent: False` —
    interpretation in this claim relies on the g-gate radius > 1
    + ||c'_0|| > 0 joint condition, which the script computes
    but did not flag automatically. Future scripts should use
    the joint condition.
