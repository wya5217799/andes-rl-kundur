  - **dM_span_mean = 71.8 %** of paper (CLM-0057..0062 baseline 9-21 %;
    CLM-0058 R50 0.4 %; CLM-0062 R55 0.5 %)
  - dD_span_mean = 35.6 %

  s50 collapsed via a critic-loss explosion in the first 10 ep
  (peak 8.155 vs 0.587/0.620 for s49/s51 — 15× higher); the saved
  best.pt is from ep 10 pre-explosion, hence dM_span 3.1 % and
  6-axis 0.109. This is a recurrent-RL seed-stability issue
  (Q-0005), NOT a hijack-channel artifact — the gradient was
  flowing into the LSTM weights properly (verified by
  `test_update_gradients_reach_actor_lstm_weights`).

  **Mechanism**: LSTM bypasses both R49-R55 pathologies simultaneously:
  (a) `π(obs_t, h_t)` outputs differ across t even at constant obs_t
  because h_t encodes trajectory phase, so deterministic eval is
  structurally time-varying (verified by integration test
  `test_lstm_eval_path_loads_and_produces_time_varying_actions`);
  (b) the reward-landscape flatness that collapsed memoryless-policy
  gradients is broken because the actor output now depends on h_t,
  not just obs_t — the gradient signal flows through the LSTM
  weights, decoupling policy variation from exploration noise.

  **Impact on prior claims**: the R49-R55 hexagon "0.334/0.365/0.351
  ceiling is rock-solid" framing (implicit in CLM-0055/0056 and
  explicit in CLM-0062's "completes the six-failure hexagon") is
  refuted. The ceiling is **policy-class-bound, not environment-
  bound**. The numerical claims of CLM-0055/0056/0057..0062
  themselves remain valid — they characterise the *memoryless* class
  ceiling, which is what they measured.
