statement: |
  R58 Phase 1 training-stability result for `paper_strict_pure` config
  (PHI_ABS=0, PHI_H=PHI_D=1.0, r^f in **Hz**) at 75 ep × 3 algos × 3
  seeds (49/50/51):

  | Algo | s49 critic loss | s50 | s51 | Outcome |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | SAC | 3→6 | 5→5 | 6→9 | **diverge 3/3** |
  | TD3 | 5→7 | 5→7 | 6→7 | **diverge 3/3** |
  | TD3+LSTM | 0.19→0.10 | 0.16→0.06 | 0.31→0.11 | **converge 3/3** |

  **Mechanism**: under Hz r^f the ratio r_h / r_f ≈ 3600 : 1 (per R18
  verdict mechanism, retained here). Memoryless policies (SAC, TD3)
  cannot learn to balance the two reward components — critic loss
  monotonically increases. LSTM's recurrent state structurally
  prevents the noise-hijack failure mode by carrying gradient signal
  across time steps regardless of the per-step reward ratio.

  **Implication**: at 75 ep under Hz frequency units, only TD3+LSTM
  is a viable algorithm for paper-faithful PHI=1.0. This validates
  R18's "PHI=1 diverges" mechanism *for memoryless policies under
  Hz units* — but see CLM-0070, which shows the conclusion does not
  generalize to rad/s units. R18 mechanism is **unit-conditioned**,
  not absolute.

  **Eval consequence** (CLM-0071): paper_strict_pure ckpts are weak
  paper-metric performers across the board (SAC -0.685, TD3 -0.917,
  TD3+LSTM -0.675). The SAC/TD3 ckpts have untrained `best.pt`
  (saved pre-training during random env-warmup, R57 mechanism); the
  TD3+LSTM ckpt is real but still suboptimal vs rescaled (-0.574) or
  V4 historical (-0.527).
