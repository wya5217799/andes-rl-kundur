  R60 Track C — Q-0009 closure via no-control paper-metric scale check.

  ### No-control baseline on paper §IV-C protocol

  Ran `zero_action_fn` against the R58 paper_strict_eval 20-scen test
  set under `paper_strict_pure_radsec` config (dt=0.2s, STEPS=50,
  t=10s, LS1/LS2 anchor bus 14/15 — all paper-aligned):

  | scenario | our no-control | paper no-control | ratio (we tighter) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | LS1 anchor | **-0.118** | **-1.61** | **13.6×** |
  | LS2 anchor | **-0.097** | **-0.80** | **8.2×** |
  | 50-scen extrapolation | -1.76 | -15.2 | 8.6× |

  **Conclusion**: the 13× tighter cum_rf magnitude we report vs
  paper is **an env-scale artifact**, not control-quality evidence.

  ### Mechanism — why absolute scale differs

  1. **Action space 1/20× smaller**: paper ΔH ∈ [-100, +300], ΔD ∈
     [-200, +600]; our V4 ΔH ∈ [-5, +15] (1/20×), ΔD bounds also
     scaled down. Smaller action range ⇒ smaller physical change ⇒
     smaller frequency excursion.
  2. **G4 GENROU + ZERO_G4_INERTIA placeholder** vs paper's REGCA1
     wind farm model: different total system inertia + structural
     differences in disturbance propagation.
  3. **N_SUBSTEPS=5 numerical integration** + ANDES vs paper's
     self-built VSG simulator: differing dt_sub precision and
     simulator implementation.

  These differences scale **all** cum_rf magnitudes (no-control AND
  controlled) by ~10× simultaneously. The ratio of (controlled /
  no-control) — the **relative improvement rate** — is what
  remains apples-to-apples comparable.

  ### Apples-to-apples comparison via relative improvement

  `improvement_rate = (no_control - ctrl) / no_control`

  | metric | paper DDIC | our SAC s50 (paper-strict-radsec, 75 ep) |
  |---|---|---|
  | LS1 improvement | (1.61-0.68)/1.61 = **58 %** | (0.118-0.054)/0.118 = **54 %** |
  | LS2 improvement | (0.80-0.52)/0.80 = **35 %** | (0.097-0.042)/0.097 = **57 %** |
  | **LS1+LS2 mean** | **46.5 %** | **55.5 %** |

  - LS1: paper +4 % stronger (large negative load disturbance)
  - LS2: us +22 % stronger (positive load disturbance)
  - **Average: we outperform paper DDIC by +9 %**
  - Paper trained 2000 ep, we trained 75 ep — paper presumably
    converged, we are not.

  50-scen extrapolation (less rigorous): paper 47 % vs us 44 %.
  Roughly tied.

  ### Implications

  **For paper framing** (CRITICAL — incorrect framing will be
  desk-rejected):
  - **DO NOT WRITE**: "We outperform paper DDIC by 12×"
    → absolute scale comparison is invalid.
  - **DO WRITE**: "Under paper §IV-C evaluation protocol (dt/M/
    episode/anchor aligned), our SAC + paper-strict-radsec at 75 ep
    achieves LS1+LS2 mean improvement rate of 55.5 %, comparable
    to paper DDIC's 46.5 %. Absolute cum_rf magnitudes differ
    by ~10× due to action-space and system-model scale
    differences detailed in §X."

  **For Q-0009 closure**: the original question "why 13× tighter"
  is answered. Three identified mechanisms are necessary and likely
