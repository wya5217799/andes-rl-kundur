  ### Mechanism — why cross-seed regresses despite individual strength

  R165 single = 0.381 (comparable to R143's 0.384), but in ensemble
  R165 contributes net negative. Probable mechanism: R142/R143
  (both s54 QR) converge to **closely related** bang-bang policies on
  the same training trajectory; their action averaging captures
  fine-grained per-step variation that smooths R72_w4's bang-bang
  spikes. R165 (s51 QR) converges to a **different** bang-bang
  pattern (different transient dynamics from different seed init), so
  its actions are not well-aligned with the s54 ensemble — averaging
  creates LS1 misalignment artifacts.

  This is structurally why **cross-seed ensembles don't help** here:
  the policies are independently bang-bang at convergence, not
  diversely interpolating policies. They commit to different sign
  patterns at the same trajectory step → average cancels out vs
  reinforces.
