  ### vs paper DDIC robust

  R67 -0.119 ≈ 85-86% improvement rate (vs no-control baseline) vs paper
  DDIC 46.5% = **~+39pp robust** (was +37.5pp at R64).

  ### Mechanism (hypothesis)

  - paper Table I: tau=0.005 is "reasonable SAC default"
  - ANDES short-horizon dynamics: target policy moves too fast at tau=0.005
    → critic target oscillates → policy overshoots
  - tau=0.001 (5× slower target update) smooths critic targets → policy
    converges more stably to U-bottom in cum_rf
  - tau=0.0005 too slow → critic learns stale rewards → undershoot
  - tau=0.01 too fast → target oscillation amplified → degradation

  Paper default 0.005 is "reasonable" but suboptimal for ANDES short-ep regime.
