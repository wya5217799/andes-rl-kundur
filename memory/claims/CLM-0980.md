statement: |
  R366 freezes the prospective unit, actuator, and deterministic-development
  contract for the fixed-title line. Every method will consume four
  seven-field per-VSG rows after an explicit 50-to-60-Hz frequency/RoCoF
  conversion and will act through four independent normalized M/D parameter
  vectors with the same bounds, rowwise clip/slew path, 0.2-s update, decoder,
  and model-parameter clamps. The only eligible deterministic family is the
  fixed nine-candidate local-neighbour adaptive M/D grid; legacy
  storage-power, common-scalar, edge-action, and centralized-input controller
  objects remain excluded.

  The static design gate passes only for the next non-learning deterministic
  efficacy and direct-action-headroom study. The full direct-MARL comparison
  remains blocked until learner capacity/parameter sharing,
  optimizer/hyperparameters, interaction and tuning budgets, training
  seeds/checkpoint selection, and the sealed evaluation bank/unit of analysis
  are frozen. Training also requires the deterministic no-harm gate and a
  bounded oracle showing additional headroom with nonconstant M/D targets.
  R366 runs no ANDES trajectory or training and supports no controller,
  decoupling, coordination, MARL, stability, safety, storage, hardware, or
  generalization result.
