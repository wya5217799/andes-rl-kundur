  ⚠️ **R192 (td3_lstm scalar, seed=54, seed-offset=100) = geo 0.2844,
  LS1=0.300, LS2=0.270, cum_rf=-0.0742** — SOTA seed s54 is ALSO
  offset-dependent. **-27% vs R72_w4 baseline 0.391 (s54, offset=0)**.

  This refutes the implicit assumption that s54 is a "stable basin"
  and exposes R72_w4 0.391 and R174 0.4139 as **single-(seed, offset)**
  results, not robust across env-RNG configuration space.

  ### Full s54+offset picture

  | Run | algo | offset | LS1 | LS2 | geo | Δ vs 0.391 |
  |-----|------|--------|-----|-----|-----|-------------|
  | R72_w4 | scalar | 0 | 0.354 | 0.431 | **0.391** | (ref) |
  | R174 | hreg λ=0.002 | 0 | 0.367 | 0.467 | 0.4139 | +5.9% |
  | **R192** | **scalar** | **100** | **0.300** | **0.270** | **0.2844** | **-27%** |

  ### s54 vs s49 at offset=100

  | seed | scalar @ offset=100 | hreg @ offset=100 |
  |------|---------------------|--------------------|
  | s49 | (not tested) | 0.2032 (R188) |
  | s49 | 0.2276 (R190) | (not tested) |
  | s54 | **0.2844** (R192) | 0.3875 (R193) |

  At offset=100, **all seeds converge to mid-range (0.20-0.28
  scalar)**. The (seed, offset) pair (54, 0) is uniquely favourable.

  ### Paper Sec.IV-D implication

  R72_w4 0.391 baseline and R174 0.4139 SOTA are both single-
  (seed=54, offset=0) results. Cross-offset mean at s54 for scalar
  would be ~0.34. Paper must disclose:

  > "We report results at (seed=54, seed-offset=0). Cross-offset mean
  > at seed=54 for scalar td3_lstm = (0.391 + 0.2844) / 2 = 0.338."

  Mechanism narrative upgrade: not just "s49 has bad RNG path", but
  **"(s54, offset=0) has a particularly good RNG path"**. The whole
  training landscape is highly RNG-path-dependent.
