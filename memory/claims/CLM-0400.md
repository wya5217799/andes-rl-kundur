  ### Implications for paper Sec.IV-D contribution 5 (qualified)

  The previous "algorithm-universal" claim (CLM-0385/0390/0395) is
  **partially refuted**: paper Eq.14 terms appear to provide
  **measurable benefit for the scalar algorithm at seed 51** (~15%
  improvement on LS2). They remain **inert for hreg across both
  seeds** (-0.6%, -0.15%) and **for scalar at seed 54** (+1.1%).

  The mechanism story (gauge invariance) still holds — that's about
  the FAILURE mode (paper-strict collapse), which is universal
  across all four cells (R218 hreg s54 = 0.010, R240 scalar s54 =
  0.010, both at bit-identical attractor mean_df=+0.0587). What is
  algorithm-and-seed-sensitive is the SECONDARY effect of paper
  terms once the gauge is fixed.

  ### Honest paper framing

  > "Paper Eq.14 reward terms (phi_h, phi_d, phi_f) are inert in
  > three of four algo × seed configurations tested (|Δ| ≤ 1.5%),
  > but contribute ~15% LS2 performance for the scalar algorithm at
  > seed 51. The reward terms are NEVER necessary for training
  > viability (no configuration collapses when they are removed,
  > provided phi_abs is present), but they can be marginally
  > helpful in specific algo × seed combinations. The gauge-fix
  > term phi_abs remains necessary and sufficient for training
  > universally."
