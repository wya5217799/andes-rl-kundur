  R373 returns BOUNDED-ENERGY-PORT-AUTHORITY-PASS for one prospectively

  drift, and the registered execution, energy/slew/SOC, achieved-power,
  electrical-coordinate, and physical-frequency-coordinate checks all pass.
  Across 12 condition-coordinate cells, minimum signed projected achieved
  power is 0.039996435254038246 system pu, diagonal frequency-response RMS is
  0.0015855414202403512--0.007708912537323694 Hz, and diagonal electrical-
  power-response RMS is 0.026315577865271472--0.04205944447014962 system pu.
  The finite response matrices also retain nonzero cross-coordinate terms; for
  the Bus14 inter-area cell, the descriptive diagonal-to-largest-cross
  frequency-response ratio is 1.714531367704426.

  This establishes bounded, energy-consistent authority only on the registered
  common/differential coordinates of one modified-Kundur topology and finite
  bank. It makes prospective deterministic cross-coordinate decoupler design
  eligible, while training remains unauthorized. It does not establish a
  controller, decoupling improvement, coordination or communication value,
  MARL increment, stability, safety, robustness, generalization, hardware, or
  publication-readiness claim.
