  identified, one rescued by hreg, the other beyond actor+critic

  After R183 (hreg s49 → still collapse), R185 (hreg s50 → rescued
  0.3515), and R186 (QR s49 → still collapse), the complete Q-0005
  mechanism picture is:

  ### s50 collapse mechanism — actor LSTM hidden-state divergence

  R56 original collapse seed. R72_w4 scalar critic at s50 = collapse;
  hreg λ=0.002 at s50 = 0.3515 (viable). The h-norm regularisation
  bounds the LSTM hidden state's drift growth, preventing the actor
  from running off into a non-recoverable region of state space.
  **CONFIRMED**: hreg rescues.

  ### s49 collapse mechanism — env/replay-side, not architecture

  Three independent algorithmic interventions tested:
  - R72_w4 scalar critic: geo 0.010, LS1=0 (CLM-0295)
  - hreg λ=0.002 (actor-state reg): geo 0.046, LS1=0 (R183/CLM-0345)
  - QR critic (distributional value head): geo 0.039, LS1=0 (R186)

  All three give **LS1=0 collapse**. Neither actor-state-norm nor
  distributional value head changes the failure mode. The mechanism
  is therefore NOT:
  - Actor LSTM state divergence (hreg would have helped)
  - Critic mean-bias / distributional-head insufficiency (QR would
    have helped)

  Remaining candidates from CLM-0345:
  - Env exploration noise trajectory at s49 traps actor in LS1-blind
    region before training escapes
  - Replay buffer's first ~5 episodes at s49 produce a buffer that
    biases the early critic toward a non-recoverable saddle

  Both are **env/replay-side** mechanisms — they would require
  intervention at training-loop level (e.g. randomized buffer warmup,
  exploration noise rescheduling) rather than at architecture level
  to test.
