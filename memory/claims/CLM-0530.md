  R266 exactly reconstructed all 20 R265 raw-gate action and alpha traces
  without rerunning ANDES (maximum action error 1.11e-16; maximum saved-alpha
  mean error 9.36e-9).  Mean actual action-TV was 5.506335: the frozen-alpha

  term was 4.064090 and occupied 67.8285% of the base-plus-switching triangle
  bound.  Across scenarios, switching TV and total TV correlated 0.999559.
  The 22.248% of transitions with absolute delta-alpha above 0.025 carried
  77.579% of switching TV.  This verifies that R265 is switching-dominant and
  supports selecting one alpha-only slew limiter for prospective testing; it
  does not establish that smoothing improves a new bank, corrected recurrent
  training, topology transfer, or closed-loop stability.
