  ### Why v2 over-scored R57-α

  v2 axes 6-7 (dH/dD utilization) use `_action_range` = span of **cross-agent mean** curve.
  When 1 agent emits large action and 3 emit tiny:
  - cross-agent mean is dominated by the 1 large
  - mean curve span is large → axis 6-7 score high
  - 6-axis aggregate looks SOTA

  But this is **single-agent monopolization** — paper Fig 7 requires 1:1:1:1.
  v2 has no axis for this. v3 axis 11 catches it.
