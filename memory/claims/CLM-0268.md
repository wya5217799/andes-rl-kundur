statement: |
  **R141-W1 (algo-class breakdown of bimodal cluster from CLM-0264,
  zero ANDES, ~10 min)**. CLM-0264 (R139) quantified bimodal attractor:
  40% degenerate / 42% LSTM SOTA / 19% mid. R141 classifies each of the
  91 ckpts by algo class and tabulates per-algo cluster fractions.

  ### Per-algo cluster assignment (truly novel breakdown)

  | Algo class | n total | degenerate | mid | LSTM SOTA |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | **SAC** | 3 | **2 (67%)** | 1 (33%) | **0 (0%)** |
  | **TD3-MLP** | 5 | **3 (60%)** | 2 (40%) | **0 (0%)** |
  | TD3-LSTM | 75 | 28 (37%) | 12 (16%) | **35 (47%)** |
  | unknown | 8 | 3 (38%) | 2 (25%) | 3 (38%) |

  **Headline finding**: **non-LSTM algorithms cannot reach the LSTM
  SOTA attractor**. 0/3 SAC + 0/5 TD3-MLP = 0/8 = 0% reach geo > 0.30.
  Combined with 75 TD3-LSTM ckpts where 47% DO reach LSTM SOTA, the
  attractor is **algo-conditional** — there exists a basin only
  reachable by LSTM-class actors.

  ### Refined cluster composition

  LSTM SOTA cluster (n=38):
  - 35 TD3-LSTM (92%) — all known-algo ckpts are LSTM
  - 3 unknown (likely LSTM by naming convention)
  - **0 non-LSTM** (0%)

  Degenerate cluster (n=36):
  - 28 TD3-LSTM (78%) — mostly FAILED LSTM training (cum_rf -0.09 to -0.60)
  - 3 TD3-MLP (8%)
  - 2 SAC (6%)
  - 3 unknown
  - The "good cum_rf" sub-cluster (best 4 by cum_rf):
    r67_w2a (TD3-MLP), r70_eval_sac_paper_s49 (SAC), r70_eval_td3_paper_s49 (TD3-MLP),
    r70_eval_td3_paper_s51 (TD3-MLP). **75% MLP / SAC, NOT LSTM**. These
    are the paper §IV-C cum_rf SOTAs per CLM-0118.
  - The "bad cum_rf" sub-cluster (worst by cum_rf): all 5 worst are
    TD3-LSTM with cum_rf -0.30 to -0.60 — failed training, NOT
    degenerate-by-design.

  ### Two distinct paths to degenerate cluster

  R141 reveals the degenerate cluster has **two structurally different
  populations**:

  1. **"Deliberate cum_rf-optimised degenerate"** (4 ckpts, MLP/SAC):
     converged to a saturated-action attractor that DOES optimise
     cum_rf. Project deliberately uses these as paper §IV-C SOTAs
     (CLM-0118). cum_rf ≈ -0.031 to -0.034.

  2. **"Failed-training degenerate"** (28+ ckpts, mostly LSTM):
     training didn't converge or fell into a bad attractor with poor
     cum_rf -0.09 to -0.60. NOT deliberately chosen — these are
     training failures.

  Conflating "warm-h_0 inference degenerate" (CLM-0204, geo 0.017,
  cum_rf -0.031) with "all degenerate" is now sharper: warm-h_0
  belongs to population (1) — saturated-action attractor with good
  cum_rf. R67/R70 paper SOTAs are the trained equivalent.

  ### LSTM bimodal failure rate

  Of 75 TD3-LSTM ckpts:
  - 47% (35) reach LSTM SOTA attractor (geo > 0.30, cum_rf -0.07 to -0.11)
  - 16% (12) mid (partial / intermediate-quality)
  - **37% (28) fail to degenerate** (geo < 0.10, cum_rf -0.09 to -0.60)

  **LSTM training has a ~37% failure rate by this measure**. This is a
  quantitative refinement of "LSTM is hard to train" intuition.

  ### Paper Sec.IV-D anchor revisions

  This sharpens R139's "two attractors" into:

  > "Across N=91 cached evaluations: 47% of TD3-LSTM training runs reach
  > the LSTM SOTA attractor (geo > 0.30, homogeneous cum_rf ≈ -0.08).
  > 37% of LSTM runs fall to the failed-training degenerate basin
  > (geo < 0.10, cum_rf -0.09 to -0.60). 16% remain in transitional
  > mid-region. SAC (0/3) and TD3-MLP (0/5) CANNOT reach LSTM SOTA —
  > they either fall to the saturated-cum_rf-optimum attractor
  > (deliberately exploited as paper §IV-C SOTA per CLM-0118) or
  > fail. The LSTM SOTA attractor is therefore **algo-exclusive**:
  > only recurrent-actor policies access this basin."

  ### Caveats

  - SAC n=3 + TD3-MLP n=5 is small; "0% reach LSTM SOTA" is a strong
    claim from N=8. Inflate to "no SAC/MLP ckpt in the evaluated set
    reached LSTM SOTA" rather than "categorically impossible".
  - Project deliberately limited SAC/TD3-MLP runs (CLM-0118 uses them
    for cum_rf SOTA role, not geo SOTA), so the sample is biased toward
    SAC/MLP candidates that ARE good cum_rf, not toward attempts to
    reach LSTM SOTA. The 0% rate is consistent with both "SAC/MLP can't
    reach LSTM SOTA" and "no one tried hard enough".
  - "unknown" 8 ckpts (hawe / ddic / mixed labels) — 3 reach LSTM SOTA
    (likely LSTM-based ensembles). Doesn't change SAC/MLP=0% finding.
