  ## Layer 2: Forensic mechanism (R84-R97, R104, R113)

  Critic + actor side-by-side post-hoc diagnostics on r72_w4 SOTA:

  - **Critic representation pathology** (CLM-0148, 0149, 0153-0156):
    cross-ckpt N=6 + agents × 200 prior obs forensics. Critic Q(s, a)
    sweep shows monotone-in-action behaviour on 5/6 ckpts; SAC s49
    only partial concavity (entropy regularisation gives weak anti-
    collapse). argmax always at boundary ±1.
  - **Curvature missing** (CLM-0150 R84-W3): high-resolution local
    Q-curvature on 4 agents × 200 obs × 201 grid pts; ``d²Q/da² ≈
    1.5×10⁻³`` (≈ 2% of |Q|), concave fraction = 0.530 (≈ 0.5 random).
    Q is essentially **affine in action**. Spectral norms healthy
    (median 1.51, max 3.44; (c) regularisation ruled out).
  - **Actor bang-bang** (CLM-0170, R92): SOTA "continuous" policy is
    effectively a 256-action discrete bang-bang controller; all 8
    action coordinates pinned at ±1 by step ~15 of every episode.
  - **Warm-h_0 universal slack** (CLM-0188, R104 N=9 ckpts): step-0
    action norm only reaches **8.5 %** of max with zero-init h_0;
    learnable h_0=MLP(obs_0) unlocks **95.6 %** + ΔQ always positive.
    Actor-side hard ceiling.
  - **Value-horizon mismatch** (CLM-0163, R91): under paper γ=0.99,
    50-step episode horizon ≈ 100-step value-horizon; offline 4-MLP
    regressor fits show obs are sufficient for action but not for
    value (R²-gap is 0.3+). γ-mismatch is parallel mechanism.
  - **Disturbance audit benign** (CLM-0215 / R113): ANDES default
    Toggler trip at t=2s contributes only ±0.9 % average max_df drop;
    R09 §2 2× max_df residual NOT explained by Toggler. Paper Sec.IV-C
    numbers stand without re-baselining over this audit deviation.

  The three layer-2 mechanism candidates (critic affine-Q + actor bang-
  bang + value-horizon mismatch + warm-h_0 slack) are **mutually
  consistent**: TD-based scalar Q critic + bilinear LSTM-cell first
  layer learns linear-in-action Q whose argmax sits at boundary, so
  actor saturates to ±1 (CLM-0170), which is the same configuration
  that wastes the warm-up h_0 slack documented in CLM-0188. The
  γ-mismatch (CLM-0163) explains why even a hypothetically perfect
  policy ranks suboptimally under paper's value-target.
