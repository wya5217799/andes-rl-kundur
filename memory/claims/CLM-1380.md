statement: |
  A geometric amplitude ladder (eps_0 x 2^-k, k=0..5, on the four R410
  evaluation profiles and the three registered signed pair kinds, two
  zero-bias controllers) measures the implemented deterministic law's
  controller-to-controller signed-pair odd response as quadratic-leading:
  log-log slopes in [1.68, 1.85] with R2 >= 0.997852 in 12/12 profile-pair
  blocks (QUADRATIC x12, summary QUADRATIC-CONSISTENT), the per-scale
  slopes rising monotonically toward 2.0 at the small-amplitude end, and
  the eps^2-compensated norm flattest (relative spread 0.50 vs 3.32/5.93
  for eps^1/eps^3); the k0 (x1.0) law bank is bit-identical to the R410
  deterministic evaluation records.  This supports the theory-audit
  prediction that asymmetric one-sided branch coefficients make the
  signed-pair difference O(eps^2) rather than cubic on the measured 32x
  amplitude range; a cubic leading term is not supported on this range.
