  ### Special findings

  **W6b zero-Δ mechanism**: 75 ep × 50 step = 3750 transitions < buffer cap
  5000 → buffer never fills → 5000 and 10000 functionally identical.
  Implication: paper buffer=10000 is over-provisioned for short-ep regime.
  For paper baseline 2000 ep (100k transitions), buffer matters.

  **W5c warmup catastrophe**: paper warmup_steps=1000 is essentially the
  *minimum* for ANDES short-horizon RL. Doubling to 2000 burns 27% of the
  75-ep budget on random actions → effective training shrunk to 55 ep →
  policy can't converge. paper choice is strict not "reasonable default".
