  **Definitive architectural reading**:

  The R72_w4 LSTM at (h, c) = (0, 0) has a **hard ceiling of ~40% of
  max action magnitude**, regardless of how obs is chosen. This is a
  property of the LSTMCell weight matrices: the input gate cannot drive
  the cell state high enough in one step to saturate the output, even
  with an adversarially-chosen obs.

  Reaching saturation REQUIRES non-zero (h, c). The only architectural
  fix is therefore some form of h-conditioning:
  - **Q-0022 / warm-h_0** (LSTM-specific): `(h_0, c_0) = MLP(obs_0)`
  - Hypothetical alternative: learn a `c_bias` parameter directly
    (no MLP, just a learnable vector for cell-state initial offset).
    Simpler, fewer params, but loses the obs-conditional flexibility.

  **R96 motivation strengthened from "addresses 10-step ramp" to
  "addresses ARCHITECTURAL HARD CEILING"**. There is NO obs-side fix
  for step-0 saturation in this LSTM.

  Caveat: tested on R72_w4 SOTA only. The 40% obs-ascent ceiling may
  vary across other LSTM ckpts (R104 showed warm-h_0 works on 9/9 — the
  symmetric obs-ascent test on the other 8 ckpts would confirm
  universality of the 40% ceiling). Synthetic obs caveat unchanged.
