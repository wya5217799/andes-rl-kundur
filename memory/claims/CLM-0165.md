  Two informative refinements over CLM-0160 (which reported overall median only):

  1. **Transient-onset effect (steps 0-5)**: advantage 0.022 is ~3×
     **lower** than settling (0.061), argmax_dist 0.275 is ~5× **wider**
     than settling (0.055). Time-series visualisation
     (`advantage_timeseries.png`) shows argmax_dist hits ~0.5-1.0 at
     step 0-3 then collapses to <0.1 by step 10. Mechanism: critic
     LSTMCell h is reset to zeros at episode start; ~5-10 forward
     steps accumulate enough context before the critic stabilises.
     **This is NOT a pathology** — every phase still passes the gate
     — but it confirms the off-manifold h_critic=0 measurements
     (CLM-0153/CLM-0154 / R84 W2/W3-syn) were probing a regime that
     **does exist** in the real eval (just only for ~5 steps per
     episode). The off-manifold artefact has a 1-second real-time
     footprint per disturbance, then disappears.

  2. **Per-agent epistemic heterogeneity**: Q1/Q2 disagreement
     ranges 14× across agents — agent_1=0.003 (most confident),
     agent_2=0.012, agent_3=0.039, agent_0=0.043 (least confident).
     Advantage_median is uniform (0.057-0.064). Inter-agent
     coordination structure to investigate, but per-agent
     advantage parity rules out "one agent's critic is broken".

  3. **obs_norm ↔ q_sota_mean correlation = −0.57** (medium-strong
     negative). Larger obs deviation from nominal frequency →
     lower predicted Q. Critic captures the physically expected
     "far-from-equilibrium = worse future return" relationship.
     obs_norm ↔ advantage = +0.08, obs_norm ↔ argmax_dist = +0.08
     — critic preference for a_sota is **not** degraded under
     high-deviation obs. obs distribution is not the bottleneck
     for critic confidence.
