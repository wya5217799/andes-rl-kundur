  R273 isolates R272's three matched zero-support TDS failures as a
  disturbance-envelope problem rather than an added zero-command ESD1-only
  DAE confound.  Under the frozen R272 disturbances, M/D=200/100, seed 42,
  solver configuration, and 300-step/60-second horizon, original V4 and the
  storage DAE with exactly zero requested, commanded, and actual active power
  reproduced the same completion vector.  Both plants failed random_00,
  random_05, and random_10 after 6-7 successful steps, while both completed
  all four signed/location controls for 300/300 steps.  All 14 trace hashes
  match, initialization and power flow succeeded, and storage SOC remained
  exactly 0.5 with zero constraint violations.  The registered primary
  classification is therefore ENVELOPE-INFEASIBLE.

  A separately sealed, completion-only four-iteration bisection at positive
  PQ_Bus14 then produced a common conditional bracket: both plants completed
  at 1.530775 system pu for 60 seconds and both failed at 1.6396625 system pu,
  leaving a 0.1088875-pu interval.  This is a local numerical/physical
  completion boundary for the frozen model, solver, timing, and disturbance
  location, not a universal voltage-stability limit and not controller
  performance evidence.  R272 remains INVALID and Gate 2 remains closed.

  R273 also adds a test-first prospective feasibility-screen contract.  It
  requires completion evidence to be frozen before any controller trace,
  retains every excluded scenario, reports the excluded fraction, and
  stratifies exclusions by disturbance sign and location.  It is not applied
  post hoc to R272; any authority re-test requires a new sealed bank and
  round.
