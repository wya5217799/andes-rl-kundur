statement: |
  R64 W4 — explore_noise effect is **weak/noise-level** under R64 combo
  (lr=3e-3). Discard from final combo; default en=0.1 is sufficient.

  ### Single seed (s50, lr=3e-3 combo)

  | EXPLORE_NOISE | best_eval |
  |---|---|
  | 0.05 | -0.128 (+3% over 0.1) |
  | 0.1 (default) | -0.132 (R64 W2 baseline) |
  | 0.2 | -0.130 (+1.5%) |

  Mild U-curve, but range only 3% — within seed variance noise.

  ### 3-seed verify en=0.05

  | seed | best_eval |
  |---|---|
  | s49 | -0.120 |
  | s50 | -0.128 |
  | s51 | -0.121 |
  | **3-seed mean** | **-0.123** |

  vs lr=3e-3 alone 3-seed mean -0.124 → **marginal +0.8 %**.
  Difference smaller than 3-seed standard error.

  ### Mechanism

  TD3's target smoothing (policy_noise=0.2 + noise_clip=0.5) already
  smooths the deterministic policy's Q-target. explore_noise is added
  only to non-deterministic rollouts during training. With our
  short-horizon (75 ep) + small action space, exploration is already
  saturated by random env-warmup phase. Further noise reduction (0.1
  → 0.05) marginally helps but is dominated by other variance sources.

  ### Implication

  Skip explore_noise tuning in production training. Default 0.1 OK.

  Q-0012 candidate: under longer training (Q-0008 500-ep) might
  explore_noise matter more? Untested.
