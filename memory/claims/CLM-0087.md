  **Winner: MAX_GRAD_NORM=0.5** — **largest single-axis lift (+8.4 %)**
  in R63 sweep. **New single-seed paper-metric SOTA = -0.153** (LS1
  -0.025, LS2 -0.015).

  ### Curious observation — gc=5 and gc=10 identical

  Both best.pt and best_eval.pt values are bit-identical between gc=5
  and gc=10. Means TD3 gradients during training **rarely exceed 5**
  in our env; gc=5+ effectively = no clipping. The active clipping
  range is gc<1.

  Strict monotone in tested range: 0.5 > 1 > 5 ≈ 10.

  ### Mechanism

  Tighter gradient clipping reduces optimizer step variance, especially
  in early-training when value bootstrap is noisy. TD3 (deterministic
  policy + twin critic) benefits more than SAC (entropy regulariser
  already smooths gradient noise). For our small-network (h=64) +
  short-horizon (75 ep) regime, gc=0.5 is the right scale.

  ### Implication

  Pre-R63 hardcoded gc=1.0 in `sac_base.py` was sub-optimal. All
  SAC/TD3 training in project history (R01–R62) used gc=1.0 → all
  was sub-optimal. R63 W2 confirms gc=0.5 should be new default for
  SAC/TD3 production.

  vs paper DDIC: with gc=0.5 alone (s50) — LS1 imp 78 %, LS2 imp
  85 %, mean **81.5 %** vs paper 46.5 % = **+35pp single-seed**.

  ### Caveat

  Single-seed result. gc=0.5 3-seed and SAC verification still
  pending. Combo includes gc=0.5 (CLM-0089) which gave 3-seed
  evidence the value is robust.
