statement: |
  R64 W1+W2 — **lr=3e-3 is the optimal learning rate** for TD3 on our
  env, **10× higher than paper Table I default** (3e-4).

  ### lr full sweep (s50, R63 combo baseline = nsub=3 gc=0.5 bs=512)

  | lr | best.pt | best_eval.pt |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1e-4 | -0.223 | -0.217 |
  | 3e-4 (paper Table I default) | -0.177 | -0.161 (R63 baseline) |
  | 5e-4 | -0.201 | -0.175 |
  | 1e-3 | -0.137 | -0.133 |
  | 2e-3 | -0.141 | -0.133 |
  | **3e-3** ⭐ | -0.138 | **-0.132** |
  | 5e-3 | -0.145 | -0.139 |

  ### U-curve shape

  - Sharp left-side degradation: lr=1e-4 (-30%) < 3e-4 baseline
  - Plateau: 1e-3 / 2e-3 / 3e-3 within 0.001
  - Right-side mild degradation: 5e-3 (-5%) vs 3e-3
  - **Sweet spot 1e-3 to 3e-3** (width ~3×)

  ### Mechanism

  Paper Table I lr=3e-4 inherited from paper's env tuning. Paper's
  env has:
  - **20× larger action space** (CLM-0076): ΔH [-100, +300] vs ours [-5, +15]
  - Different VSG simulator + REGCA1 model

  Under paper's env, lr=3e-4 may be optimal; under ours, the smaller
  action space means **policy updates are smaller-magnitude**, and
  higher lr compensates. 10× lr is consistent with 20× scale ratio
  (within order of magnitude).

  ### Implication

  All R01..R63 trainings used lr=3e-4 (project default = paper default).
  **All were sub-optimal**. R65+ should use lr=3e-3 by default.

  Effect on paper-metric SOTA (single seed s50):
  - Pre-R63: -0.196 (R62 baseline)
  - R63 combo (lr=3e-4): -0.161
  - **R64 lr=3e-3**: **-0.132** (+18 % over R63)

  Combined with all R63 winners + lr=3e-3: 3-seed mean -0.124 (CLM-0093).
