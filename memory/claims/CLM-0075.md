  ### Mechanism: reward-magnitude hijack

  Anti-smoothness term grows quadratically with action change. At
  ep 0-20 of LSTM training (before policy stabilizes), action
  fluctuations are large → reward magnitude **~7000** (vs typical
  R56/R57 ~10-200 range). Final critic loss = 815, decreasing from
  2142; 15 reward-divergence WARNs triggered; TDS failures 3/75
  (vs typical 0-1/75).

  This is consistent with R55/CLM-0062's "exploration-noise hijack
  collapses memoryless TD3" mechanism, now **generalized**: large
  per-step reward shaping terms can hijack training via magnitude
  imbalance regardless of policy class. LSTM's structurally
  time-varying output (R56/CLM-0063) bypasses the *noise* channel
  but not the *reward-scale* channel — when the reward term
  magnitude dominates the policy's per-step learning budget, the
  recurrent advantage cannot recover.

  `best.pt` is guaranteed to be a pre-training artifact and all
