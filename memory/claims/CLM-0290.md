  ## Paper Sec.IV-C / V narrative (revised)

  **Plateau characterization**:
  - R72_w4 baseline + R142 QR + R143 QR-mean-fix all near 0.39 single-policy ceiling
  - No single-axis critic-representation intervention (CLM-0157 a/b/a+b) breaks this
  - AFE (CLM-0157(b)) is structurally broken — zero-action sweet spot pathology drags critic
  - WarmH0 actor (CLM-0188 universal feasibility on trained ckpts) does NOT translate to from-scratch training improvement
  - 75 ep is sweet spot; 200 ep regresses 53% (noise-hijack CLM-0067 pathology recurring)

  **Plateau breaker (the paper's positive finding)**:
  HAWE (Heterogeneous Actor Weighted Ensemble) of 3 cross-algorithm s54
  ckpts {scalar-Q baseline + 2× QR distributional variants} at MEAN
  aggregation gives **+3.5% over best individual**. Architectural diversity
  damps individual over-correction. Constituent quality threshold matters
  (R150 at 0.35 hurts, R72_w5 at 0.32 hurts; ensemble members must each
  be near-optimal).
