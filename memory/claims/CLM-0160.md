  **R84-W3-traj (D2b on-manifold variant) — the FIRST and ONLY on-manifold
  Q-landscape test of the R72_w4 SOTA critic.** Refutes the synthetic-obs
  "actor-critic decoupling / affine-Q" mechanism story documented in
  [[CLM-0149]] / [[CLM-0153]] / [[CLM-0154]]. Script
  `scripts/r84_d2b_q_landscape_trajectory.py` runs the same Q-landscape
  probe schema (per-obs SOTA advantage, argmax_dist, Q1/Q2 disagreement,
  ∂Q/∂a) but **draws (obs, h_actor, h_critic) along an actual ANDES TDS
  deterministic rollout of the SOTA on LS1 + LS2**, with the critic's
  LSTMCell hidden state ADVANCED at each step by the realised (s, a*)
  pair — i.e. the critic is probed in the regime it actually operates
  in during eval.

  | 指标 | D2 / W3-syn (h=0, N(0,σ²I) obs) | D2b (real h, real obs) | Δ |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | advantage_median | +0.006 (+9% \|Q\|) | **+0.060 (+120% \|Q\|)** | **20×** |

  On the SOTA operating manifold the critic strongly endorses the actor:
  99.75% of states show SOTA strictly better than mean random,
  argmax_a Q is essentially co-located with a_sota (13% of action
  diagonal away), and a 100-random-action sweep finds **no** action
  better than a_sota by more than −8% of \|Q\|. **The Q surface IS
  concave-around-a_sota on the real state manifold** — the off-manifold
  monotone/affine landscape documented by W2/W3-syn is an artefact of
  probing at h_critic=0 + obs ~ N(0, σ²I).

  **The 91-round plateau is therefore NOT "critic representation /
  affine-Q / actor-critic decoupling"** as proposed by CLM-0154 R85
  PRIORITY 1 path. The LSTM hidden state h_critic does substantial
  heavy lifting in shaping the action-preference landscape, and that
  representation is competent on-manifold. Distributional critic /
  action feature engineering / wider exploration warmup are **not**
  motivated by mechanism evidence — they target a pathology that
  doesn't exist in the regime that matters.

  **R85+ priority revision (supersedes CLM-0154 §R85 candidate ordering)**:
  - ❌ Distributional critic (IQN / QR) — DOWNGRADED from PRIORITY 1.
    Critic is concave on-manifold; adding a distributional head does
    not add information.
  - ❌ Critic regularisation — RULED OUT both by Part B spectral check
    (CLM-0154) AND by on-manifold endorsement (this claim).
  - ❌ Wider exploration warmup ≥ 25 — DOWNGRADED. Justified by "narrow
    action support during early training" hypothesis, but the resulting
    critic IS competent on its operating manifold; widening support
    targets a non-pathology.
  - ✅ **D3 obs sufficiency** — PROMOTED to candidate PRIORITY 1.
    Question: does paper-faithful 7-dim obs + LSTM h carry enough
    information for an ε-optimal policy? Critic is endorsing actor on
    current obs; if obs has lower mutual information with optimal
    return than needed, then the bottleneck is obs ceiling, not actor
    or critic. Implementation: BC regressor + V regressor on cached
    SOTA trajectory; ~30 min wall, zero ANDES. Directly informs R83
    obs aug path.
  - ✅ **D4 env stochasticity floor** — PROMOTED. Question: how much
    of the 0.391 plateau is set by disturbance / scenario variance
    rather than policy quality? Need varied-disturbance eval.
  - ✅ **Reward shape ablation** — KEPT. Paper Sec.III's PHI_ABS=0
    strict vs V4's PHI_ABS=50 rescale was R58 territory; unexplored
    on R72_w4 hyper basin.

  **Q-0018 closure**: persistence hypothesis **rejected**. The synthetic-
