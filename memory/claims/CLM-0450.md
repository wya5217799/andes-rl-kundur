  any noise floor.

  ### Combined finding (paired with R254 → CLM-0455)

  Adding paper r_h alone AND paper r_d alone produces functionally
  identical controllers to using neither. **Both individual R18-
  rescaled paper terms are vestigial** on scalar+s50. The remaining
  candidate (phi_f at paper-nominal weight 100, NOT rescaled) is
  tested in R254 and identified there as the SOLE load-bearing
  paper term (CLM-0455).

  ### Why phi_h and phi_d are vestigial at R18 rescale

  R18 rescaled paper-Eq.14-nominal phi_h=phi_d=1.0 to 0.0056 (= 1/178)
  for ANDES numerical stability. At 0.0056, these terms are 4 orders
  of magnitude smaller than phi_f=100. Their gradient contribution
  to actor loss is ~4×10⁻⁴ of phi_f's — well below the precision
  threshold affecting controller updates.
