statement: |
  R113 quantitative test of Q-0025 (Toggler-Line_8 ablation) closes
  the R110/CLM-0194 audit hypothesis as **NEGATIVE**: removing the
  unintended ANDES default Toggler at t=2 s changes V4 max_df by an
  average of only **+0.9 %** across LS1+LS2 (zero-action, paper-faithful
  config, single ANDES session, 4 evals × 150 steps, 225 s wall).

  | scenario | u=1 (with trip) max_df Hz | u=0 (no trip) max_df Hz | Δ% |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | load_step_1 | 0.1890 | 0.2056 | **-8.8** % (toggler *helps*) |
  | load_step_2 | 0.1683 | 0.1506 | **+10.5** % (toggler *hurts*) |
  | **avg**     |        |        | **+0.9** % |

  11-axis geo (zero-action / is_ddic=False): u=1 geo=0.0938 vs u=0
  geo=0.1032 (+10 % gap, dominated by LS2 axis-1 max_df). Paper-§IV-C
  cum_rf: u=1 -0.2169 vs u=0 -0.2260 (-4 %, opposite sign from geo
  because LS2 trace shape differs even though peak drops).

  **Decision rule from Q-0025**: average ≥ 30 % drop ⇒ TOGGLER_DOMINANT
  R114-re-baseline; 10-30 % ⇒ TOGGLER_MEANINGFUL; < 10 % ⇒ TOGGLER_MINOR
  Q-0025 closed-negative. R113 lands at +0.9 % → **TOGGLER_MINOR**.

  **Mechanism interpretation**: Line_8 is one of two parallel 230 kV
  Area-2 internal tie-lines. Tripping it at t=2 s redistributes power
  flow within Area 2 but does not propagate to Area 1 (where LS1's Bus 14
  PQ load sits). For LS1 (Bus 14 load drop, frequency *rise*), the trip
  removes a damping pathway in Area 2 and the Area-2 frequency overshoot
  worsens slightly (-8.8 %). For LS2 (Bus 15 load increase, frequency
  *drop*), the trip isolates a stressed corridor and Area-2 frequency
  excursion is dampened (+10.5 %). The effects cancel within ≈ 1 % on
  average — not the dominant 2× max_df residual driver R110 hypothesised.

  **What this means for R57-R85 baselines**:
  - R72_w4 SOTA geo=0.391 (CLM-0094): essentially unchanged on
    Toggler-removed env. Paper Sec.IV-C number stands.
  - R85 best droop K=2 geo=0.197: same. RL 2× advantage holds.
  - R09 §2 Finding 2 (2× max_df residual at H=300): **NOT explained by
    Toggler**. Cause remains open. Likely candidates: F2 (load topology)
    + F3 (capacitive q) + F-NEW (V4Config D₀ heterogeneity not in paper,
    Q-0024 ish).
  - paper Sec.IV-C single-event vs V4 compound: **not apples-to-apples**
    in principle but **<1 % numerical impact**. Document but no rebaseline.

  **Patch verification**: ``env.ss.Toggler.u.v[:] = 0`` after
  ``env.reset`` (which warms to t=0.5 s, well before Toggler.t=2 s)
  reliably suppresses the trip — verified by post-patch log
  ``post.u=[0.0]`` and consistent geo / cum_rf differences across LS1+LS2
  matching the expected sign of removed-disturbance impact.

  **Cost**: 225.6 s wall (4 ANDES TDS × 150 steps); 1 WSL slot (shared
  with R102/R106 eval, all 3 finished within window); 0 V4 / V4Config /
  base_env / agents / train.py / ckpt mutation.

  **Falsified hypothesis** (CLM-0194 R110): "Toggler is the dominant
  remaining cause of the R09 2× max_df residual." Refuted at +0.9 %
  average.

  **Strengthened hypothesis**: R57-R85 91-round + R100/R103 in-flight
  training baselines are **robust to Toggler ablation**; paper Sec.IV-C
  numbers do not need re-baselining over this single audit deviation.
