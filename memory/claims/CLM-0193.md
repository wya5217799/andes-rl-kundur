  | ||obs|| | norm_zero | norm_star | norm_lift_pp | ΔQ_abs_med |
  |---|---|---|---|---|

  Decay = 0 percentage points across the 20× obs-magnitude range.
  H0 (constant slack) **confirmed**.

  **Important architectural reading**: the R72_w4 LSTM **categorically
  cannot saturate from (h=0, c=0)** regardless of obs magnitude. The
  saturation requires non-zero (h, c). At runtime, the actor reaches
  saturation by step 10 because the LSTM accumulates h across 10
  forward passes; this accumulation is NOT triggered by larger obs.

  This sharpens the R88 / R95 / R104 mechanism story:

  Old reading: "LSTM takes 10 steps to warm up; obs grows over the
              episode and that helps."
  New reading: **"LSTM warms up by h accumulation INDEPENDENT of
                  obs magnitude. The 10-step ramp is a pure LSTM
                  hidden-state-integration timescale; warm-h_0 fix
                  short-circuits it at step 0."**

  Implication for R96 expected lift: warm-h_0 should help **every
  step, not just transient**, because at ALL steps the actor benefits
  from h carrying useful information. But empirically the saturation
  is already achieved by step 10 via accumulation, so the operationally
  meaningful lift is in steps 0-9.

  This also predicts that R94's widen-action-bound experiment may show
  Outcome A (steady-state lift) but NOT transient lift unless the
  widened actor also gets a warm-h_0 — because widening the bound
  doesn't help if the actor still starts at ||a||=0.15. CLM-0175
  prediction strengthened.

  **2026-05-19 trust V→S downgrade + R112 env-side refutation**:
  R107-W2 evidence is grad-ascent over (h, c) on synthetic obs (random
  unit-sphere directions, ||obs||∈[0.1, 2.0]). The argmax ||h*|| ≈ 11-15
  is *out-of-distribution* for the trained LSTM's natural h trajectory.
  [[CLM-0204]] (R112) ran the on-policy follow-up: inject the grad-
  ascent-derived (h*, c*) as LSTM init in a real ANDES rollout. Phase 1
  replicated Q-side lift exactly. **Phase 2 env-side rollout collapsed
  6-axis geo from 0.391 to 0.017 (-95.8 %)** while cum_rf paradoxically
  improved +54 %. The "h_0 is the ONLY barrier" architectural reading
  is correct *for Q-side ||a||* but does NOT extend to 6-axis policy
  performance. Off-policy critic-Q lift and on-policy 6-axis geo are
  anti-correlated at the step-0 boundary on this codebase / reward.
  Trust downgraded V → S accordingly; the architectural-slack statement
  remains valid for Q-side, but propagating "warm-h_0 will lift policy
  performance" into downstream paper claims requires constrained /
  α-interpolated variants — see [[CLM-0204]] follow-up.
