statement: |
  R361 is NO-NEIGHBOUR-LEARNABLE-STRUCTURE under the pre-registered
  one-hop neighbour-message gate. On the exposed sixteen-case development
  bank, extending each edge actor's exact fifteen-field observation with the
  frozen four-field one-hop ring-neighbour messages (frequency deviation,
  RoCoF, SOC, voltage from the two ring neighbours, twenty-three fields
  total) does not let any of the four pre-registered tuning-free non-neural
  map families (fixed affine, RBF kernel ridge, k-NN with k=5, quadratic
  polynomial basis) clear both endpoint groups after the exact physical
  projection: nominal common improvements are 1.06%, 1.01%, 0.79%, and
  -0.95% against the 2% gate while nominal differential-coordinate mean
  ratios worsen by 0.94x, 19.81x, 5.05x, and 142.74x, and mismatch-bounded
  common improvements are -0.37%, -0.41%, -0.63%, and -2.38%. Every
  integrity check passes including the frozen neighbour table, and the
  classifier reads 0 holdout cases and 0 holdout residual labels. The
  surveyed information-path hypothesis is therefore further weakened: the
  strongest tuning-free information extension that keeps the frozen map
  families and the frozen bank does not introduce surveyed learnable
  structure, and training, simulation, and EVAL remain unauthorized. This
  finding is limited to this one message contract, the three registered
  non-neural families plus the fixed affine map, one fixed Kundur plant,
  two exposed operating points, and the exposed finite bank; it does not
  establish general absence or unlearnability of a nonlinear or neural
  residual, general neighbour-information insufficiency, the value of
  prediction sharing, consensus estimation, attention weighting, or a
  common-channel residual authority, or multi-agent value.
