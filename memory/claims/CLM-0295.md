  ### Three structural findings

  **1. Cross-algo (same-seed) > cross-seed (same-algo)**: CLM-0280
  conjectured "cross-seed > cross-algo same-seed" — R154 REFUTES this.
  - {s54, s51} pure cross-seed: 0.3766 (HURTS, -3.6%)
  - {s54, s51, R142} cross-seed + algo: 0.3890 (~baseline)
  - {s54, R142, R143, R100} same-seed cross-algo: **0.4119**
  R72_w4 at s51 underperforms baseline (-8.9%) and s49 collapses;
  averaging weaker members drags the ensemble down. **Same-seed cross-
  ALGORITHM diversity is the load-bearing mechanism, not seed**.

  **2. Non-bang-bang policy is the LS2 unlocker**: R100_hreg single
  policy has the highest LS2 axis (0.467) of all 5 trained policies
  (R72_w4 0.431, R142/R143 0.408, R150 0.362). It's the only
  drift-killed continuous policy (CLM-0190 R100 mechanism: hidden-norm
  reg keeps ||h|| bounded, actor stays in tanh-linear regime). Adding
  R100 to {R72_w4, R142, R143} 3-way (R152 0.4043 LS2=0.435) lifts
  LS2 to 0.461 (+6%) while keeping LS1 in same range. **R100's policy
  structure complementarity is the project-SOTA driver**.

  **3. Local optimum is 4-way uniform**: Tested 8 variations around the
  breakthrough config. All deviations regress:
  - 5-way (+R150): 0.4072 (-0.5pp)
  - weighted hreg-heavy 0.4: 0.4106 (-0.1pp)
  - drop-R143: 0.4086 (-0.3pp)
  - replace R100 with s51: 0.3948 (-1.7pp)
  **4-way uniform mean of {scalar-Q baseline, QR-buggy, QR-fixed,
  hreg-continuous} at same seed s54 is the local SOTA**.

  ### R72_w4 SOTA at s54 is a lucky seed (Q-0005 confirmed at R72_w4 hyper)

  Cross-seed training revealed R72_w4 hyper is fragile to seed:
  - s54: 0.3908 (canonical)
  - s49: **0.0100 (collapse)**
  - s51: 0.3562 (-8.9%)

  R56 Q-0005 ("Why does TD3+LSTM seed 50 collapse while seeds 49/51
  converge?") now extends: at R72_w4 hyper (lr=1e-4 clamp, tau=0.001,
  warmup=5), s49 also collapses. Basin is narrow. R72_w4 SOTA s54
  result is at the edge of working seed range; reproducibility multi-
  seed claims should use s54 as the demonstration seed with caveat.

  ### Caveats

  - All trained on V4 + compound disturbance (CLM-0194 Toggler).
