statement: |
  **R99-W1 (architectural feasibility test for Q-0022 LSTM warm-h_0,
  zero ANDES)**. CLM-0174 (R95) found the R72_w4 SOTA actor at step 0
  outputs ||a||=0.149 (10.5% of max √2) because the LSTM hidden state
  starts at (h, c) = (0, 0). Q-0022 proposes learning h_0 = MLP(obs_0)
  to unlock immediate saturation. R99 tests whether the **frozen**
  R72_w4 LSTM actor architecture has the degrees of freedom to do so.

  Method: 100 step-0-like synthetic obs (||obs||=0.25, matching CLM-0161
  measured step-0 obs_norm) × 4 frozen agents. Gradient-ascent (Adam, lr
  0.05, 500 steps) over (h_0, c_0) to maximise min(Q1, Q2)(s, actor(s,
  h_0, c_0)). Compare ||a|| and Q at h=0 vs h=h*. Critic hidden also at 0.

  Per-agent results:

  | agent | ||a||(h=0) | ||a||(h*) | norm lift | Q(h=0) | Q(h*) | Q gain |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0 | 0.167 (11.8%) | 1.408 (99.6%) | +87.8 pp | -0.131 | -0.083 | **+36.7%** |
  | 1 | 0.126 (8.9%)  | 1.403 (99.2%) | +90.3 pp | -0.022 | +0.034 | **+253.7%** |
  | 2 | 0.276 (19.5%) | 1.410 (99.7%) | +80.2 pp | -0.163 | -0.112 | +30.9% |
  | 3 | 0.080 (5.7%)  | 1.407 (99.5%) | +93.8 pp | -0.065 | -0.014 | +78.8% |

  Aggregate (median across 4 agents):
  - Norm lift: 10.4% → 99.5% of max (lift = **89.2 percentage points**)
  - Q gain: **+57.8%** of |Q_zero|
  - Optimal ||h*|| ≈ 11-15, ||c*|| ≈ 11-13 (within reach of a small MLP
    head — `nn.Linear(7, 64)` could output norm ~ √64 ≈ 8 from unit obs)

  Gate: norm_lift > 50 pp AND q_lift > 20% → **FEASIBLE**.

  Interpretation: the R72_w4 LSTM actor weights ARE capable of
  step-0 saturation; the (0, 0) initial hidden state is the ONLY barrier.
  An MLP-learned h_0 with output norm ~12 (trivially reachable) would
  give the actor architectural slack to reach 99%+ of max ||a|| from
  step 0. **CLM-0174's "LSTM warm-up lag is architectural" claim
  upgraded from observed (forward-pass ramp-up) to mechanically explained
  (h=0 is the single inhibiting state)**.

  Caveat: synthetic step-0-like obs (||obs||=0.25, random direction).
  Real ANDES step-0 obs has specific structure (P_es / d_omega / omega_dot
  scaled values); the per-direction feasibility may be tighter than the
  averaged 99.5% result. To upgrade to V-trust with on-manifold caveat
  removed, would need cached ANDES step-0 obs from r84_d2b_q_landscape_trajectory
  (which only stored obs_norm scalar, not full vector) or new ANDES eval
  rollout (R83/R85 WSL lock pending). Q-0022 architectural premise
  test is therefore **necessary but not sufficient** evidence; R96+
  training run still required to verify learned MLP h_0 captures the
  optimal h*.

  Independent of R94 widen-bound outcome (CLM-0175 prediction matrix):
  whether R94 returns Outcome A or B, Q-0022 has independent
  architectural justification from R99.
