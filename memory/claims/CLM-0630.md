  R283 (Q-0043, STRENGTH-GRADIENT-CONFIRMED): on the frozen V4 modified
  Kundur plant (R281/R282 identical build and mapping), the damping
  sensitivity of the classical GENROU inter-area mode to differential VSG
  inertia allocation grows materially as grid strength falls — a measured
  strength gradient, not a development-probe hint.

  Electrical axis (declared SCR proxy: r and x of the 7<->8 tie corridor
  Line_4/5/6 scaled by k, constant r/x, charging b untouched, PFlow re-run
  per k; k in {1.0, 1.5, 2.0} x q in {0, +/-0.25}, all 9 points on-branch):
  sensitivity S = |zeta(+0.25)-zeta(-0.25)|/|zeta(0)| = 0.458 -> 1.050 ->
  2.053 (ratio 4.48, monotone increasing as the tie weakens).  The
  R281/R282 U-shaped upturn disappears at k >= 1.5 (zeta(+0.25) < zeta(0):
  0.0148 < 0.0161 at k=1.5; 0.0069 < 0.0129 at k=2.0): the mapping becomes
  monotone over the measured range and the beneficial-direction (b=-1) gain
  grows from +55% at k=1.0 to +159% at k=2.0.  Baseline mode weakens
  consistently (zeta(0) 0.0195 -> 0.0129, f 0.527 -> 0.482 Hz).

  Inertia axis (executed M scaled by M0/200, M0 in {100,150,200,250,300} x
  q in {0, +/-0.25}, per-level zero-sum re-declared as 1400*M0/200): valid
  levels give S = 0.458 (M0=200) -> 0.304 (M0=250) -> 0.199 (M0=300),
  ratio 2.30, monotone; M0=100/q=-0.25 and M0=150/q=+/-0.25 are flagged
  off-branch by the in-script branch-validity screen (participation cosine
  0.0000 vs q=0; area contrast ~0 at M0=150/q=-0.25), reproducing and
  explaining R281's offline M0=100/q=-0.25 identification breakpoint; the
  valid M0=100 q=0 -> +0.25 pair corroborates the trend (+53%, matching
  the R281 development probe).

  Anchors M0=200 and k=1.0 reproduce the R281 anchor within |dzeta| < 1e-6;
  all guards passed at all 24 points (G4 M=0.1/D=0, per-level zero-sum,
  PFlow convergence).  The gradient is unmeasured — not absent — inside the
  flagged low-inertia hybridization zone (opened as Q-0044).  Bounded
  wording for the SCI weak-grid section: within the scanned ranges,
  allocation matters more in weaker grids; "allocation creates damping"
  and real-SCR unit conversion remain out of bounds.
