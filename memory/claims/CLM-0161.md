  Interpretation: **the R72_w4 SOTA critic is concave on the real state

  but doesn't know what action to take. Both critic heads (Q1, Q2)
  disagree more in transient than steady — high epistemic uncertainty
  EXACTLY when the policy is most decision-critical.

  plateau (CLM-0144)** that is consistent with BOTH R86 cross-ckpt
  monotone universality (CLM-0155 — off-manifold pathology) AND
  CLM-0160 on-manifold concavity (steady-state competence): the gap
  is **transient-phase critic data starvation**. In a 50-step episode,
  the critic sees only ~6% (3/50) of samples from step 0-2, but the
  6-axis paper-grade metric is dominated by early-step max_df / dD_smooth
  / settling proxy — exactly where the critic is least confident.
  Training-loss-weighted aggregates hide the asymmetry because 94% of
  the loss signal comes from steady-state.
