  R363 is COMMON-CHANNEL-HEADROOM-EXPANDED under the pre-registered
  mechanism-gap gate. On the exposed sixteen-case development bank, extending
  the R358 three-edge zero-common residual basis with the frozen common
  residual-power channel (four-channel node-action basis
  [ones(4), active_power_incidence()]) makes all sixteen cases physically
  feasible, versus the R358 ten-of-sixteen baseline, unlocking every one of
  the six previously relaxed-infeasible scenarios. In every feasible case the
  common-coordinate ratio reaches 0.0000-0.0002 (the registered 2%
  improvement is achieved with essentially the entire common endpoint
  removed) with zero differential degradation, all within the exact R358
  physical limits and with all solver diagnostics accepted. The sealed
  classifier reads 0 holdout cases and keeps training, simulation, and EVAL
  unauthorized. This confirms the surveyed mechanism gap: the zero-common
  residual contract itself is a structural limiter of common-coordinate
  headroom, since the common endpoint cannot be improved through the zero-sum
  edge channels alone while a fleet-equal common channel removes it directly.
  The result is an information-unconstrained physical feasibility statement
  on the exposed bank; it does not establish that any information path or
  learning method can select the common channel, does not overturn the
  R359-R362 information-path negatives, and authorizes only a separately
  registered successor question after a re-derived power/energy contract.
