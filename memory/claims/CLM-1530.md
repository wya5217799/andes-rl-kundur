  checkpoint and recorded-path diagnostics show large raw-TV changes when the
  time-varying previous-action input is replaced by an acausal full-record mean,
  together with frequently comparable constant-anchor and actual raw-RMS norms.
  The tested replacement remains far above the direct-M/D command contract.
  These frozen-path diagnostics do not identify feedback amplification, a
  quasi-static dominant source, preregistered equivalence, full-roster mechanism
  prevalence, training causality, physical harm, safety, topology
  generalisation, convergence or optimality, and they do not alter CLM-1525.
