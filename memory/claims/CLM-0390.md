  ### Mechanism (see gauge-invariance memo)

  Paper Eq.14 reward terms (r_f, r_h, r_d) all depend on differences
  between agent quantities, not absolute values. Under uniform shift
  Δω_k → Δω_k + c, all three terms are invariant. The paper-strict
  reward thus has a continuous family of reward-zero attractors
  parameterized by c (synchronized drift). phi_abs = -(Δω_i)² is
  the minimal-form gauge-fix that breaks this invariance.

  When phi_abs is present, the paper Eq.14 reward terms become
  effectively redundant — the gauge-fix term alone provides
  sufficient training signal — explaining why hreg AND scalar AND
  s51 AND s54 all show ±1% irrelevance of paper terms.
