  On the sealed R365 V4 electromechanical proxy, the registered per-VSG
  object/action gate passes: four unique GENCLS-based VSG units at buses 12,
  16, 14, and 15 map one-to-one to four runtime actors; the eight normalized
  actor-channel coordinates have rank-eight executed M/D readback; the
  seven-field no-delay observations reconstruct from the same-step local and
  declared ring-neighbour signals; the combined heterogeneous-damping and
  localized-load-step condition produces an above-noise differential
  transient; and each actor's bounded combined M/D intervention changes at
  least one other VSG frequency or active-power trace above the repeated-run
  numerical floor.

  This is a deterministic prerequisite result on one modified-Kundur plant,
  one disturbance, and one six-second horizon. It establishes neither
  controller efficacy nor decoupling, coordination, message, or MARL gain.
  The plant uses a 60-Hz physical base while the inherited observation
  normalization uses a 50-Hz contract, and the actuator is GENCLS parameter
  modulation without converter, energy, state-of-charge, current, or hardware
  feasibility. Those contracts must be resolved or explicitly bounded before
  the next control comparison; training remains unauthorized.
