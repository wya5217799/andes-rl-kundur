  **Training was healthy** (not a failed training):
  - R127 critic_loss declined 9.1 → 5.0 → 1.8 across ep 19/29/39 — critic
    learning the return distribution.
  - R127 action_std collapsed 0.57 → 0.10 by ep 29 (deterministic actor formed).
  - R127 best train_reward -5.9 at ep 29 (vs ep 0 -30.9, 5× improvement).
  - R129 best train_reward -6.7 at ep 11 (similar healthy training).
  - Both finished 75/75 episodes; no crash mid-train.

  **But eval-time collapse**: LS1=0 universally (TDS divergence on disturbance
  path), LS2 mostly 0 except R129 LS2=0.17 (one scenario survives). This is
  the **CLM-0067 R56 collapse pathology recurring** under critic-representation
  fixes — actor reward-games to high train_reward by being deterministic
  conservative, but on 150-step paper eval can't recover the perturbation
  trajectory.

  **AFE specifically hurts**: R124 + R127 (both contain AFE) collapse to 0.01
  floor; R129 (QR alone) gives 0.04 with one scenario partially surviving.
  Adding AFE *worsens* the collapse — likely the 4× action feature expansion
  (a, a², |a|, sign(a)) gives the actor more sharp boundary signals to exploit,
  causing more aggressive bang-bang policy at boundary ±1.

  **CLM-0240 paper Sec.V synthesis empirically falsified**: The R98+R125
  prototype design (CLM-0157(a)+(b)) does NOT break the plateau at 75 ep
  paper-faithful single-seed. Critic representation is NOT the binding
  constraint at this training horizon under these hyperparameters.

  **Falsified specifically**:
  - CLM-0189 (QR prototype): code passes 22/22 unit tests but at 75 ep
    yields geo 0.04 < no_control 0.104. Quantile-regression critic head
    alone insufficient to break plateau.
  - CLM-0190 (AFE prototype): code passes unit tests but at 75 ep yields
    geo 0.01 < no_control. Action feature engineering INPUT
    expansion makes things WORSE not better.
  - CLM-0234 (triple-stack): not yet trained (R131 queued, gated on WSL),
    but R127 stacked (a+b without warmh0) at 0.01 makes additivity hope
    weak. R131 still worth running for warmh0 contribution attribution.

  **What R98+CLM-0240 prediction REMAINS open**:
  - Multi-seed (R123 s54 AFE + R122 s54 QR queued by other sessions) —
    R124/R129 are seed=49 only; R56 history shows s50 collapse but
