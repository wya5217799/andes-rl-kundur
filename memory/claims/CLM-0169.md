  | γ | **median** | p25 | p75 | min | max | %neg |

  R2 obs→action (40 fits): median **+0.909**, range [−0.06, +0.99], 5%
  catastrophically negative.

  ### Reading the distribution

  - **[[CLM-0163]] R²=−0.41 at γ=0.99 was a left-tail outlier**, not the
    central tendency. The same single-fit methodology can produce R²
    anywhere in [−24.6, +0.89] depending purely on torch RNG init. The
    "obs cannot predict return" headline was overclaim.
  - **[[CLM-0168]] R²=+0.66 cross-ckpt was also a single-point reading**
    from the same noisy distribution. It is closer to the median (+0.64)
    so was less misleading, but still not a rigorous estimate.
  - **Median R²(γ=0.99) = +0.643** is the rigorous answer: obs IS
    moderately predictive of paper-γ discounted return on the SOTA
    trajectory.

  ### Partial value-horizon nuance (refines CLM-0168)

  Comparing median R² across γ:
  - γ=0   median R² = 0.91 (obs perfectly predicts instant reward)
  - γ=0.9 median R² = 0.29 (worst)
  - γ=0.99 median R² = 0.64
  - γ=1.0 median R² = 0.67

  Discounting at γ ∈ (0, 1) **does** make obs less predictive of return
  (medians 0.29-0.67 vs 0.91 at γ=0), but the effect is graded, not the
  catastrophic "negative R²" the CLM-0163 single fit suggested. γ=0.9
  is actually slightly *worse* than γ=0.99 in median — so "paper γ=0.99
  specifically" is not a uniquely pathological point. The R57-R82
  plateau being "value-horizon mismatch at γ=0.99" is **not supported**;
  what IS supported is that value prediction at any γ ∈ (0, 1) is
  harder than at γ ∈ {0, 1}, which is a generic property of finite-
  horizon discount mixing — not a paper-setup-specific pathology.

  ### Methodology lessons (carry-forward beyond R101)

  - **N_train=80 + 64×2-layer MLP + dropout = ~20-28% catastrophic fit
    rate** across γ > 0. Single-fit R² estimates are intrinsically
    unreliable here.
  - Reporting protocol for D3-style regressors: ≥ 10 MLP seeds, also
    vary train/test split, report median + IQR (NOT mean ± std, the
    outliers ruin mean).
  - Cross-validation with linear / ridge regression as a stability
    anchor — they have closed-form fits, no init noise.
  - For N=400 records spread across 4 agents (N=100 each), use much
    smaller MLP (e.g. 16 hidden, no dropout) OR collect ≥ 5× more data
    via multi-IC / multi-disturbance trajectories.
