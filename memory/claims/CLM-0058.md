statement: |
  R50-α: Anti-smoothness reward (`LAMBDA_SMOOTH=-100`, flips the
  existing r_smooth term from penalty to bonus on per-step action
  change) **catastrophically fails** at TD3 norm 75ep h=64.
  3-seed sweep (s49/50/51 same as R48-β):
  - mean 6-axis = **0.1104** (range [0.105, 0.118]) — **−67 %**
    below R48-β baseline (0.334), drops *below* the SAC attractor
    (0.137) and approaches no-control (0.094)
  - per-agent dM_span over 6s: **0.4 %** of paper (vs R48-β 9-21 %)
  - per-agent dD_span over 6s: **0.3 %** of paper (vs R48-β 11-13 %)
  - dH_util axis: 0.001 (vs baseline 0.040) — utilization collapsed
  - dD_util axis: 0.001 (vs baseline 0.100) — utilization collapsed
  - dH_smooth axis: 0.99, dD_smooth axis: 0.99 — actor is now
    near-perfectly smooth (paper smoothness target = 0)
  - settling axis: 0.00 — settled never; frequency control failed
  Mechanism (exploration-noise hijack): With λ=-100, the bonus
  on |action - previous action|² accumulates large positive reward
  whenever action varies. During training, TD3 exploration noise
  (Gaussian, σ ~ 0.1) creates per-step action variation regardless
  of the actor's deterministic output. Training reward ends up
  + 91 / agent / episode (vs baseline ≈ −2 / agent) — dominated by
  the noise-induced smoothness term, not by actor's choice. Critic
  cannot learn to distinguish a useful actor output from a useless
  one: both yield ~ identical reward via the same exploration-noise
  channel. Actor's deterministic policy collapses to a single
  setpoint per agent (span 0.3 - 0.7 % at eval, far worse than
  baseline 9-21 %). frequency control suffers catastrophically
  (max_df = 0.18 - 0.19 Hz, settling never).
  Implication: naive per-step anti-smoothness reward is unworkable
  for TD3 on V4 — the exploration-noise channel hijacks the
  learning signal. Any reward shaping for temporal action
  variation must (a) be tied to the deterministic policy output
  (not the noise-augmented action seen by the env), or (b) operate
  on a horizon longer than the per-step exploration noise scale,
  or (c) use a stochastic-policy algorithm (e.g., SAC) where the
  policy's entropy *is* the variation, removing the hijack
  pathway. CLM-0057 + CLM-0058 together close the two cheapest
  candidate levers for the temporal-flatness bottleneck.
