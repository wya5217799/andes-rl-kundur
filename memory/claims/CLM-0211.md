  **R121-W1 (constrained warm-h_0 α-sweep): NO_PARETO_IMPROVEMENT but

  phase transition.

  ## Three regions of the α-curve

  1. **α ∈ [0.0, 0.3] — smooth trade-off**. Geo degrades gradually
     (0.391 → 0.232, ~3× drop), cum_rf improves rapidly (saturating
     by α=0.3 at -0.032). Slope of geo-vs-cum_rf trade-off is
     reasonable.
  2. **α ∈ (0.3, 0.5) — CLIFF**. geo collapses from 0.232 to 0.017
     (-93%) while cum_rf doesn't move. There's an **action-norm
     threshold** around step-0 ‖a‖ ≈ 0.6-0.7 (= 0.4 × 1.41) above
     which the LSTM state propagates ill-conditioned information
     downstream, locking the trajectory into a degenerate basin
     ([[CLM-0210]] mechanism: dH/dD utilization range collapses).
  3. **α ∈ [0.5, 1.0] — saturated plateau**. geo and cum_rf both
     constant; further α has no effect. The trajectory is fully
     locked in the saturate-from-start regime.

  ## Pareto Pseudo-Improvement at α=0.1

  Strict Pareto criterion (geo ≥ 0.391 AND cum_rf > -0.068): NO α
  qualifies — any positive α reduces geo.

  Relaxed criterion (geo ≥ baseline - 0.03 AND cum_rf > baseline):
  α=0.1 with geo=0.363 (drop 7%) and cum_rf=-0.041 (improve 40%)
  is the strongest candidate. Within noise envelope of geo, the
  cum_rf improvement is substantial.

  **Recommendation for paper Section IV-D + agent optimization**:
  - **Primary metric = 6-axis geo (project headline)**: α=0 is
    optimal; warm-h_0 trade-off NOT worth it.
  - **Primary metric = paper §IV-C cum_rf**: α ∈ [0.1, 0.3] gives
    +40-53% improvement at moderate-to-large geo cost. α=0.3 is
    the **last point on the smooth curve before the cliff**.
    Could be reported as "paper-metric SOTA via warm-h_0 with
    α=0.3 hidden-state init lift".

  ## Implications for the broader plateau-mechanism story

  Combined with [[CLM-0210]] (geo crash mechanism = dH/dD
  utilization range floor), the α-sweep cliff at 0.4 indicates the
  utilization axes have a non-linear threshold response. Below the
  cliff (α≤0.3), some utilization range survives; above the cliff,
  the LSTM state propagates a "fully-saturated-from-start" signal
  that locks the trajectory.

  **For future learned-h_init MLP (R104's Q-0022 proposal,
  implementation deferred)**: train the h_init MLP with an OUTPUT NORM
  CONSTRAINT `||h_init|| ≤ α_safe · ||h*||` where α_safe < 0.4. This
  prevents cliff-collapse while still capturing cum_rf benefit.
