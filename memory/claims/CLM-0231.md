  | u=1 (default, ANDES says active) | 0.114 | 0.077 | 0.094 | -0.217 |

  cum_rf differs only 1.6% between u=1 and u=0 — within typical noise
  for the eval. Per R102 W2 plan gate:
  - < 1% → TGOV1 silently DAE-inactive (R08 V3 extends to V4)
  - 1-5% → partial / borderline
  - ≥ 5% → TGOV1 truly active

  R102 lands at **1.6% → partial / borderline** → **effectively
  inert in V4**, consistent with R08 Finding 3 "V3 governor
  completely ineffective". The JSON-level `u=1.0` flag is misleading
  — governors don't materially affect frequency dynamics in V4.

  geo difference (-3.2 pp) is dominated by LS1 axis flip from 0.114
  → 0.055; this is likely because TGOV1's small contribution helps
  damp LS1 specifically (Bus 14 load drop, area 1 disturbance) where
  governors at G1/G2 in area 1 happen to be wired close to the
  disturbance bus topologically. But the integrated cum_rf (paper §IV-C
  metric) shows no material difference.

  **Combined finding with R113/CLM-0215 (Toggler-OFF zero-action
  +0.9% max_df)**: both ANDES default extras (Toggler @ t=2s,
  TGOV1 governors @ u=1) are essentially inert in V4. R09 §2 2×
  max_df residual is NOT caused by either of these "ANDES default
  artefacts". The cause is genuinely in **F2 (load topology) +
  F3 (GENROU D=0)** + possibly D0_HETEROGENEOUS choice (R89 F1-F5
  remaining candidates).

  **Q-0021 closure decision**: closed-negative. The hypothesis
  "TGOV1 in V4 might be active and load-bearing" is falsified.
  Future work that wants to enable real governor primary frequency
  response in V4 would need to inspect TGOV1 → GENROU.Pm wiring
  (per R09 副线 plan, never executed).
