  ### Mechanism interpretation

  Actor hidden-state regularisation does not address the s49 collapse
  pathology. The remaining candidate mechanisms are:

  1. **Critic gradient signal** — perhaps the s49 initial RNG path
     produces a critic that is biased toward a saddle. QR distributional
     critic (R142) didn't break plateau, but Q-0005-specific test at
     s49 with QR has not been run.
  2. **Env exploration noise trajectory** — s49 noise sequence may
     trap the actor in a sub-region of action space where LS1 reward
     is sparse, before training can escape.
  3. **Replay buffer composition** — early episodes at s49 may produce
     a buffer that biases the early critic toward a non-recoverable
     attractor.

  hreg-doesn't-help rules out **actor-hidden-state divergence** as the
  cause (the strongest a-priori candidate per R100/CLM-0190 LSTM-drift
  story).

  ### Paper implication

  R174 SOTA 0.4139 has a real reproducibility caveat. With 3-seed test:
  - 2/3 seeds (s54, s51) → viable training, mean 0.4014
  - 1/3 seeds (s49) → collapse

  Paper Sec.IV-D must disclose this 33% collapse rate when reporting
  R174 SOTA. Cross-seed mean across viable seeds (0.4014) is the
  defensible robust number; s54 single-seed 0.4139 is the optimistic
  one. Same caveat applies to all prior single-seed s54 SOTA claims
  (R72_w4 baseline 0.391, R154 ensemble 0.4119 — both at s54 only).
