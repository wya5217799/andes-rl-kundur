statement: |
  R84-W3 (critic forensics, zero-ANDES) refines and sharpens the
  CLM-0149/CLM-0153 actor-critic decoupling finding. Three
  independent measurements on the r72_w4_lstm_tau001_warmup5_s54
  SOTA ckpt:

  (A) **Local Q curvature**: high-resolution sweep over
  [a_sota - 0.1, a_sota + 0.1] / 201 grid points / 200 prior obs /
  5-point stencil 2nd derivative. Median |d²Q/da²| ≈ **1.5 × 10⁻³**
  ≪ median |Q_sota| ≈ 6.3 × 10⁻² (action-curvature is ~2% of Q
  magnitude). Concave-fraction across 4 agents × 2 dims = **0.530**
  median (barely above 0.5 random-noise baseline). Per-agent
  d²Q/da² flips sign on ~half of obs samples — Q is essentially
  **affine in action**, not concave-around-a_sota, not boundary-
  saturating-convex; the global monotonicity from R84-W2
  sweep_viz is the action-axis projection of an affine landscape,
  not a saturated nonlinearity.

  (B) **Per-layer weight spectral norms**: 12 actor + 12 critic
  layers × 4 agents = 96 weight matrices. Median spectral norm =
  1.51, max = 3.44 (agent2.actor::lstm.weight_hh). **Zero layers**
  above the 10×-median threshold. Critic + actor weights are
  well-regularised; the monotone-Q pathology is NOT a spectral-
  explosion artifact. This **rules out** "critic regularisation"
  (CLM-0149 candidate c) as a viable R85 fix path; the issue is
  representational, not weight-magnitude.

  (C) **Trajectory-marginal-proxy obs (σ=0.77)**: re-run W2
  advantage measurement on obs ~ N(0, 0.77² I) (scale derived from
  late-training episode reward magnitudes; coarse approximation to
  SOTA on-manifold obs scale). 4-agent median advantage =
  **+0.006** ≈ unchanged from W2 N(0, 1) result (+0.0057);
  best_random − sota_Q = +0.045 ≈ unchanged. Advantage_positive
  _frac ranges 83-100% across agents. The pathology is
  **insensitive to obs scale** — N(0, 1) vs N(0, 0.77) gives the
  same critic behaviour, so obs-scale rescaling does not change
  conclusions; on-manifold ANDES-trajectory variant (Q-0018) still
  required for final closure but the obs-scale axis is exhausted.

  **Synthesised mechanism**: R72_w4 critic learned a Q(s, a)
  representation that is **essentially affine in action**, lacking
  the concave curvature around a_sota needed for the actor to
  settle into a critic-informed basin. d²Q/da² ≈ 0 means the
  policy-gradient signal carries **first-order info only**, no
  second-order ("which side of a_sota improves Q") feedback.
  Plausible upstream causes: warmup=5 (only 5 random actions before
  learning) keeps the critic's action-support narrow; tanh squash
  + target-policy smoothing keep actions concentrated; critic
  never trained on enough diverse actions to learn non-trivial
  curvature. This is a **representation-level plateau mechanism**,
  distinct from algorithm class and from spectral pathology — it
  explains why R57-R82 91-round algo sweep + Transformer + multi-
  layer LSTM all hit the same ceiling (they all share TD-based
  scalar Q regression on identical narrow action support).

  Pass criterion (plateau NOT from critic affine-Q): **FAIL**
  (d²Q/da² ≈ 0). R85+ candidate adjustment vs CLM-0149:
  (a) distributional critic (IQN / QR-style multi-quantile head)
  PROMOTED to **PRIORITY 1** — forces non-affine action-conditional
  representation; (b) action-feature engineering DEMOTED to
  follow-up; (c) critic regularisation **RULED OUT** by Part B;
  (d) wider action support (warmup ≥ 25, exploration noise schedule)
  is a new R85 sub-axis directly targeting the upstream cause.
