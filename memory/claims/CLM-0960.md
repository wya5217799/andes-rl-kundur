statement: |
  R362 is NO-NEIGHBOUR-LEARNABLE-STRUCTURE under the pre-registered
  shared-prediction gate. On the exposed sixteen-case development bank,
  replacing the one-hop neighbour snapshot messages with the frozen R341-model
  causal prediction trajectories (four-step open-loop frequency-deviation
  predictions from the two ring neighbours, twenty-three fields total) does
  not let any of the four pre-registered tuning-free non-neural map families
  (fixed affine, RBF kernel ridge, k-NN with k=5, quadratic polynomial basis)
  clear both endpoint groups after the exact physical projection: nominal
  common improvements are 0.68%, 1.15%, 1.29%, and -0.06% against the 2% gate
  while nominal differential-coordinate mean ratios worsen by 3.78x, 32.68x,
  4.85x, and 672.93x, and mismatch-bounded common improvements are -0.74%,
  -0.28%, -0.14%, and -1.48%. Every integrity check passes including the
  frozen neighbour table and prediction horizon, and the classifier reads 0
  holdout cases and 0 holdout residual labels. The surveyed information-path
  hypothesis is therefore further weakened across both its snapshot (R361)
  and prediction (R362) variants: the strongest tuning-free information
  extensions that keep the frozen map families and the frozen bank do not
  introduce surveyed learnable structure, and training, simulation, and EVAL
  remain unauthorized. This finding is limited to this one frozen prediction
  contract, the three registered non-neural families plus the fixed affine
  map, one fixed Kundur plant, two exposed operating points, and the exposed
  finite bank; it does not establish general absence or unlearnability of a
  nonlinear or neural residual, general neighbour-information insufficiency,
  the value of consensus estimation or a common-channel residual authority,
  or multi-agent value.
