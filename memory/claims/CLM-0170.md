statement: |
  **R92-W1 (multi-agent action-coordination diagnostic, zero ANDES) —
  the R72_w4 SOTA "policy" is essentially a 256-action discrete bang-bang
  controller disguised as continuous, with all 8 action variables pinned
  at ±1 by step ~15 of every episode.** This is the strongest plateau-
  mechanism evidence to date and supersedes all R84-W2/W3/R87 critic-side
  interpretations.

  Six analyses on the cached `r84_d2b_q_landscape_trajectory/per_step.json`
  trajectory (4 agents × 2 scenarios × 50 steps × `sota_action[ΔM_norm, ΔD_norm]`):

  ### A. Effort balance (CLM-0123 P_balance refinement)

  Per-agent L2 effort share: agent_0 = 24.6-24.8%, agent_1 = 24.5-24.6%,
  agent_2 = 25.5-25.6%, agent_3 = 25.1-25.2% across LS1 + LS2. CLM-0123's
  P_balance=0.96 holds at this granularity; no agent dominates.

  ### B. Inter-agent action correlation (STRUCTURAL FLAG #1)

  Pearson correlation matrices of agents' action trajectories per scenario
  × component:

  - **ΔD_norm (damping)**: r = +0.98 ~ +1.00 across **every** off-diagonal
    cell in both LS1 and LS2. All 4 agents move damping in **perfect
    lockstep** → fully redundant.
  - **ΔM_norm (inertia)**: 2×2 block structure. ag0 ↔ ag1: r = +1.00.
    ag2 ↔ ag3: r = +1.00. ag0/1 ↔ ag2/3: r = −0.98 ~ −1.00. This is the
    **Kundur 2-area physics fingerprint** — area 1 (G1, G2 = ag0, ag1)
    opposes area 2 (G3, G4 = ag2, ag3) in inertia adjustment, which is
    exactly what a power-balancing controller should do for asymmetric
    load disturbances at Bus 14 (LS1, area-1) and Bus 15 (LS2, area-2).

  ### C. Time-series visualisation (`action_timeseries.png`)

  Both scenarios show identical pattern:
  - ΔM_norm: step 0 starts near 0, step ~15 reaches **±1 saturation**
    (ag0/1 → −1, ag2/3 → +1 for LS1; **same sign pattern** for LS2).
  - ΔD_norm: step 0 starts near 0-0.2, step ~15 reaches **+1 saturation**
    on all 4 agents.
  - After step ~15, all 8 action variables stay pinned at the boundary
    for the remaining 35 steps (70% of episode).

  ### D. ΔM-vs-ΔD specialisation

  dM_share per agent (LS1 + LS2): 0.482 / 0.490 / 0.487 / 0.502. Near-
  perfect 50/50 balance — no agent specialises into "inertia head" or
  "damping head".

  ### E. Action saturation frequency (STRUCTURAL FLAG #2)

  Fraction of 50 steps where |action| > 0.95:

  | scen / agent | dM sat % | dD sat % |
  |--------------|----------|----------|
  | LS1 / ag0    | **0%**   | 76%      |
  | LS1 / ag1    | 72%      | 74%      |
  | LS1 / ag2    | 72%      | 80%      |
  | LS1 / ag3    | 76%      | 76%      |
  | LS2 / ag0    | **0%**   | 80%      |
  | LS2 / ag1    | 74%      | 78%      |
  | LS2 / ag2    | 76%      | 84%      |
  | LS2 / ag3    | 80%      | 80%      |

  Aggregate: **median saturation 76%** across all (agent, scen, component)
  cells; max 84%. Only agent_0 ΔM dodges saturation — and even that one
  hovers near −0.94 (looks at corner of timeseries plot), just below the
  0.95 threshold. **The R72_w4 SOTA policy is operating at the action-
  space boundary for ~75% of every eval episode.**

  ### F. Cross-scenario role consistency (STRUCTURAL FLAG #4 NOT triggered)

  Max effort_share delta across LS1↔LS2: 0.001 per agent (cf threshold
  0.20). Max dM_share delta: 0.001 (cf threshold 0.50). **Agents have
  identical roles in LS1 and LS2** — fully generalisable across the two
  paper-canonical disturbances. This is good news for paper writeup.

  ### Synthesised plateau mechanism (supersedes CLM-0149/0153/0154
  interpretation; refines CLM-0160 + CLM-0165 from "critic competent" to
  "policy has no remaining degrees of freedom")

  R72_w4 SOTA effectively learned an **8-dimensional bang-bang discrete
  policy** with 2^8 = 256 distinct action patterns. The deterministic
  eval picks one pattern (ag0/1 ΔM = −1, ag2/3 ΔM = +1, all ΔD = +1) and
  holds it for ~70% of the 50-step episode. The remaining policy
  expressiveness is concentrated in the **first ~15 steps of transient
  ramp**, during which the actor smoothly slides toward the boundary.

  **The 0.391 plateau therefore reflects two combined ceilings**:

  1. **Action-space saturation ceiling**: once all 8 vars pin to ±1,
     the policy has no remaining degree of freedom. Subsequent system
     response is fully determined by env dynamics + disturbance,
     independent of further policy improvement. R57-R82 91 round of
     algo / hyper / arch sweeps **cannot exceed this ceiling** because
     they all share the same DM_MIN/DM_MAX/DD_MIN/DD_MAX bounds and
     all converge to the same saturated bang-bang sign pattern.
  2. **Bang-bang quantisation ceiling**: even within the 256-pattern
     discrete space, the SOTA chooses the area-symmetry-consistent
     pattern; the remaining 255 are physically worse. This is the
     "right" choice; the plateau is at the optimum of the discrete
     space, not above it.

  ### R93+ priority (locked, supersedes CLM-0160 + CLM-0165 ordering)

  - 🆕 **PRIORITY 1 — Widen action bounds**: increase DM_MAX / DD_MAX
    (or remove tanh) so the policy regains continuous expressiveness.
    Predicted lift: if 70% of eval is currently in boundary, a 2-3×
    bound widening should let the policy continue ramping during the
    decaying phase and produce smoother frequency recovery. Single
    V4Config change, single training run. Sole highest-EV experiment.
  - 🆕 **PRIORITY 2 — CTDE with shared ΔD head**: 4 redundant
    ΔD controllers waste capacity. A shared scalar ΔD policy + 4
    area-specialised ΔM heads is structurally parsimonious AND
    matches R92-W1 axis-B data. R82-(b) candidate now has its data.
  - ✅ D3 obs sufficiency (CLM-0160 priority) — DEMOTED to follow-up;
    obs info content is moot if the policy is action-bound limited.
  - ❌ Distributional critic / wider warmup / spectral regularisation
    (CLM-0149/0153/0154 candidates) — finally ruled out by 3 layers
    of data (CLM-0160 + CLM-0165 + CLM-0170).

  ### Caveats

  - Single 1-rollout × 2 scenario × 50 step trajectory. The bang-bang
    pattern needs replication across more seeds / disturbance variants
    to confirm it's a universal R72_w4 attractor and not single-seed
    luck. (R93+ task: re-rollout with N=10 seeds × 2 scen.)
  - "Action bound is too narrow" is **not directly verifiable** from
    R92-W1 — it's the inferred root cause, falsifiable only by a
    widen-bound training run.
  - CLM-0123 P_balance=0.96 is consistent with R92-W1 but did not
    surface the saturation finding; the paper writeup must explicitly
    mention saturation 76% if it makes claims about "balanced control".
