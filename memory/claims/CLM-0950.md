  R360 is NO-NEIGHBOUR-LEARNABLE-STRUCTURE under the pre-registered flexible
  non-neural gate. On the exposed sixteen-case development bank, all three
  pre-registered tuning-free map families (RBF kernel ridge, k-NN with k=5,
  quadratic polynomial basis) fail both the nominal and mismatch-bounded
  endpoint groups after the exact physical projection: nominal common
  improvements are 0.92%, 0.89%, and 1.11% against the 2% gate while nominal
  differential-coordinate mean ratios worsen by 14.56x, 5.80x, and 36.93x,
  and mismatch-bounded common improvements are -0.50%, -0.54%, and -0.32%.
  Every integrity check passes and the classifier reads 0 holdout cases and 0
  holdout residual labels. The R359 negative result is therefore not specific
  to the affine class within this family scope; the registered stop fires and
  the learning route terminates with training, simulation, and EVAL
  unauthorized. This finding is limited to the three registered non-neural
  maps, one fixed Kundur plant, two exposed operating points, and the exposed
  finite bank; it does not establish general absence or unlearnability of a
  nonlinear or neural residual, general neighbour-information insufficiency,
  or multi-agent value.
