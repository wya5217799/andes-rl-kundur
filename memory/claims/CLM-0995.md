  GENCLS VSG proxies. Its actions, outcomes, claims, and actor semantics do
  not become per-VSG title evidence by relabelling. R370 creates no physical,
  comparative, learning, stability, safety, generalization, novelty, or
  publication-readiness result and authorizes no implementation, simulation,
  or training.
