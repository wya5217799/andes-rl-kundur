  🛑 **Q-0020 transient-phase replay reweighting at boost=3.0 REFUTED**.
  R172 trained td3_lstm at R72_w4 hyper (lr=1e-4 clamp, tau=0.001,
  warmup=5) on s54 with sequence-replay-buffer start-index weighting
  3× on [0,6) (i.e. step-0..5 transitions of each episode oversampled).
  Result: geo=**0.3877**, LS1=0.353, LS2=0.426, cum_rf=-0.068. Δ vs
  R72_w4 baseline 0.391: **-0.85%** (within eval noise, marginally
  worse). The transient-phase reweighting hypothesis does NOT break
  the 0.391 plateau.

  ### Result table

  | Run | hyper | LS1 | LS2 | geo | Δ baseline |
  |-----|-------|-----|-----|-----|------------|
  | R72_w4 baseline | unweighted uniform | 0.354 | 0.431 | 0.391 | — |
  | **R172** | boost=3, window=[0,6) | 0.353 | 0.426 | **0.3877** | **-0.85%** |

  Compare to single-policy lifters in same R72_w4 hyper family:
  - R170 (hreg λ=0.003): geo 0.4091 (+4.7%)
  - R174 (hreg λ=0.002): geo 0.4139 (+5.9%, single-policy SOTA)
  - R154 ensemble 4-way: 0.4119 (+5.4%, ref ensemble)

  ### Mechanism interpretation

  step-0..5 transitions are the same window where the LSTM hidden
  state is least informed (just `h_0=0` initial, no observation
  history yet). Oversampling them concentrates gradient signal on
  *under-determined* samples — more iteration on noisy targets.
  Plausible weak-harm mechanism, consistent with the observed -0.85%.
  No reason to expect boost=2 or boost=5 to qualitatively reverse this.

  ### Q-0020 closure

  Q-0020 was opened at R88 by CLM-0162 (R86 monotone-Q finding extending
  to the proposal for transient reweighting as one of three candidate
  fixes). R172 directly tested the hypothesis and refutes it. Q-0020
  → closed-negative.

  All preserved in repo for future re-testing; default boost=1.0 keeps
  pre-R172 behaviour exactly.
