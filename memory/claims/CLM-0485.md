  ### What this says (with R256 + R257 context)

  Three probes converge:

  1. **R256 / CLM-0470 (saturation)**: RL action pinned at +600 bound
     22-92% of trajectory; droop never saturates.
  2. **R257 / CLM-0475 (smoothness)**: RL is 3× smoother (lower TV)
     than droop on dD jitter; droop k=10 is jitteriest.
  3. **R259 / CLM-0485 (coupling)**: droop action LINEARLY TRACKS
     disturbance magnitude (corr +0.79 at k=0); RL action is
     decoupled or anti-correlated.

  Combined picture:
  - **RL**: saturated + smooth + decoupled = "max-out then hold,
    don't titrate to disturbance." Maximum-inertia, maximum-damping
    response that doesn't modulate as disturbance evolves.
  - **Droop k=10**: not saturated + jittery + linearly coupled =
    "instant proportional reaction." Action follows |Δω| in
    real-time, leading to noisier but synchronisation-tighter
    trajectory.

  Negative corr for R201 SOTA at k=-2 (-0.478) tells the story:
  when |Δω| was small 2 steps ago, |action| was LARGE — i.e., the
  actor saturates immediately at disturbance onset, then holds
  constant. As |Δω| later peaks, |action| has already settled
  high and slightly decreases (sign-change components cancelling).
  This is **not** lag — it's decoupling: RL responds ONCE at
  disturbance onset and stops tracking.

  ### Combined mechanism narrative (R256+R257+R259 final)

  > "The RL-vs-droop cum_rf gap (CLM-0445) is driven by a single
  > policy-class inductive bias: TD3+LSTM with tanh-projected
  > per-agent actor learns a 'fire once, then hold' strategy —
  > saturate actions at disturbance onset, then hold saturated
  > (no further tracking of Δω). This produces smooth (R257) but
  > decoupled (R259) action trajectories that achieve max-inertia
  > damping but leave residual sync error. Classical droop, by
  > construction, modulates action proportionally to instantaneous
  > Δω — jittery (R257) but tightly coupled (R259), producing
  > smaller residual sync error. The 'better' policy class for
  > cum_rf is the structurally titrated one (droop or RL with
  > linear-output actor / action-magnitude regularisation /
  > hybrid warm-start). Three converging probes (R256/R257/R259)
  > narrow the paper Sec.IV-D mechanism explanation to a single
  > root cause with three orthogonal empirical signatures."

  ### Probe-first protocol third validation

  Total cost across R255+R256+R257+R259 probes: ~2 hr (4 × 30 min).
  Counterfactual without probes: at least 3 env-modifying rounds
  (global r_f scope; widebound retrain; CTDE architecture change)
  + V4 regression risk. **Saved ~8-12 hr + V4 contract risk**
  across 4 probes.

  R258 (sync-only RL, currently training in autonomous loop) is
  the FIRST training-round candidate that probe-first didn't
  refute — it tests whether mirroring droop's reward philosophy
  (phi_f=100, phi_abs=0) closes the cum_rf gap. Outcome will
  inform paper 7th contribution direction.

  ### What R259 does NOT resolve

  Three policy-class candidates remain (paper 7th contribution
  candidates):
  - Linear/soft actor output (no tanh saturation)
  - Action magnitude regularization (-λ|action|² in reward)
  - Hybrid warm-start from droop k=10

  R258 tests a 4th approach (mirror droop's reward setup with
  current RL architecture). If R258 still saturates / decouples,
  the architecture-change candidates remain valid future work.
