  - Overall: `td3_norm_h64_s50` (V4 historical) = **-0.196**
  - Best LSTM: `td3_lstm_h64_warmup5_s51` = -0.284 (same ckpt as R57
    6-axis SOTA s51 = 0.543)
  - Best SAC: `r58_paper_strict_pure_radsec_sac_s50` = -0.397

  ### Anchor LS1/LS2 (paper-comparable scenarios)

  Best LSTM s51 warmup: LS1 = -0.053, LS2 = -0.035 — 13-15× tighter
  than paper's DDIC (LS1=-0.68, LS2=-0.52). Cause unverified
  (Q-0009 opened); likely disturbance distribution differs in
  magnitude from paper §IV-A's "load + wind capacity range".

  ### Three big findings

  **F1 — R56/R57 ranking does NOT preserve under paper metric**:
  - 6-axis (R56/R57): TD3+LSTM > TD3 > SAC
  - paper metric (V4 historical Hz): TD3 > TD3+LSTM > SAC
  - paper metric (strict-radsec): SAC > TD3+LSTM > TD3
  → CLM-0067 production recommendation needs scope split (see CLM-0072).

  **F2 — PHI_ABS=50 is a "free lunch" on paper metric**:
  V4 td3_norm mean -0.267 vs strict_rescaled_td3 (same config only
  PHI_ABS=0) -0.578 → **2× better with PHI_ABS=50**. The project's
  R18 engineering hack incidentally optimises paper's metric: PHI_ABS
  forces frequencies toward 50 Hz, which synchronises them as a
  side effect. Removing PHI_ABS to be "more paper-faithful" hurts
  paper-metric performance.

  **F3 — Paper's SAC choice is empirically validated under strict-radsec**:
  Under paper-faithful conditions (rad/s, PHI_ABS=0, PHI=1.0), SAC
  wins (-0.518). Pre-R58 "SAC < TD3" claim was a Hz-units +
  PHI_ABS-restoration compound artifact, not an intrinsic algorithm
  ranking.

  ### Caveats

  - All numbers at 75 ep training horizon. Paper uses 500-ep. Sanity
    run on s51 _pure 500-ep in progress (CLM-0073 if completes).
  - paper_strict_pure SAC/TD3 ckpts likely have untrained `best.pt`
    (R57 mechanism: best saved during random env-warmup before
    training begins). Their -0.6 to -0.9 totals reflect untrained
    policy, not a converged comparison.
  - 20-scen test set is deterministic (seed=2026) but distribution
    may differ from paper's 50-scen by magnitude (Q-0009).
