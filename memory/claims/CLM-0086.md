  **Winner: N_SUBSTEPS=3** — paper-metric +3.6 % AND train wall halved
  (7.5 min vs baseline 14 min).

  ### Mechanism

  ODE integration precision affects ANDES TDS dynamics. Eval uses
  N_SUBSTEPS=5 (project standard, paper-aligned). Training with:
  - N_SUBSTEPS=1: Euler step, very coarse → policy learns wrong
    dynamics, transfers poorly to N_SUBSTEPS=5 eval (-41 %)
  - N_SUBSTEPS=10: very fine, smoothed dynamics → policy overfits
    to smoothness, mismatches N_SUBSTEPS=5 eval (-5 %)
  - **N_SUBSTEPS=3**: enough precision for stable training, close
    enough to eval N_SUBSTEPS=5 to transfer well (+3.6 %)

  Sweet spot below baseline → potential **free win on all future
  training**: faster + better.

  ### Caveat

  Eval N_SUBSTEPS=5 is the apples-to-apples convention. If we
  changed eval to N_SUBSTEPS=3 too, the comparison would be different.
  Single-seed result; 3-seed verify in combo (CLM-0089).
