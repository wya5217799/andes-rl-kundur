  baseline (0.391)** despite using strictly fewer reward terms.

  | Algo   | phi_abs | phi_h | phi_d | phi_f | geo    | Δ vs full   | round |
  |--------|---------|-------|-------|-------|--------|-------------|-------|

  **Pattern is now algorithm-independent**:
  - phi_abs present + paper terms zeroed → near-SOTA on BOTH algos
  - phi_abs absent (paper-strict or otherwise) → collapse on BOTH

  ### Paper-Sec.IV-D contribution 5 (now universal)

  R238 established the paper-Eq.14-vestigial finding under hreg only,
  leaving an alternative narrative ("hreg's strong regularisation
  masks paper terms, scalar would need them"). R239 refutes that
  alternative: scalar shows the same pattern. The reward
  reproducibility gap is therefore a property of **the V4 ANDES Kundur
  reward landscape itself**, not of any particular regulariser.

  ### Why this strengthens the paper

  This converts a finding that could be hand-waved as
  "algorithm-specific quirk of hreg" into a substantive,
  reproducible, algorithm-agnostic methodological claim. It is
  arguably the cleanest paper-integrity finding the project has
  produced: same env, same scenarios, two distinct algorithms, both
  show paper Eq.14 reward function adds nothing.

  ### Minor counter-note

  R239 (scalar only-phi_abs, 0.3954) is slightly **better** than
  scalar full reward (R72_w4, 0.391). This is within noise (~1%) but
  is consistent: removing paper terms does not hurt, and may slightly
  help (phi_f noise reduction). The paper claim should state "no
  effect (within 1.1% noise band)" rather than "strict improvement".
