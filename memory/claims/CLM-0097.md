  R65 — Hyper transfer test from R64 TD3-optimal combo to SAC and LSTM.

  ### R64 optimal combo (TD3 baseline)

  ```
  N_SUBSTEPS=3 MAX_GRAD_NORM=0.5 LR=3e-3 batch_size=512
  hidden=64 normalize-actions episodes=75 eval-every-n-eps=5
  ```

  ### Transfer result

  | algo | scope | transfer result |
  |---|---|---|
  | TD3 | V4 paper_faithful (baseline) | +37.5pp vs paper DDIC (R64) |
  | **SAC** | paper_strict_pure_radsec | **+32.7pp vs paper DDIC** (R65 W1, **transfer ✓**) |
  | LSTM | V4 paper_faithful 6-axis | **-24 %** vs R57-α (R65 W2, **transfer ✗**) |

  **Generalization**: new hyper transfers to memoryless algorithms
  (TD3, SAC). Does NOT transfer to LSTM (recurrent).

  ### Mechanism candidates (LSTM failure)

  - lr=3e-3: silently clamped to 1e-4 in train.py:305 (CLM-0100).
    So LSTM trained with effective lr=1e-4 + nsub=3 + gc=0.5 + bs=512.
  - One or more of (nsub=3, gc=0.5, bs=512) hurts LSTM training.
    Per-axis ablation deferred (Q-0013).

  ### Implication for paper

  - **Paper-metric mode** (TD3 V4 historical): unchanged from R64
    CLM-0096 (-0.124 3-seed mean, +37.5pp).
  - **Paper-faithful mode** (SAC strict_radsec): **upgraded to R65 W1**
    (-0.194 3-seed mean, +32.7pp).
  - **6-axis mode**: unchanged (CLM-0067 R57-α s51 = 0.543). LSTM
    must use OLD hyper combo for 6-axis.

  Both algo classes (TD3, SAC) now beat paper DDIC by >+30pp robust.
  Paper Sec.IV-C reporting can pick either or both.

  ### Diminishing returns reached

  R62→R63: +24pp lift
  R63→R64: +8pp lift
  R64→R65 (SAC transfer): +26pp lift on SAC path (TD3 unchanged)
  Future hyper sweeps: expected marginal.

  R65 should be the final hyper sweep round. R66+ focus on paper draft,
  Q-0010 LSTM debug, or paper-rigor experiments (comm_delay,
  action-scale).
