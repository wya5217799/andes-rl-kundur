statement: |
  R265 evaluated frozen R201, droop k=10, static alpha=0.25, and the R264
  cap-0.25 mode-ratio gate on 20 prospectively generated no-anchor load
  disturbances. The materialised bank was sealed before evaluation at
  SHA-256 68816647eca8c0ccabe847ec883eaf59676c7afc915c09081a7851bf1e2dfae0.
  All 80 real-ANDES traces completed 150/150 steps with no TDS failure or
  runner error. Each controller's observed failure rate was 0/20, whose
  two-sided 95% exact Clopper-Pearson upper bound is still 16.84%.

  Relative to static alpha=0.25, the gate improved both pre-registered
  co-primary mean losses: physical VSG-mean IAE changed -4.3633%
  (paired percentile-bootstrap 95% interval [-5.3689%, -3.5520%]) and
  physical normalized synchronization loss changed -1.8624%
  ([-2.2532%, -1.2188%]). IAE improved in 20/20 scenarios. Normalized
  synchronization loss improved in only 9/20 and had a +0.1442% median
  paired effect, so its mean gain is heterogeneous rather than a uniform
  scenario-level win.

  Physical safety/tail guards passed: worst-bus peak and maximum sampled
  RoCoF mean effects were -1.0103% and -0.5066%; their descriptive empirical
  CVaR90 effects were -1.2501% and -0.7405%, and their bank-worst effects
  were -1.2205% and -0.8899%. All gate/static trajectories settled and had
  zero action saturation.

  The pre-registered overall replication nevertheless closes NEGATIVE because
  the action-variation guard failed decisively. Gate action L1 rose 15.2002%,
  mean action total variation rose 236.6691% (95% interval
  [180.3890%, 299.5733%]), all 20 scenarios were worse on action variation,
  and descriptive action-TV CVaR90 rose 448.0015% versus the allowed 25%.
  Thus the frozen hand-designed gate reproduces small physical benefits on
  this bank but is not an acceptable controller mechanism as specified.
  This is legacy-checkpoint mechanism evidence, not corrected-recurrent,
  training-seed, topology, stability, or publication-level superiority.
