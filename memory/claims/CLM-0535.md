statement: |
  R267 prospectively fixed one alpha-only symmetric slew limiter at
  delta_alpha_max=0.02895 per 0.2-s control step from the archived Kundur
  inter-area mode frequency (0.579 Hz), before generating a new 20-scenario
  no-anchor bank.  The bank and evaluation seal were frozen at SHA-256
  3e0cdf16758f6e3adcfeb336c891dce94340c0ee96e759783d1b5fcbcc4e2bd5
  and 4eb055838a133ecceb5817bb312f229f90d4e2f3c7cc3811515c313f627b0241.

  All 60 requested real-ANDES trajectories were attempted.  Static
  alpha=0.25, the raw mode gate, and the slew-limited gate each completed
  18/20 scenarios.  The same two positive Bus14 disturbances failed for all
  three controllers (random_01 at +2.9946 and random_18 at +2.7000);
  paired completion had 18 both-success, two both-failure, and zero
  discordant scenarios.  The pre-registered paired bootstrap was correctly
  withheld because failed rows were not dropped.

  The prospective result is NEGATIVE.  On the 18 shared complete scenarios,
  retained only as descriptive mechanism evidence, slew versus static changed
  VSG-mean IAE by -4.2214% but normalized synchronization loss by +11.2225%.
  Mean action total variation rose 140.2812% and descriptive CVaR90 rose
  201.2006% versus static.  Slew did reduce mean action total variation by
  38.5164% relative to the raw gate, so the limiter smoothed its intended
  switching mechanism, but not enough to satisfy the static action guard and
  not while retaining both physical directions.  Per the prospective kill
  rule, Q-0029 and the hand-designed selector/smoother family close negative.
  These results are legacy-checkpoint mechanism evidence, not corrected
  residual training, topology generalisation, or stability evidence.
