  ## Mechanism interpretation

  `paper_strict_pure` (R58 / ADR-0002 infrastructure) sets PHI_H = PHI_D
  = **1.0** (vs paper_faithful 0.0056). The action H/D penalty becomes
  ~180× larger per step. Combined with R72_w4 hyper (warmup=5,
  normalize-actions, td3_lstm), the policy gradient is dominated by the
  action-penalty term and collapses to **near-zero action across both
  scenarios** — no control, no damping, frequency oscillates freely,
  6-axis goes to floor.

  This is consistent with paper Eq.14 + paper §IV-D conceptual
  arguments **but** demonstrates that the project's empirical SOTA
  basin (R72_w4 at 0.391) depends critically on the non-paper PHI_ABS=50
  term plus the R18-era PHI_H/D rescale to 0.0056. Without that pair,
  the LSTM TD3 RL pipeline collapses to no-control under canonical paper
  reward.

  ## Implications

  1. **PHI_ABS=50 / 0.0056 rescale is part of the SOTA recipe** —
     not a tunable hyperparameter. This is a paper-honesty point that
     should be stated in Section IV-A reward definition.
  2. [[CLM-0168]] retraction of value-horizon-mismatch hypothesis stands
     — but this finding ALSO refutes "paper reward is suboptimal for
     6-axis". paper_strict_pure with R72 hyper is *worse*, not better.
     R85+ reward exploration should perturb PHI_ABS/PHI_H/D only in
     small bands, not switch to strict-pure.
  3. paper-faithful-modified reward (V4Config.paper_faithful) is
     empirically validated as the right reward family for the SOTA;
     CLM-0200 synthesis is updated: reward shape is "open candidate"
     → "load-bearing recipe, perturbation has narrow safe window".

  ## R85+ candidates updated
