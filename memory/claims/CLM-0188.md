  showed warm-h_0 gradient-ascent unlocks step-0 ||a|| from 10.4% to
  99.5% of max + Q +58% on the R72_w4 SOTA (4 agents). R104 replicates
  on 9 TD3-LSTM ckpts spanning R58 / R62 / R72 rounds × 7 seeds ×
  hidden = 64 and 128:

  | ckpt | norm 0% | norm * | ΔQ_abs | feasible |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | r72_w4_lstm_s54 SOTA | 11.8% | 99.6% | +0.041 | ✅ |

  | r58_lstm_s50 | 6.5-11.5% | 72-97% | +0.016 | ✅ |

  | r72_w1_lstm_s51 | 5.3-9.1% | 78-99% | +0.019 | ✅ |
  | r72_w2_lstm_s50 | 6.4-11.0% | 85-97% | +0.007 | ✅ |
  | r72_w3_lstm_s52 | 1.7-9.6% | 71-96% | +0.009 | ✅ |
  | r72_w5_lstm_s55 | 6.3-17.8% | 90-99.7% | +0.042 | ✅ |

  Cross-ckpt aggregate (median across 9 ckpts × 4 agents = 36 LSTM critics):
  - **norm_zero = 8.5%** of max (uniformly under-saturated at step 0)
  - **norm_star = 95.6%** of max
  - **norm_lift = +86.8 percentage points**
  - ΔQ_abs = +0.017 (always positive sign, range +0.005 to +0.065
    across ckpts; relative% suppressed because |Q_zero| varies)
  - Feasibility gate (norm_lift > 50 pp AND ΔQ_abs > 0):
    **9/9 = 100% PASS**

  Notable observations:
  - The h=128 ckpt (r62) needs smaller ||h*|| ≈ 10 to reach saturation
    than h=64 ckpts (||h*|| ≈ 25-37). Higher-capacity LSTMs distribute
    activation across more dims, easier to learn-h_0 in low-norm region.
  - Every individual agent (36/36) has ΔQ_abs > 0 — universal sign.
    The 4-7 agents that fall short of 95% norm_star (down to 71-72%)
    still get into the upper tail of the action distribution.
  - The R99 +254% Q gain outlier (agent 1, |Q_zero|=0.022) reproduces
    here (r58_s50 ag3 = +190%, r72_w5 ag0 = +176%, r62 ag0 = +407%);
    the relative % blows up when |Q_zero| is small; the ABSOLUTE
    lifts (ΔQ_abs +0.027 to +0.065) are the robust statistic.

  **Mechanism reading**: CLM-0174 / CLM-0183's "step-0 ||a||≈0.15
  because LSTM warms up from h=0" is now confirmed UNIVERSAL across
  TD3-LSTM ckpts trained in this codebase on the paper-faithful 7-dim
  obs. This is the architectural twin of CLM-0155's "synthetic-obs
  monotone-Q is universal" finding — both prior-obs forensics show
  the bottleneck is not ckpt-specific basin lottery but a property of
  the (LSTM actor + 7-dim obs) training regime.

  **Q-0022 implementation priority promoted to UNCONDITIONAL R96**:
  - No longer gated on R94 widen-bound outcome (R99 already decoupled
    this)
  - No longer gated on N=1 architectural feasibility (R104 = N=9 ckpts)
  - Implementation is `nn.Linear(obs_dim, 32) → tanh → nn.Linear(32,
    hidden)` for h_init AND c_init heads (2 small MLPs ≈ 4K extra
    params). 15-line diff in `networks.py::RecurrentActor`.
  - Training run: 1 seed × 75 ep R72_w4 hyper × paper-faithful obs.
    Expected ||a||_step_0 lift from 0.15 → 0.7+ (gate; 100% saturation
    would be optimal but learned MLP may not match grad-ascent argmax).

  Caveat (still applies): synthetic step-0-like obs ||obs||=0.25 random
  direction. The 9/9 universality result strengthens but does not
  remove this caveat. R96 training run is on REAL obs and provides the
  on-manifold validation.
