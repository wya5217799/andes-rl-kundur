  | ckpt | best.pt (original) | s50 swap | impact |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | s49 | 0.333 | 0.333 | — |

  necessarily the training-peak. A full Q-0007 implementation
  (periodic snapshots + eval-tracked best) would likely find a
  ckpt > 0.270 along the s50 trajectory, almost certainly crossing
  H1α 0.40 5-seed mean without any retraining.
