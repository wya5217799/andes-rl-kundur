statement: |
  **R111-W1 (cross-algo-class step-0 ||a|| control, zero ANDES)**.
  CLM-0193 (R107-W2) showed LSTM ||a||(h=0) = 10.4% of max regardless
  of ||obs||. R111 tests whether this saturation deficit is LSTM-specific
  or holds across actor architectures. Compared **6 R86 ckpts spanning
  4 independent training-config families** (r72_w4_lstm_s54;
  r58_paper_strict_pure {td3_lstm s49+s50 sister-seeds, td3_mlp s49,
  sac_mlp s49}; r63_w4_td3_combo s49) on identical step-0-like
  synthetic obs (||obs||=0.25, N=100), reporting tanh-bounded action
  magnitude:

  | Ckpt | algo class | ||a||_med | % of max |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | r72_w4_lstm_s54 | td3_lstm | 0.142 | **10.1%** |
  | r58_lstm_s49 | td3_lstm | 0.080 | 5.6% |
  | r58_lstm_s50 | td3_lstm | 0.120 | 8.5% |
  | r58_td3_mlp_s49 | td3_mlp | 0.209 | 14.8% |
  | r58_sac_mlp_s49 | sac_mlp | 0.096 | 6.8% |
  | r63_td3_mlp_s49 | td3_mlp | 0.925 | **65.4%** |

  Reading: **5 of 6 ckpts have step-0 ||a|| ≤ 15% of max** —
  saturation deficit is **NOT LSTM-specific**. SAC MLP (6.8%) and
  TD3 MLP r58 (14.8%) are similarly under-saturated. The only exception
  is r63_w4_td3_combo_s49 at 65.4%.

  **Aggregation note**: the script's own pre-registered H1/H0 test
  (gap > 20 pp = "LSTM-specific") returns the verdict line
  `"H0 (similar): LSTM=8.5% ~ non-LSTM=23.4% (gap 14.9pp)"`. That
  test medians over a non-LSTM pool of two algo classes, one of
  which (td3_mlp) is itself dominated by the r63 outlier — so the
  H1/H0 framing is brittle. The per-ckpt 5/6 ≤ 15 % observation
  above is the load-bearing reading; the 14.9 pp aggregate gap is
  documented but not used as primary evidence. See [[CLM-0204]]
  for the env-side refutation of the warm-h_0 architectural-lift
  prediction — the saturation deficit being near-universal on
  synthetic obs does NOT translate into a universal env-side fix.

  Notable: r63 is TD3-MLP trained with R63 hyper-combo
  (batch_size=512, n_substeps=3, max_grad_norm=0.5, CLM-0086/0087/0088).
  Its higher step-0 saturation is real, but its **6-axis geo did NOT
  exceed 0.391** — saturating at step 0 is necessary but not sufficient
  for breaking the plateau. The r63 outlier is consistent with "early
  saturation enables but does not guarantee better policy" rather than
  "r63 broke the mechanism we're tracking".

  **Mechanism reframing (refines CLM-0174 / CLM-0193)**: the actor
  step-0 saturation deficit is **near-universal across actor
  architectures trained on paper-faithful 7-dim obs**, not specific to
  LSTM. R83-R86 cross-ckpt forensics (R86 CLM-0155) found this same
  near-universality for critic monotone-Q on synthetic obs. The two
  findings stack into one coherent observation: **the paper-faithful
  7-dim obs alone is insufficient to produce saturated step-0
  output across any actor class trained by TD-based RL in this codebase**.

  **Implication for R96 design**: warm-h_0 (Q-0022) is the LSTM-specific
  fix for this universal problem. For SAC / TD3-MLP, the analogous fix
  would be MLP first-layer bias initialised toward saturating output —
  but this is out-of-scope for R96. R96 stays focused on warm-h_0 for
  td3_lstm. If R96 works, the same step-0 saturation deficit in SAC /
  TD3-MLP (their R57+ training never produced > 0.391 either) would
  also be addressable by an analogous "actor-output-warmth" knob.

  Caveat: synthetic obs ||obs||=0.25 with random direction. Cached
  ANDES step-0 obs may have a specific structure where some MLPs DO
  saturate (r63 is suggestive). Per-direction analysis pending.
  [[CLM-0183]] / [[CLM-0188]] caveat (synthetic obs ≠ on-manifold)
  inherited; [[CLM-0204]] env-side refutation of the warm-h_0 lift
  prediction reinforces that this synthetic-obs N=6 universality
  finding is **phenomenological only** — it does not license a
  universal mechanism claim across actor classes.
