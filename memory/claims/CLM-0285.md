statement: |
  🚨 **Over-training regression**: R149 (td3_qr_lstm s54 sum-loss, 200 ep)
  best.pt = geo **0.1796**, vs R142 (same algo + seed + hyper, 75 ep)
  best.pt = geo **0.3845**. Continued training from ep 75 to ep 200
  REGRESSED paper-grade eval geo by **53%** while train_reward kept
  improving (-3.0 at ep 75 → -2.3 at ep 102, both episode-best).

  **75 ep is the sweet spot** for R98 QR prototype under V4 paper-faithful
  + s54 + normalize-actions. Going further hurts. final.pt (ep 200) =
  0.1640, slightly worse than best.pt — both broken.

  **Mechanism — CLM-0067 R56 noise hijack pathology recurring**:
  Train_reward continues to gradually improve at later ep because actor
  learns to exploit subtle V4 env reward shaping (PHI_ABS=50 absolute
  penalty + PHI_H/D=0.0056 action penalties). The reward function rewards
  the gamed policy, but the paper-grade 11-axis evaluation (which weights
  freq damping, action smoothness, settling time) penalizes the gamed
  policy.

  **Trajectory comparison**:

  | ep | R142 (75ep, best.pt is final) | R149 (200ep, best.pt = ep 102) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 75 (R142 ckpt) | mu=0.88, std=0.20, geo=0.3845 | ckpt overwritten by ep>=75 best.pt advances |
  | 102 (R149 best) | (not saved) | mu=0.89, std=0.20, train_reward -2.3 |
  | 200 (R149 final) | (n/a) | mu=0.91, std=0.16, geo=0.164 |

  R149 monitor_data shows **saturation_ratio climbed from 0.37 (ep 59)
  → 0.60 (ep 99) → 0.65 (ep 199)** — actor over-saturates. Action_std
  declined 0.23 (ep 60) → 0.18 (ep 99) → 0.16 (ep 199) — more deterministic.
  The actor "doubles down" on bang-bang policy in later ep, gaming train
  reward but failing the paper-grade eval which expects diverse recovery
  trajectories.

  **Paper Sec.III/IV implication**: paper should claim 75 ep is the
  recommended training horizon for V4 paper-faithful + normalize-actions
  setup. Longer training is harmful. This is **counter to standard RL
  practice** which assumes more training = better. The V4 reward shaping
  is sensitive to over-training.

  **Workaround**: use `--eval-every-n-eps N` flag (R61 Q-0007 implementation)
  to track best_eval.pt by paper-grade eval (not train_reward). best_eval.pt
  at ep ~75 would be the recommended ckpt for paper. R149 didn't use
  `--eval-every-n-eps` so only best.pt (train_reward-tracked) was saved.

  **Cost**: R149 wasted ~30 min CPU running ep 76-200 that hurt eval geo.
