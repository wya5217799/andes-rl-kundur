# Context

R58 plan called for an s51 _pure 500-ep sanity to test whether the
75-ep "LSTM diverges-or-doesn't" picture persists at paper convergence
horizon. The 75-ep evidence (R58 main verdict) showed LSTM converged
3/3 in `paper_strict_pure (Hz)` while SAC and TD3-MLP both diverged
3/3. The hypothesis to falsify: maybe 75 ep is too short to see
late-onset LSTM divergence.

500-ep sanity refutes the late-onset-divergence hypothesis. The LSTM
critic loss monotonically decreases for the full run, reward improves
from -66 to -26, and TDS failure rate stays at 1.2 %. No divergence
indicator at any horizon.

But the paper-metric eval is **bit-identical** to the 75-ep variant.
This is structurally informative: the `best.pt` selection rule
(best-by-train-reward, R57 monitor convention) locks the checkpoint
early, and no further training improves it. The training stability
finding (R58 main verdict) is real; the saved-checkpoint paper-metric
performance is **separately bottlenecked** by Q-0007.

This claim is the cleanest empirical demonstration of Q-0007's
practical impact: longer training is wasted work without a better
checkpoint-selection rule.
