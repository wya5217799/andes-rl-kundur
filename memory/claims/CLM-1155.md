statement: |
  The sealed R402 Gate A canary bank returns CANARY-FAIL on the fresh eight-profile fixed-connectivity ANDES formulation: all nine training runs and all 264 evaluation records are complete and valid, yet every one of the 36 arm-seed-profile learning blocks violates the registered no-harm and action-stress budgets versus the strong deterministic reference (worst ratios: common-frequency integral 1.48, worst-unit peak 3.18, RoCoF 7.38, action RMS 5.76, total variation 8.35 against limits 1.03 and 1.10). The three-seed medians of the learning arms degrade both registered decoupling endpoints by 4.12x/4.16x/5.09x on off-diagonal energy and 2.92x/3.13x/3.30x on differential energy, and message-enabled CD-MATD3 shows a negative seed-median increment versus the matched no-message arm (-22.3%, -5.5%). Reward values never entered the gate. The learner route stops without algorithm replacement; no Gate B is authorized, and R402 supports none of the title terms. This is a bounded formulation result, not a rejection of MARL or direct-M/D learning in general.
