statement: |
  R371 returns ENERGY-PORT-DESIGN-PASS for a static, source-bound successor
  contract on the fixed-title line. Four runtime actors map one-to-one to the
  four governor-free GENCLS VSG indices and to four independent SynGen.pref
  writes; the wrapper passes exact zero actions through the stopped M/D path
  and does not instantiate or act on the independent ESD1 storage object.

  The action is an incremental system-base active-power request. After the
  registered energy projection, each command is converted to mechanical
  torque by sampled rotor speed before it is added to the baseline tm0.
  Setpoint readback and actual GENCLS.tm readback are recorded separately;
  achieved incremental power and SOC are settled from actual torque and
  endpoint speed rather than from requested, commanded, or setpoint power.
  Four-vector shape, finite values, positive speed, valid SOC, and positive
  time step fail closed.

  This is an implementation and design contract only. It authorizes one
  separately registered minimal physical object gate for setpoint resolution,
  sign, timing, zero-action equivalence, independent intervention, achieved
  power, and energy accounting. It contains no ANDES trajectory and supports
  no physical authority, validated VSG-BESS, controller, decoupling,
  coordination, MARL, stability, safety, robustness, generalization, hardware,
  novelty, or publication-readiness claim. Physical execution and training
  remain unauthorized.
