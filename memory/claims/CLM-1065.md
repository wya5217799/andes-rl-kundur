statement: |
  In one sealed R384 formal job on ANDES 2.0.0 and the unchanged packaged
  Kundur network, four active `REGCV1` records achieved the exact one-to-one
  mapping to static generators and buses 1--4 while all four `GENROU`, four
  `TGOV1`, and four `EXDC2` records read back `u=0`. Direct `RenGen` writes to
  `Pref` and `Qref` were isolated by device and restored exactly, but every
  observed baseline was zero; this establishes software routing/storage only,
  not nonzero dynamic power authority.

  Setup and power flow completed, but native TDS initialization had
  `TDS.test_ok=false`; no time-domain step executed and finite-value and drift
  gates were therefore not evaluated. R384 validly returns
  `STOP-REGCV1-OBJECT-INITIALIZATION`, closes Q-0104 negative, and stops this
  registered `REGCV1` formulation before signed P/Q authority, deterministic
  decoupling, headroom, information, MARL, robustness, or topology experiments.
  The result does not identify an equation-level cause and does not reject
  other converter models, ANDES, Kundur, or converter-level VSG research as a
  class. The immutable analysis's static auxiliary
  `next_gate="signed_dynamic_pref_qref_authority"` conflicts with the sealed
  STOP branch and is excluded as a metadata defect; it grants no successor
  authority.
