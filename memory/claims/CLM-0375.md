  | Run | offset | LS1 | LS2 | geo | Δ vs R174 |

  The "good basin" at (s54, offset=0) is narrow.

  ### Cross-offset robust SOTA (paper-defensible)

  - **mean across {0, 50, 100} = 0.3965**
  - **stdev = 0.015**
  - peak = 0.4139 at offset=0

  Paper's defensible claim: "0.40 ± 0.02 robust hreg, peak 0.4139
  single-config".

  ### Sub-observation: LS2 inversion

  R194 LS2=0.483 is the **highest LS2 ever observed at s54 hreg**,
  surpassing R174's 0.467 and R193's 0.415. But R194 LS1=0.312 (lowest
  of the three) drags geo down. Suggests offset axis trades LS1 vs
  LS2: different RNG paths preferentially fit one disturbance class
  over another.

  Not load-bearing for current paper but a potential future
  sub-finding if LS1/LS2 axis decomposition is explored further.
