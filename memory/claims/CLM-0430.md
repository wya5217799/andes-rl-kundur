  🚨 **METHODOLOGICAL CORRECTION**: This session's prior claims
  (CLM-0385, CLM-0390, CLM-0395, CLM-0400, CLM-0410, CLM-0420,
  CLM-0425) characterized paper Eq.14 reward-term ablations using
  **only the 11-axis v3.1 project ranker (`geo`)**, omitting the
  **paper's own evaluation metric (`cum_rf`, Yang2023 §IV-C global
  cumulative reward)**. Re-reading every final_eval_summary.json
  reveals a **consistent 3-6% paper-metric degradation** when paper
  terms are removed, opposite the 6-axis "vestigial" reading.

  ### Side-by-side: 6-axis vs paper-metric

  | Run     | config                  | geo    | cum_rf  | geo Δ vs full | cum_rf Δ vs full |

  **Pattern is unambiguous**: across 4 of 4 only-phi_abs vs full
  reward comparisons, paper-metric (cum_rf) degrades 3.5%, 6.0%,
  4.1%, 5.8% respectively. Always negative, always 3-6%.

  ### Why the 6-axis ranker missed this

  The v3.1 11-axis ranker (`paper_grade_axes.py`) measures:
  - Axes 1-3: max_df, final_df, settling_s (transient behaviour)
  - Axes 4-5: ΔH/ΔD smoothness
  - Axes 6-7: action utilization
  - Axis 8: improvement vs no-ctrl
  - Axes 9-11: agent activity / late-osc / P-balance gates

  None of these axes directly measures **cumulative synchronization
  deviation** (Σ_t Σ_i (f_i − f̄_t)²), which is what paper Eq.14's
  r_f term penalizes and what cum_rf integrates. The 6-axis ranker
  is "paper-FIGURE-supplement" (Fig.6-9 visual tolerances), NOT
  "paper-METRIC-replicating".

  ### What is preserved (mechanism story intact)

  Gauge-invariance mechanism for paper-strict collapse remains
  fully supported on BOTH metrics:
  - geo: R218 = R240 = 0.010 (97% degradation, both algos)
  - cum_rf: R218 = -0.183, R240 = -0.183 (165% degradation, both
    algos, bit-identical to 3 dp)

  Empirical phase-portrait analysis (R218 vs R201 vs R239) still
  shows the synchronized-drift attractor signature, confirmed on
  both metrics. **The "paper-strict collapses universally via
  gauge invariance" finding is robust.**

  ### What must be qualified

  The "**only**-phi_abs is sufficient AND paper Eq.14 terms are
  vestigial" claim must become:

  > "phi_abs is **necessary** for training viability across all
  > tested algorithms and seeds (paper-strict collapses universally
  > on both 6-axis and paper-metric). Once phi_abs is present,
  > the additional contribution of paper Eq.14 terms (r_h, r_d, r_f)
  > is **metric-dependent**:
  >
  > - On the project's 11-axis v3.1 ranker (transient+settling+
  >   smoothness+utilization+gating): paper terms contribute within
  >   ±1.5% noise (3 of 4 cells; scalar+s51 is the exception at
  >   -15.7%, scalar+s50 is unmeasured-baseline-pending R251).
  > - **On the paper's own cum_rf metric (Yang2023 §IV-C):** paper
  >   terms contribute a consistent **3-6% improvement** across 4 of
  >   4 algo×seed cells tested.
  >
  > The 11-axis ranker measures transient and engineering-utility
  > axes; the paper-metric measures cumulative synchronization
  > deviation. Paper Eq.14 terms specifically improve synchronization
  > tightness without affecting transient/settling/utilization —
  > which is consistent with their mathematical form (r_h, r_d
  > penalize per-agent inertia/damping deviation from mean;
  > r_f penalizes per-agent frequency deviation from local mean)."

  ### Practical impact on paper draft

  `docs/paper_drafts/sec_iv_d_paper_eq14_gauge_invariance.md` needs
  updating to acknowledge the metric-dependence. The "vestigial"
  framing must be softened across the memo's sections D.7 narrative
  proposal and the empirical phase-portrait section.

  The 6th paper contribution becomes **more nuanced and more
  honest**:
  - paper Eq.14 IS doing something (cum_rf 3-6% improvement)
  - phi_abs is doing MORE (training viability + gauge fix)
  - The "paper Eq.14 alone is sufficient" reading is wrong
  - The "paper Eq.14 alone is insufficient (collapse) but paper +
    phi_abs is best" reading is correct, with phi_abs being the
    necessary-not-paper patch.

  ### Why this was missed earlier

  All this session's claims compared `geo` values only because
  geo was the primary headline number in prior session activity
  (R72, R142, R154, R174 SOTA hunts), and rendered into STATE.md
  leaderboard. The summary JSON files always contained cum_rf, but
  it was not read until this audit (prompted by user question
  "评估标准是否合理，都有六轴和论文评估吗").

  Engineering note for future autonomous-loop work: when making
  paper-integrity claims, **MUST report both `geo` and `cum_rf`**.
  Single-metric framing risks reproducing the CLM-0027 R31/R33
  failure mode (a non-paper metric giving an answer the paper
  metric does not support).

  ### Supersedes nothing formally, qualifies several claims

  This claim does NOT supersede CLM-0385/0390/0395/0400/0410/0420/
  0425 (the underlying experimental data and 6-axis analyses are
  correct as reported). It **qualifies** them: their "vestigial"
  language was over-strong because they cited only one metric.

  Use this CLM-0430 as the authoritative methodological qualifier
  for all session-2026-05-20 paper-Eq.14-vestigial claims.
