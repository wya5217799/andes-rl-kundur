statement: |
  R62 hidden_size hyper recon — first-ever scan beyond TD3 MLP.

  ### Method

  R48 U-curve (CLM-0054) established h=64 as TD3 MLP peak but
  **never tested SAC or LSTM**. R62 Wave 1 ran SAC and LSTM at h=128
  (paper Sec.IV-A "4 layers × 128") to test the assumption.

  ### Result

  **LSTM h=128 vs h=64** (R57-α config + warmup-5, R62 W1 s51):

  | h | best.pt 6-axis | final.pt 6-axis |
  |---|---|---|
  | 64 (R57-α s51) | **0.526** | — |
  | 128 (R62 W1 s51) | 0.219 | 0.207 |

  → h=128 collapses LSTM by **-59 %**. h=64 strongly preferred.
  Paper's "4×128" default does NOT transfer to our LSTM training.

  **SAC h=128 vs h=64** (paper_strict_pure_radsec + Q-0007, s50):

  | h | best.pt total_cum_rf | best_eval.pt total_cum_rf |
  |---|---|---|
  | 64 (R62 W3) | -0.397 | -0.359 |
  | 128 (R62 W1) | -0.432 | **-0.347** |

  → h=128 single-seed marginally beats h=64 by **3.3 %** on
  best_eval.pt; **base best.pt is 8.8 % worse with h=128**. Within
  seed-variance noise; 3-seed h=64+Q7 needed to settle (Q-0011 opened).

  ### Mechanism candidates

  **LSTM h=128 collapse**:
  - LSTM hidden state grows with cell size; sequence-level credit
    assignment harder
  - 4×128 + LSTMCell h=128 → ~80k params per actor; over-parameterised
    for obs_dim=7 + 75-ep training
  - Consistent with R48 U-curve principle: smaller capacity wins on
    short-trajectory, low-dim-obs RL

  **SAC h=128 marginal benefit**:
  - SAC's entropy + auto-α adds regularisation that absorbs
    over-parameterisation cost
  - Or: 3.3 % is just noise; would disappear at 3-seed mean

  ### Implication

  R63+ hyper sweep should **stick with h=64** for SAC pending 3-seed
  validation. LSTM **must** stay h=64. TD3 MLP confirmed h=64 (CLM-0054).

  **Generalised rule**: paper Table I / Sec.IV-A defaults (h=128, 4
  layers) are tuned for paper's env (action space 1/20× different
  from ours, see CLM-0076). Our env has different effective
  complexity; smaller networks win.
