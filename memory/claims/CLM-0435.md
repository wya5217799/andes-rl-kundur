  ### Big new finding: scalar's basin ceiling is highly seed-dependent

  scalar full-V4 baselines across 3 seeds:
  - s54: 0.391 (high)
  - s51: 0.3562 (mid, -9% from s54)
  - s50: **0.2662 (low, -32% from s54)**

  Compare hreg full-V4 baselines:
  - s54: 0.4152
  - s51: 0.3901 (-6%)
  - s50: 0.3515 (-15%)

  **scalar's baseline range across seeds is 2× wider than hreg's**
  (-32% scalar worst-case vs -15% hreg worst-case). This is
  consistent with CLM-0181/0182 LSTM hidden-state drift mechanism:
  scalar without hidden-state regularisation has higher variance
  across RNG basins, hreg's regularisation compresses this variance.
