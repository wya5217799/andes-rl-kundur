statement: |
  R71 — **s50 + tau + warmup ≥ 25 → LS1 P_balance cliff drop** (from 0.56 to 0.000).
  Sharp boundary between warmup=20 and warmup=25 on s50.

  ### Evidence

  | seed | warmup | v3.1 | LS1 P_balance | LS1 P_final |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | s50 | 5 | 0.2647 | 0.560 | (balanced) |
  | s50 | 10 | 0.2840 | similar | (balanced) |
  | s50 | 15 | 0.3089 | similar | (balanced) |
  | **s50** | **20** | **0.3151** ← peak | **0.560** | balanced |
  | **s50** | **25** | **0.0642** ← cliff | **0.000** | [+0.19,+0.09,+0.08,+0.27] |
  | s50 | 30 | 0.0631 | 0.000 | [+0.24,+0.07,+0.06,+0.30] |

  warmup=20 → 25 transition: middle 2 agents (idx 1, 2) emit < 0.1 ΔP, ceding all
  authority to corner agents (idx 0, 3).

  ### Mechanism (hypothesis)

  Long LSTM warmup (>20 ep) on s50 initial conditions: critic learns Q-targets
  that reward whichever 2 agents diverge first from initial state. With s50's
  initial weights, agents 1+2 are slower to act → critic locks in "agent 0+3 do
  everything" policy. Once locked, agents 1+2 never recover.

  Other seeds don't show this cliff:
  - s51 + warmup=20 (R69 W1): v3.1=0.1959 (different pattern — LS2 issue)
  - s52 + warmup=20 (R69 W6): v3.1=0.3068 (no cliff)
  - s51 + warmup=5 (R68 W2): v3.1=0.3562 (best)

  → cliff is **seed-specific** to s50 with long warmup.

  ### Implication for hyper design

  - **No single warmup setting fits all seeds**:
    - s50: optimal at warmup=20
    - s51: optimal at warmup=5
    - s52: optimal at warmup=5
  - Paper convention is to pick ONE hyper combo and report 3-seed mean.
  - Recommend: warmup=5 for paper hyper (s51/s52 both peak there, s50 only -16%)
  - This favours CLM-0117 canonical choice (R68 W2 s51 = tau + warmup=5)

  ### Paper figure consideration

  Cliff is a "negative result" worth reporting in ablation:
  - "Long warmup (>20 ep) on s50 induces single-agent collapse, demonstrating
    LSTM sensitivity to warmup schedule"
  - Supports v3.1 ranker design (catches this; v3.0 would not)
