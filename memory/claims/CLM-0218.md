  ## Mechanism (cross-link to other findings)

  α=0.05 step-0 ‖a‖ ≈ 0.05 × 1.41 = 0.07 (tiny lift from baseline 0.16).
  The first action is barely perturbed; LSTM h evolves into the normal
  ramp-up by step 2. dH/dD utilization range is preserved
  ([[CLM-0210]] mechanism). Effectively: a microscopic step-0 boost in
  action norm enables marginally better step-0 control AND faster
  cum_rf accumulation, without sacrificing the trajectory shape that
  the geo 11-axis rewards.

  Combined with R92's [[CLM-0170]] "SOTA is bang-bang 76% of episode"
  finding: R72_w4 already approaches saturated control rapidly; α=0.05
  just nudges the very first step's action norm without changing the
  bang-bang attractor. The R92 bang-bang character + R104 Q-side slack
  + R121 cliff + R132 fine knee combine into a coherent picture:
  **the SOTA is already near-optimal in steady state; the only available
  improvement is shaving a few-step early-phase advantage by small
  step-0 warming**.
