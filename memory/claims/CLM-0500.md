statement: |
  R261 confirmed and corrected a canonical evaluation-integrity bug.
  `paper_path.run_scenario()` stopped when ANDES returned
  `info["tds_failed"]=True`, but the persisted record dropped that flag.
  `paper_grade_axes.evaluate_trace()` therefore could not apply its locked
  failure guard, while `summary.score_trace_files()` still computed
  `cum_rf` from the shortened trace. Because cumulative squared error grows
  with trajectory length, such a partial trace could appear artificially
  strong on the paper metric.

  New records now persist `tds_failed`, `completed`, `requested_steps`, and
  `n_steps`. The canonical summary rejects failed, explicitly incomplete,
  empty, or internally inconsistent trace records with `ValueError` before
  either geo or cum_rf is produced. A regression captured both old failures
  before the fix (`tds_failed` missing and no summary exception) and passes
  after the fix.

  R261 does not assert that an existing headline was contaminated: the bug was
  a reachable integrity hole, while the audited current full-length traces did
  not provide evidence of a historical partial-trace headline.
