statement: |
  **R93-W0b (LSTM hidden-state drift forensics, zero ANDES, on R72_w4
  SOTA) — the R72_w4 LSTMCell learned a divergent internal dynamic
  that drives the actor to bang-bang saturation INDEPENDENT of the
  observation content.** This identifies the load-bearing mechanism
  behind CLM-0170's 76% saturation finding: it is the LSTM cell, not
  the actor head, that produces the saturated policy.

  Setup: 4 distinct 50-step obs streams (A_constant_zero,
  A_constant_e1, B_alternating_e1, C_random_sigma0p5) forwarded through
  each of 4 actor LSTMCells starting from h_actor = 0. Per-stream
  results are **virtually identical** across all 4 streams for each
  agent, demonstrating obs-content-invariance:

  ### Cross-stream logit max |z|

  | Obs stream             | median (over 4 agents) | max  | Predicts saturation |
  |------------------------|------------------------|------|---------------------|
  | A_constant_zero        | **2.59**               | 3.37 | YES                 |
  | A_constant_e1          | 2.65                   | 3.40 | YES                 |
  | B_alternating_e1       | 2.60                   | 3.38 | YES                 |
  | C_random_sigma=0.5     | 2.61                   | 3.38 | YES                 |

  All 4 streams reach logit |z| > 2 (the tanh-saturation point) within
  the 50-step rollout. The agents' realised actions are identical to
  the bang-bang pattern observed in R92-W1 on real ANDES:
  - **agent 0**: action[0]=ΔM_norm → −1, action[1]=ΔD_norm → +1 (matches
    R92-W1 LS1 + LS2 SOTA observation: ag0 ΔM=-0.94 boundary, ΔD=+1)
  - **agent 1**: similar pattern (ΔM=-1, ΔD=+1)
  - **agent 2/3**: opposite ΔM (=+1) per Kundur 2-area split

  ### LSTM hidden-state norm growth

  ||h_actor|| trajectories grow from ~0.25 (step 0) to ~5.0 (step 49)
  in every stream:

  | Agent | step 0  | step 49 | ratio | saturated steps / 100 (zero obs) |
  |-------|---------|---------|-------|----------------------------------|
  | 0     | 0.25    | 4.85    | 19×   | 38                               |
  | 1     | 0.24    | 5.42    | 23×   | 73                               |
  | 2     | 0.26    | 5.62    | 22×   | 78                               |
  | 3     | 0.26    | 5.49    | 21×   | 76                               |

  **Even with obs = 0 for all 50 steps, the LSTMCell drives ||h||
  to ~5 and tanh saturates 38-78% of the action variables**. This is
  a property of the trained LSTM cell's recurrent weight + bias —
  the cell state c has a self-reinforcing fixed point that pulls h
  away from zero regardless of input.

  ### Mechanism story (replaces CLM-0170's preliminary interpretation
  and supersedes CLM-0149/0153/0154 + CLM-0160's mechanism interpretations)

  The R72_w4 LSTM SOTA's 0.391 plateau is mechanistically:

  1. **At episode reset**: h_actor = h_critic = 0. Actor outputs interior
     action ≈ 0 (CLM-0180 confirms this with logit median 0.10). Critic
     is poorly calibrated in this regime (CLM-0149/0153/0154 + R87
     impulse phase weaker confidence, CLM-0160 + CLM-0165 confirm).
  2. **Steps 1-15 (transient ramp)**: regardless of obs, the LSTMCell's
     internal divergent dynamics push h away from 0; logits grow from
     |z| ≈ 0.1 to |z| ≈ 2-3; tanh saturates.
  3. **Steps 15-50 (post-saturation)**: actions pinned at ±1; the
     "policy" effectively has zero degrees of freedom remaining.
     The Kundur 2-area sign pattern (ag0/1 ΔM<0, ag2/3 ΔM>0, all ΔD>0)
     is determined by which side of zero each agent's drift initially
     falls on, which is in turn determined by the (small but nonzero)
     fc_out biases + LSTM gate biases.

  **R57-R82 91 round of algo / hyper / arch sweeps all converge to
  this same attractor** because:
  - Algorithm class (SAC / TD3 / Transformer / multi-LSTM) does not
    affect the LSTM drift in TD3-LSTM ckpts.
  - Hyperparameter choices (lr, batch, tau, warmup, hidden_size) do
    not regularise the LSTM cell-state norm.
  - Architecture changes (Transformer, multi-layer LSTM) replace the
    LSTM with a different stateful recurrent block that has similar
    drift potential (R82 W1/W2 results consistent with this).
  - **Nothing in R57-R82 added a hidden-state regularisation term**.

  ### R94+ priority lock (supersedes CLM-0170's R93+ candidates)

  Updated mechanism gives different candidate ordering:

  - 🆕 **PRIORITY 1 — LSTM hidden-state norm regularisation**: add
    a penalty term to training loss = `lambda_h * ||h_actor||² mean`.
    Predicts: with regularised h, the policy stays in tanh-linear
    regime; smooth continuous control replaces bang-bang; geo lifts
    above 0.391 if and only if smooth control out-performs the
    discrete optimum at given action bounds.
  - 🆕 **PRIORITY 2 — Replace LSTMCell with non-recurrent MLP at
    training-time, recurrent only at inference**: removes drift
    entirely; tests whether the 91-round MLP plateau was due to
    capacity (then this won't help) or due to lacking sequence
    info (then this fixes both).
  - 🟡 **PRIORITY 3 (originally PRIORITY 1) — Widen action bounds**:
    still informative as a falsification test. If LSTM-drift is the
    correct mechanism, widening DM_MAX / DD_MAX 2× will not help
    (h will drift to a new boundary). If widening does help, the
    tanh-slope-times-bound interaction matters and the LSTM-drift
    story is incomplete.
  - ❌ Distributional critic / critic regularisation / wider warmup
    / D3 obs sufficiency — all targeted hypotheses now explained
    away by LSTM-drift; obs distribution is irrelevant (W0b proved
    obs-invariance), critic alignment is competent on-manifold
    (CLM-0160 / 0165).

  ### Caveats

  - The drift uses h_actor only. critic LSTM dynamics may differ;
    R87 / CLM-0165 showed critic confidence high on-manifold, which
    is consistent: actor drives to boundary, critic correctly
    endorses that drive.
  - The fixed-point analysis is empirical (forward 50 steps);
    full mathematical confirmation needs eigenvalue analysis of
    nn.LSTMCell's recurrent matrix. Worth doing in R94.
  - Only one ckpt tested (R72_w4 s54). R86 / CLM-0167 (parallel
    session) showed cross-ckpt monotone-Q replicates universally;
    the same N=6 ckpt sample should be re-forensicked at R93-W0b
    granularity to confirm the LSTM-drift attractor is universal.
