statement: |
  R275 closes the sealed fast-layer authority gate FAST-LAYER-POSITIVE.
  Relative to the immutable R274 slow droop+PI controller with fixed
  M/D=200/100, the identical controller plus one prospectively frozen
  common-inertia pulse (all four normalized M actions +0.25 for the first
  15 steps/3.0 s, then zero; D always zero) completed all 24 paired
  real-ANDES scenarios with zero TDS failures and zero physical-contract
  violations.

  The pulse cleared every registered fast endpoint. Mean physical maximum
  sampled RoCoF changed by -28.373628% with paired-bootstrap 95% interval
  [-30.8504%, -25.4617%]; worst-bus peak absolute frequency by -11.077230%
  [-14.2936%, -7.52073%]; normalized synchronization loss by -4.290160%
  [-6.67573%, -2.46646%]; and first-3-s inter-area IAE by -9.914721%
  [-13.1469%, -7.03780%]. Registered upper-tail effects were respectively
  -34.2597%, -10.9604%, -4.33965%, and -16.2372%.

  Slow restoration remained within the frozen guards: VSG-mean IAE changed
  by -0.203227%, while final-10-s common absolute frequency changed by
  +0.528270% with 95% upper bound +0.630888%. Every action budget matched
  exactly; storage SOC stayed within [0.486090666, 0.511449272], maximum
  actual power was 0.313399250 system pu, and storage energy/action effects
  remained within the registered +5% limits.

  This establishes independent, simple fast-inertia value above the validated
  slow active-power layer and supports a common/differential, fast/slow
  decoupling architecture. It does not establish that MARL, HAWE, adaptive
  damping, topology generalisation, or a unified GFM-BESS improves on this
  strong classical baseline.
