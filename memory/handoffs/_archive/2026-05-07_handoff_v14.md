## 5. Headline-claim changes (paper draft must reframe)

### Claim 1 — HAWE method novelty

**Pre-this-round**: "HAWE recovers 99 % of a non-reproducible single-seed
controller from saved checkpoints; ten alternative paths fail to exceed
this; novelty defended."

**Post-this-round**: "HAWE recovers 98.9 % of R21 at the headline weight;
**SWA / model soups (Izmailov 2018, Wortsman 2022) achieve the same recovery
within 0.7 %** at sweet-spot weights; HAWE retains a small (1-12 %)
advantage off-sweet-spot. Cite both, downgrade method claim to
'recipe / ablation' — see r26 verdict §V4."

### Claim 2 — Domain M2 modal alignment

**Pre-this-round**: "Counter-intuitive Gini regularity — high-Gini
ES2-dominant controllers preserve the spatial decomposition predicted by
Yang Fig. 7, which the panel inferred is the inter-area mode shape."

**Post-this-round**: "DROP the modal-alignment explanation entirely —
inter-area mode shape (0.579 Hz, ζ=2.45 %) shows ES1 (Bus 12) is the
dominant ESS coordinate (|W|=77, P_std=0.035), but high-score controllers
act on **ES2** (|W|=43), and the Spearman correlation between mode-shape
projection and 6-axis score is **-0.93** — the opposite direction.
Reframe regularity as 'spatial selectivity, mechanism TBD'. See r27 §V."

### Claim 3 — LS2 settling = ∞

**Pre-this-round**: "LS2 settling is unbounded in our ANDES model whereas
paper achieves 2.5 s." (Appendix B, repeated in §VI-B's per-axis table)

**Post-this-round**: "**LS2 settling is finite at 6.8 s** (own-final band)
or 7.4 s (paper-target band) for R21 — within episode horizon, not ∞.
The ∞ in the previous-version ranker came from a 6 s window truncation
in `_settling_time`, fixed in r28 / N1c. Paper Appendix B settling row
must be replaced with the corrected numbers; the previous '∞ (>30 s)'
phrasing is wrong on both terms (R21 settles, AND in <10 s)."
