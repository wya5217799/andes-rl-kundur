## Why it matters

R313 isolated the global convex-interpolation failure to HP1 edge-0 responses
at both amplitudes while the matched retained-cross comparison passed strongly.
The next admissible optimization is therefore operating-point locality, not an
amplitude term, threshold relaxation, cross-block deletion, controller, or
algorithm sweep.

## Candidates

- Admit the already observed R313 HP1 records only as a declared development
  point; never reuse any R313 case as headline validation.
- Freeze one local/simplex interpolation rule, the exact simplex, a new
  untouched operating-condition bank, the unchanged R313 error thresholds,
  and EVAL timing before fitting or executing new outcomes.
- Keep the full and block-diagonal predictor arms matched exactly as in R313 so
  retained-cross information value remains separately visible.
- Stop at predictor PASS, predictor NO-GO, or invalid execution. Do not build a
  controller, distributed-agent runtime, reward, or neural training loop.
