## Why it matters

R58 ran the full paper-strict matrix at **75 ep**, matching R56/R57
training horizon. The paper uses **500 ep** for convergence (see
critic verdict C3, audit Phase 0). CLM-0071's ranking conclusions are
therefore subject to the question: **does the 75-ep ranking persist at
500 ep, or do later-converging algorithms catch up / overtake**?

Specifically:
- TD3+LSTM converged earliest at 75 ep in all configs — its 75-ep
  numbers are likely close-to-converged.
- SAC and TD3-MLP under paper_strict_pure (Hz) **diverged** at 75 ep —
  500 ep would give them more chances to either stabilize or fully
  collapse. Either outcome refines our understanding.
- SAC under paper_strict_pure_radsec was *decreasing* at 75 ep but
  absolute critic loss ~100-300 (large vs typical RL). 500 ep would
  reveal whether SAC saturates at -0.4 cum_rf or improves further to
  match paper Table III numbers.

If 500-ep results invert the 75-ep ranking, CLM-0072's
production-candidate selection needs revision. If 500-ep preserves
ranking, CLM-0072 is robust and we have a stronger basis for paper
comparison.
