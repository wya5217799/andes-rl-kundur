## Why it matters

R356 proves that six exposed development cases cannot reach the simultaneous
two-percent endpoint target even without physical or information constraints.
Its ten accepted optimal cases are only relaxed witnesses. A neighbour-local
selector cannot be studied from those statuses until an independent solver
separates physically feasible positive cases from relaxed-only cases.

## Prospective contract

- Rebuild exactly the sixteen exposed R356 development cases and read no
  residual holdout, new simulator trajectory, or training output.
- Keep both registered endpoint targets at 98 percent of their baselines.
- Keep the same three edge actions, base and previous node commands, node-power
  and node-ramp limits, horizon, and device limits.
- Inherit the six accepted R356 relaxed-infeasibility certificates because
  adding physical constraints cannot restore their feasibility. For the ten
  relaxed-optimal candidates, keep the common target and every physical limit
  as constraints and minimize the normalized differential endpoint in one
  convex quadratic program.
- Accept feasibility only from a reconstructed optimal witness within both
  targets and every physical bound. Accept infeasibility only when an optimal
  dual lower bound lies strictly above the differential target. Any ambiguous
  bound, numerical exception, or failed reconstruction is invalid.
- At least one accepted physical witness returns `PHYSICAL-HEADROOM-FOUND`;
  all accepted infeasible returns `NO-PHYSICAL-HEADROOM`; any missing case,
  unaccepted exit, source drift, or failed reconstruction returns
  `ANALYSIS-INVALID`.
- A positive result authorizes only a separately registered neighbour-
  observability study. No branch authorizes neural training, simulation,
  holdout reading, target reduction, or a multi-agent claim.
