## Why it matters

R272 showed a large diagnostic common-frequency-restoration signal on its
complete pairs, but its formal bank was INVALID because the zero-support plant
failed 3/20 cases.  R273 attributed those failures to the disturbance
envelope, not uniquely to the added zero-command ESD1 DAE, and implemented a
prospective completion-screen contract.  The mechanism question is still
unanswered at bank level.

The next experiment must avoid both post-hoc deletion and a trivially easy
bank.  Feasibility evidence therefore has to be collected before any
controller trace, frozen with all exclusions retained, and stratified by
disturbance location, sign, and severity.  Controller performance can only be
evaluated on the resulting sealed feasible stratum.

## Pre-commitment for the next round

1. Generate a new signed, multi-location development/formal scenario bank
   without inspecting droop+PI or learned-controller performance.
2. Run completion-only zero-support screening on the exact storage DAE before
   any controller trace.  Retain all failed scenarios, report the excluded
   fraction by sign/location/severity, and freeze the screening contract.
3. Reject a bank that is either numerically infeasible after screening or
   lacks a prospectively declared nontrivial severity distribution; do not
   tune the envelope after controller endpoints are visible.
4. If the screened bank passes its generation gate, compare the already frozen
   R272 droop+PI contract against identical-DAE zero support once.  Keep
   capacity, placement, gains, M/D, solver, horizon, and physical endpoint
   definitions unchanged.
5. Retain every controller failure and report physical 60-Hz IAE, final-window
   common-frequency error, paired uncertainty, completion, tail risk, action,
   SOC, power, energy, ramp, and converter-capability guards.
6. Do not train RL/GNN, change the actuator, open topology work, or open Gate 2
   unless the new sealed authority gate is valid and positive.

## Decision direction

- **AUTHORITY-POSITIVE:** the prospectively screened bank is nontrivial, both
  arms satisfy completion/safety guards, and both co-primary physical
  endpoints pass their frozen paired thresholds.
- **AUTHORITY-PARTIAL:** the experiment is valid and safe, but only part of the
  registered physical gate passes.
- **NO-MATERIAL-AUTHORITY:** the valid comparison does not clear the frozen
  effect thresholds.
- **INVALID:** feasibility, completion, provenance, nontriviality, or physical
  contract guards fail.
