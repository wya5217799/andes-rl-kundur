## Why it matters

R386 proves only that the structurally clean four-device object initializes and
remains numerically quiet for 0.2 seconds at zero input. A software setpoint
write is not yet physical actuator authority. Before any deterministic
decoupler or learning method is designed, the same object must show that each
device's active- and reactive-power reference can be perturbed in both signs,
that only the requested setpoint changes, and that the intended device's
achieved power response is signed and distinguishable from network-mediated
responses at the other devices.

## Prospective contract

- Keep ANDES 2.0.0, the exact R386 static-only Kundur construction, network
  connectivity, four REGCV1 mappings, device card, source-capture order, and
  initialization diagnostics unchanged.
- Execute 17 independent trajectories: one zero-step reference and, for each
  of four devices, one `Pref` and one `Qref` step in each sign. Apply the step
  immediately after successful native TDS initialization.
- Set each signed step to `0.09` system-base per unit, exactly one percent of
  the registered 900-MVA device rating on the 100-MVA system base. Run every
  arm for 2.0 seconds with the native TDS tolerance fixed at `1e-4`.
- Require requested/applied setpoint equality within `1e-12`, with all seven
  non-commanded device-channel setpoints unchanged within the same tolerance.
- Measure achieved `Pe` for `Pref` and achieved `Qe` for `Qref` at the terminal
  time relative to the zero-step arm. Require the requested sign with absolute
  response at least `2e-4` system per unit, target-device magnitude exceeding
  every non-target same-channel magnitude by at least `2e-4`, and positive to
  negative paired separation of at least `4e-4`.
- Record, but do not gate or claim decoupling from, each target's cross-channel
  response and all cross-device responses. Require finite complete DAE/model
  values, bus voltage in `[0.9, 1.1]` pu, device current magnitude no greater
  than `10.0` pu, apparent power no greater than the registered device rating,
  and virtual speed in `[0.95, 1.05]` pu throughout every trajectory.
- Any malformed or incomplete record is analysis-invalid. A valid failure of
  initialization, convergence, electrical guards, action identity, signed
  response, or target attribution stops this REGCV1 formulation. The round
  may not add a controller, reward, training, changed topology, changed device
  model, or in-round retry.
