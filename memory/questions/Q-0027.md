## Why it matters

R262 showed that a fixed convex blend only moves smoothly along the existing
R201/droop Pareto frontier. The strict research objective needs synergy rather
than a global compromise: retain learned transient quality when it helps, but
inject proportional disturbance coupling only in the states or time windows
where it improves synchronization.

## Candidates

- Low-cost probe: a pre-registered state-dependent gate
  `alpha=g(|delta_f|, dispersion, time)` using frozen R201 and droop k=10.
- Higher-confidence algorithm: train a corrected-R261 recurrent residual
  actor around an analytic droop prior, keeping `phi_abs` as the structural
  stabilizer identified in R258.
- Evaluation gate: both `geo` and `cum_rf`, multiple training seeds, multiple
  evaluation seeds, and at least one held-out disturbance or topology.
