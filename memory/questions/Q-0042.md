## Why it matters

R280 closed Q-0041 as `CENTRALIZED-EXPLANATION-SUFFICIENT` (CLM-0610): the
learned zero-sum differential inertia allocation along the `[1, 1, -1, -1]`
two-area mode reduces synchronization loss by 24.35% and early inter-area IAE
by 17.04% versus `q=0`, with all guards passing. The SCI upgrade plan
(idea-evaluator verdict 2026-07-29: Accept with Revisions) reframes the
journal extension around the inertia-placement lineage and requires a
mechanism section explaining *why* the differential allocation helps and what
physical quantity sets the gain. The named validation experiment that gates
that manuscript section is this question: a linearized eigenvalue analysis
mapping static allocation amplitude along the frozen action mode to the
inter-area oscillation mode's damping ratio.

Cost of leaving it open: the mechanism section would rest on post-hoc
narrative, and the F2 fatal flaw (the analysis degenerating into a restatement
of known inertia-heterogeneity results) would remain undefended.

## Pre-commitment

1. Keep the modified Kundur plant, R274 slow droop+PI storage layer, R275
   common-inertia pulse, solver, operating point, and the `[1, 1, -1, -1]`
   rank-one action coordinate frozen. No retraining and no controller change.
2. Sweep only the static allocation amplitude `q` inside the learned
   controller's feasible bound (|q| <= 0.25, the R279/R280 frozen action
   contract), plus one predeclared inertia-heterogeneity axis at development
   level. The sweep grid is frozen before the first eigenvalue computation.
3. Register the inter-area mode identification rule (mode-shape heuristic or
   participation factor, frozen in plan.md) before looking at any eigenvalue.
4. The pre-registered primary endpoint is the damping ratio of the identified
   inter-area mode as a function of `q`; the materiality threshold and the
   expected sign (negative `q`-to-damping gradient in the direction the learned
   controller exploited) are frozen in plan.md before results.
5. Compare the mapping's shape against the R280 measured gains only as a
   qualitative consistency check; do not fit any parameter to make them agree.
6. Analysis-first: use existing trajectories and linearization only. No new
   time-domain bank, no topology change, no manuscript edit inside this round.

## Candidates

- ANDES eigenvalue analysis (`EIG` routine) on the frozen plant at the R279
  operating point, static `q` sweep, damping-ratio and frequency extraction.
- Fallback if EIG is unusable on this model: reduced-order two-area equivalent
  derived from the frozen model parameters, same frozen grid and thresholds.
- Development-level heterogeneity sweep (area inertia ratio) to probe F2
  overlap with Adrees-Milanovic-style heterogeneity effects.

## Decision direction

- **MECHANISM-CONFIRMED:** the frozen inter-area mode's damping ratio varies
  materially and monotonically over the feasible `q` range in the registered
  direction, clearing the frozen materiality threshold, and the shape is
  qualitatively consistent with the R280 gain signs.
- **MECHANISM-PARTIAL:** a real but weak or non-monotone mapping; the
  manuscript mechanism section degrades to bounded empirical claims.
- **MECHANISM-ABSENT:** damping ratio is insensitive to `q`; the SCI plan
  degrades to the (a) pure-empirical track and the inertia-placement framing
  loses its mechanism pillar.
- **INVALID:** linearization, operating point, mode identification, or
  provenance contracts fail.
