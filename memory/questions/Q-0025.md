## Why it matters

CLM-0194 (R110 audit) discovered ANDES default kundur_full has a
Toggler trip on Line_8 (Area 2 internal, 1 of 2 parallel paths) at
t=2s. V4 env inherits unchanged. **Every LS1/LS2 scenario is therefore
a compound disturbance** (paper load step at t=0.5s + unintended line
trip at t=2s).

R08 §2 Finding 2 (2× max_df residual at H=300) and R89's F1/F2/F3/F4/F5
audit (CLM-0173) didn't include this. F1 fix (fn=60→50) made residual
LARGER. R110 hypothesis: Toggler is the dominant remaining cause.

Quantitative test (Toggler u=0 vs u=1, zero action, LS1+LS2 × 1 seed)
distinguishes three regimes:

- **max_df drops ≥30% with Toggler off**: Toggler is dominant residual
  cause. R09 mystery 90% closed. Document as ADR / training-rebaseline
  decision.
- **max_df drops 10-30%**: meaningful but not dominant. Toggler + F2
  load topology together close the residual.
- **max_df drops <10%**: Toggler is minor; F2/F3 (R89) genuinely
  dominant. Q-0025 closed-negative; R09 mystery still open.

The answer dictates whether R110 / R89 / R57-R85 baseline numbers need
re-running on a "Toggler-removed" V4 variant.
