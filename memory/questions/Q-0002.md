## Why it matters

R41-A showed SAC + phi=0 plateaus at 0.117 (CLM-0045), refuting the
naive H3 ("action cost alone is the trap"). R41-B showed TD3 +
normalized reaches 0.275 (CLM-0047). The remaining ambiguity: did
SAC fail in R41-A because the reward shape was too extreme (PHI=0
leaves zero shaping signal on the action term), or because SAC's
entropy regularization is fundamentally incompatible with the V4
narrow-target frequency control? The normalized-mode rescale gives
SAC the *exact* paper Eq.14 shaping but at sane magnitudes — if SAC
matches TD3 here, the trap was reward shape after all; if SAC stays
at 0.13, algorithm itself is the issue.
