## Why it matters

R358 establishes physical action-space headroom in ten of sixteen exposed
cases under the exact three-edge zero-common residual basis, and R359-R362
reject all information extensions of that basis. A mechanism-level gap
remains untested: the surveyed deep research
(`deep_research_neighbour_information_learnability.md` section 8, direction
2) notes that a zero-common edge residual carries no net fleet power
authority, so the common-coordinate endpoint can only be improved indirectly
through cross-coupling (R344/R350 confirmed). If a common residual-power
channel (all four nodes acting with a shared fleet component) enlarges the
physical headroom, the zero-common contract itself is a structural limiter
and a mechanism change is the only route; if the headroom does not enlarge,
the zero-common contract is not the binding constraint and the mechanism-gap
hypothesis is weakened.

## Prospective contract

- Reuse the R358/R359 exposed development bank byte-for-byte: sixteen R352
  pairs, twenty-five samples, two startup-zero steps, the frozen R341 point
  models, the exact physical projection, and the registered 2% endpoint
  improvement target with the exact three-edge physical limits.
- The only change is the action basis: the three-edge zero-common basis is
  extended with one common residual-power channel, giving four independent
  control coordinates (common plus the three tree edges). The physical
  projection, node-power, node-ramp, SOC, energy, and voltage/current limits
  are unchanged.
- Solve the same physical joint-endpoint QP per development case with the
  extended four-channel action basis, minimizing the differential endpoint
  under the registered common-coordinate 2% improvement and physical limits.
  The decision is feasibility on the exposed development bank only.
- A positive result (strictly more physically feasible cases than the R358
  ten, or common-channel expansion of headroom in the six previously
  relaxed-infeasible cases) establishes that the zero-common residual
  contract limits headroom; a negative result (no additional feasible case)
  weakens the mechanism-gap hypothesis.
- No branch authorizes neural or reinforcement-learning training, a new ANDES
  trajectory, holdout evaluation, EVAL, title validation, stability, safety,
  robustness, topology generalization, hardware, or deployment claims.
