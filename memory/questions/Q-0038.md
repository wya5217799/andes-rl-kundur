## Why it matters

R274 and R275 now provide a strong, simple classical reference: slow bounded
droop+PI active power restores the common frequency, while one frozen 3-s
common-inertia pulse independently improves RoCoF, peak, synchronization, and
inter-area motion. This physical decomposition is sufficient to remove HAWE
from the main method. A paper that keeps "with Multi-Agent Reinforcement
Learning" in its title must still establish that corrected MARL contributes
something beyond this reference rather than comparing only against no control,
fixed M/D, or a weaker droop controller.

## Pre-commitment for the next round

1. Use the immutable R274+R275 controller as the strongest primary baseline.
   Do not retune its slow gains, pulse amplitude, duration, bank, endpoints, or
   thresholds after learned results are visible.
2. Use one corrected, memoryless, parameter-shared multi-agent policy; no
   HAWE, ensemble, checkpoint soup, best-seed headline, LSTM, or damping action.
   The only learned degree of freedom is one scalar coefficient on the
   two-area mode `[+1,+1,-1,-1]`.
3. Expose common frequency and local differential frequency explicitly. Keep
   the slow active-power controller and common-inertia pulse fixed; constrain
   the learned output to `q_t * [+1,+1,-1,-1]`, with scalar magnitude and slew
   bounds, so it cannot impersonate or undo the validated common layer.
4. Freeze residual amplitude/rate/action budgets before training. Train a
   small multi-seed development set and report every seed; select the method
   contract without evaluating the sealed formal bank.
5. On a fresh sealed bank, require material differential improvement over the
   R274+R275 baseline with paired uncertainty, while preserving RoCoF, peak,
   common restoration, completion, storage power/SOC/energy, action TV, and
   tail-risk guards.
6. A non-positive result removes MARL as a claimed contribution. Do not rescue
   it with HAWE, seed selection, architecture sweeps, reward sweeps, or
   post-hoc threshold changes.

## Decision direction

- **MARL-RESIDUAL-POSITIVE:** corrected shared MARL materially improves the
  registered differential endpoint(s) over the strong simple baseline and
  passes every common-mode, physical, action, completion, and tail guard.
- **NO-ADAPTIVE-MARL-VALUE:** the valid learned comparison does not clear the
  frozen incremental-value gate; keep the simple fast-slow controller and
  remove MARL/HAWE from the contribution claims.
- **INVALID:** provenance, seed reporting, completion, projection, endpoint,
  action, or physical-contract guards fail.
