least 92% of its nominal improvement on both primary endpoints when the
Line_4/5/6 corridor resistance and reactance are scaled through k=2.0. That
result closes the original C2 time-domain gap, but it leaves the observed
survival boundary unmeasured beyond k=2.0. The user explicitly authorized one
additional experiment-only round on 2026-07-30. This round therefore tests the
next two declared stress levels without changing the controller, scenario bank,
corridor definition, endpoints, or evidence standard.
