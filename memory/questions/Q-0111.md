## Why it matters

R396/CLM-1125 proves only that the two-unit PPVSM1 cell initializes cleanly,
stays inside a 0.2-second zero-input stationarity envelope, and has a guarded
reduced spectrum. A software setpoint write is not physical actuator
authority. Before any droop-slope verification, decoupler, or learning
method, the same cell must show that each device's active- and
reactive-power reference can be perturbed in both signs, that only the
requested setpoint changes, and that the intended device's achieved power
response is signed and distinguishable from network-mediated responses at
the other device.

## Prospective contract

- Keep ANDES 2.0.0, the exact R396 two-unit construction (PPVSM1 at buses
  1-2 on the unchanged packaged/derived Kundur static case, StaticGen 3-4
  static anchors, 900-MVA device rating, 100-MVA system base, frozen PPVSM1
  card, forbidden models absent, no PLL), source-capture order, and
  initialization diagnostics unchanged.
- Execute 9 independent fresh-system trajectories: one zero-step reference
  and, for each of two devices, one Pref and one Qref step in each sign.
  Apply the step immediately after successful native TDS initialization as
  an absolute write to the device's frozen reference service array element
  (PPVSM1.Pref.v / PPVSM1.Qref.v by device uid), the same mechanism as the
  ANDES RenGen set_setpoint array write.
- Set each signed step to 0.09 system-base per unit, exactly one percent of
  the registered 900-MVA device rating on the 100-MVA system base. Run every
  arm for 2.0 seconds with the native TDS tolerance fixed at 1e-4.
- Require requested/applied setpoint equality within 1e-12, with all three
  non-commanded device-channel setpoints unchanged within the same
  tolerance.
- Measure achieved Pe for Pref and achieved Qe for Qref at the terminal time
  relative to the zero-step arm. Require the requested sign with absolute
  response at least 2e-4 system per unit, target-device magnitude exceeding
  every non-target same-channel magnitude by at least 2e-4, and positive to
  negative paired separation of at least 4e-4.
- Record, but do not gate or claim decoupling from, each target's
  cross-channel response and all cross-device responses. Require finite
  complete DAE/model values, bus voltage in [0.9, 1.1] pu, device current
  magnitude no greater than 10.0 pu, apparent power no greater than the
  registered device rating on the system base (9.0 system pu), and virtual
  frequency in [0.95, 1.05] pu throughout every trajectory.
- Any malformed or incomplete record is analysis-invalid. A valid failure of
  initialization, convergence, electrical guards, action identity, signed
  response, or target attribution stops this PPVSM1 formulation before any
  controller, decoupling, or learning work. A complete valid pass opens only
  a separately registered droop-slope matching verification. The round may
  not add a controller, reward, training, changed topology, changed device
  model, or in-round retry.

## Log

- R397: opened after the PI authorized the signed P/Q authority gate for the
  two-unit cell (default next milestone in the R396 handoff). The bank is
  intentionally small and executes serially; process-launch and construction
  overhead dominate any useful parallel speed-up. The rehearsal canary
  executes every record step of all nine arms before sealing.
- R397: closed negative by CLM-1130. The sealed nine-arm bank is admissible
  (integrity, receipts, signed responses, paired separation, solver,
  electrical envelope, finite values, and zero diagnostics all pass), but
  target attribution fails on the two PPVSM1_1 Pref arms: the non-target
  PPVSM1_2 achieved Pe magnitude (0.04644/0.04663) exceeds the target's own
  (0.04329/0.04309). The PPVSM1 formulation stops before any controller,
  decoupling, or learning work. Any successor requires a new prospective
  route decision.
