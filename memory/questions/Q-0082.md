## Why it matters

R328 proves that the fixed retained controller strongly attenuates the complete
development bank when supplied its exact augmented state, while the current
delayed-output observer makes the same controller amplify disturbances. Exact
state is privileged model-only information. The next causal step is therefore
not a controller, solver, decoupling, or learning change, but one implementable
estimator that approaches the demonstrated headroom using only the declared
runtime information.

## Candidates

- Keep the retained model, development bank, objective, solver, horizon,
  action map, limits, thresholds, execution budget, and conference title
  fixed. Preserve the conditional holdout and every physical,
  distributed-agent, reward, and training block.
- Diagnose the existing estimator's error timing, unknown-disturbance response,
  and conditioning on the development bank before selecting one architecture.
  Register one theory-backed repair, such as a disturbance-aware observer or
  finite-window estimator, rather than a gain grid or architecture sweep.
- Require deterministic replay, state-estimation error diagnostics, valid
  controller traces, and the unchanged absolute development gates. A failed
  fixed repair returns to diagnosis; it does not open the holdout or authorize
  post-hoc tuning.
- Only a prospectively successful development repair may reserve a separate
  fresh-holdout round. Physical execution, distributed runtime, agents,
  rewards, training, and diagnostic evaluation remain blocked until their
  own registered gates are reached.
