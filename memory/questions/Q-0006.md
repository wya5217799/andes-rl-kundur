## Why it matters

R55 verdict's mechanism analysis (CLM-0062) established that
per-step / windowed anti-smoothness reward terms `(a[t] - a[t-W])²`
collapse memoryless TD3 actors via exploration-noise hijack: the
deterministic policy becomes constant because noise dominates the
reward signal, regardless of W.

R56's mechanism analysis (CLM-0063) showed LSTM bypasses this
because the policy is structurally time-varying — the gradient
flows through `h_t`, not just `obs_t`, breaking the noise-flat
reward landscape.

**Open question**: does LSTM combined WITH the anti-smoothness
reward reach a HIGHER ceiling than LSTM alone? Two competing
hypotheses:

- **Synergistic (positive)**: anti-smoothness explicitly rewards
  temporal variation. With LSTM's structurally time-varying output
  no longer noise-dominated, the reward term might align with the
  policy's actual variation and amplify it. Could push s51's 0.526
  toward 0.6+.

- **Antagonistic (negative)**: anti-smoothness with λ=-100 may
  still hijack via SOME channel (e.g., LSTM hidden state stochastic
  drift looks like noise to a smoothness-penalising critic), in
  which case combining them gives no improvement or worse.

This is the only remaining structural-pivot lever from the R55
verdict that hasn't been tested. Resolving it sharpens the
boundary of what LSTM unlocks.

## Candidates

(cheapest first)

- **Single-seed pilot**: re-run s51 with `LAMBDA_SMOOTH=-100
  SMOOTHNESS_WINDOW=1` (R50 setting) and same hyperparameters as
  R56-α. ~12 min wall. If s51's geo > 0.526 → synergistic.
- **3-seed sweep at W=1**: 49/50/51 × W=1. ~12 min wall parallel.
- **3-seed sweep at W=10**: same seeds, R55 setting. ~12 min wall.
- **Both W=1 and W=10**: 6 trainings, 2 batches of 3-parallel.
  ~25 min total wall.

Suggested order: single-seed pilot first (kill-switch — if s51 stays
< 0.526, abandon; if s51 > 0.526, expand to 3-seed).
