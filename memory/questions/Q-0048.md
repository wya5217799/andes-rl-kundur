## Why it matters

The current controller separates fast common-inertia support from slow
droop--PI active-power restoration, but it withdraws the fast layer after an
open-loop 3 s clock. That is a valid frozen benchmark, not evidence that the
controller has solved general fast--slow coupling. The professor feedback and
the bounded Deep Research synthesis identify the missing causal object as the
handoff decision itself: recovery phase, slow-layer takeover, differential
state, hysteresis, dwell time, and bumpless withdrawal.

This question tests the cheapest falsifiable repair before any learned gate or
new MARL architecture. A fixed 5 s comparator is mandatory because an adaptive
gate may otherwise appear better only by consuming a longer fast-support
budget.

## Candidates

- Selected in R291: deterministic common-frequency recovery gate with causal
  filtered error rate, hysteresis, confirmation dwell, and 1 s taper.
- Selected ablation in R291: full handoff gate adding inter-area differential
  frequency and slow requested--actual power gap.
- Duration controls in R291: slow-only, fixed 3 s, and fixed 5 s.
- Not authorized: learned switching, actor horizon extension, reward changes,
  per-device MARL actions, MPC, topology, GFM-BESS, motor-load, or EMT changes.
