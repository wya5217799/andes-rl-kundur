## Why it matters

R294 rejected exact common--differential decoupling, but established a genuine
neighbour-only DAPI controller with independent local states and actions.  Its
held-out synchronization loss was slightly better than the centralized vector
PI reference, while its fast inter-area IAE was slightly worse.  The DAPI
integral-consensus time scale was fixed heuristically at `1.0 1/s`; on the ring
communication graph, its differential decay is therefore comparable to the
roughly 0.9 s period of the registered inter-area mode.  Before adding a
neural residual, test whether that physically interpretable time-scale
mismatch explains and can reduce the observed tradeoff.

## Candidates

- Run a sealed three-arm, four-scenario development probe with the explicit
  local-agent DAPI implementation, changing only the consensus gain from the
  registered `1.0 1/s` baseline to graph-spectrally derived `2.0` and
  `4.0 1/s` candidates.
- If one candidate passes, freeze it for a separate held-out confirmation
  before any neural residual is authorized.
- If neither candidate passes, stop gain tuning and formulate a bounded
  antisymmetric neighbour-edge residual with an explicit common-mode guard.
