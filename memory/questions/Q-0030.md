## Why it matters

R267 closes the hand-designed state-selector family.  Its slew limiter reduced
raw-gate variation but could not retain both physical directions or approach
the static controller's action variation.  Continuing to tune thresholds,
capacities, or smoothing constants would now be post-hoc algorithm hunting.

The next scientifically distinct mechanism is training/deployment-consistent
bounded residual control around a physical droop prior.  It directly tests the
programme thesis while avoiding any claim based on recurrent checkpoints
affected by the R261 target-alignment defect.

This question remains in P0: the first task is to validate the corrected
residual interface, objective, and feasibility envelope.  A successful pilot
may open a separate P1 multi-seed mechanism question; it is not itself that
claim.

## Pre-commitment for the future round

1. Reuse the V4 ANDES environment, existing off-policy agent infrastructure,
   physical endpoint code, and sealed-bank statistics wherever their contracts
   remain correct.
2. Begin with a corrected memoryless policy so recurrent-target history cannot
   contaminate the mechanism test.
3. Apply the same bounded residual composition during training and evaluation;
   unit-test bounds, reset semantics, checkpoint reload, and deterministic
   evaluation before launching training.
4. Define a reference-feasible disturbance envelope prospectively.  Common
   baseline infeasibility must be reported separately from residual-specific
   failure and may not be hidden by dropping sealed rows.
5. Use a subtractive pilot kill gate before multi-seed training.  A failed
   pilot closes the exact residual contract; a passed pilot advances to at
   least three independent training seeds and a new sealed evaluation bank.
6. Keep topology generalisation, graph policies, stability certification,
   cross-simulator work, and manuscript production out of this question.

## Decision direction

- Advance only if the residual implementation is training/evaluation
  identical and the pilot improves the predeclared physical mechanism without
  action, saturation, failure, or settling regression.
- Treat any single-seed pilot as feasibility evidence only.  A reproducible
  algorithm claim requires independent seeds and a sealed bank.
- If the pilot fails, diagnose whether the blocker is objective design,
  residual parameterisation, or environment feasibility before any second
  architecture; do not restart a broad algorithm sweep.
