## Why it matters

R388 validly stops the exact four-REGCV1 card and Pref/Qref intervention before
deterministic control or learning. A validated five-family route census and an
installed-source audit identify stock REGF2 as the only immediately eligible
successor that is materially different, remains a VSM/VSG object, preserves
the ANDES/Kundur platform, and admits one upstream falsifiable test. Its model
name and source structure do not establish that four devices can coexist on
this operating point or initialize cleanly.

## Prospective contract

- Keep ANDES 2.0.0, the packaged Kundur XLSX/JSON static tables, the 10-bus,
  15-line connectivity, and controlled buses/generators 1--4 unchanged.
- Derive the same deterministic static-only case used by the clean REGCV1
  route, then add exactly four stock REGF2 devices mapped one-for-one to
  static generators and buses 1--4. Freeze every explicit REGF2 parameter at
  the installed 2.0.0 default, except the declared 900-MVA device rating and
  identity/mapping fields.
- Bind the installed REGF1/REGF2 source hashes. Require exact source/derived
  static-table identity, four active REGF2 records, their required PLL
  inventory, zero legacy synchronous-machine/governor/exciter/event records,
  and no forbidden model token in the initialized DAE names.
- Run one zero-action job through setup, power flow, a post-power-flow/pre-init
  static P/Q snapshot, native TDS initialization, and 0.2 seconds of TDS at
  native tolerance `1e-4`. No post-init Pref, Qref, Paux, Qaux, controller,
  disturbance, reward, or training action is permitted.
- Serialize complete initialization diagnostics, all nonzero/non-finite
  residual rows, clamped-limit rows, solver flags, trace identity, and finite
  model/DAE values. Require exact mapping/reference preservation at absolute
  tolerance `1e-12`, bus voltage in `[0.9, 1.1]` pu, device current magnitude
  no greater than `10.0` pu, apparent power no greater than the declared
  device rating, virtual frequency in `[0.95, 1.05]` pu, and zero-input drift
  no greater than `2e-4` system pu over the short trajectory.
- A malformed source, schema, diagnostic, trace, or provenance record is
  `ANALYSIS-INVALID`. A complete native initialization, convergence, residual,
  finite-value, electrical-guard, or drift failure is a scientific STOP and
  closes stock REGF2 before authority testing. PASS opens only a separately
  registered audit of the actual post-init Paux/Qaux dynamic seam.
