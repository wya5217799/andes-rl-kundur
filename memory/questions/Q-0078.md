## Why it matters

R317--R322 reject static inversion, unconstrained quadratic feedback, direct
pole targeting, and a post-hoc scalar repair path. R322 does not identify one
dominant failure mechanism, but it does show that usable actuator authority and
estimation behavior must enter synthesis jointly rather than being checked
only after nominal pole design.

## Candidates

- Formulate one finite-horizon output-feedback problem with the exact one-step
  delay, retained cross blocks, four coordinate actions, node mapping, and hard
  power/ramp/SOC limits. Derive output/action normalization from the sealed
  R319 scales and physical ceilings; do not search weights or horizons after a
  response is visible.
- Keep one matched cross-deleted synthesis arm with the same information,
  action, limits, timing, horizon, solver budget, development bank, and
  objective. Execute both on the same full retained plants.
- Use only nominal matrices and the registered development bank to establish
  finite feasibility, nominal behavior, and one fixed future candidate. Do not
  load R321 examination outcomes into formulation or tuning.
- Stop at formulation infeasibility, development no-go, or one bounded fresh-
  holdout candidate. Do not run ANDES, implement distributed agents, define a
  reward, or train a neural controller.
