## Why it matters

The new line changes the physical device object rather than the network.  No
controller, P/Q-decoupling comparison, or learning study is meaningful until
the four candidate converter-level VSG devices can replace the existing
dynamic generator chains, initialize together, expose independently addressable
active/reactive references, and remain at their no-event operating point.

## Prospective contract

- Use ANDES 2.0.0 and the packaged `kundur/kundur_full.xlsx` case without
  changing buses, branches, loads, static-generator locations, or connectivity.
- Disable the four `GENROU` devices and every attached governor/exciter chain;
  attach exactly one `REGCV1` device to each existing static generator at buses
  1--4.
- Use linked static-generator `Sn`, 60-Hz rating, the registered `REGCV1`
  parameter card, default double-loop gains, no disturbance, no controller,
  no reward, and no training.
- Before TDS, verify exact `RenGen` `Pref/Qref` read-write-restore behavior with
  one-next-representable-value probes and confirm no non-target setpoint moves.
  This is an interface-identity test, not dynamic authority evidence.
- Run one fresh zero-input formal job through setup, power flow, TDS
  initialization, and 0.2 seconds of TDS.  Require four active `REGCV1`
  identities with the exact bus/static-generator mapping, no active replaced
  synchronous chain, successful native solver checks, finite DAE/model values,
  and maximum registered zero-input drift no larger than the recorded native
  TDS convergence tolerance.
- PASS opens a separate signed dynamic `Pref/Qref` authority question.  A valid
  object, initialization, interface, finite-value, or drift failure stops this
  `REGCV1` formulation.  Provenance or execution-integrity failure is invalid
  and cannot be repaired or retried inside R384.
