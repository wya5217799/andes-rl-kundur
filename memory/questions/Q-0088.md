## Why it matters

The ICEMS manuscript is titled "Decoupling-Oriented Coordination of
Paralleled VSGs With Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning", but its current R280
comparison has one centrally aggregated scalar action. R292 executed a genuine
three-edge distributed policy but its frozen matrix was invalid, and R293 was
aborted before a formal conclusion. The paper therefore still lacks a valid,
matched distributed-versus-single-agent comparison.
