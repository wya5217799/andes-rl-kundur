## Why it matters

CLM-0161 + CLM-0162 narrow the mechanism candidate for the R57-R82
91-round 0.391 plateau to **transient-phase critic data starvation**:
the SOTA critic is competent in steady-state (step ≥ 10, argmax_dist =
0.158, 2.5% bad-fraction) but as confused as off-manifold during
disturbance immediate (step 0-2, argmax_dist = 0.913, 100% bad). The
critic sees only ~6% of training samples from the regime where the
6-axis paper-grade metric is dominated.

If transient-replay-reweighting breaks 0.391 (geo > 0.45 say, > +15%
over R72_w4 SOTA): mechanism layer confirmed sufficient, paper has a
clean fix with quantitative defense. Cheapest single-knob experiment
in the whole R57+ series.

If it does not (geo ≤ 0.40, within 2.5% of baseline): mechanism candidate
falsified, search continues. Q-0014's "obs ceiling" sub-hypothesis
becomes priority.

## Candidates

- (cheapest) Modify TD3 replay sampling weight: for sample at episode
  step `t`, set weight `w(t) = max(1, 5 × (1 - t/5))` so steps 0-4 get
  5×, step 5 gets 1×. Implement as importance-weighted sampling in
  the replay buffer. 1 seed × 75 ep on R72_w4 hyper. ~30 min WSL.
- 2-stage curriculum: train normally for 50 ep, then up-weight transient
  for 25 ep. More expensive (~45 min).
- Synthetic transient pre-training: generate 10K synthetic (s₀,
  random_a, env.step(s₀, a)) tuples by re-running ANDES from t=0 with
  varied initial perturbations, train critic on these for 5 ep before
  normal RL. Most expensive (~90 min).
