## Why it matters

R310 isolated the remaining end-to-end failure to one interface seam. Its
fresh source manifest and all non-EVAL physical/solver guards were intact, but
EVAL-v2 rejected every derived edge view because Stage-1 records had no
pre-aggregated `guards` object and the adapter did not synthesize EVAL-v2's
required mapping from authoritative source fields. This synthesis must be
specified and tested independently before another
claim-bearing fresh Stage-1 bank is justified.

## Candidates

- Define the smallest explicit fail-closed synthesis of EVAL-v2 `guards` from
  authoritative Stage-1 completion, initialization, per-step exit-code, and
  finite-telemetry fields; reject missing, false, or unknown inputs.
- Use a source-hash-bound positive/negative R310 edge pair only as immutable
  adapter-test fixtures. Do not rewrite R310 records, views, scorecards,
  analysis, provenance, or verdict.
- Require the canary EVAL input-integrity and execution-contract checks to pass
  under the unchanged `vector_power`, 1-s active-window profile.
- Stop after adapter-contract PASS or INVALID. The canary cannot authorize a
  physical claim, predictor, controller, distributed-agent comparison, MARL,
  or training.
