## Why it matters

R318 shows that the R317 DC-inverse static law fails structurally at the
augmented-pole gate even at the smallest registered scalar. A replacement must
design against the causal delay itself instead of hiding it in a smaller
post-outcome gain. It must also expose the state-estimation assumption before
any physical closed-loop bank.

## Candidates

- Freeze one full-order observer-based discrete-time quadratic-regulator
  construction on each R316 realization augmented with the exact one-sample
  measurement/action timing. Declare state/output/action weights, observer
  poles or covariance model, scheduling information, and initial estimator
  state before calculation.
- Preserve the four common/differential actions, full cross blocks, physical
  node mapping, power/ramp/SOC limits, development/examination separation, and
  a matched cross-deleted comparator with identical information and budget.
- Require nominal controller and observer poles, finite estimation error,
  governed development feasibility, the finite R316 mismatch stress, and the
  same two-percent practical improvement floor before admitting the law.
- Stop at offline PASS, NO-GO, or invalid. Do not run ANDES, implement
  distributed agents, define rewards, or train a neural controller.
