## Why it matters

R325 passes every source, comparison, trace, and replay guard but cannot finish
the registered development bank because the sealed SLSQP implementation
terminates early in both arms. This is not evidence that the underlying convex
program is mathematically infeasible. A scratch-only engineering diagnostic
found that a specialized sparse-QP path may remove the numerical bottleneck,
but that diagnostic is not formal evidence and cannot repair R325 post hoc.

## Candidates

- Preserve the R325 reduced models, one-sample delay, information pattern,
  horizon, objective, physical limits, action map, development cases, matched
  comparison, estimand, gates, and stopping tree exactly. Change only the
  prospective solver implementation and its explicitly pinned dependency and
  numerical settings.
- Establish convexity and a unique optimum, then report primal, dual, and
  explicit physical-constraint residuals. Compare both actions and predicted
  outputs against every successful R325 SLSQP prefix before reading any
  conditional holdout.
- Require one complete deterministic development-bank execution and an
  independent full replay with identical row-level results. Any solver error,
  execution error, residual failure, prefix disagreement, or replay mismatch
  is a no-go.
- Keep the R325 holdout inaccessible until development admission passes under
  the new prospective seal. Do not run ANDES, alter the plant, design a reward,
  implement distributed agents, or train a neural controller in this question.
