## Why it matters

CLM-0191 (R105 audit) documented that R72_w4 LSTM SOTA was trained
with `phi_abs=50, phi_h=phi_d=0.0056` — not paper Eq.14 literal
(`phi_abs=0, phi_h=phi_d=1`). CLM-0192 decided to keep R72_w4 as
canonical but disclose the divergence in paper write-up.

For paper-purity defense, the natural follow-up: train an LSTM agent
with `V4Config.paper_strict_pure()` (phi_abs=0, phi_h=phi_d=1, paper
Eq.14 literal) and report its `paper_grade_axes` 11-axis geo on
LS1+LS2. Three possible outcomes:

- **paper_strict_pure geo ≥ 0.39** (≥ R72_w4): PHI_ABS=50 doesn't
  matter for `paper_grade_axes` performance. R72_w4 is incidentally
  the SOTA on this reward, but paper-pure reward would have done as
  well. Drop PHI_ABS from V4Config default, retrain everything from
  paper-pure starting point. Big rewrite.
- **paper_strict_pure geo ∈ [0.30, 0.39]**: PHI_ABS modestly helps;
  the augmentation has a real ~5-25% advantage. Defend as engineering
  choice in paper, cite R20 archive for original motivation.
- **paper_strict_pure geo < 0.30**: PHI_ABS is critical for performance
  on paper_grade_axes eval. Strong argument that the paper's literal
  reward is under-specified — paper's DDIC -8.04 cum_rf at 50 ep might
  not be the right RL formulation for this benchmark. Big claim,
  publishable as "paper Eq.14 ill-fits Kundur tight coupling".

Either way, the answer disambiguates CLM-0191/0192's disclosure caveat.

## Candidates

- **A1 (~2h wall)**: train 1 seed × 75 ep with `V4Config.paper_strict_pure()`
  + R72_w4 hyper (td3_lstm h64 tau=0.001 lstm-lr-warmup=5 normalize-actions),
  evaluate via standard `paper_grade_axes`. Single ANDES TDS session.
  Lowest-effort entry.
- **A2 (~6h wall, 3 seeds × 75 ep)**: full multi-seed for statistical
  confidence. After A1 result if borderline.
- **A3 (~24h wall, 3 seeds × 500 ep)**: paper-budget run (paper says
  "after 500 ep stabilises"). Highest cost, only if A1+A2 inconclusive.
