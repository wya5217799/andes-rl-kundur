## Why it matters

R359 rejects only the pre-registered fixed affine information-to-action map:
on the exposed sixteen-case bank its common-coordinate gain is about 0.503%
against the 2% threshold and the differential coordinate degrades 6.1-6.8x.
The manuscript line still cannot distinguish "the exact neighbour information
path lacks learnable structure" from "the affine function class is too
restrictive". That distinction is the preregistered stop-or-continue fork:
if a pre-registered flexible non-neural map family also fails both endpoint
groups, the learning route terminates by contract; if any member passes, a
separately registered successor question is opened and training stays
blocked.

## Prospective contract

- Keep the R352 neighbour-local deterministic controller as the frozen base
  controller, the development/holdout split, the exact fifteen-field
  `LocalEdgeObservation` information contract, the three-edge independent
  normalized action path, the physical projection, the startup mask, and the
  R358 development targets exactly as in R359.
- Replace the per-edge fixed affine map with one pre-registered flexible
  non-neural map family containing exactly three frozen members: (1) an RBF
  kernel-ridge regressor whose kernel width is the fixed training-pair median
  distance heuristic and whose regularization is frozen at `1e-3`; (2) a
  k-nearest-neighbour regressor with `k=5`; and (3) a quadratic polynomial
  basis with standardization and no regularization. Every member is fit per
  edge by leave-one-scenario-out on development rows only. No hyperparameter,
  kernel, seed, reward, or candidate scan exists; no neural network and no
  reinforcement-learning algorithm is used.
- Judge each member by the same two endpoint groups as R359 -- inertia-
  weighted common-coordinate IAE and differential-coordinate energy under
  both nominal and mismatch-bounded response maps -- with the registered
  2% paired mean, one-sided 95% upper bound, subgroup-direction, and 1.05
  worst-case ratio gates. Endpoint consequences after the exact physical
  projection are primary; action-vector imitation of the non-unique oracle
  remains diagnostic only.
- A pass by at least one member authorizes only one separately registered
  successor question (e.g., a deterministic nonlinear residual design); it
  does not authorize holdout evaluation, neural training, new simulation, or
  EVAL.
- A fail by every member fires the preregistered stop: the learning route
  terminates, Q-0097 closes negative, and no in-place repair, seed change,
  kernel scan, threshold relaxation, or expanded bank is authorized.
- No branch authorizes neural or reinforcement-learning training, a new ANDES
  trajectory, large simulation, EVAL, title validation, stability, safety,
  robustness, topology generalization, hardware, or deployment claims.
