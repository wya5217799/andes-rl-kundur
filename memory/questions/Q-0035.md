## Why it matters

R272 established a source-hashed, energy-feasible active-power interface and
found a large restoration signal on 13 complete matched pairs, but the
registered formal result is INVALID: the identical-DAE zero-support baseline
failed 3/20 scenarios and droop+PI failed 7/20.  Performance cannot be
interpreted until the baseline numerical failures are attributed.

There are two materially different explanations.  The existing `[-3,+3]`
system-pu disturbance generator may exceed the numerical or physical
feasibility envelope of the Kundur plant, or adding four zero-command ESD1
states may itself introduce a DAE/initialization confound.  Shrinking the bank
or changing the actuator before distinguishing them would turn a correctness
problem into post-hoc tuning.

## Pre-commitment for the next round

1. Run no learned controller, droop+PI comparison, gain/capacity/placement
   change, topology study, or manuscript work.
2. Reproduce the three shared R272 baseline failures and a signed set of
   complete controls under two plants only: original V4 and the identical
   zero-support storage DAE.  Use the same environment seed, M/D=200/100,
   timing, solver configuration, and disturbance definitions.
3. Hash the source, cases, solver configuration, and every diagnostic trace
   before interpretation.  Retain every failed row and report the exact TDS
   termination time and initialization/DAE state.
4. Use completion and solver diagnostics only for the primary attribution.
   Do not inspect or reuse droop+PI performance endpoints to select a future
   envelope.
5. If both plants fail the same cases, map a prospectively bounded signed,
   multi-location zero-support feasibility envelope and report the excluded
   fraction.  If only the storage DAE fails, repair its initialization/model
   contract and rerun V4 equivalence tests before proposing another bank.
6. Any future authority re-test requires a separately sealed round and bank;
   R272's 13 complete pairs remain diagnostic only.

## Decision direction

- **ENVELOPE-INFEASIBLE:** original V4 and identical-DAE zero support fail the
  same registered cases.  Freeze a narrower, explicitly conditional plant
  envelope without changing controller or actuator parameters.
- **STORAGE-DAE-CONFOUND:** original V4 completes while identical-DAE zero
  support fails.  Repair initialization/model correctness; do not shrink the
  bank to hide the confound.
- **MIXED:** failures have both causes.  Close the model defect first, then
  establish a separate feasible-envelope contract.
- **UNRESOLVED/INVALID:** attribution or provenance checks fail.  Stop without
  another authority experiment.
