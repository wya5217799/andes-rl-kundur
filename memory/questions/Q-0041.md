## Why it matters

R278 was a valid negative pilot: one parameter-shared, memoryless TD3 policy
improved synchronization but did not clear the registered inter-area
co-primary endpoint. External adversarial review then identified a narrower
unresolved question. The executed controller is one centrally aggregated
rank-one scalar, and its only measured reference was `q=0`; therefore the
observed signal may be explained by a simple causal inter-area feedback law,
a size-matched centralized scalar actor, or a favorable training seed.

This is a new prospective identifiability study, not a continuation or rescue
of the stopped Q-0038 gate. R278 remains `PILOT-NO-GO` regardless of the
outcome.

## Pre-commitment

1. Keep the R274 slow droop+PI storage layer, R275 common-inertia pulse,
   rank-one action coordinate, plant, solver, physical endpoints, action
   bounds, and storage guards unchanged.
2. First quantify what the action contract actually guarantees: exact
   aggregate inertia-budget preservation and one rank-one inter-area input
   direction. Treat any common/differential dynamic separation as an
   empirical leakage result, never as an algebraic consequence.
3. Freeze one causal classical law using only current and previous
   observations available to the learned controller. Its gain family,
   development-bank selection budget, and guards must be sealed before any
   new trajectory.
4. Compare the parameter-shared actor with a size-matched centralized actor
   that receives the same joint observation and emits the same bounded scalar
   `q`. Match TD3 critic, reward, training steps, seeds, and tuning budget.
5. Use newly predeclared training seeds; report every seed. Do not select or
   headline seed 49.
6. Freeze all controllers before generating and completion-screening a fresh
   signed, multi-location 24-case formal bank. Preserve paired scenarios,
   hierarchical seed/scenario uncertainty, tail risk, failures, and all
   physical/action/storage guards.
7. Do not change HAWE, reward, architecture, amplitude, horizon, or thresholds
   after any formal endpoint is visible. Do not modify the manuscript during
   this question.

## Decision direction

- **MARL-IDENTIFIABLE-POSITIVE:** the shared architecture reproducibly clears
  both differential endpoints and materially beats `q=0`, the frozen causal
  law, and the size-matched centralized actor on the fresh bank while every
  guard passes.
- **CAUSAL-EXPLANATION-SUFFICIENT:** the causal law explains the attainable
  margin and the shared policy has no guarded incremental value over it.
- **CENTRALIZED-EXPLANATION-SUFFICIENT:** the centralized scalar actor matches
  or exceeds the shared architecture, so the multi-agent factorization has no
  demonstrated incremental value.
- **NO-REPRODUCIBLE-LEARNED-VALUE:** neither learned architecture clears the
  prospective multi-seed unseen-bank gate.
- **INVALID:** provenance, sealing, completion, matching, action, physical,
  storage, statistical, or implementation contracts fail.
