## Why it matters

R359 and R360 reject four endpoint-only information-to-action map families
(fixed affine, RBF kernel ridge, k-NN, quadratic polynomial) on the exposed
development bank, and the preregistered stop fires: no holdout, simulation, or
training is authorized. The deep-research survey
(`working/deep_research_neighbour_information_learnability.md`, section 8)
identifies the bottleneck as the information path rather than map capacity:
distributed-coordination results (mutual damping, DMPC prediction sharing,
dynamic average consensus, attention-weighted neighbour observations) all rely
on explicitly exchanged neighbour messages, and no surveyed result shows that
a frozen fifteen-field endpoint observation is sufficient to recover the R358
physical optimum. This question tests the strongest surveyed direction by
changing only the information path: each edge actor receives, at the same
causal instant, the endpoint observations of its two one-hop neighbour nodes
on the frozen communication ring, while every other contract term stays
byte-identical to R360.

## Prospective contract

- Keep the R352 neighbour-local deterministic controller as the frozen base
  controller, the R359/R360 development/holdout split, the physical
  projection, the startup mask, the R358 development targets (ten accepted
  physical witnesses plus six all-zero negative controls), the three-edge
  independent normalized action path, and the registered 2% paired mean,
  one-sided 95% upper bound, subgroup-direction, and 1.05 worst-case ratio
  gates under both nominal and mismatch-bounded response maps.
- Extend the information path exactly as follows and nothing else: on the
  frozen communication ring `{(0,1),(1,2),(2,3),(0,3)}`, each action-edge
  actor `(i,j)` receives the four-field neighbour message (frequency
  deviation, RoCoF, SOC, voltage) of its two one-hop neighbour nodes, ordered
  as `(source-side neighbour, target-side neighbour)`. This adds exactly
  eight fields to the fifteen-field vector, giving a fixed twenty-three field
  per-edge observation. The message content is exactly the neighbour node's
  causal pre-action frequency, RoCoF, SOC, and voltage; it is never the
  neighbour's action, edge flow, command, scenario identity, disturbance
  identity, future value, realized endpoint, or oracle value. The four-field
  message is chosen so that every frozen family, including the quadratic
  polynomial basis (299 columns at 23 fields), remains fittable under the
  frozen 345-row leave-one-scenario-out training folds.
- Judge the same four pre-registered tuning-free non-neural map families as
  R359/R360 (fixed affine, RBF kernel ridge with frozen median-width and
  `1e-3` regularization, k-NN with `k=5`, quadratic polynomial basis with
  standardization and no regularization) per edge by leave-one-scenario-out
  development projections under the unchanged two endpoint groups. Every
  family stays frozen; no hyperparameter, kernel, seed, reward, or candidate
  scan exists; no neural or reinforcement-learning algorithm is used.
- A pass by at least one family authorizes only one separately registered
  successor question (e.g., a deterministic nonlinear residual design or a
  neural-go verification); it does not authorize holdout evaluation, neural
  training, new simulation, or EVAL by itself.
- A fail by every family further weakens the information-path hypothesis as
  surveyed: it authorizes no in-place repair, seed change, kernel scan,
  threshold relaxation, expanded bank, or alternative information variant
  under this contract; any continuation must register a mechanistically
  different falsifiable question.
- No branch authorizes neural or reinforcement-learning training, a new ANDES
  trajectory, large simulation, EVAL, title validation, stability, safety,
  robustness, topology generalization, hardware, or deployment claims.
