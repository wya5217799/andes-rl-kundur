## Why it matters

R391/CLM-1100 and R392/CLM-1105 stop the stock REGF2 object: two positive-real
local modes carried jointly by coupled internal loops, plus eight conserved
integrator directions (two per device) that make the equilibrium manifold
non-isolated. An external control-design analysis proposes a structural
redesign: delete the power-sensing/limit-PI chain, restore a dissipative swing
equation with projected P limits, use a gradient-flow Q-V outer loop with
projected Q limits, and add a virtual resistor to the inner loop. This
question asks the smallest upstream gate that can falsify that proposal on a
survey-conformant two-unit diagnostic cell before any authority, decoupling,
controller, or learning work.

## Prospective contract

- Keep ANDES 2.0.0 and the unchanged packaged/derived Kundur static case
  (ten-bus, fifteen-line). Replace StaticGen 1 and 2 (buses 1, 2) with two
  PPVSM1 devices; StaticGen 3 and 4 remain static anchors. Forbidden models
  absent; system base 100 MVA; device rating 900 MVA.
- Frozen PPVSM1 card: fn=60, mf=0.15, wdrp=0.033, D_P=1/wdrp, Qdrp=0.045,
  k_rho=20, rho_rate_max=10, KPv=3, KIv=10, KPi=0.5, KIi=20, Te=0.005,
  rf=0, xf=0.2 (device base), Rv=0.05 (system base), Pmax/Pmin=+-1 and
  Qmax/Qmin=+-1 (device base), dwmax/dwmin=+-75.
- One serial arm: build, setup, power flow, native TDS initialization/test,
  zero-input 0.2-second trajectory, and equilibrium EIG of the reduced state
  matrix; all integrity, residual, finite-value, catalog, and no-time-advance
  guards as in R389-R391.
- Frozen gates: drift ceiling 2e-4 system pu (R389); any eigenvalue with
  Re > 1e-7 is STOP-PPVSM1-POSITIVE-REAL; any root with |lambda| < 1e-6
  beyond exactly one allowed network common-angle degeneracy is
  STOP-PPVSM1-NEUTRAL-DEGENERACY; a complete valid pass is
  PPVSM1-OBJECT-PASS and opens only a signed authority gate.
- No authority, controller, decoupling, MARL, topology change, EMT, HIL, or
  deployment work is authorized by this round. Exact steady-state droop-slope
  matching against the stopped object is a later verification, not this gate.
