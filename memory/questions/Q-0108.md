## Why it matters

R389 validly stops the exact stock four-REGF2 object because a no-exogenous-
action 0.2-second trajectory leaves the registered stationarity envelope. The
sealed trace alone cannot distinguish growth already present in the local
linearized model from a trajectory-integration or other numerical/model-solver
effect. This mechanism-only gate asks the smallest upstream question that can
separate those explanations without changing the converter object or opening
an authority, controller, or learning experiment.

## Prospective contract

- Keep ANDES 2.0.0, the packaged and derived Kundur static cases, 10-bus and
  15-line connectivity, four controlled locations, stock REGF2 model, PLL
  ownership, 900-MVA ratings, and every explicit model parameter identical to
  R389. Bind all installed source and case hashes independently.
- Build two fresh systems serially. The reference arm uses the R389 native TDS
  tolerance `1e-4`; the numerical-sensitivity arm changes only that solver
  initialization tolerance to `1e-6`. Neither arm calls `TDS.run()` or applies
  an action, controller, disturbance, reward, or training operation.
- In each arm require power-flow convergence, native TDS initialization/test,
  bitwise-identical time and exactly identical archived x/y/z vectors before
  and after the EIG call, complete residual and limit diagnostics, finite
  x/y/z/f/g/Jacobian/state-matrix data, exact object inventory, actual solver-
  tolerance readback, and unique reduced-state names.
- Recompute the eigenspectrum independently from the serialized finite reduced
  matrix. Require dimension/name consistency, bounded normalized eigenpair
  residuals, the registered eigenvector-conditioning guard, and one-to-one
  agreement with the ANDES spectrum. Treat the spectral sign as the scientific
  endpoint, not as record invalidity.
- Require the two fresh arms to agree on positive-real-mode count and on the
  exact DAE/reduced-state catalogs, matrix dimension, positive-real-mode count,
  and canonical unordered leading-mode set within the prospectively frozen
  numerical tolerance. A
  disagreement or ill-conditioned leading mode is a scientific numerical-
  resolution STOP, not evidence of stability or instability.
- A complete and reproducible mode with real part greater than `1e-7` is
  `STOP-REGF2-POSITIVE-REAL-GUARD`. It may support only a bounded statement that
  the exact ANDES reduced model contains a reproducible local growing direction;
  participation is diagnostic association, not causal or hardware evidence.
- A valid record with no mode above `1e-7` is
  `REGF2-EIG-ELIGIBLE-NO-POSITIVE-REAL-MODE`; it does not repair R389 or prove
  stability. Malformed provenance/schema/evidence is `ANALYSIS-INVALID`, and a
  complete equilibrium qualification failure is a scientific STOP.
- No branch authorizes Paux/Qaux authority, deterministic control, topology
  change, controller tuning, MARL, EMT, HIL, deployment, or an R389 retry.
