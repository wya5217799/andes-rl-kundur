## Why it matters

R58 best LSTM s51 warmup scores LS1 = -0.053 / LS2 = -0.035 on paper
Sec.IV-C `total_cum_rf` metric. Paper Table III reports DDIC baseline
at LS1 = -0.68 / LS2 = -0.52 — **13-15× larger magnitude** than our
control results. Two interpretations:

1. **Our 20-scen distribution is smaller-magnitude than paper's
   50-scen.** Paper §IV-A describes disturbances as "by load + wind
   capacity range" without a specific magnitude. Our R58
   `generate_test_scenarios()` uses magnitude ∈ [-300, +300] MW and 10
   PQ buses, anchored to LS1 (-248 MW @ bus 8) and LS2 (+188 MW @
   bus 8). Paper's exact distribution unknown.

2. **Our control is genuinely 13× better than paper DDIC.** Possible
   — DDIC is decentralized droop, not RL. Paper's own RL (BC-Centralized)
   reaches -0.04 on similar scenarios. Our -0.053 is in the same
   ballpark, suggesting interpretation (1) might be wrong and our
   numbers are actually paper-comparable.

The two interpretations have opposite implications for paper
positioning:

- If (1): we cannot directly cite "our control beats DDIC by 13×"
  because the disturbance distributions differ. Must replicate
  paper's exact distribution before claiming SOTA-vs-paper.
- If (2): we have a genuine SOTA-vs-paper result and should write it
  up as such — but need a tighter argument about why our env is
  paper-comparable.

This question gates any direct paper-table comparison in the eventual
manuscript.

## Candidates

(cheapest first)

- **Read paper §IV-A more carefully**: scan paper for explicit
  capacity-range or disturbance-magnitude specification. ~20 min.
  Likely insufficient (paper §13 Q-C ambiguity already noted).

- **Email paper authors** (if reachable): ask for disturbance
  distribution code or magnitude range. Slow but definitive.

- **Replicate paper's anchors only**: paper cites specific LS1 = -248
  MW, LS2 = +188 MW examples. Our R58 anchors match. So the
  per-anchor numbers (LS1=-0.053, LS2=-0.035) ARE
  apples-to-apples — only the 50-scen aggregate magnitude
  differs. Conclusion: interpretation (1) is **partially wrong** —
  anchors are paper-comparable. ~10 min analytic.

- **Inspect ANDES Kundur param values**: H, D, T constants of our
  Kundur 4-VSG vs paper's. Different param values would scale the
  control response. ~30 min.

- **Run paper-Eq.14 reward + paper Δω convention with our env, score
  ANDES-DDIC as a baseline**: gives our own DDIC reference number on
  our exact distribution. If our DDIC ≈ paper DDIC, distribution
  matches; if our DDIC is 13× smaller, distribution differs. ~2 hr
  to implement adaptive-inertia [25] baseline.
