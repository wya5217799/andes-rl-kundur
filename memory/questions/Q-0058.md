## Why it matters

CLM-0710 validates fixed 2Kv on held-out cases, but the underlying gain ladder
was anchored to one filter magnitude rather than a closed-loop stability or
dissipativity calculation. Continuing with 3Kv or 4Kv simulations would
therefore be an outcome-driven parameter sweep. The next decision must come
from the implemented graph/filter/actuator model and must preserve the
difference between controller-level zero-sum separation and plant decoupling.

## Candidates

- Exact graph-coordinate and discrete-filter analysis using the implemented
  ring, sample period and filter constant.
- Sampled augmented small-signal diagnostic using the retained R294 fixed-anchor
  ANDES matrix and explicit ESD1/DAPI/filter states.
- At most one nonlinear sufficiency probe if the model identifies a unique
  candidate; otherwise retain 2Kv and stop.
- EVAL-v2 diagnostic on completed formal records, without treating its
  scorecard as claim authority.
