## Why it matters

R281's development-level heterogeneity probe (CLM-0615) showed that the
damping sensitivity to differential inertia allocation roughly doubles when
aggregate VSG inertia is halved (M0=100: +53% at q=+0.25; M0=300: ~0-20%),
and mode identification broke at M0=100, q=-0.25. This is the first measured
anchor for the SCI upgrade's weak-grid axis (C2 in
`paper/sci_upgrade_survey/REPORT.md` §7): if allocation matters more in
weaker/lower-inertia systems, the generalization section of the journal
extension has a physical gradient to report rather than a flat robustness
claim. Leaving it open keeps C2 without measured grounding.

Note: not yet ranked in `RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md` priority_questions, so it is
not authorized programme work until the PI/programme adopts it.

## Candidates

- VSG aggregate-inertia scaling probe refinement (fill M0=100 mode-tracking
