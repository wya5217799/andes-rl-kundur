## Why it matters

R332 blocks the direct R329-to-ANDES bridge because the frozen reduced package
injects its test disturbance through the same input channel as control, whereas
the declared physical experiment requires an independently applied PQ/load or
outage disturbance. Without a separately identified physical disturbance
channel, a closed-loop mismatch would confound mathematical input error,
actuator projection, simulator dynamics, and controller performance.

## Candidates

- Prospectively freeze the smallest open-loop HS0/HS1 bank of signed PQ/load
  perturbations that can identify the physical disturbance-to-delivered-output
  map. Do not execute the R329 controller in this bank.
- Freeze device indices, units, bases, signs, initialization, event time,
  stored pre-switch or explicit pre-callback observation, wrapper-owned
  one-sample delay, five wrapper segments with no inherited override, actual TDS
  time-grid readback, and unchanged electrical topology before trajectory access.
- Compare the physical response with a separately identified disturbance
  channel at the same delivered-output endpoint. Retain R329 unchanged as
  reduced-model evidence.
- Record requested, externally projected, internal, and achieved actuator power
  even with zero open-loop control. Fail on M/D drift, hidden limiter/recovery
  activation, source drift, solver failure, non-finite states, or boundary
  ambiguity.
- Apply any charging margin to internal system-base `Psum`, requiring
  `Psum > -pmx + epsilon` or exact installed behavior. Separately guard achieved
  power, current lag, SOC motion, ramp, and one-step energy.
- Use a prospective `ALLOW`, `QUALIFY`, `BLOCK`, or invalid-data rule. Unless
  the identified physical channel is proven equivalent to R329's frozen channel
  under predeclared criteria, reserve a separately sealed successor estimator-
  controller instead of tuning R329.
- Preserve the conference title exactly. No deterministic physical closed loop,
  distributed runtime, reward, agent, neural training, or EVAL is authorized by
  this question alone.
