## Why it matters

R358 proves that physically admissible residual actions exist in ten exposed
linear-response cases, but those actions were selected with the complete
realized outcome. The paper still lacks evidence that three independently
executed edge actors can select a useful residual from only their two endpoint
observations and causal history. Without that bridge, neural training would
confound physical headroom with information leakage.

## Prospective contract

- Keep the R352 neighbour-local deterministic controller as the frozen base
  controller and keep its development/holdout split unchanged.
- Give each residual edge controller exactly one `LocalEdgeObservation`: its
  two endpoint frequency deviations, RoCoFs, previous commands, states of
  charge, voltages, lower and upper residual-power bounds, plus its own
  previous executed edge flow. Exclude achieved power, point, disturbance,
  sign, scenario identity, other-edge values, joint coordinates, future
  samples, realized endpoints, and oracle labels from the evaluated case.
- Emit one normalized action per edge in the frozen three-edge order. Apply
  every arm through the same magnitude, slew, node-power, node-ramp, energy,
  efficiency, state-of-charge, voltage/current, and readback path.
- Use the ten accepted R358 physical witnesses as development targets and
  all-zero residual targets for the six accepted relaxed-infeasible negative
  controls. The first two residual actions are fixed to zero because the
  stored parent traces cannot reconstruct their complete pre-action history.
- Fit one deterministic standardized affine map per edge with no tuning
  parameter. Development uses leave-one-scenario-out predictions; a formal
  map is fit once on all development rows only. Holdout features may be
  transformed but holdout outcomes and oracle actions may not enter fitting,
  selection, thresholds, or repair.
- Judge the fixed controller by paired common-coordinate IAE and differential-
  coordinate energy after the exact physical projection, not by action-vector
  imitation of a non-unique oracle. Keep the registered two-percent mean,
  one-sided paired, subgroup-direction, and 1.05 worst-case ratio gates.
- A positive result authorizes only one separately registered non-learning
  physical residual intervention. A valid negative result rejects this one
  affine information-to-action formulation, not nonlinear policies or
  multi-agent learning as a class. Any leakage, source drift, incomplete
  inventory, numerical ambiguity, or physical mismatch is invalid.
- No branch authorizes neural or reinforcement-learning training, a new ANDES
  trajectory, large simulation, EVAL, title validation, stability, safety,
  robustness, topology generalization, hardware, or deployment claims.
