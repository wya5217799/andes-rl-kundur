## Why it matters

R274 validates the slow droop+PI/storage layer, and R275 validates independent
fast transient value from one frozen common positive-inertia pulse above that
slow layer. This proves that both time scales are useful, but it does not show
a non-additive interaction or justify training a residual policy.

The cheapest valid diagnosis needs only one missing arm. R274 already froze
identical-storage-DAE zero-support and slow-only traces; R275 froze the
slow-plus-fast traces. A new fast-only arm with zero BESS active-power request
can complete the four-arm factorial comparison without rerunning 72 existing
trajectories.

## Pre-commitment for the next round

1. Reuse by immutable hash the R274 zero-support and slow-only traces and the
   R275 slow-plus-fast traces on the exact 24-case formal bank.
2. Add exactly one fast-only arm: the unchanged R275 `common_M_pos` action
   contract on the identical storage DAE with requested BESS active power
   fixed at zero.
3. Freeze a four-arm interaction on transparent absolute physical endpoint
   scales: `combined - slow - fast + zero`. Also compare combined against the
   best single layer for every registered fast and slow endpoint.
4. Register completion, physical RoCoF/peak/synchronization/inter-area,
   common-frequency restoration, action, SOC/power/energy, tail, paired
   uncertainty, and provenance guards before the first fast-only trajectory.
5. Use three disjoint WSL ANDES shards on the measured 16-core/32-thread,
   24-GiB WSL host; write each of the 24 new trajectories exactly once and
   reuse all 72 existing traces.
6. Do not train RL/GNN, tune either classical layer, change the bank/plant, or
   claim non-additivity from combined-versus-slow improvement alone.

## Decision direction

- **NONADDITIVE-POSITIVE:** the sealed factorial interaction shows material,
  uncertainty-supported beneficial fast/slow synergy and the combined arm
  beats the best single layer while every guard passes.
- **NONADDITIVE-PARTIAL:** only a registered subset shows beneficial
  interaction, with valid contracts and no material harm.
- **ADDITIVE-ONLY:** both layers may remain useful as a classical benchmark,
  but no material non-additive margin survives; remove dual-layer novelty.
- **INVALID:** any reused-trace hash, completion, endpoint, action, storage,
  factorial, or provenance contract fails.
