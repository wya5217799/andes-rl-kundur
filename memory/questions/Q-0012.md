## Why it matters

R48 U-curve (CLM-0054) established h=64 as TD3 MLP peak. But that was
at default hyper (lr=3e-4, gc=1.0, bs=256, ns=5). R64 W5 single-seed
sweep at NEW hyper (lr=3e-3 + combo) shows:

| hidden | best_eval s50 |
|---|---|
| 32 | -0.128 |
| 48 | -0.128 |
| 64 | -0.132 (R64 W2 baseline) |
| 96 | -0.126 (marginal winner) |

Differences within 5% noise band. **U-curve flattens or shifts under
new hyper**. R48's h=64 conclusion was config-dependent.

If h=96 truly marginal best at 3-seed, R64 production candidate
(currently h=64) could be lifted further. If h=32 truly marginal
best, smaller ckpt + faster train (more capacity-efficient).

## Candidates

(cheapest first)

- **3-seed verify h=96** (s49+s51, s50 done): ~16 min wall, decides
  if h=96 worth switching.
- **3-seed verify h=32** (s49, s50, s51): ~24 min wall, decides if
  even smaller is sweet.
- **Comprehensive U-curve at 3-seed** {32, 48, 64, 96}: ~64 min wall,
  full picture but diminishing returns since all within 5%.
