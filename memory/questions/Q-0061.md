## Why it matters

R303 reproduced projection-induced common/differential recoupling, but a
neighbour-only classical allocator closed that ESD1 power-coordinate seam.
That mechanism therefore cannot justify neural training or repair the current
conference title.  A genuinely distributed VSG study first needs a distinct
failure axis: network configuration must materially change the best
differential-inertia allocation, the necessary information must be available
locally at execution, and the strongest matched local classical law must still
leave a reproducible gap.

## Candidates

- Freshly qualify nominal, `Line_0`, and `Line_9` configurations through the
  public topology setter, PFlow, TDS initialization, finite EIG spectrum, and
  positive-real guard.  Prior topology rounds select candidates only.
- Freeze one title-aligned action contract: three edge flows mapped to four
  strictly zero-sum VSG inertia residuals; keep damping, storage, common layer,
  limits, timing, and disturbance bank matched across arms.
- First rerun a fresh `3 topology x 7 zero-sum allocation` EIG matrix.  Require
  material per-configuration oracle headroom before designing any time-domain
  controller comparison.
- Add an explicit `vector_inertia` EVAL profile and request/command/readback
  telemetry before any time-domain result.  A positive R304 can authorize only
  one later 12-case matched classical information gate, never training.
