## Why it matters

R295 showed that large reductions in DAPI's graph-differential integral state
barely changed its differential power request or fast inter-area endpoint.
The next intervention must therefore act directly on the dominant edge-power
path while preserving explicit local execution and the common-mode guard. A
causal filtered relative-RoCoF term is a graph-local virtual-inertia/phase-lead
residual that can be derived from the measured inter-area frequency rather
than selected by a broad gain sweep.

## Candidates

- Add one independently stateful filtered-RoCoF estimator to each local agent,
  exchange that scalar with ring neighbours, and inject `-Kv*L*rocof_filtered`
  on top of the frozen DAPI request.
- Test the frozen baseline and two gains derived as 25% and 50% of the static
  synchronizing action magnitude at the 1.135 Hz anchor mode.
- If a candidate passes the development gate, run a separate full evaluation
  on a new bank not used by R294--R296 selection.
