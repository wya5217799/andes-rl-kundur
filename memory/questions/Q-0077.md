## Why it matters

R321 rejects the exact fixed pole target despite correct nominal placement,
finite traces, and zero projected constraint violations. The governor changes
nearly every raw command and the output-energy endpoint is orders of magnitude
worse than zero control, but that co-occurrence is not a causal diagnosis. A
development-only decomposition is required before selecting any repair; the
used examination bank cannot become tuning data.

## Candidates

- Use only the sealed nominal matrices, fixed gains, and the unchanged 32-case
  development bank. Do not use the 80 examination outcomes to choose a model,
  target, gain, threshold, or repair.
- Separate raw versus projected actions, state-feedback versus estimation-error
  contributions, gain/scaling conditioning, and governor intervention timing.
  Register explicit signatures that distinguish the alternatives before the
  diagnostic execution.
- Admit at most one analytic, structurally distinct repair only if the
  development-only diagnosis identifies it without a target or gain grid.
  Any later efficacy test must reserve a fresh holdout and a new round.
- Stop at diagnostic conflict, mechanism-not-identified, or one bounded repair
  candidate. Do not run ANDES, implement distributed agents, define rewards,
  or train a neural controller.
