## Why it matters

CLM-0910 shows that the frozen centralized deterministic bridge already removes
most of both registered finite-bank coordinate errors. Neural training is only
scientifically justified if a remaining error is material, repeatable,
observable under the intended local/neighbor information pattern, and
correctable within unused actuator, ramp, energy, and state-of-charge headroom.
Otherwise training would spend compute on an unidentified or physically
unavailable residual.

## Prospective contract

- Start with create-only offline analysis of the existing R344 paired records.
  Do not run ANDES, alter R344 artifacts, train a policy, define a reward, or
  choose an architecture during that step.
- Freeze the residual estimand, materiality threshold, local/neighbor
  information test, actuator-headroom test, subgroup handling, and decision
  tree before any new physical or learning execution.
- Separate residual common-frequency error, differential synchronization
  error, controller action, achieved power, limiter margin, ramp margin,
  energy/state-of-charge margin, and model-prediction error. A small nonzero
  residual alone is not training headroom.
- `NO-TRAINING`: the residual is immaterial, not repeatable, not observable
  under the intended deployed information, or lacks physically usable action
  headroom.
- `RESIDUAL-PROBE-ELIGIBLE`: existing evidence supports one separately sealed
  non-learning residual intervention. This does not yet authorize training.
- Neural training becomes eligible only after a later prospective physical
  residual intervention passes and freezes matched deterministic, neural,
  information, action, budget, seed, and evaluation contracts.
