## Why it matters

R283's inertia axis (CLM-0630) hit a branch-swap identification-failure zone
at M0=100/q=-0.25 and M0=150/q=+/-0.25: the max-contrast rule no longer
finds a clean inter-area mode there (area contrast collapses toward 0 at
M0=150/q=-0.25), while M0=200..300 and the q=0 chain stay on-branch. The
inertia-axis strength gradient is therefore unmeasured — not absent — in
exactly the low-inertia region where the C2 weak-grid argument is most
relevant. Characterizing the hybridization zone (where branches mix, and
whether a trackable inter-area branch survives) decides whether the
inertia-axis gradient can ever be sealed at low M0.

Note: not ranked in `RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md` priority_questions, so it is not
authorized programme work until the PI/programme adopts it.

## Candidates

- Dense M0 x q map over M0 in [100, 200) x q in {+/-0.25, +/-0.125} with the
  R283 branch-validity screen, purely to chart where identification fails.
- Mode-tracking alternative: eigenvector-continuation identification (track
  the branch by participation-vector continuity from q=0 instead of
  max-contrast per point) — needs a prospective rule amendment before use.
- Physical attribution: participation of VSG local modes vs GENROU
  inter-area mode across the zone boundary.
