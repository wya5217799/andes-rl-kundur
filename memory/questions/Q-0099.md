## Why it matters

R361 rejects the strongest snapshot-based information extension: adding the
current one-hop neighbour node states (frequency, RoCoF, SOC, voltage) to the
fifteen-field observation still leaves all four pre-registered tuning-free
non-neural map families below both registered endpoint gates. The surveyed
deep-research direction with the strongest external precedent that remains
mechanistically different is DMPC-style shared prediction: distributed
coordination literature (Stewart 2010; Conte 2016; Jin 2021; Dobbe 2017)
validates exchanging predicted future trajectories, not current snapshots, as
the mechanism that lets local controllers approach centralized solutions. This
question tests exactly that information upgrade: each edge actor receives the
frozen R341 model's causal open-loop prediction of its two one-hop neighbour
nodes' future frequency trajectories, while every other contract term stays
byte-identical to R361.

## Prospective contract

- Keep the R352 neighbour-local deterministic controller as the frozen base
  controller, the R359/R360/R361 development/holdout split, the physical
  projection, the startup mask, the R358 development targets (ten accepted
  physical witnesses plus six all-zero negative controls), the three-edge
  independent normalized action path, and the registered 2% paired mean,
  one-sided 95% upper bound, subgroup-direction, and 1.05 worst-case ratio
  gates under both nominal and mismatch-bounded response maps.
- Extend the information path exactly as follows and nothing else: on the
  frozen communication ring `{(0,1),(1,2),(2,3),(0,3)}`, each action-edge
  actor `(i,j)` receives a four-field prediction message from each of its two
  one-hop neighbour nodes, ordered `(source-side neighbour, target-side
  neighbour)` as in R361. Each four-field message is the frozen R341
  separate-input point model's causal open-loop prediction of that neighbour
  node's frequency deviation over the next four `0.2`-second samples,
  generated from the neighbour's causal pre-action sample without reading any
  future, realized, oracle, or other-scenario value. This adds exactly eight
  fields to the fifteen-field vector, giving the same fixed twenty-three
  field per-edge observation as R361, with the message content upgraded from
  snapshot states to causal model predictions.
- The prediction generator is frozen and tuning-free: one R341 order-12
  separate-input point model per operating point, one frozen
  disturbance-augmented estimator (R344 frozen output scales, `0.05`
  disturbance scale, `0.01` measurement fraction), causal estimation advance
  over the observed trace, then open-loop transition with zero future
  residual control and the estimated disturbance held constant. No parameter,
  horizon, seed, reward, or candidate scan exists.
- Judge the same four pre-registered tuning-free non-neural map families as
  R359/R360/R361 (fixed affine, RBF kernel ridge with frozen median-width and
  `1e-3` regularization, k-NN with `k=5`, quadratic polynomial basis with
  standardization and no regularization) per edge by leave-one-scenario-out
  development projections under the unchanged two endpoint groups.
- A pass by at least one family authorizes only one separately registered
  successor question; it does not authorize holdout evaluation, neural
  training, new simulation, or EVAL by itself.
- A fail by every family further weakens the surveyed information-path
  hypothesis: it authorizes no in-place repair, seed change, kernel scan,
  threshold relaxation, expanded bank, prediction-horizon change, or
  alternative information variant under this contract; any continuation must
  register a mechanistically different falsifiable question (e.g., a
  common-channel residual authority).
- No branch authorizes neural or reinforcement-learning training, a new ANDES
  trajectory, large simulation, EVAL, title validation, stability, safety,
  robustness, topology generalization, hardware, or deployment claims.
