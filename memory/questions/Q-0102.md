## Why it matters

R365 established four independently actuated VSG parameter objects, but the
inherited observations still encode the legacy 50-Hz controller scale while
the ANDES plant is 60 Hz.  Existing DAPI and relative-RoCoF assets act through
storage power, whereas this line acts through each VSG's inertia and damping
parameters.  Reusing those controllers directly would make the comparator
more privileged or physically different and would repeat the old mismatch.

## Prospective contract

- Preserve the 60-Hz ANDES plant and adapt every local and neighbour frequency
  and RoCoF observation to the physical 60-Hz scale before any controller or
  learner consumes it.  The inherited 50-Hz reward is outside the new line.
- Treat bounded GENCLS inertia/damping parameter modulation as the first-paper
  actuator.  Record its model-parameter bounds and slew explicitly; do not
  call it storage-energy-feasible, hardware-valid, or safe actuation.
- Build one deterministic local-neighbour adaptive M/D family in which each of
  four device agents consumes only its own seven-field row and independently
  emits its own two-coordinate action.  Reuse locality, state, clipping, and
  testing patterns from older controllers, not their active-power output.
- Freeze a finite gain grid and a development-only selection rule.  All later
  deterministic and learning arms must share observation fields, message
  graph, action coordinates, limits, slew, update rate, plant, disturbances,
  and evaluation endpoints.
- The deterministic family must first beat zero action on physical
  differential-mode endpoints without common-mode or control-stress harm.
  Before training, a separate non-learning direct-action oracle must also show
  time-varying M/D headroom beyond the selected deterministic controller.
- No training is authorized by this design question.  Failure of the unit
  adapter, object-matched controller contract, deterministic efficacy, or
  oracle-headroom gate stops the formulation rather than starting an
  algorithm sweep.
