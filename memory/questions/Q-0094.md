## Why it matters

R352 removes the former information/action mismatch and shows that one strong
neighbour-local three-edge controller sharply reduces differential motion on a
new waveform bank. Neural work remains unjustified unless a further bounded
edge correction can still improve both registered coordinates, can be inferred
from exactly the deployed neighbour information before action, and fits the
same physical governor and device limits.

## Prospective contract

- Use only R352's guarded zero and selected-local records. The joint-information
  diagnostic is excluded from loading, fitting, classification, and claims.
- Treat the exposed ramp-hold bank as development and the already executed
  staggered-rise bank as a residual-analysis holdout. R353 residual targets and
  predictions have not been computed before this registration.
- Fit only on causal endpoint observations and local feasibility history. No
  operating-point, disturbance, sign, scenario, future, joint-coordinate, or
  oracle-result field may enter a deployed edge estimator.
- A positive offline gate requires certified and physically feasible oracle
  residuals, an adequate parent response model on the holdout, and neighbour-
  local residual predictions that improve both common and differential
  endpoints by the frozen paired rules under nominal and mismatch-bounded
  reads, with no single scenario more than five percent worse.
- `RESIDUAL-PROBE-ELIGIBLE` authorizes only one separately sealed non-learning
  physical intervention. `NO-TRAINING` closes this formulation negative.
  Neither branch authorizes neural training.
