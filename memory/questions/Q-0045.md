## Why it matters

The C2 weak-grid section of the SCI journal extension currently rests on
linearization evidence only: R283 (CLM-0630) measured the inter-area mode
damping gradient over aggregate inertia and tie-corridor strength on the
frozen plant, and R285 (CLM-0640) charted where that gradient is measurable.
The actual controller claim (CLM-0610: centralized differential allocation
improves normalized_sync_loss_hz2 by -24.35% and fast_inter_area_iae_hz_s by
-17.04% vs q0 on the sealed R279 bank) was measured only at nominal tie
strength (k=1). If the gain collapses when the tie corridor is weakened, the
C2 section overclaims; if it survives or degrades gracefully, the C2 section
gains a time-domain survival statement with a pre-registered verdict tree.
The sealed bank also spans four disturbance locations (PQ_0, PQ_1, PQ_Bus14,
PQ_Bus15); a location-grouped read of the same traces states whether the
gain is a location artifact.
