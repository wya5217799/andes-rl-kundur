## Why it matters

The validated 2Kv controller has exact zero sum before device projection, but
R300 covers homogeneous synchronous devices and explicitly leaves projection-
induced common/differential coupling unresolved.  This is a concrete mechanism
that fixed equal-gain control may not solve and that could require local SOC,
power-headroom or neighbour information.  It must be established by a cheap
deterministic probe before any neural residual is trained.

## Candidates

- Model requested power as a common DAPI component plus a graph-incidence
  differential residual, then apply heterogeneous per-device power, ramp and
  SOC-feasible projections.
- Measure executed common leakage, retained differential authority and
  feasibility under predeclared headroom patterns without reading performance
  outcomes.
- Compare fixed 2Kv with a deterministic headroom-aware edge-flow allocator;
  reject the mechanism if leakage is negligible or the deterministic repair
  closes it.
- Only if a reproducible residual failure and local-information necessity
  survive that gate, freeze a matched classical comparator, action authority
  and one neural smoke protocol in a later round.
