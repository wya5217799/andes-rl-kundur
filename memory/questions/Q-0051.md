## Why it matters

The current controller family fixes a common-inertia pulse, an equal-sharing
active-power droop--PI layer, and a heuristic edge-inertia allocation before
learning.  This isolates narrow experimental factors, but it does not derive
the control law from the nonlinear power-system DAE, quantify residual
common--differential coupling, justify the time-scale split, or establish that
inertia is the best differential actuator.  A multi-agent title also requires
the physical-agent, information, communication, and decentralized-execution
seams to be present in the mathematical problem rather than added only at the
policy implementation layer.

## Pre-commitment

1. Treat the full ANDES phasor-domain DAE as the truth model and distinguish it
   from every control-oriented reduction.
2. Compare equilibrium LTI/modal, descriptor-LPV/trajectory-linearized,
   reduced nonlinear DAE, and graph-local surrogate formulations against the
   same plant, actuator, uncertainty, and information contract.
3. Decompose common and differential coordinates without assuming exact
   decoupling; measure the cross-block coupling and time-scale separation.
4. Audit transient and modal authority of active power, virtual inertia, and
   virtual damping before choosing the controller input set.
5. Select the simplest model that preserves the relevant common-frequency,
   RoCoF, inter-area, storage, and constraint behavior across declared
   operating points and topology-strength conditions.
6. Define centralized and neighbour-distributed constrained controllers on the
   same physical action coordinates.  Neural learning is not authorized until
   the selected model and a strong classical controller pass validation.
7. Validate the reduction by non-learning simulations before controller or
   neural performance claims; analytical derivation alone is insufficient.

## Decision direction

- **DESCRIPTOR-LPV-SUFFICIENT:** a descriptor/LPV reduction preserves the
  registered modes, transient sensitivities, constraints, and coupling across
  the declared domain and supports constrained distributed synthesis.
- **NONLINEAR-MODEL-REQUIRED:** trajectory dependence or coupling error makes
  the LPV reduction inadequate, requiring reduced nonlinear DAE/NMPC.
- **DECOUPLING-NO-GO:** no tested coordinate/time-scale reduction supports the
  claimed decomposition over the declared domain; narrow the paper claim.
- **MULTIAGENT-SEAM-NOT-IDENTIFIED:** the physical action or information
  contract still reduces to centralized or scalar coordination, so a MARL
  headline remains unsupported.
