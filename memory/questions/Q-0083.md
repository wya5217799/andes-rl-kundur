## Why it matters

disturbance-aware estimator on the same development bank. The estimator's
nominal error poles have little margin below one, and no output mismatch or
noise was examined. The already registered separated-doublet holdout and five
output-mismatch modes are therefore the minimum independent test before any
physical execution can be considered.

  pass may open a separate physical reduced-model-versus-ANDES execution gate,
  but not distributed agents, reward, training, or EVAL.
