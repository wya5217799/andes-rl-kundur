## Why it matters

R351 proved only that three endpoint-local edge actions can traverse the
future policy's physical governor. It did not close the controller around a
disturbed nonlinear plant or show useful performance. The next gate must
therefore establish a fair deterministic baseline before residual headroom or
neural training can be reconsidered.

## Prospective contract

- The deployed deterministic arm consists of three independently evaluated
  edge actors. Each actor receives only its two endpoint observations, its own
  previous edge flow, and endpoint feasibility fields.
- The zero, local-controller, and diagnostic joint-information arms share
  exactly three tree-edge actions, the R351 governor, physical limits, timing,
  plant, disturbance bank, and endpoint metrics. The joint arm is an upper
  reference, not the paper's distributed baseline.
- Tune only the shared local frequency-difference and RoCoF-difference gains
  on the registered exposed development bank. Select once by the frozen rule,
  then bind the selected gains into a create-only formal seal before any
  holdout trajectory is executed.
- The untouched bank changes the disturbance waveform but not the two already
  qualified operating points. Claims are limited to this finite plant, these
  points, this waveform bank, and this controller instance.
- A positive result requires complete paired records, every structural and
  physical guard, at least 2% paired mean differential-energy improvement,
  no scenario with more than 5% differential or common-coordinate worsening,
  and exact requested-command zero sum within the registered tolerance.
- A pass authorizes only a new residual-headroom decision. A failure records
  bounded non-demonstration. Neither branch authorizes neural training.
