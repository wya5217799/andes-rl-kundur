## Why it matters

R366 made the deterministic controller and future learner physically and
informationally comparable, but the earlier programme failed when neural
actions had no useful dynamic residual to learn.  Training is justified only
if a strong fixed controller works and a separately labelled non-deployable
oracle still demonstrates conditional, time-varying direct-action headroom.

## Prospective contract

- Run zero action and all nine frozen deterministic candidates on the same
  eight balanced strong development scenarios with identical four-VSG plant,
  observations, M/D actions, limits, slew, timing, and heterogeneous damping.
- Select one global candidate using differential-frequency energy, then
  differential-power energy, subject to common-frequency, saturation, bound,
  slew, completion, and failure guards.
- Require at least 10% aggregate differential-frequency improvement versus
  zero action.  Replace the undefined relative action-variation comparison
  against zero by prospective absolute actuator guards; retain total variation
  as descriptive telemetry.
- Derive only after complete execution a per-scenario best-of-nine outcome
  oracle.  Require at least 5% incremental headroom, nonconstant executed M/D
  actions in every selected trace, and at least two distinct selected candidates.
- The oracle is not deployable and no result authorizes training.  Failure of
  either scientific gate stops this formulation without an algorithm sweep.
