## Why it matters

R56 / R57-α s50 collapse mechanism is now narrowed to a single root
cause: `TrainingMonitor` saves `best.pt` whenever a NEW best **train
reward** is seen. For s50:

```
ep 0: reward -104  (initial random rollout, env-warmup)
ep 3: reward -1181 (catastrophic env interaction, random actions)
ep 10: reward -5   (lucky random action sequence, BECOMES best.pt)
ep 20: ... env-warmup ends, training begins
ep 74: reward -70  (trained policy — WORSE than ep 10 random)
```

`best.pt` is locked at ep 10 — **before any gradient update**. The
6-axis eval of those initial untrained weights is 0.109 across both
R56 and R57-α. lr-warmup, longer training, gradient clipping, and
other training-time interventions are all powerless because they
cannot affect a checkpoint saved pre-training.

CLM-0065 / CLM-0067 are both gated on this question. If best.pt
were chosen by held-out eval score instead of train reward:
- s50's end-of-training policy (reward -70, but with trained LSTM
  weights) likely scores > 0.30 on the 6-axis — moving 5-seed mean
  from 0.3674 to ~0.43 (clearing H1α 0.40 threshold).
- Identifies the actually-best ckpt per seed, eliminating
  "lucky-early-random" artifacts that look good in training but
  are pre-training.
- Enables paper-grade reproducibility: the saved ckpt is the one
  that performs best on the disturbance scenarios paper actually
  cites.

## Candidates

(cheapest first)

- **Cheap fix**: add `--eval-every-N-eps` flag to `train.py` that
  every N episodes runs the trained actors through one paper-grade
  disturbance scenario, computes 6-axis, and saves best.pt only if
  score improves over a tracked best-eval-score. ~30 min impl. Cost:
  N × 2 scenarios × ~10 s eval = ~20 s eval per check. With N=5,
  total overhead ~5 % wall.
- **Hybrid**: save BOTH `best_train.pt` (current behavior) and
  `best_eval.pt` (new). Lets us check the eval-tracked one without
  losing the train-reward heuristic for monitoring. Cheaper for the
  monitor's "best reward callback" mechanism.
- **Eval-only fallback**: if `best_eval.pt` doesn't exist (no eval
  during training, legacy ckpt), fall back to `best.pt`. Makes the
  feature opt-in via `--track-best-by-eval`.
- **Periodic snapshots** (Q-0005 candidate "best-by-eval-score"
  alternative): save every-10-ep snapshots, score all post-hoc,
  pick the best for headline reporting. Costs more disk but no
  in-training eval overhead.
