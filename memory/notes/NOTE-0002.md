## Summary
A late-R57 audit found that V4's `paper-faithful` label overstated agreement with Yang et al. 2023: V4 adds a non-paper `PHI_ABS=50` frequency-restoration term, uses 1/178-rescaled action penalties, and scores on a 6-axis composite rather than the paper's cumulative global reward. R58 renamed V4's mode to `paper-faithful-modified` and introduced two new `V4Config` classmethods — `paper_strict_pure()` (paper Eq.14 nominal, `phi_abs=0, phi_h/d=1.0`) and `paper_strict_rescaled()` (`phi_abs=0, phi_h/d=0.0056`) — to empirically verify whether R18's penalty-rescale mechanism holds and whether algorithm rankings are reward-sensitive. A new module `paper_strict_eval.py` implements the paper's `compute_global_cum_rf` scalar metric.

## Key facts (claim candidates)
- V4's `PHI_ABS=50` shifts the optimization objective from frequency synchronization (paper `r^f`) to frequency restoration; at typical `df=0.1 Hz`, `PHI_ABS·r_abs` dominates `PHI_F·r^f` (R58 audit finding).
- Action penalty rescale `PHI_H=PHI_D=0.0056` (≈ 1/178) was introduced in R18 to prevent `r_h/r_f ≈ 36000:1` divergence given V4's wider action range; paper Eq.14 nominal is `phi_h=phi_d=1.0`.
- Existing `V4Config.paper_faithful()` classmethod is retained unchanged to preserve R56/R57 checkpoint reproducibility.

## Open threads
- If `paper-strict-pure` trains but `paper-strict-rescaled` does not, R18's rescale verdict is empirically falsified (ADR flagged as acceptable testable hypothesis).
- If `paper-faithful-modified` results are comparable to `paper-strict-rescaled`, PHI_ABS utility and CLM-0067 production decision may need re-issue.
