## Summary
Repository-wide hygiene is enforced by one machine-readable contract and validator; skills remain adapters.

## Key facts (claim candidates)
- Repository structure is governed by a separate contract and validator rather
  than by expanding the research-ledger validator.
- Existing debt is checked in as a visible baseline; new violations fail and
  resolved entries must be removed.
- Repository and external skills are adapters to the validator, not policy
  authorities or alternate research ledgers.
