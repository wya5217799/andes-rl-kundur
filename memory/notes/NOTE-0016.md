## Summary
A 16-hour session (R67–R75) evolved both the evaluator and the LSTM SOTA. The evaluator was upgraded from v2 to v3.0 (R69, +3 new axes: agent_min_activity, late_oscillation_inv, agent_P_balance) and then to v3.1 (R71, multiplicative gating: geo_mean(axes 1-8) × min(axes 9-11)) to prevent gating axes from being diluted. The 9th hyper axis (tau=0.001) was found optimal. LSTM 6-axis SOTA reached 0.4301 (s59+warmup=20, R75 W2). Ensemble was confirmed negative for LSTM (hidden-state divergence: all 4 HAWE configs underperform single best). 12 seeds tested, 7 healthy, 5 dead (~44% drift rate). Paper draft strongly recommended as next step with 4 tables and canonical figures already generated.
