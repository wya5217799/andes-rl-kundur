## Summary
Six-hour forensic audit session (2026-05-07) that identified and fixed 11 distinct bugs in the ANDES Kundur 4-VSG environment (R10-R19), producing the paper-faithful V4/V4.1 environment. Key env-level findings: IEEEG1 governor not entering DAE, G4 inertia zeroed by default, action range 17× too narrow, VSG_M0 too small (H₀=15→100s), PHI_D×action-range explosion (178×), M/D clamp floor missing, and the critical dt-bug (`current_t` ndarray reference causing 3× time-scale error). After all fixes, 3-seed × 200-ep V4.1 training produced anti-paper behavior: trained DDIC was 20-30% WORSE than no-control on max|Δf|, with r_d accounting for 91% of reward signal and action-collapse warnings in all seeds. The session concluded with five ranked hypotheses for anti-paper behavior (PHI_ABS=0 most likely root cause) and deferred further training pending R20-R23 probes.

## Key facts (claim candidates)
- V4.1 env fixed 11 bugs spanning DAE construction, action mapping, reward formula, and time integration; dt fix alone changed effective timestep from 0.6s to 0.2s per step.
- Post-fix V4.1 baseline (no control) reached paper-magnitude: 1.3-1.7× on max|Δf| for LS1/LS2 scenarios.
- V4.1 SAC training (3 seeds × 200 ep, PHI_F=100, PHI_H=PHI_D=0.0056, PHI_ABS=0): max|Δf| 0.222/0.202/0.223 Hz for LS1 vs no-control 0.183 Hz — worse than no-control in all 3 seeds.
- r_d component dominated reward (91% across all seeds); action-collapse warnings present in all seeds.
- PI-AC physics regularization ruled out as methodology error: ANDES TDS enforces swing equation analytically; J residual = 1.069e-07 (floating-point noise), gradient = 0.
- CTDE feasibility confirmed: param ratio 1.10× (137K→151K params), CPU-tractable, but deferred until paper-aligned baseline achieved.
- Hypothesis ranking for anti-paper behavior: PHI_ABS=0 removes absolute-frequency recovery signal (A) > r_h paper formula forces net-zero ΔH (E) > entropy collapse (C) > episode length (D) > ring-sync degeneracy (B).
- Proposed V4.2 test: re-enable PHI_ABS=50, designed but training killed before run.
