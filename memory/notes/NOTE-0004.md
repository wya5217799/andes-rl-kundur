## Summary
V4 uses two approximate wind-farm proxies: G4 at Bus 11 as GENROU with `ZERO_G4_INERTIA=True`, and W2 at Bus 8 as GENCLS M=0.1. A proposal to upgrade these to ANDES REGCA1 (industry-standard Type-4 wind model) was initially framed as "more paper-faithful." An R80 cross-reference of the paper's Sec.II revealed the opposite: paper explicitly states "dynamics of the inner loop can be neglected," making GENCLS (pure swing equation) closer to the paper's stated assumption than REGCA1 (which adds PE current inner loop, limiters, and LVRT). R80 created V5 (`andes_vsg_env_v5.py` + `v5_config.py`) as a V4 subclass upgrading G4/W2 to REGCA1+REECA1, but explicitly framed as an ANDES engineering plant-upgrade and paper-deviation, not paper-faithful. V4 remains the canonical paper-path env; V5 checkpoints use the `r80+_*` namespace to avoid contaminating V4 results.

## Key facts (claim candidates)
- Paper Sec.II explicitly states inner-loop dynamics are neglected; REGCA1 contradicts this — GENCLS M=0.1 is the more paper-aligned plant model per the paper's own framing.
- V5 checkpoints use `r80+_*` namespace separation; cross-eval of V4 / V5_w2_only / V5_gencls_fall runs via `r80_v5_cross_eval.py`.
- V5 results cannot be used as "paper-equivalent SOTA" in the main result unless a Phase D (C1) breakthrough occurs.
