## Summary
The R58–R66 session evaluated 36 checkpoints (9 historical baselines × 3 seeds + 18 paper-strict ckpts + 9 radsec ckpts) across 20 scenarios (2 paper anchors + 18 random) using paper Sec.IV-C total_cum_rf metric. The key evaluation finding is that V4 historical TD3 (td3_norm_h64_s50, PHI_ABS=50) achieves -0.196 — best on paper-metric — while LSTM and SAC rank lower under paper metric despite LSTM being SOTA on the project's 6-axis ranker. This reveals a metric-rank inversion: the 6-axis project ranker and paper Sec.IV-C metric disagree on algorithm ordering. The production strategy at end of R65: Mode paper-metric → TD3 R64 combo -0.124; Mode paper-faithful → SAC R65 combo -0.194; Mode 6-axis → LSTM R57 SOTA 0.543.

## Key facts (claim candidates)
- Metric-rank inversion: 6-axis ranking (LSTM > TD3 > SAC) does NOT preserve under paper Sec.IV-C metric (V4 TD3 wins paper-metric, SAC wins under paper-strict-radsec)
- Best overall on paper-metric: td3_norm_h64_s50 (V4 historical) = -0.196; production 3-seed TD3 combo R64: -0.124 (+37.5pp robust vs paper DDIC -0.465)
- Paper-faithful SAC SOTA (R65 W1, rad/s config): 3-seed mean -0.194, best single -0.163 (+32.7pp robust vs paper DDIC)

- Three-mode production decision: paper-metric → TD3 R67 combo; paper-faithful → SAC R68 W1 combo; 6-axis → LSTM R57 SOTA
