## Summary
ADR-0009 separates global skills, project governance, manuscript lines, document lifecycle, and venue gates.

## Key facts (claim candidates)
- Global academic skills are project-neutral and have no independent authority
  to write ANDES ledger, experiment, or manuscript state.
- Every active manuscript line owns one `LINE.md`, one exclusive write scope,
  and one `ARTIFACTS.json`; shared project evidence is read-only.
- Generated research and review output is ephemeral by default. A promoted
  artifact records purpose, inputs, producer, authority, status, supersession,
  and review date, with at most one active canonical artifact per purpose.
- Venue selection proceeds through constraints, shortlist, lock, and
  pre-submission refresh; official journal sources govern policy while the
  author or PI confirms institutional ranking requirements.
