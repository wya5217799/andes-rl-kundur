## Summary
R01–R36 implicitly pursued Simulink-to-ANDES 1:1 numerical reproduction of paper Fig.6 numbers, but the paper withholds all Simulink configuration details (wind model type, load model, line damping, TDS solver, disturbance injection). R08 showed that even at H=300 (physical limit) max_df remains 2× the paper value (0.266 vs 0.13 Hz), and R10–R17 invested 120 min wall time in forensic Simulink calibration with zero root-cause found. R80 formally closed this path: ANDES is the single canonical simulation platform, Yang et al. 2023 is used as an algorithm/topology/reward design reference (not a numerical benchmark), and residual max_df discrepancies are documented as platform-attributable differences in future writeups.

## Key facts (claim candidates)
- R08 Finding 2: H=300 physical-limit max_df is 2× paper Fig.6 (0.266 vs 0.13 Hz); root cause attributed to undisclosed Simulink defaults that cannot be reverse-engineered from paper text.
- R10–R17 forensic effort: 120 min wall time, zero root cause found — "找 Simulink 默认值匹配 ANDES" path confirmed zero ROI.
- Paper writeup must explicitly name ANDES as simulation platform and attribute any platform-level numerical divergence to platform difference, not to algorithm or parameter error.
