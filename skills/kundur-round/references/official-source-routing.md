# Official-source routing

Open official publisher or society pages at audit time. Record the page heading
and access date because requirements change.

## IJEPES

- Guide for Authors:
  <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-electrical-power-and-energy-systems/publish/guide-for-authors>
- Journal and scope:
  <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-electrical-power-and-energy-systems>

Verify the selected article type, review model, manuscript format and length,
abstract and keyword constraints, highlights and graphical-abstract status,
editable-source requirements, figures and supplements, data statement,
authorship/CRediT, funding, conflicts, generative-AI disclosure, permissions,
submission declaration, and checklist.

## EPSR

- Guide for Authors:
  <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/electric-power-systems-research/publish/guide-for-authors>
- Journal and scope:
  <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/electric-power-systems-research>

Verify the same publisher-wide fields as IJEPES plus EPSR-specific article types,
scope boundary, formatting, length, and required submission artifacts.

## IEEE PES and TPWRS

- TPWRS journal page:
  <https://ieee-pes.org/publications/transactions-on-power-systems/>
- IEEE PES Transactions author information:
  <https://ieee-pes.org/publications/authors-kit/information-for-authors-of-ieee-power-energy-society-transactions-papers/>
- IEEE journal submission checklist:
  <https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/submit-your-article-for-peer-review/checklist-for-submitting-your-article-for-peer-review/>
- IEEE article structure:
  <https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-the-text-of-your-article/structure-your-article/>

Verify journal scope, first-submission page limit, template, prior conference
disclosure, originality, ORCID, author metadata, abstract and keywords, graphics,
supplemental material, data/code policy, AI policy, copyright, and any overlength
or open-access implications.

## Other journals

Start from the journal's publisher-owned page. Follow society author kits and
submission-portal instructions when they are explicitly incorporated. Use
third-party summaries only to locate the official source; exclude them from the
compliance decision.

## Source conflict

When official pages disagree:

1. prefer the journal- and article-type-specific instruction over a generic
   publisher page;
2. prefer the version explicitly marked current;
3. record both sources and the conflict;
4. obtain written clarification when the conflict affects eligibility or a hard
   submission requirement.
