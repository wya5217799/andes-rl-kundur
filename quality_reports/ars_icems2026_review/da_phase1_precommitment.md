# Devil's Advocate Phase-1 Precommitment

This record was produced before the reviewer received the manuscript.

## Contract interpretation

- D1 methodology rigor: distinguish the claimed MARL mechanism from simpler explanations through matched comparators, development/evaluation separation, seed handling, method identity, uncertainty, and reproducibility.
- D2 domain accuracy: test the meanings of decoupling-oriented, coordination, paralleled VSGs, and MARL against the actual model and controller.
- D3 argumentative coherence: challenge whether the evidence uniquely supports the title-level claims rather than classical control, lucky-seed selection, privileged information, or tuning.
- D4 cross-disciplinary relevance: reconstruct the agent/environment/information structure and its mapping to power-system control.
- D5 writing and structure: judge whether a six-page conference paper is intelligible and auditable.

## Scoring thresholds fixed before paper access

- D1 block if the central mechanism is unidentifiable because of leakage, an unmatched comparison, privileged access, or an inability to distinguish MARL from a simpler explanation. Warn for incomplete seed, uncertainty, split, baseline, information-structure, or reproducibility reporting.
- D2 block if a title-level construct is materially misdefined or contradicted. Warn if terminology or the physical mapping is ambiguous.
- D3 block if the main conclusion does not follow or an alternative explanation defeats the claimed novelty. Warn for an overextended mechanism or title.
- D4 block if the central interdisciplinary mapping is invalid or cannot be evaluated. Warn when important mappings are left implicit.
- D5 block if presentation prevents reconstruction. Warn for excessive density, undefined symbols, or weak captions.

The contract was acknowledged as terminal before Phase 2.
