# ADR-0019: Separate the Yang-compatible M/D decoupling-MARL successor

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-15
- **Deciders:** repository owner and Codex research mission

## Decision

The stopped `paralleled-vsg-marl` experiment route cannot be reopened by another learner, but the fixed title still admits a materially different Yang-compatible question. We therefore register `yang-md-decoupling-marl` as a separate title-goal line: it preserves four VSG-owned inertia/droop actions and local-plus-neighbour decentralized execution, changes the benchmark to prospectively frozen heterogeneous common/differential excitation, and treats common-mode no-harm as a constraint on differential-mode improvement. Old checkpoints, values, claims, thresholds, and prose remain non-transferable; only implementation and failure boundaries may be revalidated.

## Consequences

- The old line remains experiment-side stopped and readable as bounded evidence.
- The new line owns any future positive support for the fixed title.
- Its first evidence gate is non-learning joint headroom, not MARL training.
- Coordinate decomposition, reward improvement, or centralized training alone cannot establish decoupling or coordination.
